Abstract

Construction project management is increasingly focused on cost-efficiency and risk reduction. High-precision land surveying serves as the primary quantitative instrument for economic optimization in civil engineering. This paper explores how accurate geospatial data prevents fiscal leakages caused by earthwork miscalculations, structural redesigns, and legal boundary disputes. By integrating Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS) and Digital Terrain Models (DTM), engineers can achieve sub-centimeter accuracy in site planning. The study concludes that an initial investment in professional surveying typically yields a 10:1 return on investment by eliminating unforeseen operational expenditures.

1. Introduction

In the lifecycle of a construction project, the pre-construction phase dictates the financial trajectory of the entire endeavor. Land surveying is often viewed as a peripheral cost; however, empirical evidence suggests it is the bedrock of project economy. In geographically diverse regions such as Bali, topographical variances can lead to significant discrepancies in material procurement if not mapped with geodetic precision.

2. Methodology: Volumetric Accuracy and Cost Control

The surveyor's role in cost control is most evident in earthwork calculations. Utilizing the Average End Area method, the volume of material ($V$) is determined to prevent over-ordering of soil or concrete:

$$V = L \times \frac{A_1 + A_2}{2}$$

Where $L$ represents the distance between cross-sections and $A$ represents the area of the cut or fill. Without precise topographical data, the variables $A_1$ and $A_2$ are based on estimates, which historically leads to budget overruns of 15% to 25% in the excavation phase.

3. Structural Integrity and Long-Term Savings

Accurate surveying ensures that the building load is distributed according to the structural design. Misalignment in column placement leads to eccentric loading, necessitating expensive retrofitting. The precision of verticality is monitored using the following tilt formula:

$$\Delta = H \times \tan \theta$$

Where $\Delta$ is the horizontal displacement and $\theta$ is the measured angle of deviation. By maintaining $\theta$ within strict engineering tolerances, surveyors prevent structural failures that could result in total capital loss.

2. Analisis Teknis: Menghindari "Pajak Kesalahan" Struktur

Kesalahan penempatan titik pondasi hanya sebesar 5 cm dapat mengubah distribusi beban seluruh bangunan. Dalam teknik sipil, pergeseran ini menciptakan momen tambahan yang memaksa struktur diperkuat dengan biaya yang sangat mahal. Surveyor memastikan setiap milimeter bangunan berada pada koordinat yang benar sesuai rencana teknis.

Untuk menghitung daya dukung tanah yang optimal berdasarkan elevasi hasil survey, digunakan parameter berikut:

$$q_{all} = \frac{q_u}{FS}$$

Di mana $q_u$ adalah daya dukung ultimat dan $FS$ adalah faktor keamanan. Data elevasi yang salah dari surveyor amatir dapat menyebabkan kesalahan penentuan kedalaman pondasi, yang berujung pada pemborosan material beton.
