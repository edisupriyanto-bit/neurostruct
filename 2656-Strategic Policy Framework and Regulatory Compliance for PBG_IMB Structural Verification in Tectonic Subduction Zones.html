Abstract

The transition from the traditional Building Construction Permit (Izin Mendirikan Bangunan or IMB) to the Building Approval (Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung or PBG) through the centralized SIMBG electronic platform marks a significant shift in infrastructure compliance in Indonesia. This paper investigates the administrative barriers, mathematical calculation methods for structural safety metrics, and regulatory workflows required to secure building clearances in highly regulated and geologically sensitive tourism macro-regions like Bali. Underestimating subduction zone dynamics and zoning laws often leads to prolonged delays and critical submission rejections. We establish an engineering framework that integrates automated calculations for technical parameters with legal requirements. The ultimate objective is to provide developers, architects, and engineers with an optimized compliance matrix that reduces approvals timelines while ensuring safety under seismic risk criteria.

1. Introduction

Securing a building permit is no longer merely a bureaucratic formality; it has evolved into a highly technical structural verification process. With the enactment of the Job Creation Law (UU Cipta Kerja), Indonesia replaced the conventional IMB framework with the PBG platform operated via the centralized Sistem Informasi Manajemen Bangunan Gedung (SIMBG). This transition was designed to standardize safety protocols, eliminate localized corruption, and ensure structural compliance across municipal boundaries.

However, international investors and regional developers operating in specialized economies, such as the tourism-driven coastal and mountainous landscapes of Bali, face distinct challenges. These challenges include navigating strict cultural setbacks (Sempadan Jurang/Pantai), building height limitations derived from local spiritual edicts (maximum 15 meters), and demanding seismic calculations. This section breaks down the administrative techniques and engineering justifications needed to navigate the modern SIMBG workflow without relying on unauthorized third-party fixers.

A. Spatial Planning & Zoning Validation (KKPR)

Before any architectural or structural calculations can be assessed, the project must obtain the Kesesuaian Kegiatan Pemanfaatan Ruang (KKPR). The spatial layout engine validates the geographical coordinates against provincial zoning maps (RTRW), calculating the permissible Building Coverage Ratio (Koefisien Dasar Bangunan or KDB):

$$KDB = \frac{A_{ground\_floor}}{A_{total\_lot}} \times 100\% \le KDB_{limit}$$

Where $A_{ground\_floor}$ represents the horizontal footprint of the building envelope, and $A_{total\_lot}$ represents the total land parcel size.

B. Architectural Compliance and Environmental Coefficients

Architectural evaluation checks building volume utilization via the Floor Area Ratio (Koefisien Lantai Bangunan or KLB) and green open space allocations via the Koefisien Dasar Hijau (KDH):

$$KLB = \frac{\sum A_{floor\_total}}{A_{total\_lot}}$$

$$KDH = \frac{A_{permeable\_green}}{A_{total\_lot}} \times 100\% \ge KDH_{limit}$$

In high-density eco-tourism zones across Bali, the $KDH_{limit}$ is heavily monitored to prevent flash flooding and manage groundwater depletion.

C. Structural Engineering and Seismic Validation

The defining technical obstacle in securing a PBG is the structural calculation review. The building must withstand lateral forces generated by tectonic activity. The basic design base shear ($V$) is determined using a linear static formulation that complies with national seismic codes:

$$V = C_s \times W$$

Where $C_s$ represents the seismic response coefficient, and $W$ represents the total effective dead load plus applicable live loads of the structure. The seismic response coefficient is calculated using:

$$C_s = \frac{S_{DS}}{\left(\frac{R}{I_e}\right)}$$

Where:

  • $S_{DS}$ = Design spectral response acceleration at short periods.
  • $R$ = Response modification coefficient (based on the structural ductility system, e.g., Special Reinforced Concrete Moment Frames).
  • $I_e$ = Importance factor of the building occupancy category.

Submitting these formulas, alongside soil profile data obtained through standard SPT (Standard Penetration Test) or CPT (Cone Penetration Test) soundings, is mandatory to verify that the building will remain stable during seismic events.

4. Discussion: Addressing Regional Compliance Bottlenecks in Bali

The most common cause of application rejections within the SIMBG system is a mismatch between the certificate of land ownership (Sertifikat Hak Milik) and the proposed spatial layout. In Bali, sub-district offices frequently flag projects that encroach upon protected Subak agricultural canal buffers or river gorges.

Furthermore, if the building already exists and the owner is seeking retrospective approval, a standard PBG cannot be issued. Instead, the owner must apply for a Sertifikat Laik Fungsi (SLF) alongside a retrofitted PBG. This process requires a comprehensive structural safety audit, which involves performing destructive or non-destructive testing (such as Schmidt Hammer tests and ultrasonic pulse velocity tests) to verify the concrete's actual compressive capacity.

1. Pendahuluan

Banyak pemilik proyek, ekspatriat, dan kontraktor di Indonesia, khususnya di Bali, mengeluhkan lamanya proses penerbitan izin mendirikan bangunan. Ketahuilah bahwa sejak dikeluarkannya UU Cipta Kerja, istilah IMB (Izin Mendirikan Bangunan) telah resmi dihapus dan digantikan oleh PBG (Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung). Pengurusan PBG kini dilakukan secara online melalui satu pintu, yaitu sistem SIMBG.

Masalahnya, sistem baru ini tidak lagi menggunakan sistem "kedekatan personal" atau jalur belakang. Jika berkas gambar teknik dan perhitungan struktur Anda salah sedikit saja atau tidak sinkron, sistem akan langsung menolak aplikasi Anda secara otomatis (reject). Artikel ini akan membongkar rahasia dan metode terbaru cara mengurus PBG/IMB agar cepat disetujui oleh Tim Profesi Ahli (TPA) Dinas PUPR, tanpa hambatan, dan bebas dari biaya tambahan yang tidak resmi.

2. Alur Rahasia Pengurusan PBG Perdana Lewat SIMBG yang Jarang Diketahui

Untuk memastikan pengurusan PBG Anda berjalan lancar, proses permohonan harus dibagi menjadi tiga tahapan verifikasi yang runtut dan tidak boleh terbalik.

Tahap 1: Pengurusan KKPR (Kesesuaian Kegiatan Pemanfaatan Ruang)

Dulu dikenal sebagai ITR (Informasi Tata Ruang). Sebelum membuat gambar arsitektur, Anda wajib memastikan koordinat lahan Anda masuk dalam zona yang boleh dibangun (bukan zona jalur hijau atau pertanian basah). Di sini, Koefisien Dasar Bangunan (KDB) dihitung secara ketat menggunakan rumus:

$$\text{KDB} = \frac{\text{Luas Lantai Dasar Bangunan}}{\text{Luas Total Lahan}} \times 100\%$$

Jika zona lahan menentukan KDB maksimal 40%, dan lahan Anda seluas $1000\ m^2$, maka luas lantai dasar bangunan Anda tidak boleh melebihi $400\ m^2$.

Tahap 2: Standardisasi Arsitektur & Koefisien Dasar Hijau (KDH)

Arsitek sering kali mengabaikan batas resapan air demi mengejar estetika desain. Padahal, pemeriksa SIMBG akan menghitung nilai KDH dengan rumus:

$$\text{KDH} = \frac{\text{Luas Area Hijau Terbuka (Resapan)}}{\text{Luas Total Lahan}} \times 100\%$$

Untuk wilayah Bali, pelestarian lingkungan sangat dijaga ketat. Jika luas resapan air kurang dari batas minimum, maka berkas arsitektur akan dikembalikan untuk direvisi.

Tahap 3: Validasi Perhitungan Struktur dan Beban Gempa (SNI 1726:2019)

Ini adalah batu sandungan terbesar bagi para pemohon PBG. Dinas PUPR mewajibkan adanya lampiran hitungan struktur (Structural Analysis Report) yang membuktikan bahwa bangunan Anda aman dari risiko gempa. Rumus gaya geser dasar gempa ($V$) yang wajib disimulasikan dalam software analisis struktur adalah:

$$V = C_s \times W$$

Nilai koefisien $C_s$ harus disesuaikan dengan peta penyerapan gempa di wilayah masing-masing (misalnya Badung Selatan memiliki nilai percepatan batuan dasar $S_{DS}$ yang sangat tinggi dibandingkan Bali Utara).

4. Solusi Bangunan Terlanjur Berdiri: Cara Mengurus SLF (Sertifikat Laik Fungsi)

Bagaimana jika bangunan vila atau hotel Anda di Bali sudah terlanjur dibangun tanpa IMB/PBG? Tenang, Anda tidak perlu membongkar bangunan tersebut. Solusi hukum terbarunya adalah mengurus SLF (Sertifikat Laik Fungsi) sekaligus mengajukan PBG Retroadopsi.

Proses ini membutuhkan jasa Pengkaji Teknis resmi yang akan melakukan audit forensik struktur bangunan, meliputi:

  • Pengujian mutu beton eksisting menggunakan Concrete Hammer Test atau Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV).
  • Pemeriksaan kelurusan bangunan (verticality check).
  • Evaluasi sistem proteksi kebakaran dan instalasi listrik.
