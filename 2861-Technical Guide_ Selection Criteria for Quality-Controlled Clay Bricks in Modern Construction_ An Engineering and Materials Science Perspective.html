Abstract (English)

The selection of high-quality clay bricks is a fundamental yet critical decision in construction, directly influencing structural integrity, durability, thermal performance, and long-term project costs. This paper presents a comprehensive, engineering-driven guide for contractors, merging empirical field knowledge with established materials science principles. We systematically analyze key quality indicators—including compressive strength, water absorption, dimensional tolerance, efflorescence potential, and acoustic properties—correlating them with production methods (traditional vs. industrial). The discussion is supported by data from international journals on ceramic materials and civil engineering. Furthermore, this guide introduces a structured decision-making framework to assist contractors in vendor assessment and material verification. 1. Introduction

Clay brick masonry remains a cornerstone of global construction, valued for its fire resistance, thermal mass, and aesthetic versatility. In regions like Indonesia, and specifically Bali, its use is ubiquitous in both traditional and contemporary architecture. However, significant quality variability exists within the market, stemming from differences in raw material composition, firing processes, and quality control protocols. Substandard bricks lead to pathologies such as excessive cracking, moisture ingress, poor insulation, and reduced structural lifespan, ultimately increasing maintenance costs and safety risks.

This paper bridges the gap between academic materials science and practical on-site application. Its objective is to provide contractors with a technically robust, evidence-based framework for selecting quality clay bricks, moving beyond heuristic "rule-of-thumb" methods. The methodology involves a literature review of material properties, synthesis of international standards (ASTM C62, C216; SNI 15-2094-2000), and the formulation of a practical inspection checklist.

2. Literature Review: Key Quality Parameters from an Engineering Perspective

2.1. Compressive Strength

Compressive strength is the primary indicator of a brick's ability to carry load. Research by Zhang et al. (2019) in Construction and Building Materials demonstrates a direct correlation between firing temperature (>950°C) and enhanced crystalline structure, leading to higher strength. For low-rise load-bearing walls, a minimum compressive strength of 10-15 MPa is typically recommended (ACI 530.1). Strength can be estimated on-site through simple, non-destructive methods like the Schmidt hammer rebound test, though lab testing provides definitive results.

2.2. Water Absorption

Water absorption (WA) critically affects durability, frost resistance (where applicable), and bond with mortar. Bricks with high WA (>20%) are prone to moisture-related expansion, salt efflorescence, and reduced thermal performance. A study by Cultrone et al. (2004) in Ceramics International established that optimal WA for general use should be between 12-18%. A simple field test involves weighing a dry brick, submerging it in water for 24 hours, and re-weighing it. WA (%) = [(Wet Weight - Dry Weight) / Dry Weight] * 100

2.3. Dimensional Uniformity and Visual Inspection

Dimensional consistency ensures uniform mortar joints, structural alignment, and aesthetic finish. Variations should not exceed ±3mm per unit as per standards. Visual inspection should note:

  • Color: Uniform red hue indicates consistent firing. Black cores or patches signal under-firing.
  • Texture and Sound: A well-fired brick has a slightly vitrified surface and produces a clear, metallic ringing sound when struck.
  • Edges and Corners: Should be sharp and intact, not crumbled.

2.4. Efflorescence Potential

Efflorescence, the white salt deposit, is aesthetically damaging and can indicate excessive soluble salts. It is influenced by raw clay purity and water used during production. Bricks should be tested per ASTM C67 by boiling in distilled water and observing for salt deposition.

3. Proposed Technical Selection Framework for Contractors

We propose a 5-step decision matrix:

  1. Define Application Requirements: Load-bearing vs. non-load-bearing, exposed vs. plastered finish, seismic zone considerations (highly relevant for Bali).
  2. Supplier Technical Audit: Request certified lab test reports for compressive strength and WA. Visit the kiln to observe firing consistency and raw material storage.
  3. On-Site Sampling and Simple Testing: Conduct the water absorption, sound, and visual tests on a random sample from the delivery batch.
  4. Cost-Performance Analysis: Calculate not just per-unit cost, but cost per unit of strength or durability (e.g., cost/MPa). Factor in reduced mortar use from dimensional accuracy.
  5. Batch Acceptance/Rejection: Establish clear pass/fail criteria based on the above before full delivery.

4. Case Context: The Bali Construction Market

Bali's construction boom, catering to tourism and residential demand, places unique stresses on material supply chains. The humid tropical climate exacerbates moisture-related brick failures. Furthermore, the island's rich architectural heritage often integrates brick aesthetically, demanding high visual quality. Contractors must navigate a market with both highly automated factories and traditional kampung kilns. Understanding this context is vital for appropriate specification.

2. Tinjauan Pustaka: Parameter Kualitas Kunci dari Perspektif Teknik

2.1. Kuat Tekan

Kuat tekan adalah indikator utama kemampuan bata untuk menahan beban. Penelitian oleh Zhang et al. (2019) di Construction and Building Materials menunjukkan korelasi langsung antara suhu pembakaran (>950°C) dan struktur kristalin yang ditingkatkan, menghasilkan kekuatan yang lebih tinggi. Untuk dinding penahan beban rendah, kuat tekan minimum 10-15 MPa biasanya direkomendasikan (ACI 530.1). Kekuatan dapat diperkirakan di lokasi melalui metode non-destruktif sederhana seperti uji pantul Schmidt hammer, meskipun pengujian lab memberikan hasil yang definitif.

2.2. Penyerapan Air

Penyerapan air (WA) sangat mempengaruhi daya tahan, ketahanan beku (jika berlaku), dan ikatan dengan mortar. Bata dengan WA tinggi (>20%) rentan terhadap ekspansi terkait kelembaban, efflorescence garam, dan kinerja termal yang berkurang. Sebuah studi oleh Cultrone et al. (2004) di Ceramics International menetapkan bahwa WA optimal untuk penggunaan umum harus antara 12-18%. Uji lapangan sederhana melibatkan penimbangan bata kering, perendaman dalam air selama 24 jam, dan penimbangan ulang. WA (%) = [(Berat Basah - Berat Kering) / Berat Kering] * 100

2.3. Keseragaman Dimensi dan Inspeksi Visual

Konsistensi dimensi memastikan sambungan mortar yang seragam, keselarasan struktural, dan penyelesaian estetika. Variasi tidak boleh melebihi ±3mm per unit sesuai standar. Inspeksi visual harus mencatat:

  • Warna: Warna merah seragam menunjukkan pembakaran yang konsisten. Inti atau bercak hitam menandakan pembakaran kurang.
  • Tekstur dan Suara: Bata yang dibakar dengan baik memiliki permukaan sedikit termakan dan menghasilkan suara dering logam yang jernih saat dipukul.
  • Tepi dan Sudut: Harus tajam dan utuh, tidak remuk.

2.4. Potensi Efflorescence

Efflorescence, endapan garam putih, merusak estetika dan dapat menunjukkan garam larut berlebihan. Hal ini dipengaruhi oleh kemurnian tanah liat mentah dan air yang digunakan selama produksi. Bata harus diuji sesuai ASTM C67 dengan merebus dalam air suling dan mengamati pengendapan garam.

3. Kerangka Kerja Seleksi Teknis yang Diusulkan untuk Kontraktor

Kami mengusulkan matriks keputusan 5 langkah:

  1. Tentukan Persyaratan Aplikasi: Penahan beban vs. non-penahan beban, finishing ekspos vs. diplester, pertimbangan zona seismik (sangat relevan untuk Bali).
  2. Audit Teknis Pemasok: Minta laporan uji lab bersertifikat untuk kuat tekan dan WA. Kunjungi kiln untuk mengamati konsistensi pembakaran dan penyimpanan bahan baku.
  3. Sampling dan Pengujian Sederhana di Lokasi: Lakukan uji penyerapan air, suara, dan visual pada sampel acak dari batch pengiriman.
  4. Analisis Biaya-Kinerja: Hitung bukan hanya biaya per unit, tetapi biaya per unit kekuatan atau daya tahan (mis., biaya/MPa). Pertimbangkan pengurangan penggunaan mortar dari akurasi dimensi.
  5. Penerimaan/Penolakan Batch: Tetapkan kriteria lulus/gagal yang jelas berdasarkan hal di atas sebelum pengiriman penuh.
