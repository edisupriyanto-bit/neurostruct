Recommendations emphasize proactive engineering consultancy, Dispute Adjudication Boards (DAB), and specialized structural solutions. 1. Introduction

Bali's rapid infrastructure and tourism-driven construction boom has led to a surge in complex projects, from luxury villas and hotels in Uluwatu and Canggu to major infrastructure like airports, roads, and coastal developments. However, this growth is accompanied by frequent disputes arising from unique geotechnical, regulatory, cultural, and environmental challenges.

International literature highlights that construction disputes globally can account for 10-30% of project costs in delays and claims. In developing island contexts like Bali, factors such as volcanic soil, high seismic activity, monsoon climates, and overlapping customary (adat) and national laws exacerbate risks.

This paper examines the role of specialized engineering consultancies in dispute prevention and resolution, positioning them as critical intermediaries between owners, contractors, and regulators.
