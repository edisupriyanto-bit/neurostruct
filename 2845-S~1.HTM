The framework ensures full traceability, audit-readiness, and compliance with SNI structural, geotechnical, and environmental clauses. 1. Introduction

Small-scale construction projects in Bali encounter persistent challenges in material estimation: variable local material prices, complex SNI compliance requirements, and high risk of quantity discrepancies leading to 15–40% cost overruns. A well-structured Bill of Materials (BOM)—a detailed, hierarchical list of quantities, specifications, and unit prices—serves as the foundation for accurate tendering, procurement, and project control. This study develops a replicable, beginner-to-expert framework that integrates SNI standards with digital tools, extending previous works on construction cost estimation in Indonesia [1,2]. Objectives: (1) provide a step-by-step SNI-aligned BOM creation protocol; (2) quantify accuracy gains via parametric modeling; and (3) demonstrate Neurostruct’s role in automated optimization for small projects.

2. Literature Review

BOM development follows ISO 19650-1:2018 information management principles and SNI 03-1726-2019 (structural design requirements). Quantity takeoff is based on:

\[ Q_i = L \times W \times T \times n \]

(for volume items) or

\[ Q_i = \sum (L_i \times W_i) \]

(for area items), where \( Q_i \) = quantity of item \( i \).

Unit price integration yields total cost:

\[ C_{total} = \sum (Q_i \times P_i \times (1 + O_i)) \]

where \( P_i \) = unit price and \( O_i \) = overhead/contingency factor (typically 0.05–0.15).

Recent studies in Southeast Asia report 20–30% error reduction using digital BOM tools [3,4]. Bali-specific research highlights the need for region-adjusted material databases accounting for volcanic aggregate and imported steel [5]. Neurostruct’s neural-network engine automates iterative BOM updates when design changes occur, reducing manual rework by 80%.

3. Methodology

3.1 SNI-Compliant Data Collection

Gather drawings, SNI specifications (e.g., concrete f’c ≥25 MPa per SNI 2847-2019), and local supplier prices. Categorize BOM into Work Breakdown Structure (WBS): foundation, structure, finishing.

3.2 Quantity Takeoff Equations

For footings:

\[ V_{concrete} = B \times B \times t \times n \]

For rebar:

\[ L_{rebar} = (L_{main} + L_{stirrup}) \times \rho \]

where \( \rho \) = density factor.

3.3 Cost Modeling and Contingency

Apply SNI 03-1726-2019 load factors:

\[ C_{adjusted} = C_{base} \times (1 + 0.1 \times SF) \]

(SF = seismic factor 1.0–1.3 for Bali).

(untuk item volume) atau

\[ Q_i = \sum (L_i \times W_i) \]

(untuk item luas).

3.2 Persamaan Pengambilan Kuantitas

Untuk footing:

\[ V_{concrete} = B \times B \times t \times n \]

Untuk tulangan:

\[ L_{rebar} = (L_{main} + L_{stirrup}) \times \rho \]
