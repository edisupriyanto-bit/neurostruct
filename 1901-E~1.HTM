1. Introduction

Uneven wall surfaces are common in masonry construction using brick, batako (concrete block), or stone, especially in low-cost and medium-scale projects. Deviations can reach 10–30 mm over a 2-meter length, making direct plastering difficult and prone to cracking or delamination.

The goal of plastering on irregular surfaces is to achieve a flat, smooth, and durable finish while maintaining structural integrity. Standard acceptance criteria for finished plastered walls are usually ±3 mm deviation under a 2 m straight edge.

Basic approach involves:

1. Surface preparation and cleaning

2. Application of bonding agent or scratch coat

3. Leveling with guide strips (screeds)

4. Filling and floating in multiple layers

5. Final troweling and curing

In tropical climates like Bali, high temperature and humidity accelerate drying, increasing the risk of plastic shrinkage cracking. This paper presents a complete technical guide based on engineering best practices.

2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed research on rendering and plastering highlights the importance of substrate preparation and controlled thickness. Studies show that uneven substrates increase the likelihood of map cracking and debonding. Polymer-modified mortars and fibers significantly improve performance on irregular surfaces. In tropical conditions, research stresses the need for extended curing periods and moisture-retaining methods to mitigate rapid evaporation.

3. Methodology and Technical Guidelines

3.1 Surface Preparation

Remove loose particles, dust, oil, and efflorescence. Dampen the wall 24 hours before plastering. Apply bonding slurry (cement + water + adhesive) on smooth concrete surfaces.

3.2 Scratch Coat and Leveling

Apply a 8–12 mm rough scratch coat to improve mechanical key. Install vertical and horizontal guide strips (screeds) at 1.5–2 m intervals to control thickness and flatness.

3.3 Main Coat Application

Fill between guides in 10–15 mm layers. Use wooden or aluminum floats for leveling. Total thickness on uneven walls typically ranges from 15–30 mm depending on deviation.

3.4 Finishing and Curing

Apply final 3–5 mm coat with steel trowel for smooth finish. Cure by misting water for at least 7 days.

3.5 Crack Prevention Measures

- Control total thickness (<25 mm recommended)

- Use fiber-reinforced plaster

- Install control joints every 4–5 m

- Maintain proper water-cement ratio (0.45–0.55)

4. Case Studies in Tropical Projects

In Bali residential developments, contractors using guide strips and multi-layer application on uneven batako walls achieved smooth finishes with less than 5% cracking incidence, compared to over 30% when applying plaster directly without preparation. Proper curing further reduced surface defects.

5. Challenges and Solutions in Tropical Conditions

High evaporation rates cause rapid drying and plastic shrinkage. Solutions include adding retarders, using fog curing, and applying plaster during cooler hours. Poor adhesion on old walls is mitigated by mechanical roughening and bonding agents.

1. Pendahuluan

Permukaan dinding yang tidak rata sangat umum pada konstruksi masonry menggunakan bata, batako, atau batu kali, terutama pada proyek skala kecil dan menengah. Penyimpangan dapat mencapai 10–30 mm dalam panjang 2 meter, sehingga menyulitkan plesteran langsung dan rentan terhadap retak atau delaminasi.

Tujuan plesteran pada permukaan tidak rata adalah mencapai hasil datar, halus, dan tahan lama sambil mempertahankan integritas struktural. Kriteria penerimaan standar untuk dinding plester selesai biasanya ±3 mm penyimpangan di bawah straight edge 2 m.

Pendekatan dasar meliputi:

1. Persiapan dan pembersihan permukaan

2. Aplikasi bonding agent atau scratch coat

3. Perataan dengan guide strip (screed)

4. Pengisian dan floating dalam beberapa lapis

5. Troweling akhir dan curing

Di iklim tropis seperti Bali, suhu tinggi dan kelembaban mempercepat pengeringan, meningkatkan risiko retak susut plastis. Makalah ini menyajikan panduan teknis lengkap berdasarkan praktik rekayasa terbaik.

3. Metodologi dan Panduan Teknis

3.1 Persiapan Permukaan

Hilangkan partikel lepas, debu, minyak, dan efflorescence. Basahi dinding 24 jam sebelum plesteran. Oleskan slurry bonding (semen + air + perekat) pada permukaan beton halus.

3.2 Scratch Coat dan Perataan

Oleskan lapis dasar kasar 8–12 mm untuk meningkatkan mechanical key. Pasang guide strip vertikal dan horizontal dengan jarak 1,5–2 m untuk mengontrol ketebalan dan kerataan.

3.3 Aplikasi Lapisan Utama

Isi di antara guide dalam lapisan 10–15 mm. Gunakan float kayu atau aluminium untuk perataan. Ketebalan total pada dinding tidak rata biasanya 15–30 mm tergantung penyimpangan.

3.4 Finishing dan Curing

Oleskan lapis akhir 3–5 mm dengan steel trowel untuk hasil halus. Cure dengan penyiraman air minimal 7 hari.

Laju evaporasi tinggi menyebabkan pengeringan cepat dan retak susut plastis. Solusi meliputi penambahan retarder, fog curing, dan aplikasi plester pada jam yang lebih sejuk. Adhesi buruk pada dinding lama diatasi dengan roughening mekanis dan bonding agent.
