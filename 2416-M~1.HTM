2. Literature Review

The minimum clear spacing between reinforcing bars is governed by three main considerations: aggregate size, concrete placement (vibration), and bond development. SNI 2847:2019 adopts a more rigorous approach compared to its previous version, aligning closer to ACI 318-19 while considering local construction practices.

International studies published in *ACI Structural Journal* and *Magazine of Concrete Research* show that insufficient bar spacing significantly reduces the effective bond strength and increases the risk of splitting cracks. In seismic design, proper spacing is also essential to ensure adequate concrete confinement and ductile behavior.

3. Minimum Spacing Requirements According to SNI 2847:2019

The latest SNI 2847:2019 specifies the following key requirements:

Clear spacing between parallel bars shall not be less than the greatest of:

- 25 mm

- db (nominal bar diameter)

- (4/3) × maximum aggregate size

For bundled bars:

- Equivalent diameter concept is used

- Minimum spacing measured between bundles

For columns:

- Minimum clear spacing between longitudinal bars = 1.5 db or 40 mm (whichever is greater)

Copy-pasteable Equation (Minimum Clear Spacing):

\[ s_{min} = \max \left( 25 \, \text{mm}, \, d_b, \, \frac{4}{3} d_{agg} \right) \]

Copy-pasteable Equation (Equivalent Diameter for Bundled Bars):

\[ d_{eq} = d_b \sqrt{n} \]

(where n = number of bars in the bundle)

4. Practical Implications and Common Mistakes

Common mistakes observed in Bali construction projects include:

- Using 25 mm as fixed spacing regardless of bar diameter

- Ignoring aggregate size in congested columns

- Insufficient spacing at beam-column joints

- Incorrect application to bundled bars

These errors frequently lead to honeycombing, especially in columns with high reinforcement ratios.
