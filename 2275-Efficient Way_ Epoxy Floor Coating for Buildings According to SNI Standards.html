Efficient Way: Epoxy Floor Coating for Buildings According to SNI Standards

Author: edisupriyanto@gmail.com

Epoxy floor coating has become one of the most efficient and durable flooring solutions for modern buildings in Bali. From luxury villas and boutique resorts to warehouses, showrooms, and commercial spaces, epoxy flooring offers superior performance in tropical conditions. In 2026, applying epoxy coatings according to SNI standards (especially SNI 7952:2019 for floor coating systems and related building codes) is essential to ensure quality, safety, and longevity while meeting regulatory requirements for PBG (Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung).

Epoxy is a two-component thermosetting polymer that, when mixed, creates a hard, seamless, and chemical-resistant surface. Its high compressive strength (up to 80–100 MPa), excellent abrasion resistance, and impermeability to liquids make it far superior to conventional tiles or paint in high-traffic and wet areas.

Key Advantages of Epoxy Flooring in Bali’s Climate:

- High resistance to humidity, salt air, and UV exposure when using UV-stable topcoats.

- Seamless surface prevents mold, bacteria, and water penetration — critical during monsoon season.

- Easy to clean and maintain, reducing long-term operational costs.

- Available in anti-slip finishes for pool decks, bathrooms, and kitchens.

- Fast curing time (walkable in 24 hours, full strength in 7 days) minimizing project downtime.

Efficient Application According to SNI Standards:

1. Surface Preparation (Most Critical Step)

Concrete substrate must achieve CSP 3–5 surface profile (shot blasting or diamond grinding). Moisture content must be below 4% (tested with moisture meter). Repair cracks and holes with epoxy mortar.

2. Primer Application

Use low-viscosity epoxy primer to ensure excellent bonding. SNI requires minimum 0.3–0.5 mm thickness.

3. Main Epoxy Coating

Apply self-leveling epoxy in 1–3 mm thickness or build-up system for heavy-duty areas. For decorative finishes, incorporate quartz aggregates or flakes.

4. Topcoat

Apply polyurethane or polyaspartic topcoat for UV protection and higher abrasion resistance.

5. Quality Control

Perform pull-off adhesion test (minimum 1.5 MPa), thickness measurement, and visual inspection. All materials must have SNI certification or equivalent international standards (ASTM, ISO).

Efficient Strategies for Small to Medium Projects in Bali:

- Use solvent-free, low-VOC epoxy systems for better indoor air quality and faster application.

- Schedule work during dry season to reduce moisture-related failures.

- Combine with underfloor heating or cooling systems in luxury villas.

- Choose anti-static or conductive epoxy for server rooms and industrial spaces.

Common mistakes that cause failure include poor surface prep (80% of failures), incorrect mixing ratios, and applying on wet or contaminated substrates. Professional application can deliver a floor that lasts 10–20 years with minimal maintenance.

Cara Efisien: Cat Epoxy untuk Lantai Bangunan Sesuai Standar SNI

Cat epoxy lantai telah menjadi salah satu solusi lantai paling efisien dan tahan lama untuk bangunan modern di Bali. Mulai dari villa mewah, resort boutique, gudang, showroom, hingga ruang komersial, epoxy flooring menawarkan performa unggul di kondisi tropis. Tahun 2026, penerapan cat epoxy sesuai Standar Nasional Indonesia (SNI) khususnya SNI 7952:2019 menjadi keharusan untuk menjamin kualitas, keamanan, dan daya tahan sekaligus memenuhi persyaratan regulasi PBG.

Epoxy adalah polimer thermosetting dua komponen yang saat dicampur membentuk lapisan keras, seamless, dan tahan bahan kimia. Kekuatan tekan hingga 80–100 MPa, ketahanan aus tinggi, dan kedap air menjadikannya jauh lebih unggul dibandingkan keramik atau cat biasa.

Keunggulan Cat Epoxy di Iklim Bali:

- Tahan kelembaban tinggi, udara asin, dan sinar UV (dengan topcoat UV-stable).

- Permukaan seamless mencegah jamur, bakteri, dan rembesan air saat musim hujan.

- Mudah dibersihkan dan dirawat, menghemat biaya operasional jangka panjang.

- Tersedia finishing anti-slip untuk deck kolam, kamar mandi, dan dapur.

- Waktu curing cepat sehingga proyek lebih cepat selesai.

Cara Aplikasi Efisien Sesuai Standar SNI:

1. Persiapan Permukaan

Profil permukaan beton harus CSP 3–5 melalui shot blasting atau grinding. Kadar air beton maksimal 4%.

2. Aplikasi Primer

Gunakan epoxy primer berkualitas dengan ketebalan 0,3–0,5 mm.

3. Lapisan Utama

Self-leveling epoxy 1–3 mm atau sistem build-up untuk area berat.

4. Topcoat

Polyurethane atau polyaspartic untuk perlindungan UV dan ketahanan aus lebih tinggi.

5. Pengujian Kualitas

Lakukan pull-off adhesion test, pengukuran ketebalan, dan inspeksi visual.

Strategi Efisien untuk Proyek Skala Kecil-Menengah di Bali:

- Pilih epoxy solvent-free dan low-VOC.

- Kerjakan pada musim kemarau.

- Kombinasikan dengan sistem lantai dingin untuk villa mewah.

Kesalahan umum seperti persiapan permukaan buruk, perbandingan campuran salah, dan aplikasi pada substrat basah sering menyebabkan kegagalan. Aplikasi profesional dapat menghasilkan lantai yang awet 10–20 tahun.
