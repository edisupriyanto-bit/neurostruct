# II. Literature Review

The F-Number system, formalized in ASTM E1155 “Standard Test Method for Determining FF Floor Flatness and FL Floor Levelness Numbers,” replaced the subjective 10-ft straightedge method with a statistically robust approach. FF quantifies local surface bumpiness (variation from a plane over short distances), while FL measures deviation from horizontal over longer distances. ACI 117 “Specifications for Tolerances for Concrete Construction and Materials” provides recommended values:

- Conventional: SOFF 20 / SOFL 15

- Moderately Flat: SOFF 25 / SOFL 20

- Flat: SOFF 35 / SOFL 25

- Very Flat: SOFF 45 / SOFL 35

- Super Flat: SOFF 60 / SOFL 40

Minimum local values are typically 60% of overall values. Testing must occur within 72 hours of placement and before formwork removal to avoid distortion from curling or deflection. Indonesian SNI 6880:2016 references ACI 117 for concrete tolerances, emphasizing watertight construction and surface quality. Recent studies confirm that achieving higher F-Numbers through laser screeds, proper finishing sequences, and early curing reduces long-term maintenance costs by 30–50% in tropical climates.

Key statistical formulas from ASTM E1155 include:

\[ FF = \frac{3.28 \times (100 - 3 \times \sigma_{FF})}{1 + 0.03 \times \sigma_{FF}} \] (simplified for illustration; full profile index calculation uses successive 300 mm intervals).

\[ FL = \frac{3.28 \times (100 - 3 \times \sigma_{FL})}{1 + 0.03 \times \sigma_{FL}} \]

# IV. Practical Professional Tips and Step-by-Step Solutions

Step 1: Specify Clearly Using F-Numbers

Define both Specified Overall (SO) and Minimum Local (ML) values in contract documents, referencing ACI 117 and ASTM E1155.

Step 2: Pre-Construction Planning

Conduct mock-up panels and pre-pour meetings to align on testing timing and crew responsibilities.

Step 3: Concrete Placement and Finishing

Use laser screeds for large pours; follow sequential finishing to minimize waves.

Step 4: Timely Testing

Perform measurements within 72 hours using approved profilers; test before shoring removal on elevated slabs.

Step 5: Data Analysis and Acceptance

Calculate FF/FL per ASTM E1155; verify both overall and local values meet or exceed specifications.

Step 6: Corrective Actions

For non-compliant areas, apply diamond grinding or self-leveling overlays with verified compatibility.

Step 7: Documentation and Handover

Provide certified F-Number reports, including raw data and statistical summaries.

Step 8: Maintenance Planning

Incorporate periodic re-testing in high-traffic or precision areas.
