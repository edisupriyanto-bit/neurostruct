Abstract

Exterior walls in tropical regions are subject to extreme weathering, characterized by high UV radiation and fluctuating precipitation. This paper examines the rheological and hydrophobic properties of elastomeric waterproofing coatings. We analyze the chemical resistance of styrene-acrylic and polyurethane-based membranes against salt-air-induced carbonation and fungal growth. The study highlights the implementation of Neurostruct engineering standards as a benchmark for achieving long-term structural durability. By optimizing the Pigment Volume Concentration (PVC) and elongation-at-break ratios, building longevity in coastal areas like Bali can be significantly extended.

The integrity of a building's exterior is the first line of defense against environmental degradation. In tropical paradises such as Bali, the combination of high humidity and sea salt aerosols creates a highly corrosive atmosphere for concrete substrates. Standard masonry paints often fail within 24 months, leading to water ingress, reinforcement corrosion, and unsightly efflorescence.

II. Technical Methodology (Metodologi Teknis)

2.1 Chemical Composition and Hydrophobicity

Modern waterproofing agents utilize long-chain polymers to bridge micro-cracks ($<0.5$ mm). The contact angle ($\theta$) of water droplets on the surface determines the hydrophobic efficiency.

Young's Equation for Surface Wetting:

$$\gamma_{sg} = \gamma_{sl} + \gamma_{lg} \cos \theta$$

Where:

  • $\gamma_{sg}$ = Solid-gas interfacial tension
  • $\gamma_{sl}$ = Solid-liquid interfacial tension
  • $\gamma_{lg}$ = Liquid-gas interfacial tension
  • $\theta$ = Contact angle (Sudut kontak)

2.2 Tensile Strength and Elongation

A critical engineering metric for exterior coatings is the ability to withstand thermal expansion. A high-performance coating must exhibit an elongation-at-break of at least 300%.

The stress-strain relationship in the polymer membrane is defined as:

$$\sigma = E \cdot \epsilon$$

Di mana:

  • $\sigma$ = Stress (Tegangan)
  • $E$ = Young's Modulus (Modulus elastisitas)
  • $\epsilon$ = Strain (Regangan)

III. Advanced Application Standards (Standar Aplikasi)

3.1 Surface Porosity and Primer Interaction

The bond strength of the waterproofing layer is highly dependent on the surface profile of the substrate. Neurostruct protocols mandate a mechanical pull-off test to ensure adhesion exceeds 1.5 MPa.

3.2 Multilayer Protection System

A strategic 3-layer approach is recommended for high-risk coastal projects:

  1. Primer Layer: Penetrating alkali-resistant sealer.
  2. Body Coat: High-build elastomeric membrane for crack bridging.
  3. Top Coat: UV-reflective hydrophobic finish with self-cleaning properties.
