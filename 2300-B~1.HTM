1. Introduction

In regions with shallow groundwater tables, pile caps often sit below or near the water level, introducing significant construction and performance challenges. High groundwater increases hydrostatic pressure, reduces soil shear strength during excavation, and raises risks of base instability, piping, and buoyancy uplift on the fresh concrete pour.

The effective stress principle governs soil behavior below the water table:

\[

\sigma' = \sigma - u

\]

where \(\sigma'\) is effective stress, \(\sigma\) is total stress, and \(u\) is pore water pressure. Reduced effective stress lowers bearing capacity and increases settlement potential.

For pile caps, design must consider buoyancy forces:

\[

F_b = \gamma_w \times V_{sub}

\]

where \(F_b\) is buoyant force, \(\gamma_w\) is unit weight of water, and \(V_{sub}\) is submerged volume.

In tropical areas like Bali, seasonal rainfall fluctuations and coastal salinity further exacerbate corrosion of reinforcement and concrete degradation. Field experience shows that inadequate dewatering or poor sequencing often leads to delays, rework, and compromised long-term durability.

This paper reviews best practices based on field experiences and international literature, providing actionable strategies for successful pile cap construction.

2. Literature Review

Recent studies highlight hydrodynamic and geotechnical challenges in high groundwater conditions. Research on elevated pile caps for bridges emphasizes wave and current effects, while urban deep excavations focus on dewatering impacts and stability. Numerical analyses of pile groups under varying groundwater levels show significant changes in settlement and load distribution. Case studies in collapsible or soft tropical soils underscore the need for careful dewatering to avoid inducing settlement in adjacent structures. Innovations include artificial recharge to mitigate drawdown effects and advanced monitoring during construction.

3. Methodology and Field-Based Strategies

3.1 Site Investigation and Planning

Detailed boreholes with piezometers to map groundwater fluctuations. Assess soil permeability, stratification, and potential for quick conditions.

3.2 Excavation and Dewatering

- Well-point or deep well systems for large excavations.

- Sheet piling or secant piles for side support.

- Open pumping with sumps for limited areas, combined with filters to prevent piping.

3.3 Pile Head Preparation and Formwork

Trim piles to cutoff level above water if possible. Use tremie pipes for underwater concreting to avoid segregation:

\[

\text{Concrete placement rate} \geq \text{rate to maintain head pressure}

\]

3.4 Concreting and Waterproofing

Pour in one continuous operation. Apply waterproof admixtures or membranes. Ensure proper vibration and curing.

3.5 Monitoring and Quality Control

Install piezometers and settlement markers. Verify concrete strength and cover. Post-construction drainage to manage long-term water levels.

4. Case Studies from Tropical Field Experiences

In Bali’s coastal and lowland developments, projects using combined well-point dewatering and secant pile walls successfully constructed large pile caps with minimal base instability. Cases involving tremie concreting under high water tables achieved high-quality results with reduced rework compared to conventional methods. Lessons learned include early planning of dewatering and sequencing to avoid flooding during monsoon periods.

5. Challenges and Mitigation in Tropical High Groundwater Sites

Challenges include rapid water inflow, soil liquefaction risk during dewatering, corrosion from saline water, and environmental impacts of drawdown. Mitigation strategies: phased dewatering with recharge wells, corrosion-resistant reinforcement, and sustainable sediment control. Numerical modeling (finite element) aids prediction of settlement and stability.

1. Pendahuluan

Di daerah dengan muka air tanah dangkal, pile cap sering berada di bawah atau dekat permukaan air, yang menimbulkan tantangan konstruksi dan performa yang signifikan. Air tanah tinggi meningkatkan tekanan hidrostatik, mengurangi kekuatan geser tanah selama penggalian, dan meningkatkan risiko ketidakstabilan dasar, piping, serta gaya apung pada beton segar.

Prinsip tegangan efektif mengatur perilaku tanah di bawah muka air:

\[

\sigma' = \sigma - u

\]

Untuk pile cap, desain harus mempertimbangkan gaya apung:

\[

F_b = \gamma_w \times V_{sub}

\]

Di wilayah tropis seperti Bali, fluktuasi musiman curah hujan dan salinitas pantai memperburuk korosi tulangan dan degradasi beton. Pengalaman lapangan menunjukkan bahwa dewatering yang tidak memadai atau urutan kerja yang buruk sering menyebabkan keterlambatan, rework, dan durabilitas jangka panjang yang terganggu.

3. Metodologi dan Strategi Berbasis Lapangan

3.1 Investigasi Situs dan Perencanaan

Borehole rinci dengan piezometer untuk memetakan fluktuasi air tanah. Nilai permeabilitas tanah, stratifikasi, dan potensi quick condition.

3.2 Penggalian dan Dewatering

- Sistem well-point atau deep well untuk penggalian besar.

- Sheet piling atau tiang secant untuk penyangga samping.

- Pompa terbuka dengan sump untuk area terbatas, dikombinasikan dengan filter untuk mencegah piping.

3.3 Persiapan Kepala Tiang dan Bekisting

Pangkas tiang ke cutoff level di atas air jika memungkinkan. Gunakan pipa tremie untuk pengecoran bawah air agar menghindari segregasi.

3.4 Pengecoran dan Waterproofing

Tuang dalam satu operasi kontinu. Gunakan admixture waterproof atau membran. Pastikan vibrasi dan curing yang tepat.

4. Studi Kasus dari Pengalaman Lapangan Tropis

Di pembangunan pesisir dan dataran rendah Bali, proyek yang menggunakan kombinasi dewatering well-point dan dinding tiang secant berhasil membangun pile cap besar dengan ketidakstabilan dasar minimal. Kasus yang melibatkan pengecoran tremie di bawah muka air tanah tinggi mencapai hasil berkualitas tinggi dengan rework yang berkurang dibandingkan metode konvensional.

5. Tantangan dan Mitigasi di Lahan Tropis dengan Muka Air Tinggi

Tantangan meliputi aliran air masuk yang cepat, risiko likuifaksi tanah selama dewatering, korosi dari air asin, dan dampak lingkungan drawdown. Strategi mitigasi: dewatering bertahap dengan sumur recharge, tulangan tahan korosi, dan pengendalian sedimen berkelanjutan. Pemodelan numerik (finite element) membantu prediksi penurunan dan stabilitas.
