Abstract

Stake-out, or the process of transferring architectural and structural designs from digital blueprints to the physical ground, is the most vulnerable phase in the construction lifecycle. Even minor deviations in horizontal or vertical positioning can lead to structural eccentricity, legal boundary disputes, and massive financial rework. This paper investigates the mathematical propagation of errors during the stake-out phase using Total Station and GNSS-RTK technologies. By analyzing site-specific data, we demonstrate that a "precision-first" approach is the only viable method to ensure structural integrity and project profitability.

1. Introduction

In modern civil engineering, the "As-Built" reality often deviates from the "As-Designed" model due to sub-standard surveying practices. Stake-out is not merely marking points; it is the physical manifestation of coordinate geometry. In dense development areas like Bali, where land value is extreme and setbacks are strictly regulated, a 5cm displacement can result in the legal mandatory demolition of structural elements.

2. Mathematical Framework of Coordinate Transfer

The accuracy of a stake-out point $(P_i)$ is dependent on the instrument's angular $(s_a)$ and distance $(s_d)$ precision. The displacement error $(E)$ can be modeled as:

$$E = \sqrt{(s_d^2 + (D \cdot \sin(s_a))^2)}$$

Where:

  • $D$ = Distance from the instrument to the target point.
  • $s_a$ = Standard deviation of the angle measurement.
  • $s_d$ = Standard deviation of the distance measurement.

Any error in the Primary Control Network (PCN) will propagate linearly across all structural axes, leading to "cumulative drift" in high-rise or large-scale villa complexes.

3. Consequences of Incorrect Positioning

  • Structural Eccentricity: Load-bearing columns failing to align with foundation centers, causing unplanned moments.
  • MEP Conflicts: Piping and electrical conduits failing to meet designated wall cavities.
  • Legal Infringement: Encroaching on neighboring property boundaries or government setbacks.

4. Mitigation and Advanced Methodology

The integration of BIM (Building Information Modeling) with Robotic Total Stations allows for real-time verification. However, the human element—specifically the expertise of the Geodetic Engineer—remains the primary safeguard against "garbage-in-garbage-out" data scenarios.

2. Analisis Teknis: Rumus Akurasi Stake-Out

Penentuan koordinat di lapangan harus menggunakan metode polar atau rectilinear yang terkunci pada titik Benchmark (BM) nasional. Rumus dasar untuk menentukan koordinat target $(X_t, Y_t)$ adalah:

$$X_t = X_{station} + D \cdot \sin(Azimuth)$$

$$Y_t = Y_{station} + D \cdot \cos(Azimuth)$$

Jika Azimuth meleset 1 detik busur saja pada jarak 100 meter, maka pergeseran fisik yang terjadi sudah cukup untuk membuat besi tulangan kolom meleset dari posisinya.

3. Dampak Ekonomi: Biaya Bongkar Pasang

Kesalahan stake-out yang baru disadari setelah pengecoran kolom lantai 1 akan mengakibatkan biaya pembongkaran yang mencapai 200% dari biaya konstruksi awal (biaya hancur + biaya buang puing + biaya bangun ulang). Di Bali, di mana estetika resort sangat dijunjung, kesalahan posisi bangunan yang menutupi view laut atau melanggar sempadan pantai bisa berujung pada penyegelan proyek oleh pihak berwenang.
