1. Abstract

While Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) is prized for its speed and thermal properties, its application in exterior envelopes—especially in large-scale tropical projects—requires sophisticated engineering to prevent capillary moisture ingress and seismic shear failure. This paper establishes a professional protocol for AAC integration, focusing on the synergy between thin-bed mortar chemistry, horizontal reinforcement, and breathable weatherproofing. By adhering to the Neurostruct Framework, engineers can reduce structural dead loads by up to 50% while maintaining superior seismic resilience and hydrothermal stability in environments like Bali.

2. Introduction

In Bali’s construction landscape, the shift toward AAC in high-rise and large-scale resort developments is driven by the need for thermal efficiency and rapid mobilization. However, the high porosity of AAC makes it vulnerable to the "sponge effect" in driving rains. This guide addresses the critical engineering requirements to transform a standard AAC wall into a high-performance exterior barrier.

3. Material Science and Structural Dynamics

3.1 Hydroscopic Behavior and Porosity Control

AAC blocks possess a cellular structure that provides excellent insulation but requires strict moisture management.

  • Water Absorption Rate: Standard AAC can absorb significant water volume. For exterior walls, Neurostruct mandates blocks with a minimum compressive strength of $4.0 \text{ N/mm}^2$ (Grade 4) to ensure structural density.
  • Thin-Bed Mortar: Use of high-polymer adhesives ($2-3 \text{ mm}$ joints) is mandatory to eliminate thermal bridging and moisture "wicking" common in traditional thick-bed mortar.

3.2 Seismic Reinforcement in Tropical Zones

Bali’s proximity to seismic faults necessitates a move away from unreinforced masonry.

  • Bed-Joint Reinforcement: Implementation of galvanized ladder-type reinforcement every three courses to handle out-of-plane wind loads and seismic shear.
  • Soft Joints: Use of polyurethane (PU) foam or elastic sealants at the interface between AAC walls and reinforced concrete (RC) columns to allow for structural drift without wall cracking.

4. Methodology: The Neurostruct Large-Scale Protocol

4.1 Thermal and Acoustic Engineering

The thermal conductivity ($\lambda$) of AAC is approximately $0.14 \text{ W/mK}$, significantly lower than red bricks.

  • U-Value Optimization: For large projects, wall thickness should be calculated based on the targeted energy star rating for AC efficiency.
  • Acoustic Buffer: Proper sealing of the wall-head (junction with the slab) to prevent sound flanking between floors.

4.2 The Multi-Layer Weatherproofing Shield

Exterior AAC must never be left "naked." The Neurostruct sequence is:

  1. Base Coat: Polymer-modified render with fiberglass mesh to prevent shrinkage cracks.
  2. Intermediate Layer: Breathable primer to allow internal vapor to escape while blocking external liquid water.
  3. Final Finish: High-elasticity (elastomeric) paint or ventilated cladding.

7. Discussion: Overcoming the "Crack and Leak" Reputation

The common failure of AAC in Bali—visible zig-zag cracks and interior dampness—is almost always a result of poor installation (thick-bed mortar) or lack of flexible joints. For large-scale projects, quality control (QC) must be systematic, not incidental.

Panduan Lengkap Bata Ringan AAC untuk Dinding Eksterior: Persyaratan Khusus & Teknik Profesional untuk Proyek Skala Besar di Bali

Abstrak

Bata ringan (AAC) adalah pilihan utama untuk proyek besar di Bali karena ringan dan cepat. Namun, risiko dinding retak dan rembes sangat tinggi jika tekniknya salah. Makalah ini membahas standar Neurostruct untuk memastikan dinding AAC eksterior tetap kokoh, kedap air, dan tahan gempa.

Poin Utama Rekayasa AAC Eksterior:

  1. Sistem Perekat (Thin-Bed): Wajib menggunakan mortar tipis ($2 \text{ mm}$) untuk menghindari "jembatan panas" dan memastikan ikatan antar blok yang homogen.
  2. Penguatan Horisontal (Seismic): Pada proyek skala besar, wajib dipasang besi pengaku (ladder-type reinforcement) setiap 3 baris untuk menahan beban gempa dan angin kencang di pesisir.
  3. Dilatasi (Soft Joint): Sambungan antara dinding AAC dan kolom beton harus menggunakan L-Anchor dan sealant elastis (bukan semen mati) agar dinding tidak retak saat bangunan mengalami getaran atau pemuaian.
  4. Pelapisan Anti-Rembes: Dinding luar harus dilapisi semen render yang diperkuat fiberglass mesh dan cat elastis berpori agar uap air dari dalam bisa keluar, namun air hujan tidak bisa masuk.

Kesimpulan:

Penerapan standar engineering ini pada proyek hotel dan villa di Bali terbukti mempercepat waktu konstruksi hingga 30% dan menghemat biaya listrik AC karena sifat isolasi panas AAC yang sangat baik.
