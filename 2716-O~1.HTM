1. Introduction

Skim coating (acian) is the final thin layer (2–5 mm) applied over base plaster to achieve a smooth, paint-ready surface. Applying it too early, before the base plaster has sufficiently cured and dried, is one of the most common causes of map cracking, debonding, and poor finish quality in tropical construction.

The optimal timing depends on the hydration process of Portland cement. Initial setting occurs within hours, but full strength development and significant moisture loss require days to weeks. In tropical conditions (temperature 28–35°C, relative humidity 70–90%), surface drying is rapid while internal moisture migration is slow, creating differential shrinkage stresses.

Recommended minimum waiting time:

- Conventional cement plaster: 7–14 days

- Polymer-modified plaster: 5–10 days (faster due to reduced shrinkage)

A simple rule of thumb used in practice is to wait until the base plaster reaches at least 70–80% of its 28-day strength and surface moisture content drops below 8–10%.

This paper synthesizes scientific principles and field experience into a practical engineering guide.

2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed research on plaster curing and finishing layers consistently shows that premature application of skim coats leads to high tensile stresses at the interface due to differential shrinkage. Studies indicate that waiting at least 7 days in normal conditions and up to 14 days in high-humidity tropical climates significantly reduces cracking. Research on cement hydration kinetics (using isothermal calorimetry) confirms that the majority of shrinkage and strength gain occurs between days 3 and 14. In tropical environments, papers emphasize the role of extended moist curing and controlled drying to balance surface hardness with internal moisture equilibrium before skim coating.

3. Methodology and Recommended Timing Guidelines

3.1 Hydration and Strength Development

Cement hydration follows a sigmoidal curve. Compressive strength development can be approximated by:

\[

f_c(t) = f_{c28} \cdot \frac{t}{a + b \cdot t}

\]

where \(a\) and \(b\) are empirical constants, and \(t\) is time in days.

3.2 Moisture Content Criterion

Measure surface moisture using a moisture meter. Target: <8–10% before applying acian.

3.3 Visual and Tactile Indicators

- Surface color changes from dark to light gray.

- No visible damp patches.

- Hard to the touch with minimal powdering when rubbed.

3.4 Recommended Waiting Periods

- Standard cement-sand plaster (1:4 to 1:6): 10–14 days in tropical conditions.

- With curing compounds or moist curing: can be reduced to 7–10 days.

- Polymer-modified or ready-mix skim base: 5–7 days.

4. Case Studies from Tropical Projects

In Bali residential and commercial projects, contractors who waited 10–12 days after base plaster before applying acian achieved smooth, crack-free finishes with excellent long-term performance. Projects that applied acian after only 3–5 days experienced widespread map cracking and required costly repairs.

5. Challenges and Solutions in Tropical Climates

High humidity slows internal drying while accelerating surface crusting, leading to trapped moisture and delamination. Solutions include:

- Extended moist curing (wet hessian or curing compound)

- Application during cooler hours

- Use of breathable skim coats

- Controlled ventilation without forced drying

1. Pendahuluan

Acian (skim coating) adalah lapisan tipis akhir (2–5 mm) yang diaplikasikan di atas plester dasar untuk mencapai permukaan halus siap cat. Penerapan terlalu dini, sebelum plester dasar cukup kering dan mengeras, merupakan salah satu penyebab paling umum retak peta, debonding, dan kualitas finishing yang buruk di konstruksi tropis.

Waktu optimal bergantung pada proses hidrasi semen Portland. Setting awal terjadi dalam hitungan jam, tetapi perkembangan kekuatan penuh dan kehilangan kelembaban signifikan memerlukan hari hingga minggu. Di kondisi tropis (suhu 28–35°C, RH 70–90%), pengeringan permukaan cepat sementara migrasi kelembaban internal lambat, menciptakan tegangan susut diferensial.

Aturan praktis yang banyak digunakan: tunggu hingga plester dasar mencapai minimal 70–80% kekuatan 28 hari dan kadar kelembaban permukaan turun di bawah 8–10%.

Makalah ini mensintesis prinsip ilmiah dan pengalaman lapangan menjadi panduan rekayasa praktis.

2. Tinjauan Pustaka

Penelitian terindeks Scopus tentang curing plester dan lapisan finishing secara konsisten menunjukkan bahwa aplikasi skim coat prematur menyebabkan tegangan tarik tinggi pada interface akibat susut diferensial. Studi menunjukkan bahwa menunggu minimal 7 hari di kondisi normal dan hingga 14 hari di iklim tropis kelembaban tinggi secara signifikan mengurangi retak. Penelitian kinetika hidrasi semen (menggunakan kalorimetri isotermal) mengonfirmasi bahwa sebagian besar susut dan peningkatan kekuatan terjadi antara hari ke-3 hingga ke-14.

3.1 Hidrasi dan Perkembangan Kekuatan

Perkembangan kekuatan tekan semen mengikuti kurva sigmoid. Perkiraan sederhana:

\[

f_c(t) = f_{c28} \cdot \frac{t}{a + b \cdot t}

3.2 Kriteria Kadar Kelembaban

Ukur kelembaban permukaan menggunakan moisture meter. Target: <8–10% sebelum aplikasi acian.

3.3 Indikator Visual dan Taktile

- Warna permukaan berubah dari gelap menjadi abu-abu terang.

- Tidak ada bercak lembab yang terlihat.

- Keras saat disentuh dengan sedikit bubuk saat digosok.

3.4 Periode Tunggu yang Direkomendasikan

- Plester semen-pasir standar (1:4 hingga 1:6): 10–14 hari di kondisi tropis.

- Dengan curing compound atau moist curing: dapat dikurangi menjadi 7–10 hari.

- Plester modifikasi polimer: 5–7 hari.
