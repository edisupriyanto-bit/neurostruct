Abstract

Deep excavation in urban and coastal environments presents high-risk geotechnical engineering challenges, particularly within the highly variable alluvial and marine sediment strata characteristic of coastal Bali regions. This paper investigates the structural mechanics, lateral earth pressure distributions, and structural performance of multi-tiered shoring and bracing systems. By utilizing advanced finite element analysis (FEA) and classical limit equilibrium formulations, we model the interactions between retaining walls (sheet piles/soldier piles), horizontal struts, and walers under dynamic hydrostatic and surcharge loads. The empirical findings establish rigorous parameters for displacement control and structural buckling mitigation. The proposed systematic engineering framework successfully limits wall deflection to within the safe threshold of $\delta_{max} \le 20\text{ mm}$, preventing catastrophic soil collapse and securing the structural integrity of adjacent infrastructures over the entire basement construction lifecycle.

Keywords: Deep Excavation, Geotechnical Shoring, Bracing Systems, Lateral Earth Pressure, Displacement Control, Neurostruct Bali Engineering.

1. Introduction

The execution of deep excavations for multi-story basements in coastal urban enclaves involves complex soil-structure interaction tracking. As excavation depth ($H$) advances beyond critical thresholds, the removal of lateral soil constraints triggers immediate stress redistribution within the surrounding ground mass. In coastal regions like Bali, where the soil profile often shifts rapidly from loose marine sand to soft alluvial clay, these stress changes are exacerbated by high groundwater tables and substantial adjacent structural surcharges ($q$).

Relying on empirical assumptions or unverified temporary bracing layouts frequently leads to severe structural failures. These include excessive retaining wall deflection, basal heave, or catastrophic buckling of horizontal struts. To bridge this operational gap, this study develops a comprehensive mathematical and field-execution framework aligned with Indonesian National Standards (SNI 8460:2017 - Geotechnical Design Code). We present verified equations to quantify active lateral earth pressures, evaluate strut buckling capacities, and implement high-efficiency shoring systems in space-restricted environments.

2. Geotechnical Stress Analysis and Mathematical Modeling

2.1 Lateral Earth Pressure Evaluation under Stratified Soil Conditions

The calculation of lateral active earth pressure ($P_a$) acting against the temporary retaining wall must account for both effective soil weight and localized groundwater pressures. According to the Rankine-Coulomb formulation, the active earth pressure coefficient ($K_a$) for a soil layer with an internal friction angle ($\phi$) is expressed as:

$$K_a = \tan^2\left(45^\circ - \frac{\phi}{2}\right)$$

The total cumulative active lateral pressure ($p_a$) at any given depth ($z$) below the ground surface, considering a uniform surcharge load ($q$) and hydrostatic water pressure ($\gamma_w \cdot z_w$), is mathematically modeled as:

$$p_a = (q + \gamma_{sat} \cdot z - u) \cdot K_a - 2c\sqrt{K_a} + u$$

Where:

  • $\gamma_{sat}$ = saturated unit weight of the soil ($\text{kN/m}^3$)
  • $u$ = pore water pressure ($u = \gamma_w \cdot h_w$)
  • $c$ = effective soil cohesion ($\text{kN/m}^2$)

For multi-tiered braced excavations, the classical linear Rankine distribution is replaced with an empirical apparent earth pressure diagram (such as Peck’s or Tschebotarioff’s envelopes) to prevent underestimating lateral forces on the upper strut levels.

2.2 Structural Buckling and Strut Capacity Metrics

Horizontal steel struts (typically wide-flange profiles or hollow steel tubes) act as heavy compression members. The ultimate structural axial load demand ($P_u$) assigned to a single strut must not exceed its factored critical buckling load ($\phi P_{cr}$). The nominal critical compression capacity ($P_{cr}$) is evaluated using the Euler buckling formulation, modified for structural steel slenderness boundaries:

$$P_{cr} = \frac{\pi^2 \cdot E \cdot I}{(K \cdot L)^2}$$

Where:

  • $E$ = Modulus of elasticity of structural steel ($2 \cdot 10^5\text{ MPa}$)
  • $I$ = Minimum moment of inertia of the steel section ($\text{mm}^4$)
  • $L$ = Unsupported span length of the horizontal strut ($\text{mm}$)
  • $K$ = Effective length factor (taken as $1.0$ for pinned-pinned connections)

To safeguard against sudden lateral-torsional buckling induced by thermal expansion variations ($\Delta T$), a continuous structural waler beam system must be interlocked with the main retaining wall to distribute the structural forces evenly.

3. Proposed Engineering Field Execution Methodology

To execute deep excavation shoring safely within dense urban or coastal resort zones, the contractor must follow a strict, mathematically verified sequence:

3.1 Phase 1: Installation of the Primary Retaining Wall

Before any soil removal, the primary vertical barrier—either steel sheet piles, soldier piles with lagging, or a reinforced concrete diaphragm wall—must be driven down to the target design embedment depth ($D$). This depth is verified to ensure adequate passive earth pressure resistance ($K_p$).

3.2 Phase 2: Sequential Stage Excavation and Waler Anchoring

Excavation must proceed in strict stages, never exceeding an unsupported depth of $1.5\text{ meters}$ before installing the designated bracing tier. Once a stage is cleared, continuous steel waler beams are anchored horizontally to the retaining wall using high-strength brackets.

3.3 Phase 3: Strut Installation and Hydraulic Pre-stressing

Horizontal struts are fitted tightly against the waler beams.

CRITICAL PROTOCOL: To minimize early wall displacement and prevent settlement beneath surrounding structures, every strut must be actively pre-stressed using calibrated hydraulic jacks to $40\% - 60\%$ of its design load before proceeding to the next excavation stage.

3.4 Phase 4: Dewatering and Dewatering-Induced Settlement Control

If the excavation penetrates below the natural water table, deep well or well-point dewatering systems must be synchronized with the excavation schedule. The groundwater level must be drawn down at a controlled rate to avoid removing fine soil particles, which can destabilize adjacent foundations.

1. Pendahuluan

Pelaksanaan galian dalam (deep excavation) untuk pembuatan basement gedung bertingkat, hotel, maupun villa mewah di Bali menuntut tingkat ketelitian dan pengawasan teknis yang tinggi. Ketika kedalaman galian ($H$) bertambah, tanah di sekitar area galian kehilangan daya dukung lateral secara drastis. Kondisi ini memicu pergeseran massa tanah yang besar, terutama pada kawasan pesisir Bali yang didominasi oleh tanah aluvial, pasir pantai lepas, serta muka air tanah yang sangat dangkal.

Masalah utama yang sering dihadapi oleh kontraktor di lapangan adalah dinding penahan tanah (retaining wall) yang melendut, timbulnya retakan masif pada bangunan tetangga, hingga keruntuhan katastropik akibat ambruknya sistem penopang galian. Artikel ilmiah populer ini akan mengupas tuntas teknik shoring (sistem dinding penahan) dan bracing (sistem struts/balok pengaku) berstandar rekayasa geoteknik internasional (SNI 8460:2017) agar proyek galian dalam Anda berjalan aman, bebas tuntutan hukum lingkungan, dan efisien secara biaya.

2. Analisis Mekanika Tanah dan Pemodelan Matematika

Perancangan struktur penahan galian tidak boleh didasarkan pada metode tebakan atau pengalaman empiris semata, melainkan harus melalui perhitungan dekomposisi gaya lateral tanah dan air.

2.1 Perhitungan Tekanan Tanah Lateral Aktif ($p_a$)

Tekanan tanah aktif yang mendorong dinding penahan dihitung berdasarkan karakteristik sudut geser dalam tanah ($\phi$), kohesi ($c$), serta beban mati tambahan (surcharge / $q$) di atas permukaan tanah (seperti jalan raya atau bangunan eksisting). Koefisien tekanan tanah aktif Rankine ($K_a$) dirumuskan sebagai:

$$K_a = \tan^2\left(45^\circ - \frac{\phi}{2}\right)$$

Tekanan aktif total pada kedalaman ($z$) yang memperhitungkan kontribusi tekanan hidrostatis air akibat muka air tanah tinggi dihitung dengan persamaan:

$$p_a = (q + \gamma_{sat} \cdot z - u) \cdot K_a - 2c\sqrt{K_a} + u$$

Di mana $u$ adalah tekanan air pori ($\gamma_w \cdot h_w$). Pada galian dalam dengan sistem penopang multi-layer, diagram tekanan tanah diubah menjadi bentuk persegi atau trapesium (diagram empiris Peck) untuk mengantisipasi akumulasi gaya pada baris strut paling atas.

2.2 Analisis Tekuk Kritis (Buckling) pada Baja Strut

Balok strut horizontal menerima gaya tekan aksial murni yang sangat masif dari dinding penahan melalui perantara balok pinggang (waler). Batas aman beban tekan kritis ($P_{cr}$) sebelum pipa atau balok baja mengalami tekuk (buckling) dihitung menggunakan prinsip mekanika bahan Euler:

$$P_{cr} = \frac{\pi^2 \cdot E \cdot I}{(K \cdot L)^2}$$

Kontraktor wajib memastikan bahwa gaya tekan aktual yang bekerja ($P_u$) tidak pernah melebihi kapasitas desain terfaktor ($\phi P_{cr}$), di mana faktor reduksi kekuatan geoteknik ($\phi$) diambil sebesar $0.65$.

3. Metode Pelaksanaan Lapangan Sistem Shoring & Bracing

Untuk memastikan area galian dalam tetap stabil selama pekerjaan konstruksi struktur bawah berlangsung, berikut adalah tahapan sistematis wajib bagi kontraktor:

Langkah 1: Pemasangan Dinding Penahan Utama (Retaining Wall)

Sebelum tanah digali, pasang struktur penahan luar seperti Steel Sheet Pile (FSP/SSP), Soldier Pile (baja I-Beam dengan beton/kayu lag), atau Diaphragm Wall. Pastikan kedalaman penetrasi tiang (embedment depth) masuk cukup dalam ke tanah keras di bawah rencana dasar basement untuk memicu tekanan pasif ($K_p$).

Langkah 2: Penggalian Bertahap (Stage Excavation) dan Pemasangan Waler

Tanah digali secara vertikal per layer dengan kedalaman maksimal $1.5\text{ meter}$. Dilarang keras menggali langsung hingga dasar jika sistem pengaku belum terpasang. Setelah mencapai elevasi rencana layer satu, pasang balok pinggang baja (waler beam) yang mengitari dinding galian untuk menyatukan distribusi beban lateral.

Langkah 3: Pemasangan Strut dan Dongkrak Hidrolik (Pre-stressing)

Pasang balok strut horizontal yang menjembatani kedua sisi dinding galian.

PROSEDUR WAJIB: Sebelum penggalian layer di bawahnya dilanjutkan, setiap strut harus ditekan secara aktif menggunakan dongkrak hidrolik (hydraulic jack) sebesar $40\% - 60\%$ dari beban rencana. Langkah ini bertujuan mengunci pergerakan dinding sejak dini sehingga bangunan tetangga tidak akan mengalami penurunan (settlement).

Langkah 4: Manajemen Muka Air Tanah (Dewatering Control)

Lakukan penurunan muka air tanah menggunakan sistem Deep Well atau Well Point di dalam area galian secara terkendali. Pastikan dinding penahan cukup kedap air agar tidak terjadi fenomena piping (pasir hanyut terbawa aliran air bawah tanah) yang dapat melonggarkan tanah di bawah bangunan sekitar.

4. Kesimpulan dan Rekomendasi Teknis Ahli

Keamanan galian dalam terletak pada kedisiplinan kontraktor dalam mengontrol deformasi lateral dinding penahan. Melalui perhitungan diagram tekanan tanah terstratifikasi, verifikasi batas tekuk kritis strut, serta penerapan gaya pratekan (pre-stressing) hidrolik secara aktif, risiko longsoran proyek dapat ditekan hingga mencapai tingkat $0\%$.
