1. Introduction

Pondasi batu kali (rubble stone foundation) is a traditional shallow foundation system widely used in small-scale residential construction in Indonesia, especially in rural and suburban areas of Bali. It utilizes locally available river stones or field stones arranged with mortar to form a stable base for load-bearing walls.

While economical and environmentally friendly, improper construction frequently causes problems such as uneven settlement, wall cracking, water ingress, and reduced load-bearing capacity. This paper provides clear, professional guidance on the correct techniques for building rubble stone foundations, tailored for small-scale projects and beginner-to-intermediate contractors.

The content is fully aligned with SNI 8460:2017 (geotechnical design) and good construction practices, while remaining practical and cost-effective for tropical conditions.

2. Literature Review

Traditional stone foundations have been studied in the context of vernacular architecture and sustainable construction. Research in *Construction and Building Materials* and *Journal of Cultural Heritage* shows that well-built rubble stone foundations can achieve satisfactory performance when properly bonded and founded on stable subgrade.

In tropical regions, studies highlight challenges including high rainfall causing mortar erosion, variable stone quality, and the need for adequate drainage. Indonesian geotechnical standards (SNI 8460:2017) require minimum foundation depth, proper stone arrangement, and sufficient mortar strength. Hybrid systems combining rubble stone base with reinforced concrete beam (sloof) are increasingly common in Bali to improve seismic resistance and uniformity.

3. Theoretical Background and Design Principles

# 3.1 Bearing Capacity

The allowable bearing capacity for rubble stone foundations is typically determined from soil investigation. A common empirical range for good compacted soil is 1.5–3.0 kg/cm². The foundation width should satisfy:

\[ B \geq \frac{P}{q_{allow} \times L} \]

where \( P \) is the total load per meter length, \( q_{allow} \) is allowable bearing pressure, and \( L \) is length.

# 3.2 Stability and Settlement

The foundation must have adequate depth (minimum 60–80 cm in Bali conditions) and stepped construction on sloping sites to prevent differential settlement.

# 3.3 Mortar Mix Design

Recommended mortar mix for rubble stone foundations is 1:4 to 1:6 (cement:sand) by volume, with a minimum compressive strength of 2.5–5 MPa at 28 days.

4. Practical Step-by-Step Techniques for Constructing Rubble Stone Foundations

# 4.1 Site Preparation

- Excavate to firm subgrade and remove soft or organic soil.

- Compact the base thoroughly.

- Install a 10–15 cm layer of sand or gravel as leveling course.

# 4.2 Stone Selection and Preparation

- Choose hard, durable stones (andesite, basalt, or river stones) free from cracks.

- Clean stones from dirt and clay.

- Sort stones by size: larger stones for the base, smaller for upper layers.

# 4.3 Laying Techniques (Hearting and Facing)

1. Lay the first course on the prepared base with larger flat stones.

2. Use “hearting” technique: fill the core with smaller stones and mortar.

3. Create two outer “faces” with larger, better-shaped stones for stability and appearance.

4. Ensure proper bonding: stones must overlap joints in the course below (minimum 1/3 stone length).

5. Maintain horizontal and vertical alignment using string lines and level.

6. Fill all voids completely with mortar.

# 4.4 Integration with Reinforced Concrete Sloof

For modern hybrid construction (highly recommended in Bali):

- Cast a reinforced concrete tie beam (sloof) on top of the rubble stone foundation.

- Provide starter bars from the stone foundation into the sloof for monolithic behavior.

# 4.5 Curing and Protection

- Keep the foundation moist for at least 7 days.

- Protect from direct rain and sun during initial setting.

- Backfill only after the sloof has gained sufficient strength.

# 4.6 Quality Control

- Check levelness and alignment every course.

- Verify mortar mix ratio and consistency.

- Ensure no large voids remain inside the foundation.

- Perform simple load tests or visual inspection before proceeding to superstructure.

3. Latar Belakang Teori dan Prinsip Desain

# 3.1 Daya Dukung

Kapasitas dukung yang diizinkan untuk pondasi batu kali biasanya ditentukan dari investigasi tanah. Kisaran empiris umum untuk tanah padat yang baik adalah 1,5–3,0 kg/cm².

# 3.2 Stabilitas dan Penurunan

Pondasi harus memiliki kedalaman yang memadai (minimal 60–80 cm di kondisi Bali) dan konstruksi bertingkat pada lahan miring untuk mencegah penurunan diferensial.

# 3.3 Desain Campuran Mortar

Campuran mortar yang direkomendasikan untuk pondasi batu kali adalah 1:4 hingga 1:6 (semen:pasir) dengan kekuatan tekan minimal 2,5–5 MPa pada umur 28 hari.

4. Teknik Langkah demi Langkah Membuat Pondasi Batu Kali

# 4.1 Persiapan Lahan

- Gali hingga mencapai tanah keras dan buang tanah lunak atau organik.

- Padatkan dasar dengan baik.

- Pasang lapisan pasir atau kerikil setebal 10–15 cm sebagai leveling course.

# 4.2 Pemilihan dan Persiapan Batu

- Pilih batu keras dan tahan lama (andesit, basalt, atau batu kali) bebas retak.

- Bersihkan batu dari tanah dan lumpur.

- Sortir batu berdasarkan ukuran: batu besar untuk dasar, batu kecil untuk lapisan atas.

# 4.3 Teknik Pemasangan (Hearting dan Facing)

1. Letakkan lapisan pertama di atas dasar yang telah disiapkan dengan batu datar besar.

2. Gunakan teknik “hearting”: isi inti dengan batu kecil dan mortar.

3. Buat dua “wajah” luar dengan batu yang lebih besar dan berbentuk baik.

4. Pastikan ikatan yang baik: batu harus tumpang tindih dengan sambungan di lapisan bawah (minimal 1/3 panjang batu).

5. Pertahankan alignment horizontal dan vertikal menggunakan benang dan waterpass.

6. Isi semua rongga sepenuhnya dengan mortar.

# 4.4 Integrasi dengan Sloof Beton Bertulang

Untuk konstruksi hibrida modern (sangat direkomendasikan di Bali):

- Cor sloof beton bertulang di atas pondasi batu kali.

- Berikan starter bar dari pondasi batu ke sloof untuk perilaku monolitik.

# 4.5 Curing dan Perlindungan

- Jaga pondasi tetap lembab minimal 7 hari.

- Lindungi dari hujan dan matahari langsung selama pengikatan awal.

- Backfill hanya setelah sloof mencapai kekuatan yang cukup.

# 4.6 Pengendalian Kualitas

- Periksa kerataan dan alignment setiap lapisan.

- Verifikasi rasio campuran mortar.

- Pastikan tidak ada rongga besar di dalam pondasi.

- Lakukan inspeksi visual sebelum melanjutkan ke superstructure.
