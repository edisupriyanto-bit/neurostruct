Differentiations in Structural Performance and Load Path Mechanisms: Structural Columns vs. Practical Columns in Seismic-Prone Masonry Buildings

Awas Rumah Roboh! Bedah Tuntas Rahasia Perbedaan Kolom Praktis vs Kolom Struktur Agar Bangunan Tahan Gempa di Bali

Author: edisupriyanto@gmail.com

Abstract

Reinforced concrete columns are the most critical elements in building stability. In Indonesian construction, vertical members are bifurcated into structural columns and practical columns. This paper investigates the mechanical behavior of both types under various loading conditions. Structural columns (SC) are designed to withstand axial, shear, and flexural loads derived from the entire superstructure, whereas practical columns (PC) function as confinement for masonry walls. Utilizing ACI 318-19 and SNI 2847:2019 standards, this study provides a mathematical framework for dimensioning and reinforcement detailing. The findings suggest that misapplication of PC in place of SC is a primary cause of structural failure in low-rise residential buildings during seismic events.

1. Introduction

In the architectural and engineering landscape of tropical regions like Bali, masonry infill remains the predominant walling system. However, the structural integrity of these buildings depends heavily on how vertical loads are distributed. Structural columns are the primary "bones" of the building, while practical columns act as "stays" for the walls.

2. Mechanical Characteristics of Structural Columns

Structural columns are designed based on the Tributary Area Method. They must support the cumulative weight of slabs, beams, and roof loads. The cross-sectional area must be sufficient to prevent crushing of concrete and buckling of reinforcement.

The design axial strength ($\phi P_n$) is calculated as follows:

$$\phi P_n = \phi 0.80 [0.85 f'_c (A_g - A_{st}) + f_y A_{st}]$$

Where:

  • $A_g$: Gross area of the column section.
  • $A_{st}$: Total area of longitudinal reinforcement.
  • $f'_c$: Specified compressive strength of concrete.
  • $f_y$: Specified yield strength of steel.

3. Practical Columns: The Confinement Mechanism

Practical columns are not intended to carry primary gravity loads. Their presence is mandatory to divide large masonry surfaces into smaller, more stable panels. According to standard practice, a practical column (typically 11x11 cm to 15x15 cm) should be placed every 3 to 4 meters horizontally.

1. Pendahuluan: Mengapa Memahami Perbedaan Ini Penting?

Banyak kegagalan struktur pada rumah tinggal di Bali disebabkan oleh ketidaktahuan pemilik proyek akan fungsi kolom. Seringkali, kolom praktis dianggap sudah cukup untuk menahan beban lantai dua. Padahal, secara mekanika, kedua jenis kolom ini memiliki "tugas" yang berbeda total.

2. Kolom Struktur: Penopang Utama Beban Gedung

Kolom struktur adalah tiang utama yang bertugas menyalurkan beban dari atap, pelat lantai, dan balok langsung ke fondasi. Dimensinya tidak boleh asal-asalan dan harus dihitung berdasarkan luas area yang dipikul (tributary area).

Rumus Estimasi Dimensi Kolom Struktur:

$$A_{perlu} = \frac{P_u}{0.35 \cdot f'_c + 0.65 \cdot f_y \cdot \rho}$$

Jika Anda membangun di daerah pesisir Bali, disarankan menggunakan mutu beton minimal K-250 atau $f'_c$ 20 MPa untuk menjaga durabilitas besi tulangan dari korosi air laut.

3. Kolom Praktis: Pengikat Dinding Agar Tidak Ambruk

Fungsi utama kolom praktis adalah menjaga agar dinding bata tetap tegak dan tidak runtuh akibat gaya angin atau getaran kecil. Kolom ini tidak dirancang untuk memikul beban dak beton di atasnya. Jika sebuah dinding memiliki luas lebih dari 12 $m^2$, maka wajib dipasang kolom praktis di tengahnya.

4. Bahaya Salah Kaprah dalam Konstruksi

Kegagalan yang paling sering ditemukan adalah "Soft Story Effect", di mana lantai bawah hanya menggunakan kolom praktis sementara lantai atas memiliki beban berat. Saat gempa terjadi, kolom praktis akan "meledak" karena tidak mampu menahan gaya geser lateral, menyebabkan bangunan runtuh seketika.
