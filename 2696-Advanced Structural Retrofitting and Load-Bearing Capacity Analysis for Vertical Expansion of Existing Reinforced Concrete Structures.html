Abstract

Vertical expansion of existing structures presents complex geotechnical and structural challenges, particularly in seismic-prone tropical regions like Bali. This paper investigates the efficiency of structural retrofitting through column jacketing and carbon fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP) integration to support additional floor loads. By utilizing Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) data, we formulate a displacement-based design approach to verify the remaining life of foundations. The results demonstrate that a 35% increase in axial capacity can be achieved without significant footprint expansion, making it a viable strategy for high-density urban redevelopment.

1. Introduction

The escalating value of land and the density of tourism zones in Bali necessitate vertical growth as a sustainable alternative to horizontal expansion. However, adding floors to an existing reinforced concrete building (vertical expansion) is not merely a task of piling bricks; it requires a rigorous assessment of the existing structural capacity versus new axial and lateral loads. According to seismic codes (SNI 1726:2019), any structural modification exceeding 5% of the original load requires a total re-evaluation of the building’s seismic performance. This study explores advanced retrofitting strategies, focusing on the "structural injection" method to ensure safety without total demolition.

2. Structural Verification and Modeling

Prior to any physical intervention, the current state of the building must be established via Non-Destructive Testing (NDT). The ultimate axial load $P_u$ acting on a critical column must be compared with the factored nominal strength $\phi P_n$. The Demand-Capacity Ratio (DCR) is the primary metric:

$$DCR = \frac{P_{existing} + P_{additional}}{\phi \cdot P_n}$$

If $DCR > 1.0$, the column is considered overstressed and requires retrofitting. Furthermore, the foundation’s settlement under the new load is predicted using the compression index $C_c$ and initial void ratio $e_0$:

$$S_c = \left[ \frac{C_c \cdot H}{1 + e_0} \right] \cdot \log \left( \frac{\sigma'_0 + \Delta \sigma}{\sigma'_0} \right)$$

3. Column Jacketing and Foundation Reinforcement

One of the most effective strategies for vertical expansion is Concrete Jacketing. This involves adding a new layer of reinforced concrete around the existing column. The composite axial capacity of the jacketed section $P_{n,composite}$ is modeled as:

$$P_{n,composite} = 0.85 \cdot f'_c \cdot (A_g - A_{st}) + f_y \cdot A_{st}$$

Where $A_g$ is the total cross-sectional area of the new jacketed column. For the foundation, if the soil bearing capacity $q_a$ is exceeded, Micropiling or Underpinning is necessary to transfer the additional load to deeper, more competent soil layers.

4. Conclusion

Vertical expansion is a high-precision engineering field. Using column jacketing combined with lightweight materials (such as AAC blocks and lightweight concrete slabs) reduces the burden on the original structure while maximizing space efficiency.

1. Pendahuluan

Keterbatasan lahan di Bali memaksa pemilik properti untuk tumbuh secara vertikal. Menambah lantai pada bangunan lama (renovasi tingkat) sering kali menjadi pilihan ekonomis dibandingkan membangun dari nol. Namun, secara teknik sipil, penambahan beban ini sangat berisiko jika tidak dibarengi dengan "Suntik Struktur" atau penguatan fondasi. Artikel ini membahas strategi teknis yang jarang diketahui publik untuk memastikan bangunan tetap kokoh meski diberikan beban lantai tambahan di atasnya sesuai regulasi SNI 2847:2019.

2. Analisis Perhitungan Beban Tambahan

Penambahan satu lantai biasanya menambah beban mati (dead load) dan beban hidup (live load) sebesar 400-600 kg per meter persegi. Beban ultimit $U$ dihitung dengan kombinasi pembebanan:

$$U = 1.2D + 1.6L$$

Jika kolom eksisting tidak mampu menahan beban $U$, maka luas tulangan tambahan $A_{s,add}$ harus dihitung untuk proses jacketing:

$$A_{s,add} = \frac{P_u - \phi \cdot 0.85 \cdot f'_c \cdot A_g}{f_y}$$

Penggunaan beton ringan (beton busa atau dek baja) sangat direkomendasikan untuk meminimalisir penambahan beban mati tanpa mengurangi kekuatan struktural lantai baru.

3. Metode "Suntik Kolom" dan Fondasi

Strategi terbaik dalam penambahan lantai adalah melakukan Concrete Jacketing. Proses ini melibatkan pembobokan sedikit permukaan beton lama, pemasangan besi tulangan baru yang diikat dengan chemical anchor, dan pengecoran ulang dengan beton bermutu tinggi.

Pada bagian bawah, jika fondasi tapak lama tidak kuat, metode Micropile dapat diaplikasikan. Tiang-tiang kecil berdiameter 15-20 cm dimasukkan di sekitar fondasi lama untuk membantu memikul beban tambahan langsung ke tanah keras.

4. Kesimpulan

Menambah lantai bukan sekadar menyambung besi, tapi memastikan seluruh jalur beban dari atap hingga ke tanah terkoneksi dengan aman. Audit struktur dengan alat Hammer Test dan Rebar Scan adalah langkah wajib pertama yang harus dilakukan.
