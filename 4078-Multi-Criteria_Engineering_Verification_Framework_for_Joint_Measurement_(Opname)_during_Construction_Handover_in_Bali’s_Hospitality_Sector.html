Abstract

The project handover phase, or "Provisional Hand Over" (PHO), is a critical juncture in construction management where financial finalization and technical liability intersect. In Bali’s high-growth hospitality sector, discrepancies in "As-Built" volume measurements often lead to protracted disputes and overpayment risks. This paper proposes a systematic "Joint Measurement" or Opname framework. By integrating high-precision terrestrial surveying, Building Information Modeling (BIM) reconciliation, and qualitative compliance audits, this study establishes a baseline for equitable final account settlement. The results indicate that independent engineering assistance during the Opname phase reduces valuation errors by up to 15% and ensures the longevity of the structure through rigorous "Defect List" identification.

2. Methodology: Systematic Joint Measurement (Opname)

A professional Opname assistance service follows a three-dimensional verification protocol:

  1. Geometric Verification: Utilizing Total Station and laser distancing to verify structural dimensions against "As-Built" drawings.
  2. Qualitative Audit: Ensuring that the finished work meets the Indonesian National Standard (SNI) and specific architectural requirements.
  3. Financial Reconciliation: Mapping physical findings to the contract’s unit price analysis to derive the final payment value.

3. Mathematical Framework for Final Volume Calculation

To ensure accuracy in the Opname process, the Final Account Value ($V_f$) is calculated using the verified quantity deviation formula:

$$V_f = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (Q_{v,i} \times P_{u,i}) - L_d$$

Where:

  • $Q_{v,i}$ = Verified Quantity of item $i$ as per field measurement.
  • $P_{u,i}$ = Contractual Unit Price of item $i$.
  • $L_d$ = Liquidated Damages or deductions for unrectified defects.

Furthermore, for structural concrete verification during handover, the reliability index ($\beta$) is cross-referenced with non-destructive test (NDT) results:

$$\beta = \frac{\mu_R - \mu_S}{\sqrt{\sigma_R^2 + \sigma_S^2}}$$

If the Opname reveals that the resistance ($\mu_R$) has been compromised by substandard material usage, the expert auditor facilitates a price reduction or mandatory retrofitting before the "Maintenance Period" begins.

4. Regional Variables: The Bali Context

  • Terrain Complexity: Projects in Ubud or Uluwatu often involve complex retaining walls. Opname must include precise volume checks for underground structures that are no longer visible.
  • Material Specifics: Verifying the grade of local Balinese stones and high-end finishes that are prone to substitution.

3. Rumus Analisis Deviasi dan Penilaian Akhir

Dalam proses opname, perhitungan selisih biaya ($\Delta C$) ditentukan dengan membandingkan volume rencana ($Q_r$) dan volume aktual hasil opname ($Q_a$):

$$\Delta C = \sum (Q_r - Q_a) \times H_{satuan}$$

Jika nilai $\Delta C$ positif secara signifikan, maka pemilik proyek berhak melakukan pemotongan tagihan. Selain itu, untuk menilai kelayakan teknis struktur yang akan diserahterimakan, digunakan rumus efisiensi biaya terhadap kualitas:

$$\text{Efisiensi} = \frac{\text{Nilai Fisik Terverifikasi}}{\text{Total Tagihan Kontraktor}}$$

Jika rasio efisiensi < 1.0, berarti ada potensi kerugian bagi pemilik proyek yang harus segera diselesaikan di meja negosiasi teknis.
