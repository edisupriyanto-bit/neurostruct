Abstract

The surge in construction activities in Bali has resulted in a growing number of claims and disputes related to project delays, cost overruns, design variations, defects, and contractual conflicts. This paper examines the role of specialized consulting services in claims and dispute management for construction projects in Bali. Through integrated forensic engineering analysis, schedule and cost quantification, technical compliance evaluation, and strategic mediation support, these consulting services provide objective, evidence-based solutions that facilitate efficient resolutions. The study synthesizes Scopus-indexed literature and regional practices to propose a comprehensive claims and dispute management framework adapted to Bali’s unique conditions. Particular attention is given to local challenges including volcanic soil variability, seismic risks, environmental regulations, cultural considerations, and tourism-driven timelines. The Neurostruct structural management model is integrated to enhance analytical accuracy, claim defensibility, and long-term project performance. Practical recommendations are offered for project owners, contractors, consultants, and legal teams to minimize financial losses and maintain project momentum in Bali’s competitive construction sector.
