Abstract

Structural failure and performance disputes in Bali’s built environment are increasingly complex due to the intersection of rapid development, aggressive coastal environments, and high seismic risk (Zone 4/5). This paper presents a comprehensive framework for Forensic Structural Auditing (FSA). By combining Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), Finite Element Analysis (FEA), and site-specific seismic response spectra, this methodology provides an objective basis for resolving construction disputes. The study highlights that most disputes arise from inadequate soil-structure interaction analysis and material degradation. A data-driven forensic approach is demonstrated to be the most reliable "Expert Witness" in legal and arbitration proceedings.

2. Methodology: The Forensic investigative Protocol

A systematic Forensic Audit follows a four-pillar investigation:

  1. Visual Inspection and Mapping: Documentation of crack patterns (flexural, shear, or settlement-induced).
  2. In-Situ Material Verification: Utilizing Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) for concrete uniformity and Rebar Scanning for reinforcement density.
  3. Geotechnical Reconciliation: Verifying as-built foundation depths against soil bearing capacity reports.
  4. Structural Re-Modeling: Re-evaluating the building using modern software (ETABS/SAP2000) under current SNI 1726:2019 seismic loads.

3. Mathematical Framework for Structural Reliability

In forensic engineering, we evaluate the Performance Ratio (PR) of structural elements. The demand-to-capacity ratio is formulated as:

$$PR = \frac{R_u}{\phi R_n}$$

Where:

  • $R_u$ = Required strength (factored loads)
  • $\phi R_n$ = Design strength (reduced nominal strength)

For seismic disputes, the Base Shear ($V$) calculation is critical to determine if the building was under-designed:

$$V = \frac{C_s \cdot I_e \cdot W}{R}$$

Where:

  • $C_s$ = Seismic response coefficient
  • $I_e$ = Importance factor
  • $W$ = Effective seismic weight
  • $R$ = Response modification coefficient

If $PR > 1.0$ under forensic re-analysis, the building is deemed non-compliant, providing indisputable evidence for the claimant in a construction dispute.

4. Coastal and Seismic Challenges in Bali

Structural audits in coastal areas like Canggu or Uluwatu must account for chloride-induced corrosion. The carbonation depth ($x$) is modeled to predict service life:

$$x = k \cdot \sqrt{t}$$

Where $k$ is the carbonation coefficient and $t$ is time. Disputes often arise when $t$ is significantly shorter than the design life, indicating substandard concrete cover or poor mix design.

2. Tahapan Audit Forensik Struktur

Audit yang kredibel harus mencakup:

  • Pemeriksaan NDT (Non-Destructive Test): Mengetahui kualitas beton dan posisi besi tulangan tanpa merusak bangunan (Hammer Test & Scan Tulangan).
  • Uji Plumbness: Mengukur kemiringan kolom dan dinding untuk mendeteksi penurunan pondasi (settlement).
  • Analisis Kapasitas Terpasang: Menghitung ulang apakah besi yang terpasang mampu menahan beban gempa Bali yang cukup tinggi.

3. Rumus Analisis Kegagalan Struktur

Dalam audit forensik, kita menghitung kapasitas sisa dari elemen struktur. Rumus momen nominal ($M_n$) untuk balok yang sering menjadi sengketa akibat lenturan berlebih adalah:

$$M_n = A_s \cdot f_y \cdot \left( d - \frac{a}{2} \right)$$

Dimana:

  • $A_s$ = Luas penampang besi tulangan terpasang
  • $f_y$ = Tegangan leleh baja
  • $d$ = Jarak efektif dari serat tekan ke pusat tulangan tarik
  • $a$ = Kedalaman blok tegangan tekan beton

Jika hasil hitung menunjukkan $M_n$ lebih kecil dari beban rencana ($M_u$), maka secara legal-teknis bangunan tersebut cacat produk dan membahayakan penghuni.

4. Masalah Khusus di Bali: Korosi & Gempa

Di daerah pesisir seperti Seminyak atau Sanur, besi beton sangat rentan terhadap karat (korosi) akibat uap air laut. Audit forensik akan mengukur laju penetrasi klorida. Jika beton tidak mencapai mutu K-300 (FC 25) ke atas, maka perlindungan terhadap besi akan gagal, menyebabkan retak beton secara prematur. Sengketa mutu beton inilah yang paling sering dimenangkan oleh pemilik proyek jika didukung data audit yang akurat.
