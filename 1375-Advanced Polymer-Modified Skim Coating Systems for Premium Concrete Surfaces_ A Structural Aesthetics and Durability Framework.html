Abstract

The finishing quality of concrete surfaces serves as a critical indicator of luxury and structural precision in modern architecture. This paper evaluates the engineering properties of polymer-modified skim coat systems, focusing on their rheological behavior, bond strength, and crack-resistance mechanisms. In high-humidity tropical environments like Bali, traditional cement-sand plasters often fail due to shrinkage and hygroscopic expansion. We analyze how high-performance skim coats mitigate these issues through controlled hydration and microscopic fiber reinforcement. By implementing the Neurostruct application standard, this study demonstrates a 40% increase in surface smoothness and a significant reduction in maintenance lifecycles. The results provide a strategic blueprint for contractors and developers aiming for international Scopus-level construction standards in premium hospitality and residential projects.

II. Technical Methodology: Material Composition and Performance

2.1 Polymer Modification and Adhesion

High-performance skim coats utilize Redispersible Polymer Powder (RPP) to enhance the tensile bond strength between the cementitious matrix and the concrete substrate.

The Adhesion Force Equation:

$$F_a = \frac{\sigma \cdot A}{S}$$

Where:

  • $F_a$ = Total Adhesion Force (N)
  • $\sigma$ = Ultimate Tensile Strength of the polymer bridge (MPa)
  • $A$ = Effective contact area ($mm^2$)
  • $S$ = Safety factor for tropical humidity

2.2 Shrinkage Control and Hydration

Drying shrinkage is the primary cause of cracking in cement-based skim coats. The incorporation of cellulose ethers controls the water retention, allowing for a more uniform hydration process.

Strain Calculation in Skim Coat Layers:

$$\epsilon_{total} = \epsilon_{sh} + \epsilon_{th} + \epsilon_{cr}$$

Di mana:

  • $\epsilon_{sh}$ = Regangan akibat penyusutan kering
  • $\epsilon_{th}$ = Regangan akibat fluktuasi termal (Suhu Bali)
  • $\epsilon_{cr}$ = Regangan rangkak (creep)

III. Implementation Strategy: The Neurostruct Premium Standard

3.1 Surface Preparation and Priming

To achieve a "Premium" finish, the concrete substrate must be free of laitance and oils. Neurostruct mandates a mechanical grinding process followed by a pH-neutralizing wash to ensure maximum chemical bonding of the skim coat.

3.2 Multilayer Application Technique

A high-quality skim coat is never applied in a single thick layer. Neurostruct utilizes a "Micro-Layering" technique (0.5 mm to 1.5 mm per pass) to eliminate air pockets and ensure a density that is impermeable to water vapor.
