Abstract

Contract administration and meticulous project documentation serve as the critical backbone for the success and legal safety of construction projects in Bali. As the region experiences a surge in complex infrastructure and luxury hospitality developments, the risk of contractual disputes and technical failures increases. This paper proposes a systematic framework for managing contract lifecycles and technical records. By integrating International Federation of Consulting Engineers (FIDIC) standards with local Indonesian National Standards (SNI), this study outlines how robust documentation—ranging from RFI logs to site diaries—acts as primary forensic evidence in dispute resolution. The results demonstrate that standardized administration reduces financial exposure by up to 30% and ensures structural compliance in Bali’s unique seismic environment.

2. Methodology: The Lifecycle of Contract Administration

Professional engineering consultancy in Bali must adopt a multi-phased administrative approach:

  • Pre-Contractual Phase: Reviewing Bill of Quantities (BoQ) and Technical Specifications to ensure "Design-to-Contract" alignment.
  • Operational Phase: Managing Variation Orders (VO), Requests for Information (RFI), and Interim Payment Certificates (IPC).
  • Close-out Phase: Finalizing As-Built Drawings and Maintenance Manuals to ensure long-term structural integrity and legal compliance.

3. Mathematical Framework for Documentation & Cost Control

Effective contract administration utilizes Earned Value Management (EVM) to track project health. The Schedule Variance ($SV$) and Cost Variance ($CV$) are calculated as:

$$SV = EV - PV$$

$$CV = EV - AC$$

Where:

  • $EV$ (Earned Value) = Physical % Complete $\times$ Total Budget.
  • $PV$ (Planned Value) = Scheduled % Complete $\times$ Total Budget.
  • $AC$ (Actual Cost) = Actual expenditure recorded in project documentation.

Without precise documentation of $AC$ and $EV$, stakeholders in Bali face the risk of "Progress Inflation," where payments exceed physical reality. Furthermore, for structural safety, the reinforcement ratio ($\rho$) must be documented and verified:

$$\rho = \frac{A_s}{b \cdot d}$$

Where $A_s$ is the area of steel and $b \cdot d$ is the effective concrete section. Discrepancies in these records often form the basis of quality-related disputes.

1. Pendahuluan: Mengapa Administrasi Penting bagi Proyek di Bali?

Membangun di Bali (Seminyak, Ubud, Uluwatu) memiliki tantangan unik, mulai dari regulasi lokal hingga kondisi tanah tebing. Seringkali, proyek mengalami sengketa bukan karena kesalahan teknik, melainkan karena lemahnya dokumentasi. Tanpa administrasi kontrak yang rapi, perintah perubahan kerja (Change Order) atau keterlambatan akibat cuaca seringkali tidak bisa diklaim secara sah, yang berujung pada kerugian finansial besar bagi pemilik proyek maupun kontraktor.

3. Rumus Analisis Kinerja dan Dokumentasi Finansial

Dalam mengelola kontrak, konsultan menggunakan indikator kinerja finansial untuk mendeteksi pembengkakan biaya. Rumus Cost Performance Index ($CPI$) adalah:

$$CPI = \frac{EV}{AC}$$

Jika $CPI < 1.0$, maka proyek mengalami pemborosan. Keakuratan nilai $AC$ (biaya aktual) sepenuhnya bergantung pada kualitas dokumentasi invoice dan kuitansi proyek.

Selain itu, untuk menjamin mutu, setiap pengecoran harus didokumentasikan dengan nilai Slump beton ($S$):

$$S = H_{cetakan} - H_{beton}$$

Data ini wajib ada dalam dokumen mutu proyek sebagai jaminan bahwa beton yang digunakan tidak terlalu encer yang dapat menurunkan kuat tekan ($f'_c$).

4. Strategi Anti Sengketa: Dokumentasi Forensik

Di Bali, sering terjadi sengketa mengenai "volume tersembunyi" seperti pondasi atau pembesian. Strategi mitigasi terbaik adalah melalui dokumentasi foto dan video yang ter-arsip secara sistematis. Konsultan engineering memastikan bahwa sebelum pengecoran dilakukan, ada rekaman visual dan "checklist" yang ditandatangani bersama sebagai bukti permanen kepatuhan spek.
