Abstract

Construction projects in the tropical and seismic environment of Bali frequently encounter disputes ranging from structural failures to contractual non-compliance. This paper explores the methodology of technical auditing as a primary mechanism for dispute resolution. By integrating high-precision data acquisition—including 3D scanning, non-destructive testing (NDT), and advanced structural modeling—engineers can provide objective evidentiary support. The study emphasizes a data-driven approach to mitigate financial losses and ensure structural longevity in Bali’s unique hospitality and residential sectors.

1. Introduction

The rapid expansion of the construction sector in Bali has led to an increase in complex legal and technical disputes. These conflicts often arise from discrepancies between design specifications and field execution, material degradation due to maritime exposure, or seismic non-compliance. A technical audit serves as a forensic bridge between engineering reality and legal adjudication.

2. Methodology: Data-Driven Technical Auditing

A robust technical audit follows a multi-layered investigative protocol:

  • Document Review: Analysis of As-Built drawings vs. Design Intent.
  • Physical Inspection: Utilizing Schmidt Hammer tests and Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) for concrete integrity.
  • Topographical Verification: Leveraging Total Station and Drone Photogrammetry to assess land deformation or boundary disputes.
  • Digital Simulation: Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to verify structural load capacity under seismic loads as per SNI 1726:2019.

3. Mathematical Framework for Structural Assessment

To quantify the reliability of a disputed structure, the probability of failure ($P_f$) is calculated based on the limit state function $G(X)$:

$$G(X) = R - S$$

Where:

  • $R$ = Structural Resistance (Capacity)
  • $S$ = Load Effects (Demand)

The safety index ($\beta$) is derived as:

$$\beta = \frac{\mu_R - \mu_S}{\sqrt{\sigma_R^2 + \sigma_S^2}}$$

If $\beta$ falls below the threshold defined by international standards (e.g., Eurocode or ACI), the structure is deemed technically non-compliant, providing indisputable evidence for legal claims.

1. Pendahuluan: Mengapa Sengketa Konstruksi di Bali Meningkat?

Bali adalah pusat pembangunan properti yang masif, namun tantangan geografisnya unik. Tanah yang bervariasi, kelembaban tinggi, dan risiko gempa bumi sering menyebabkan deviasi antara rencana (shop drawing) dan realisasi lapangan. Tanpa audit teknis, pemilik proyek sering menjadi pihak yang dirugikan saat terjadi kegagalan bangunan.

2. Audit Teknis: Lebih dari Sekadar Inspeksi Visual

Audit teknis yang serius melibatkan pengumpulan data keras (hard data). Di Bali, Jasa Audit Teknis harus mencakup:

  • Audit Struktural: Pengujian kuat tekan beton (Hammer Test/Core Drill).
  • Audit Geoteknik: Memastikan pondasi sesuai dengan daya dukung tanah setempat.
  • Audit MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing): Verifikasi instalasi kabel dan pipa agar tidak terjadi kegagalan fungsi di masa depan.

3. Analisis Beban dan Perhitungan Teknis

Dalam menentukan apakah sebuah bangunan "gagal" secara teknis, auditor menggunakan perhitungan distribusi beban. Rumus momen lentur ($M$) pada balok sederhana yang sering menjadi sengketa akibat lendutan (deflection) adalah:

$$M = \frac{1}{8} \cdot q \cdot L^2$$

Dimana:

  • $q$ = Beban merata (kg/m)
  • $L$ = Bentang balok (m)

Jika lendutan aktual melebihi batasan izin $\Delta_{max} = L/360$, maka secara legal teknis, kontraktor wajib melakukan perbaikan (remediasi).
