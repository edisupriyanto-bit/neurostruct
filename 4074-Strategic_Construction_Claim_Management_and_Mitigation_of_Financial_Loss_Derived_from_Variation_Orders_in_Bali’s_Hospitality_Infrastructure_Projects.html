Abstract

Variation orders, commonly referred to as Change Orders (CO), are the primary catalysts for financial instability and contractual disputes in Bali’s construction sector. This paper investigates the systematic management of construction claims arising from these variations. By employing a Proactive Claim Identification (PCI) framework and rigorous documentation protocols, stakeholders can mitigate the adverse effects of "Scope Creep." This study utilizes a quantitative approach to assess the impact of CO on the Final Account, offering a mathematical model for equitable price adjustment. Results indicate that early-stage intervention and independent engineering verification significantly reduce the probability of litigation in high-value resort and villa projects in Bali.

1. Introduction

3. Mathematical Models for Variation Pricing

To determine the fair value of a construction claim ($C$), the following formula for Direct and Indirect costs is applied:

$$C = \sum (Q_v \times P_u) + (OH_p \times T_d) + \Delta M$$

Where:

  • $Q_v$ = Variation Quantity
  • $P_u$ = Contractual Unit Price
  • $OH_p$ = Daily Prolongation Overhead
  • $T_d$ = Delay duration caused by the Change Order
  • $\Delta M$ = Additional material price fluctuation (Escalation)

Furthermore, the Efficiency Loss due to disruption is calculated using the Measured Mile Analysis:

$$\text{Efficiency Loss} = \frac{\text{Actual Productivity} - \text{Planned Productivity}}{\text{Planned Productivity}}$$

2. Metode Penanganan Klaim yang Efektif

Untuk menghindari kerugian, setiap pihak harus mengikuti protokol berikut:

  • Penerbitan VO (Variation Order): Jangan pernah memulai pekerjaan tambahan tanpa instruksi tertulis yang ditandatangani kedua belah pihak.
  • Audit Volume: Menghitung selisih volume antara gambar tender dengan gambar revisi (For Construction).
  • Analisis Dampak Waktu: Mengecek apakah perubahan tersebut menggeser jalur kritis (Critical Path) yang berakibat pada denda keterlambatan.

3. Rumus Teknis Perhitungan Klaim Biaya Tambahan

Dalam menghitung nilai klaim akibat perubahan, kita tidak hanya menghitung material, tapi juga biaya tidak langsung. Rumus total klaim perubahan ($\text{Klaim}_{tot}$) adalah:

$$\text{Klaim}_{tot} = (V_{tambah} \times H_{satuan}) + \text{Biaya Alat} + \text{Biaya Overhead}$$

Jika perubahan tersebut menyebabkan pekerjaan lain terhenti (Disruption), maka biaya "Downtime" dihitung dengan:

$$\text{Downtime Cost} = (L_r \times W_h) \times \text{Jumlah Pekerja}$$

Dimana:

  • $L_r$ = Loss Ratio (Persentase kehilangan efisiensi)
  • $W_h$ = Wage per Hour (Upah per jam)

Data angka inilah yang harus disajikan di meja negosiasi agar klaim tidak dianggap sebagai "karangan" semata.
