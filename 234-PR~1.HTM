1. Introduction

Small-scale construction projects in Bali, such as private villas, boutique guesthouses, and medium-sized residential houses, form a significant part of the local building industry. These projects typically operate with limited budgets, smaller workforces, minimal heavy equipment, and often irregular architectural layouts. Concrete placement in such contexts must be simple, practical, and still produce dense, durable concrete that meets SNI 2847:2019 requirements.

This paper provides a rigorous yet highly practical analysis in the English segment for broader engineering audiences and a field-oriented Indonesian segment specifically designed for local contractors, foremen, and small-project engineers working on Bali villa constructions.

2. Literature Review and Code Framework

SNI 2847:2019 Section 26 governs concrete execution, including placement, consolidation, and curing. ACI 304R Guide for Measuring, Mixing, Transporting, and Placing Concrete offers foundational recommendations adaptable to small projects. Field observations in Bali show that many small-scale projects still rely on manual methods due to site constraints, yet simple professional techniques can significantly reduce defects such as honeycombing, segregation, and plastic shrinkage cracking. In coastal Bali, proper placement and curing are essential to ensure durability against humidity and salt exposure.

3. Concrete Mix Design for Small-Scale Placement

For small projects, the mix should be workable yet cohesive:

- Target slump: 100–120 mm (adjustable with superplasticizers if available).

- Well-graded local aggregates to reduce segregation risk.

- Use of retarders in hot weather to maintain workability during manual placement.

4. Transportation and Delivery for Small Sites

- Ready-mix trucks with accurate scheduling to avoid waiting time.

- Manual buckets or wheelbarrows for sites with limited access.

- Small concrete pumps or conveyors when budget allows.

5. Practical Placement Techniques for Small-Scale Projects

Columns

- Place in 0.5–0.8 m lifts to allow proper vibration with limited crew.

- Use internal vibrators inserted vertically and withdrawn slowly.

Beams and Slabs

- Place continuously from one end.

- Vibrate in a simple overlapping pattern (recommended spacing ≈ 10 × vibrator head diameter).

6. Curing and Quality Control for Small Projects in Bali

Immediate curing is one of the most cost-effective ways to improve quality. In Bali, use wet burlap covered with plastic sheets or curing compound for a minimum of 7 days. Protect fresh concrete from direct sun and wind, especially during the dry season.

Simple on-site quality control includes slump testing, visual inspection for segregation, and cover verification after form stripping.
