Abstract

The determination of foundation dimensions is a critical phase in residential civil engineering, directly impacting the building's structural stability and construction cost efficiency. In small-scale residential projects in Bali, Indonesia, foundations are often either over-designed, leading to material waste, or under-designed, risking differential settlement. This paper evaluates the technical parameters for dimensioning isolated pad foundations (footplat), focusing on the relationship between service loads and allowable soil bearing capacity. Utilizing the "Terzaghi Bearing Capacity" theory and the "Elastic Settlement" model, the research investigates the synergy between footing area and soil pressure distribution. Results demonstrate that utilizing a standardized calculation framework reduces foundation material costs by up to 20% while maintaining a safety factor of 3.0. This study provides a replicable methodology for architects and site engineers to optimize foundation reliability in tropical soil conditions.

1. Introduction

For small-scale residential buildings (typically 1 to 2 stories), pad foundations (footplat) are the most common solution due to their simplicity and cost-effectiveness. However, the diverse geotechnical profile of Bali—ranging from volcanic ash in the highlands to alluvial clay in the south—requires a deterministic approach to sizing. Improper dimensioning often leads to wall cracks or structural tilting. This paper transitions from "rule-of-thumb" sizing to "Precision Geotechnical Engineering," providing a rigorous yet accessible calculation protocol.

2. Theoretical Framework: Soil-Structure Interaction

The design of a footplat foundation must satisfy two primary criteria: Ultimate Limit State (no bearing failure) and Serviceability Limit State (no excessive settlement).

2.1. Soil Bearing Capacity and Effective Stress

The required area of the footing ($A_{req}$) is inversely proportional to the allowable bearing capacity ($q_a$):

$$A_{req} = \frac{P + W_f}{q_a}$$

Where:

  • $P$ = Service load from the column ($kN$).
  • $W_f$ = Self-weight of the foundation and soil surcharge ($kN$).
  • $q_a$ = Allowable bearing capacity ($kN/m^2$), derived from $q_{ult} / SF$.

2.2. Shear and Flexural Requirements

The thickness of the footing ($h$) is governed by the two-way (punching) shear capacity ($\phi V_c$). For a square footing, the punching shear strength is modeled as:

$$V_c = 0.33 \cdot \lambda \cdot \sqrt{f'_c} \cdot b_o \cdot d$$

Where $b_o$ is the critical perimeter and $d$ is the effective depth.

3. Methodology: High-Precision Dimensioning Loop

  1. Load Summation: Utilizing the "Tributary Area" method to calculate axial loads on each column.
  2. Geotechnical Assessment: Implementation of Sondir (CPT) or Hand Auger tests to determine the $q_a$ at a depth of $1.5\text{--}2.0$ meters.
  3. Iterative Sizing: Calculating the footing width ($B$) and checking for overturning and sliding stability.
  4. Reinforcement Detailing: Ensuring the reinforcement ratio ($\rho$) satisfies the minimum shrinkage requirements of SNI 2847:2019.

Abstrak

Penentuan dimensi pondasi merupakan fase kritis dalam teknik sipil residensial yang berdampak langsung pada stabilitas bangunan dan efisiensi biaya. Di wilayah Bali, pondasi sering kali mengalami over-design yang memboroskan biaya atau under-design yang berisiko menyebabkan penurunan bangunan. Makalah ini mengevaluasi parameter teknis untuk dimensi pondasi footplat (terisolasi) dengan fokus pada hubungan antara beban layanan dan daya dukung tanah. Hasil penelitian menunjukkan bahwa penggunaan kerangka perhitungan standar dapat mengurangi biaya material pondasi hingga 20% dengan tetap menjaga faktor keamanan sebesar 3,0.

1. Pendahuluan: Mengapa Ukuran Pondasi Tidak Boleh "Kira-kira"?

Banyak kontraktor atau tukang di Bali menggunakan ukuran pondasi standar (misal $80 \times 80 \, cm$) untuk semua jenis rumah. Secara engineering, ini sangat berisiko. Beban rumah di Ubud yang tanahnya lembek berbeda dengan beban rumah di Pecatu yang tanahnya berbatu. Pondasi yang terlalu kecil akan ambles, sementara yang terlalu besar akan membuang-buang uang Anda untuk semen dan besi. Artikel ini membedah teknik menghitung dimensi pondasi standar insinyur agar rumah Anda aman, kokoh, namun tetap hemat biaya.

2. Analisis Teknik: Menghitung Daya Dukung dan Luas Tapak

Pondasi footplat menyalurkan beban dari kolom ke tanah. Luas tapak pondasi harus mampu menyebarkan beban sehingga tidak melebihi kekuatan tanah.

Rumus Luas Pondasi ($A$)

Luas dasar pondasi dihitung dengan membagi total beban dengan daya dukung tanah yang diijinkan:

$$A = \frac{P_{total}}{\sigma_{tanah}}$$

Dimana:

  • $P_{total}$ = Beban bangunan + berat sendiri pondasi ($kN$).
  • $\sigma_{tanah}$ = Daya dukung tanah hasil uji Sondir atau perkiraan teknis ($kN/m^2$).

Jika Anda memiliki beban $200 \, kN$ dan daya dukung tanah $100 \, kN/m^2$, maka Anda butuh luas pondasi $2 \, m^2$, atau dimensi sekitar $1,45 \times 1,45 \, m$.

3. Langkah Strategis Menghitung Pondasi Akurat

  1. Hitung Beban Mati & Hidup: Masukkan berat beton, pasangan bata, atap, serta beban penghuni sesuai peraturan pembebanan Indonesia.
  2. Cek Kondisi Tanah: Lakukan uji minimal dengan bor tangan atau sondir ringan untuk mengetahui pada kedalaman berapa tanah keras ditemukan di lokasi proyek Anda di Bali.
  3. Tentukan Ketebalan (Punching Shear): Pondasi tidak boleh terlalu tipis agar kolom tidak "menusuk" pondasi saat menerima beban berat.
  4. Penulangan yang Tepat: Gunakan besi tulangan yang cukup (biasanya diameter $12 \, mm$ atau $13 \, mm$ ulir) dengan jarak antar besi yang presisi untuk menahan gaya lentur.
