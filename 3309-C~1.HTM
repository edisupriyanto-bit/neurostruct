1. Introduction

Door frames, locally known as *kusen pintu*, serve as the structural and aesthetic interface between door leaves and surrounding walls or partitions. In residential and villa construction in Bali, kusen pintu must satisfy multiple requirements: precise dimensions, weather resistance, security, ease of installation, and compliance with SNI standards for doors and windows.

Poorly detailed drawings frequently cause costly on-site modifications, material mismatches, installation delays, and long-term problems such as warping, leakage, or misalignment. This paper provides a clear, practical methodology for creating professional, cost-effective detail drawings of door frames for timber, aluminum, and steel systems. It is specifically tailored for contractors, site engineers, and drafters working on residential projects.

3. Theoretical Background and Detailing Principles

# 3.1 Drawing Standards and Conventions

Professional door frame details should include:

- Plan view at head, sill, and typical section

- Vertical sections through jamb and head

- Elevation with dimensions and hardware locations

- Material schedule and notes

Use consistent scales (1:10 or 1:5 for details) and follow SNI/ISO drawing conventions for line weights, symbols, and annotations.

# 3.2 Tolerance and Clearance Requirements

Typical clearances:

- Door leaf to frame: 2–3 mm per side

- Frame to wall opening: 10–15 mm for adjustment and sealing

- Bottom gap (for swing doors): 5–10 mm or according to floor finish

# 3.3 Weatherproofing and Sealing

Door frames in tropical climates require effective weather stripping, drip edges, and sill design with proper slope (minimum 1:20 outward) to prevent water ingress.

4. Practical Techniques for Creating Cost-Effective Kusen Pintu Details

# 4.1 Timber Kusen Pintu Detailing

- Show mortise-and-tenon or dowel joints

- Detail threshold, weather bar, and insect screen provisions

- Include treatment notes (pressure-treated or natural oil finish)

# 4.2 Aluminum Kusen Pintu Detailing

- Specify profile sections with thermal break where required

- Detail glazing bead, weather seal, and drainage slots

- Show fixing method to RC frame (anchor bolts or chemical anchors)

# 4.3 Steel Kusen Pintu Detailing

- Use hollow section or pressed steel profiles

- Detail anti-corrosion coating and fire rating if applicable

- Show adjustable anchors for easy installation

# 4.4 Standardized Drawing Template (Recommended for Cost Saving)

Create reusable template with:

- Title block with project information

- Standard notes for material, tolerance, and installation

- Common details (head, jamb, sill, meeting stile)

- Hardware schedule table

This approach reduces drafting time by 40–60% for repetitive villa projects.

# 4.5 Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Insufficient clearance for door swing and floor finish

- Missing weather sealing details

- Incorrect anchoring to RC structure

- Lack of coordination with electrical and MEP services

3. Latar Belakang Teori dan Prinsip Detailing

# 3.1 Standar dan Konvensi Gambar

Detail kusen pintu profesional harus mencakup:

- Tampak rencana pada kepala, ambang, dan potongan tipikal

- Potongan vertikal melalui jamb dan kepala

- Elevasi dengan dimensi dan lokasi hardware

Gunakan skala konsisten (1:10 atau 1:5) dan ikuti konvensi gambar SNI/ISO.

# 3.2 Persyaratan Toleransi dan Clearance

Clearance tipikal:

- Daun pintu ke kusen: 2–3 mm per sisi

- Kusen ke bukaan dinding: 10–15 mm untuk penyesuaian dan sealing

# 3.3 Weatherproofing dan Sealing

Kusen pintu di iklim tropis memerlukan weather stripping, drip edge, dan desain ambang dengan kemiringan yang tepat.

4. Teknik Praktis Membuat Detail Kusen Pintu yang Hemat Biaya

# 4.1 Detailing Kusen Pintu Kayu

- Tampilkan sambungan mortise-tenon atau dowel

- Detail threshold, weather bar, dan ketentuan screen serangga

# 4.2 Detailing Kusen Pintu Aluminium

- Tentukan profil dengan thermal break jika diperlukan

- Detail glazing bead, weather seal, dan slot drainase

# 4.3 Detailing Kusen Pintu Baja

- Gunakan hollow section atau pressed steel

- Detail lapisan anti-korosi

# 4.4 Template Gambar Standar (Rekomendasi Hemat Biaya)

Buat template reusable yang mencakup title block, catatan standar, detail umum, dan tabel jadwal hardware.

# 4.5 Kesalahan Umum yang Harus Dihindari

- Clearance tidak cukup untuk ayunan pintu

- Tidak ada detail sealing cuaca

- Anchoring yang salah ke struktur RC
