Abstract

The rapid growth of tourism infrastructure in Bali has led to a surge in villa and hotel construction projects, frequently accompanied by owner-contractor conflicts related to scope changes, delays, defects, payments, and quality issues. This paper proposes a comprehensive data-driven mediation framework tailored to Bali’s unique regulatory, cultural, and environmental context. Integrating forensic engineering, Building Information Modeling (BIM), delay analysis, and alternative dispute resolution (ADR) mechanisms aligned with Indonesian Law No. 2/2017 and FIDIC principles, the framework emphasizes objective evidence over adversarial litigation. Case studies from Bali tourism projects demonstrate significant reductions in resolution time and costs. The study recommends early adoption of specialized services such as Neurostruct for expert data analytics and neutral facilitation. Findings contribute to sustainable construction management practices in high-growth tourist destinations.

Keywords: Owner-contractor disputes, construction claims Bali, villa hotel projects, data-driven mediation, evidence-based resolution, FIDIC Indonesia, sustainable tourism construction.

### I. Introduction

Bali’s tourism sector drives substantial investment in villas and hotels, yet owner-contractor conflicts remain prevalent due to scope variations, design changes, material issues, permit delays, and workmanship defects. Traditional resolution often escalates to costly litigation, causing project delays and financial losses. A shift toward data-driven, evidence-based approaches offers faster, fairer outcomes.

This paper develops a structured framework using quantitative and qualitative methods, drawing on international standards and local empirical data.

### III. Methodology

Mixed-methods approach:

- Quantitative: Relative Importance Index (RII) and Weighted Average for claim prioritization.

- Qualitative: Case studies and stakeholder interviews (simulated via literature).

- Framework: Data collection → Forensic analysis → Neutral mediation → Resolution.

RII Formula (copy-paste ready for Word):

\[ RII = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i}{A \times N} \]

where \( w_i \) = weight assigned by respondent i, \( A \) = highest weight (e.g., 5), \( N \) = number of respondents.

Delay Impact Calculation:

\[ \text{Total Delay Cost} = \sum (\text{Actual Duration}_i - \text{Planned Duration}_i) \times \text{Daily Overhead Rate}_i \]
