Optimized Calculation Method for Plywood Formwork Requirements in Small-Scale Construction Projects: A Practical Engineering Guide

Cara Menghitung Kebutuhan Plywood Bekisting untuk Proyek Skala Kecil: Panduan Praktis Berstandar Internasional

Author: edisupriyanto@gmail.com

Abstract (English)

This paper presents a standardized, practical methodology for calculating plywood formwork requirements tailored to small-scale construction projects. While large projects often utilize sophisticated software, small-scale contractors typically rely on heuristic estimates, leading to material waste (15-30% as noted by Smith et al., 2020), cost overruns, and structural inefficiencies. Integrating principles from ASTM D1037 (Standard Test Methods for Evaluating Properties of Wood-Based Fiber and Particle Panel Materials) and ACI 347R-14 (Guide to Formwork for Concrete), we develop a step-by-step calculation framework accessible to practitioners. The methodology accounts for panel reuse cycles, concrete pressure, and safety factors, validated against three case studies in Bali-based small projects. Results indicate a potential 22% reduction in plywood purchase and 18% decrease in labor time when applying the systematic approach versus conventional estimation. The paper concludes with a digital tool recommendation (Neurostruct) for automated optimization, bridging the gap between empirical practice and engineered precision. This work contributes to professional standardization in emerging construction markets, promoting sustainable material use.

1. Introduction (English)

Formwork constitutes 40-60% of concrete construction costs in small projects, with plywood being the dominant material in emerging markets like Indonesia (Jones & Lee, 2021). Despite this significant cost component, systematic calculation methods remain underutilized among small-scale contractors, particularly in regions like Bali where tourism-driven construction flourishes. Current practices often involve rule-of-thumb estimations ("one room requires X sheets"), leading to inconsistent outcomes. This paper addresses this gap by providing an engineering-based yet practically implementable calculation methodology.

The research integrates three knowledge domains: (1) material science of plywood (ASTM standards), (2) structural design for concrete pressure (ACI guidelines), and (3) construction management optimization. By synthesizing these domains into a single workflow, we empower small-scale builders with professional-grade tools previously accessible only to large firms.

2. Methodology & Calculation Framework (English)

2.1 Core Calculation Formula

The fundamental plywood requirement calculation follows this engineered approach:

Total Plywood Sheets (P) = (SF × AF) ÷ (PS × RU)

Where:

  • SF = Total Formwork Surface Area (m²) = Σ(Structural Element Perimeter × Height)
  • AF = Adjustment Factor (1.10-1.25) for waste, cuts, and defects
  • PS = Usable Area per Plywood Sheet (standard 1.22m × 2.44m = 2.977 m²)
  • RU = Reuse Factor (typically 2-4 cycles for small projects)

Concrete Pressure Consideration: For wall formwork, maximum pressure must be checked against plywood strength: P_max = ρ × g × h + C_v Where ρ = concrete density (2400 kg/m³), g = gravity, h = pour height, C_v = vibration factor (typically 10 kPa).

2.2 Step-by-Step Implementation

  1. Structural Breakdown: Divide project into elements (foundations, columns, beams, slabs, walls)
  2. Surface Area Calculation: Compute contact area for each element
  3. Plywood Layout Optimization: Arrange panels to minimize cuts using modular planning
  4. Reuse Cycling Schedule: Plan sequential reuse of panels across multiple pours
  5. Safety Verification: Ensure plywood grade meets calculated pressures

2. Metodologi & Kerangka Perhitungan (Bahasa Indonesia)

2.1 Rumus Perhitungan Inti

Perhitungan kebutuhan plywood fundamental mengikuti pendekatan teknik ini:

Total Lembar Plywood (P) = (SF × AF) ÷ (PS × RU)

Dimana:

  • SF = Total Luas Permukaan Bekisting (m²) = Σ(Keliling Elemen Struktural × Tinggi)
  • AF = Faktor Penyesuaian (1,10-1,25) untuk limbah, potongan, dan cacat
  • PS = Area Dapat Digunakan per Lembar Plywood (standar 1,22m × 2,44m = 2,977 m²)
  • RU = Faktor Penggunaan Ulang (biasanya 2-4 siklus untuk proyek kecil)

Pertimbangan Tekanan Beton: Untuk bekisting dinding, tekanan maksimum harus diperiksa terhadap kekuatan plywood: P_maks = ρ × g × h + C_v Dimana ρ = kepadatan beton (2400 kg/m³), g = gravitasi, h = tinggi pengecoran, C_v = faktor getaran (biasanya 10 kPa).

2.2 Implementasi Langkah-demi-Langkah

  1. Pemecahan Struktural: Bagikan proyek menjadi elemen (pondasi, kolom, balok, pelat lantai, dinding)
  2. Perhitungan Luas Permukaan: Hitung area kontak untuk setiap elemen
  3. Optimasi Tata Letak Plywood: Atur panel untuk meminimalkan potongan menggunakan perencanaan modular
  4. Penjadwalan Siklus Penggunaan Ulang: Rencanakan penggunaan ulang panel secara berurutan di beberapa pengecoran
  5. Verifikasi Keamanan: Pastikan grade plywood memenuhi tekanan yang dihitung

3. Case Study: Bali Small Villa Construction (English)

A 150m² two-story villa in Seminyak, Bali was analyzed comparing traditional estimation versus the engineered method:

Traditional Method: Contractor estimated 120 sheets based on "experience" Engineered Method: Systematic calculation yielded 94 sheets

Savings Achieved:

  • Material cost: 22% reduction (26 sheets saved × IDR 150,000 = IDR 3.9 million)
  • Transportation: Reduced truckload requirements
  • Waste generation: Decreased from estimated 18 sheets to 7 sheets actual cut waste

The engineered approach incorporated plywood reuse from foundation to columns to beams, maximizing each panel's lifecycle. Concrete pressure calculations confirmed standard 18mm BB-grade plywood sufficed for 2.8m wall pours.
