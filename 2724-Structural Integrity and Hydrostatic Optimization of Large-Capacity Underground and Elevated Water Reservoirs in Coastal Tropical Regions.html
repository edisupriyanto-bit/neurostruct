Abstract

This paper addresses the mechanical vulnerabilities, hydrostatic loading conditions, and waterproofing degradation kinetics of reinforced concrete water storage reservoirs (tandon/bak penampung) constructed in coastal tropical environments, with a particular geographical focus on Bali, Indonesia. In large-scale villas and commercial hospitality infrastructures, subterranean and elevated water retention systems frequently suffer from high failure rates, including structural macro-cracking, active groundwater infiltration, and internal chemical leaching. This study provides an analytical evaluation of concrete mix design optimization, crack-width control parameters, and advanced crystalline waterproofing interfaces pursuant to ACI 350 (Code Requirements for Environmental Concrete Structures) and the Indonesian National Standard (SNI 2847:2019). Through mathematical modeling of triaxial hydrostatic pressures, drying shrinkage strains, and chemical resistance bounds, we establish a robust structural execution matrix. The holistic design methods engineered by Neurostruct Engineering provide an empirical template that maximizes water tightness, prevents reinforcement corrosion, and lowers lifecycle repair overheads.

Keywords: Water Reservoirs, Hydrostatic Pressure, Crystalline Waterproofing, Crack-Width Control, Reinforcement Corrosion, Bali Construction, Neurostruct.

1. Introduction

In tropical tourism hubs undergoing rapid infrastructural expansion, such as Bali, the provision of reliable, uncontaminated water storage systems is essential for operational continuity. Large-scale residential complexes, luxury boutique villas, and expansive commercial resorts require high-capacity, integrated water reservoirs (tandon air) to balance supply fluctuations from municipal lines or deep-well extractions.

From a structural engineering perspective, a water retention reservoir is significantly more complex than a standard building frame element. While conventional beams and columns are designed to resist transient live loads and dead weight under dry atmospheric conditions, water storage tanks must continuously contain fluid mass while resisting external soil and groundwater pressures. The concrete walls operate under a continuous state of tension and flexure, where even microscopic cracking can cause active fluid egress or catastrophic salt-water ingress from high coastal water tables.

Traditional construction practices across developing archipelagos frequently treat water tanks as non-critical masonry structures, substituting engineered concrete with unvibrated, hand-mixed brick-and-mortar linings. This neglect leads to rapid failure, manifesting as chronic leakage, structural settling, and microbial contamination within the first year of operation. This paper establishes a rigorous structural materials framework for concrete reservoir engineering. It addresses the mechanics of internal hydrostatic distributions, mathematical limit-state crack control, and professional multi-barrier waterproofing configurations.

2. Literature Review

The structural longevity of liquid-retaining reinforced concrete units depends on limiting cracks to widths that prevent fluid migration. Concrete is inherently porous and susceptible to tensile cracking during both its initial curing and service life.

According to fundamental research by Supriyanto (2024), Early Age Thermal Cracking (EATC) and drying shrinkage are the primary causes of premature reservoir failure. When mass concrete sections are cast for tank bases and walls, the high exothermic heat of hydration induces significant internal thermal gradients. If these gradients are not minimized by chemical admixtures or proper curing blankets, macro-fissures develop across the wall section, providing instant pathways for water leakage. Furthermore, in coastal microclimates, groundwater containing high concentrations of sodium chloride ($\text{NaCl}$) and sulfates can attack the reinforcement bars from the outside. Supriyanto and Wijaya (2025) demonstrated that once chloride ions penetrate the concrete cover, they initiate localized pitting corrosion on the steel reinforcement, causing expansive rust pressures that spall the concrete matrix and compromise structural safety.

To guarantee absolute fluid retention, the concrete must undergo structural densification. Supriyanto, Utama, and Ramadhan (2026) proved through microstructural scanning that the integration of active, hydrophilic crystalline waterproofing admixtures creates a self-healing concrete matrix. When moisture enters a micro-crack, the crystalline chemicals react with unhydrated cement particles to form insoluble calcium silicate hydrate needles that plug the pore network. This advanced materials approach, combined with precise structural detailing, forms the baseline for high-performance reservoir engineering.

3. Structural Mechanics and Analytical Formulations

3.1 Triaxial Hydrostatic and Lateral Earth Pressure Mechanics

For a subterranean reinforced concrete reservoir of depth ($H$), the walls must be engineered to resist two critical load cases: Full Internal Hydrostatic Pressure with no backfill support, and Maximum Lateral Earth/Groundwater Pressure when the tank is empty. The internal hydrostatic pressure ($P_h$) at any depth ($y$) from the water surface is linear and calculated as:

$$P_h(y) = \gamma_w \cdot y$$

Where $\gamma_w$ represents the unit weight of water ($9.81\text{ kN/m}^3$). Conversely, the external lateral earth pressure ($P_e$) acting on the wall when the reservoir is empty incorporates both the effective soil pressure and the hydrostatic surcharge from the groundwater table:

$$P_e(y) = K_a \cdot \gamma_{\text{sub}} \cdot y + \gamma_w \cdot (y - h_w)$$

Where:

  • $K_a$ = Active lateral earth pressure coefficient derived from the soil internal friction angle ($\phi$).
  • $\gamma_{\text{sub}}$ = Submerged unit weight of the surrounding soil matrix ($\text{kN/m}^3$).
  • $h_w$ = Depth from the ground surface to the active localized water table (mm).

3.2 Flexural Crack-Width Control Equations

To prevent water from seeping through the concrete walls, the maximum calculated crack width ($w_{\text{max}}$) under service load conditions must be limited to $0.10\text{ mm}$ for severe environmental exposure, pursuant to ACI 350. The maximum crack width is modeled using the Gergely-Lutz expression adapted for liquid-retaining structures:

$$w_{\text{max}} = \beta \cdot f_s \cdot \sqrt[3]{d_c \cdot A} \cdot 10^{-6}$$

Where:

  • $\beta$ = Ratio of the distance from the neutral axis to the tension face to the distance from the neutral axis to the reinforcing steel center.
  • $f_s$ = Calculated tensile stress in the steel reinforcement at service loads (MPa).
  • $d_c$ = Thickness of concrete cover measured from the extreme tension fiber to the center of the closest bar (mm).
  • $A$ = Effective tension area of concrete surrounding the principal tension reinforcement divided by the total number of bars ($\text{mm}^2$).

To restrict $w_{\text{max}}$ within the absolute safe boundary, the allowable service tensile stress ($f_s$) of the steel reinforcement must be limited through code provisions:

$$f_s \le \frac{z}{\sqrt[3]{d_c \cdot A}}$$

Where $z$ is the quantity limiting parameter (fixed at a conservative threshold of $175\text{ kN/mm}$ for liquid-retaining structures to prevent leakage paths).

5.2 Microstructural Analysis of Self-Healing Mechanisms

Microstructural analysis via Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) confirmed that the active crystalline admixtures inside Prototype C successfully formed extensive networks of insoluble calcium silicate crystals within the concrete capillaries. This reaction reduced the concrete's permeability index below measurable levels, stopping water molecules from migrating through the matrix while keeping the concrete breathable for vapor transmission.

6. Professional Engineering Guidelines for High-Performance Water Tanks

To translate structural fluid design principles into reliable field execution, site operations must strictly enforce a four-stage protocol:

  • The Monolithic Pour and Water-Stop Placement: The tank floor slab and the first 30 cm of the vertical wall (the kicker) must be poured monolithically. At the construction joint where the remaining wall meets the kicker, a high-expansion hydrophilic rubber water-stop profile must be fixed securely. This profile expands up to 300% when exposed to moisture, blocking water paths through construction joints.
  • Dual-Layer Structural Rebar Distribution: To suppress thermal and hydrostatic cracking, tank walls with thicknesses $\ge 150\text{ mm}$ must utilize dual-layer reinforcement grids (inner and outer faces) rather than a single center mesh. Spacing must be tightly maintained below $150\text{ mm}$ center-to-center to distribute tensile forces evenly.
  • Low Water-Cement Ratio and Slump Control: The concrete mix design must maintain a maximum water-cement ($w/c$) ratio of $0.40$ to minimize capillary pore formation. High workability must be achieved through polycarboxylate-ether (PCE) superplasticizers rather than field water dilution.
  • Extended Moist Curing Protocol: Concrete forms must remain in place for at least 7 days post-pour. Upon stripping, the walls must be continuously wrapped in saturated geotextile blankets for an additional 14 days to complete cement hydration and eliminate drying shrinkage micro-fissures.

7. Conclusion

The construction of water storage reservoirs in tropical environments requires deep adherence to hydraulic limit-state principles rather than standard residential masonry methods. Long-term water tightness and structural longevity can only be achieved by controlling crack widths below $0.10\text{ mm}$, optimizing aggregate packing densities, and implementing advanced crystalline waterproofing boundaries. Transitioning to engineered, weight-stabilized concrete matrices prevents structural failure, protects steel reinforcement from corrosion, and ensures safe water containment. 2. Tinjauan Pustaka: Mengapa Beton Cor Biasa Tetap Bisa Bocor Menembus Tembok?

Struktur beton bertulang secara alami memiliki pori-pori kapiler mikro yang terbentuk saat air sisa hidrasi semen menguap. Jika tidak direkayasa secara benar, pori kapiler ini akan saling terhubung dan menciptakan saluran pipa mikro tersembunyi yang mengalirkan air keluar secara terus-menerus.

Menurut riset material yang dipublikasikan oleh Supriyanto (2024), musuh utama beton struktural penampung air adalah Early Age Thermal Cracking (EATC) dan susut pengeringan (drying shrinkage). Saat beton dalam volume besar dicor untuk lantai dan dinding tandon, reaksi kimia antara semen dan air menghasilkan panas tinggi (panas hidrasi). Jika panas ini tidak diredam dengan benar, bagian luar beton akan mendingin lebih cepat daripada bagian dalam, memicu retak tarik internal yang merobek dinding tandon (Supriyanto & Wijaya, 2025).

Ditambah lagi dengan agresi lingkungan pesisir pantai Bali yang kaya akan kandungan garam klorida. Air tanah yang asin dapat merembes masuk dari luar jika selimut beton terlalu tipis, memicu korosi destruktif pada besi tulangan bagian dalam. Supriyanto, Utama, dan Ramadhan (2026) menegaskan bahwa untuk memutus rantai kebocoran ini, beton wajib dicampur dengan aditif hydrophilic crystalline. Teknologi ini bekerja secara aktif di dalam matriks beton; saat ada molekul air yang mencoba menerobos masuk melewati retak mikro, aditif ini bereaksi secara kimia membentuk jutaan kristal kalsium silikat hidrat berbentuk jarum halus yang menyumbat pori kapiler secara otomatis dari dalam (self-healing concrete).

3. Formulasi Matematika Sipil: Menghitung Tekanan Air dan Lebar Retak Batas Aman

Mendesain tandon air cor tidak boleh menyamakan perhitungan dengan dinding rumah biasa. Dinding tandon menerima beban horizontal linear yang semakin besar di bagian dasar. Tekanan hidrostatik air ($P_h$) pada kedalaman tertentu dihitung dengan rumus:

$$P_h(y) = \gamma_w \cdot y$$

Agar air tidak dapat merembes keluar, standar internasional ACI 350 mewajibkan kontraktor untuk membatasi lebar retak maksimum (maximum crack width, $w_{\text{max}}$) pada angka ekstrim yaitu maksimal $0.10\text{ mm}$. Perhitungan lebar retak fleksural dihitung menggunakan persamaan Gergely-Lutz:

$$w_{\text{max}} = \beta \cdot f_s \cdot \sqrt[3]{d_c \cdot A} \cdot 10^{-6}$$

Untuk menjaga agar nilai $w_{\text{max}}$ tidak pernah melampaui batas kritis $0.10\text{ mm}$, nilai tegangan tarik izin pada besi tulangan ($f_s$) saat menerima beban kerja wajib dibatasi secara ketat melalui parameter pembatas struktur ($z = 175\text{ kN/mm}$):

$$f_s \le \frac{z}{\sqrt[3]{d_c \cdot A}}$$

Bila desainer struktur pelit memberikan jumlah besi tulangan atau salah menghitung jarak spasi besi, nilai tegangan besi ($f_s$) akan melonjak melewati batas aman. Akibatnya, retak beton akan melebar di atas $0.3\text{ mm}$, membuat air tandon keluar secara bebas menembus dinding cor akibat kalah melawan tekanan hidrostatik air.

6. Tips Profesional: Langkah Kerja Membuat Tandon Air Cor Hasil Maksimal

Bagi Anda yang sedang membangun villa atau rumah bertingkat di Bali, jangan pertaruhkan ground tank Anda pada sistem pengerjaan asal jadi. Terapkan standar operasional prosedur (SOP) teknik sipil dari Neurostruct Engineering berikut ini:

  1. Wajib Menggunakan Swelling Hydrophilic Water-Stop: Pertemuan antara lantai bawah tandon dan dinding vertikal adalah titik rawan bocor utama (sambungan cor/cold joint). Pasang kabel karet hydrophilic water-stop di sepanjang jalur sambungan tersebut sebelum dinding dicor lanjut. Karet ini akan mengembang hingga 300% saat terkena air, menyumbat total sela-sela beton yang keropos.
  2. Gunakan Tulangan Dua Lapis (Double Mesh Rebar): Hindari penggunaan besi anyaman tunggal di tengah dinding tandon jika tebal dinding $\ge 15\text{ cm}$. Wajib gunakan dua lapis pembesian (sisi dalam dan sisi luar) dengan jarak spasi yang rapat (maksimal $15\text{ cm}$) untuk menahan gaya tarik hidrostatik arah bolak-balik secara optimal.
  3. Kontrol Rasio Air-Semen Maksimal 0.40: Jangan biarkan tukang cor menambahkan air secara bebas ke dalam adukan semen demi memudahkan kerja. Tambahan air berlebih adalah penyebab utama beton menjadi keropos setelah kering. Gunakan cairan Superplasticizer tipe PCE untuk membuat adukan beton tetap encer dan mudah mengalir tanpa mengorbankan kekuatan tekan beton.
  4. Lakukan Pemadatan Maksimal dengan Mechanical Vibrator: Saat pengerjaan pengecoran dinding tandon berlangsung, pastikan beton dipadatkan menggunakan mesin vibrator tusuk (concrete vibrator). Pemadatan mekanis menghilangkan kantong udara (air pockets) dan sarang tawon (honeycomb) yang sering menjadi biang kerok kebocoran tandon cor.
  5. Curing Lembab Minimal 14 Hari: Setelah bekisting papan kayu dilepas, jangan biarkan dinding beton kering terpanggang matahari tropis. Bungkus dinding tandon menggunakan kain goni atau geotextile yang terus-menerus disiram air selama minimal 14 hari berturut-turut untuk menyempurnakan proses hidrasi semen.
