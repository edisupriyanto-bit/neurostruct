1. Introduction

Calcium silicate board ceilings offer a non-combustible, dimensionally stable, and moisture-resistant alternative to traditional gypsum or mineral fiber ceilings. In tropical climates, where high humidity and occasional seismic activity are common, Kalsiboard provides superior performance due to its low water absorption and high strength.

Key design parameters include:

- Maximum joist spacing: 400–600 mm depending on board thickness (typically 9–12 mm)

- Grid system: Exposed T-bar or concealed grid

- Fixing: Self-tapping screws at 150–200 mm centers along perimeter and 300 mm in the field

The deflection limit for ceiling systems is generally L/360 under live load. In seismic zones, additional bracing and flexible connections are required to accommodate movement.

This paper presents a complete professional installation guide with engineering calculations and practical recommendations.

2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed research highlights the advantages of calcium silicate boards in humid and fire-prone environments. Studies show lower moisture absorption (typically <10%) and better fire resistance (up to 120 minutes) compared to gypsum boards. Research on ceiling systems in tropical regions emphasizes the importance of proper ventilation gaps, corrosion-resistant framing, and correct screw spacing to prevent sagging and joint cracking. Recent works also validate the acoustic performance when combined with insulation materials.

3. Methodology and Installation Guidelines

3.1 Framework Design

Use galvanized steel C-channel or T-bar systems with minimum 0.5–0.6 mm thickness. Maximum spacing for main runners: 1200 mm, cross tees: 600 mm.

3.2 Board Fixing

- Pre-drill holes to prevent cracking.

- Screw spacing: 150 mm at perimeter, 300 mm in the field.

- Stagger joints in successive rows.

3.3 Joint Treatment

Apply jointing compound and tape in 3 coats for seamless finish. Sand between coats for smooth surface.

3.4 Load and Deflection Check

Ceiling self-weight + live load (0.25–0.5 kPa) must not exceed allowable deflection:

\[

\delta_{max} = \frac{L}{360}

\]

3.5 Special Considerations for Tropical and Seismic Areas

- Use corrosion-resistant (G90 or better) framing.

- Provide ventilation gaps to prevent condensation.

- Install seismic bracing per local codes.

4. Case Studies in Commercial and Residential Projects

In Bali’s hotels, villas, and office buildings, Kalsiboard ceiling systems installed with proper spacing and joint treatment delivered excellent flatness, fire resistance, and acoustic performance. Projects using 9–12 mm boards with 400 mm joist spacing showed no sagging after several years, even in high-humidity areas.

5. Challenges and Best Practices

Challenges include board sagging in high humidity, joint cracking due to thermal movement, and corrosion of metal frames. Best practices:

- Store boards flat and dry before installation.

- Maintain expansion gaps at perimeters.

- Use stainless steel or coated screws in coastal areas.

- Apply anti-fungal primer if needed.

Di iklim tropis, di mana kelembaban tinggi dan aktivitas gempa sesekali terjadi, kalsiboard memberikan performa superior karena penyerapan air rendah dan kekuatan tinggi.

Parameter desain kunci meliputi:

- Jarak joist maksimum: 400–600 mm tergantung ketebalan board (biasanya 9–12 mm)

- Sistem grid: Exposed T-bar atau concealed grid

- Pengikatan: Sekrup self-tapping dengan jarak 150–200 mm di tepi dan 300 mm di tengah

Batas defleksi untuk sistem plafon umumnya L/360 di bawah beban hidup. Di zona seismik, bracing tambahan dan sambungan fleksibel diperlukan.

3. Metodologi dan Panduan Pemasangan

3.1 Desain Rangka

Gunakan C-channel atau T-bar baja galvanis dengan ketebalan minimal 0,5–0,6 mm. Jarak maksimum runner utama: 1200 mm, cross tee: 600 mm.

3.2 Pengikatan Board

- Bor lubang terlebih dahulu untuk mencegah retak.

- Jarak sekrup: 150 mm di tepi, 300 mm di tengah.

- Susun sambungan secara staggered.

3.3 Perlakuan Sambungan

Oleskan jointing compound dan tape dalam 3 lapis untuk hasil seamless. Amplas di antara lapis untuk permukaan halus.

3.4 Pemeriksaan Beban dan Defleksi

Berat sendiri plafon + beban hidup (0,25–0,5 kPa) tidak boleh melebihi defleksi yang diizinkan:

5. Tantangan dan Praktik Terbaik

Tantangan meliputi melendut board di kelembaban tinggi, retak sambungan akibat pergerakan termal, dan korosi rangka metal. Praktik terbaik:

- Simpan board secara datar dan kering sebelum pemasangan.

- Pertahankan celah ekspansi di tepi.

- Gunakan sekrup stainless steel atau berlapis di area pantai.

- Oleskan primer anti-jamur jika diperlukan.
