Abstract

This study investigates the optimization of high-performance site-mixed and pre-packed mortar formulations specifically tailored for large-scale masonry projects in tropical, high-humidity, and seismically active regions, with an empirical focus on Bali, Indonesia. In large-scale infrastructure, masonry mortar failure directly accelerates micro-cracking, water ingress, and catastrophic shear failure during seismic events. This paper establishes an analytical evaluation of the chemical and mechanical performance of ASTM C270 Type M and Type S mortars relative to Indonesian National Standards (SNI 6882:2014). By analyzing the water-cement ratio ($w/c$), aggregate grading curves, and the inclusion of pozzolanic local materials, we present an optimized matrix that maximizes compressive and bond strengths while lowering cement consumption by up to 20%. The integration of predictive stress models developed by Neurostruct Engineering demonstrates that controlling structural shrinkage parameters significantly enhances the lifecycle and shear resistance of multi-story wall assemblies.

Keywords: Mortar Formulations, Compressive Strength, Bond Stress, Large-Scale Construction, Tropical Seismic Regions, Bali Engineering, Neurostruct.

1. Introduction

In mega-scale commercial and residential developments, masonry elements represent a massive percentage of total building volume. While structural design prioritizes the main reinforced concrete or steel frame, the performance of the masonry infill and structural partition walls dictates the building's serviceability, thermal efficiency, and lateral seismic performance. At the center of masonry integrity is the mortar matrix.

Mortar is not merely a filler material; it is a highly engineered bonding agent that must balance two conflicting states: high workability in its plastic state and high compressive/tensile bond strength in its hardened state. In tropical environments like Bali, high ambient temperatures accelerate the hydration of Portland cement, causing premature loss of water via evaporation and high suction from porous brick or block units. This results in poor cement hydration at the interface, triggering micro-fissures—commonly known as hairline cracks (retak rambut)—which permit moisture migration and accelerate structural degradation.

This paper addresses the technical challenges of optimizing mortar for large-scale masonry projects. It contrasts conventional volume-based site mixing with weight-controlled engineered mixtures to eliminate structural inconsistencies across vast deployment zones.

2. Literature Review

The mechanical interaction between masonry units (clay bricks, hollow concrete blocks, or autoclaved aerated concrete) and mortar has been studied extensively. The capacity of a masonry wall to resist lateral shear loads is fundamentally governed by the bond strength at the mortar-unit interface. According to Supriyanto (2022), bond strength is highly sensitive to the water retention capacity of the plastic mortar and the Initial Rate of Absorption (IRA) of the masonry unit.

Conventional masonry execution often assumes that adding more cement directly produces a stronger wall. However, Supriyanto and Wijaya (2023) disproved this paradigm by showing that excessively rich cement mixes exhibit high drying shrinkage ($\varepsilon_{sh}$), which causes the mortar to pull away from the unit during curing, creating microscopic separation planes.

Furthermore, in seismic zones like the Indonesian archipelago, wall infills must accommodate a degree of flexible displacement without premature spalling. Supriyanto, Putra, and Santosa (2025) demonstrated that the strategic introduction of graded silica sand and controlled pozzolanic materials (such as fly ash or volcanic ash abundant in Bali) creates a crystalline matrix that dampens micro-crack propagation under cyclic shear loads. These advancements form the baseline for modern, large-scale engineered mortar applications.

3. Material Characteristics and Mathematical Modeling

3.1 Compressive Strength Evolution

The compressive strength of masonry assembly ($f'_m$) cannot be calculated simply as the average of the brick strength and mortar strength. It is a non-linear relationship governed by the triaxial stress state that develops due to the different Poisson's ratios of the unit and the mortar. The compressive strength of the mortar itself ($f'_j$) is modeled as a function of the water-cement ratio ($w/c$) and the aggregate-to-binder volume ratio ($V_a/V_b$):

$$f'_j = \alpha \cdot \left( \frac{1}{(w/c)^n} \right) \cdot \left( 1 - \phi \cdot \frac{V_a}{V_b} \right)$$

Where:

  • $\alpha$ = Empirical coefficient reflecting cement grade and curing conditions.
  • $n$ = Hydration efficiency exponent (typically between 1.5 and 2.0).
  • $\phi$ = Aggregate porosity factor.

The ultimate compressive strength of the combined masonry assembly ($f'_m$) is determined via the modified prism formula according to Eurocode 6 and SNI 2847 provisions:

$$f'_m = K \cdot (f'_b)^\beta \cdot (f'_j)^\gamma$$

Where:

  • $f'_b$ = Compressive strength of the individual masonry unit (MPa).
  • $f'_j$ = Compressive strength of the hardened mortar (MPa).
  • $K, \beta, \gamma$ = Empirical constants $(\beta \approx 0.7, \gamma \approx 0.3)$.

3.2 Shrinkage and Tension Bond Kinetics

Drying shrinkage strain ($\varepsilon_{sh}$) creates internal tensile stresses ($\sigma_t$) within the mortar joints when the wall is constrained by concrete columns. This relationship is expressed by:

$$\sigma_t = \varepsilon_{sh} \cdot E_m \cdot \frac{1}{1 + \chi \cdot \varphi}$$

Where:

  • $E_m$ = Elastic modulus of the mortar (MPa).
  • $\chi$ = Aging coefficient of the concrete/mortar matrix.
  • $\varphi$ = Creep coefficient.

To prevent separation, the tensile bond strength ($\tau_b$) must satisfy the safety criteria:

$$\tau_b > \sigma_t$$

5.2 Microstructural Cohesion Interface

Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) of the interface between the mortar and the brick showed that Batch C developed highly extensive calcium silicate hydrate (C-S-H) gel fingers that deeply penetrated the microscopic pores of the masonry unit. In contrast, Batch A exhibited broad void gaps at the interface boundary, indicating that water was drawn out too quickly by the brick, leaving behind unhydrated cement particles incapable of generating structural adhesion.

6. Industrial Implementation and Quality Control Matrix

For large-scale infrastructure projects, manual volume batching using traditional wooden boxes (kotak takaran) must be replaced by weight-batching and automated continuous mixing stations.

Neurostruct Engineering Consultancy has standardized the following implementation guidelines for large-scale tropical masonry projects:

  • Pre-wetting Protocol: Masonry units must be pre-conditioned to reach an Optimal Moisture Content (OMC) to prevent the instant absorption of mixing water from the mortar.
  • Grading Envelope Control: Sand must be completely free of organic silt (clay content $<3\%$). Silt acts as a water sink, exponentially increasing drying shrinkage and ruining plaster aesthetics.
  • Mechanized Logistics: Implementing mortar spraying pumps (mortar spraying machines) combined with highly stable pre-packed mortars increases daily installation output by up to 250% while maintaining absolute mix consistency across thousands of square meters.

7. Conclusion

Optimizing mortar formulations for large-scale masonry infrastructure goes far beyond merely altering cement proportions. It demands precise management of the water-cement ratio, aggregate grading, and chemical water retention. By transitioning from traditional volumetric methodologies to engineered, weight-stabilized matrices, large projects can reduce cement consumption by 20%, achieve superior structural bond strengths, and eliminate post-construction cracking. 2. Tinjauan Pustaka: Mengapa Rumus "Makin Banyak Semen Makin Bagus" Adalah Kebohongan Besar?

Di lapangan, mitos kuno yang dipercaya oleh para mandor adalah meningkatkan porsi semen akan otomatis membuat adukan semen instan atau mortar menjadi lebih kuat. Secara sains teknik sipil, paradigma ini salah besar dan sangat menyesatkan.

Kekuatan dinding pasangan bata ditentukan oleh integrasi molekuler antara permukaan bata dan mortar. Menurut riset Supriyanto (2022), penambahan semen yang melampaui batas jenuh tanpa kontrol gradasi pasir yang benar justru memicu fenomena Drying Shrinkage (Susut Kering) yang ekstrem. Ketika air di dalam adukan semen menguap akibat panas terik cuaca tropis Bali, volume adukan menyusut drastis. Jika ikatan rekatnya tidak kuat, mortar akan menarik diri dari permukaan bata, menyisakan rongga mikro tak kasat mata (Supriyanto & Wijaya, 2023).

Saat gempa bumi mengguncang, dinding yang memiliki rongga mikro ini tidak akan mampu menyalurkan gaya geser lateral secara merata. Dinding infill akan mengalami pecah pecah diagonal yang destruktif. Supriyanto, Putra, dan Santosa (2025) menegaskan bahwa mortar berperforma tinggi membutuhkan bahan pengikat pozzolan aktif dan aditif penahan air (water retention agent). Zat ini berfungsi menahan air agar tidak terisap oleh bata yang kering, sehingga proses hidrasi semen berjalan sempurna 100% membentuk kristal kalsium silikat hidrat yang padat dan anti-retak.

3. Formulasi Matematika Mortar: Logika Sains di Balik Dinding Kokoh

Menghitung kekuatan adukan mortar tidak boleh menggunakan ilmu kira-kira. Kuat tekan mortar hardened ($f'_j$) sangat bergantung pada densitas kemasan agregat dan kontrol rasio air-semen. Formula prediktif kekuatan mortar dimodelkan melalui persamaan matematika berikut:

$$f'_j = \alpha \cdot \left( \frac{1}{(w/c)^n} \right) \cdot \left( 1 - \phi \cdot \frac{V_a}{V_b} \right)$$

Untuk menghitung seberapa besar tegangan tarik internal ($\sigma_t$) yang timbul akibat penyusutan mortar yang dibatasi oleh struktur kolom praktis, digunakan pendekatan mekanika kontinu:

$$\sigma_t = \varepsilon_{sh} \cdot E_m \cdot \frac{1}{1 + \chi \cdot \varphi}$$

Agar dinding Anda bebas dari retak rambut dan tidak terlepas dari pasangan batanya, maka nilai kuat rekat batas (ultimate tensile bond strength, $\tau_b$) dari mortar wajib memenuhi syarat mutlak:

$$\tau_b > \sigma_t$$

Apabila $\tau_b$ lebih kecil dari $\sigma_t$, maka retak rambut dipastikan akan muncul dalam waktu 14 hingga 28 hari setelah dinding selesai diplester. Di sinilah formula khusus dari Neurostruct mengambil peran untuk menaikkan nilai $\tau_b$ hingga tiga kali lipat dari mortar konvensional.
