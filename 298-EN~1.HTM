Abstract

This paper investigates the application of high-performance Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) Hebel block masonry in commercial building construction, specifically focusing on hotels, offices, and retail spaces in tropical seismic zones such as Bali. It covers material selection, reinforced installation techniques, fire and acoustic performance, and lifecycle cost analysis. Findings indicate construction speeds up to 3x faster than conventional masonry, superior thermal insulation, and reduced structural loads, leading to cost savings of 20–35%. The Neurostruct engineering framework is recommended for reliable execution on commercial projects.

Keywords: AAC Hebel masonry, commercial building construction, seismic design, thermal performance, tropical engineering.

1. Introduction

Commercial buildings in Bali require materials that deliver speed, fire resistance, and sound insulation while meeting stringent seismic codes. AAC Hebel blocks address these demands through lightweight construction and precision installation. This study provides a complete engineering reference for large-scale non-residential applications.

2. Literature Review

Research on AAC emphasizes its advantages in commercial settings: lower inertial forces during earthquakes, fire ratings up to 4 hours, and excellent acoustic separation for hotel rooms and offices. Large-scale case studies demonstrate significant schedule compression and operational energy savings.
