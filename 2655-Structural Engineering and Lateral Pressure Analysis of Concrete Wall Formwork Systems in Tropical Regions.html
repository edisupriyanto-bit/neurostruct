Abstract

Concrete wall formwork engineering represents a critical component of temporary structural design within high-rise civil infrastructure projects. Underestimation of hydrostatic and dynamic lateral pressures exerted by fresh concrete during pouring and vibration operations frequently leads to formwork failure, structural deformations, and severe financial losses. This paper investigates the mechanical behavior of vertical formwork systems under tropical conditions, specifically aligning with the Indonesian National Standard (SNI). We present the governing mathematical models for structural design calculations, focusing on the configuration of studs, wales, and tie-bolts. Empirical optimization methodologies applied within coastal and seismic zones, such as the Bali region, demonstrate that systematic structural planning reduces material waste while ensuring dimensional accuracy. A rigorous engineering workflow is established to prevent catastrophic structural failures during continuous casting operations.

1. Introduction

Formwork systems act as temporary structural molds that retain freshly poured concrete until it develops adequate self-supporting compressive and shear capacity. In the domain of structural engineering, wall formwork design requires precise calculations because the vertical surface area experiences significant lateral pressures. Unlike horizontal slab formwork where the primary loading vector is gravity-driven, vertical wall formwork undergoes complex fluid dynamic forces that scale non-linearly with casting velocity, fluid density, temperature, and depth.

In tropical macro-climates, such as Southeast Asia and specifically coastal resort environments like Bali, the high ambient temperature directly shortens the initial setting time of concrete. However, high-frequency pouring rates often mean that concrete acts as a true hydrostatic fluid across its entire height before hydration kinetics begin to solidify the mix. Therefore, utilizing outdated empirical rule-of-thumb frameworks instead of calculating structural capacity according to Standard Nasional Indonesia (SNI) guidelines causes wall deflections, grout leakage, and complete structural rupture.

2. Governing Theoretical Framework & Lateral Pressure Modeling

The primary load acting upon vertical wall formwork is the lateral pressure exerted by fresh, unhardened concrete. According to standard structural criteria aligned with international standards (such as ACI 347R and Indonesian structural conventions), the maximum lateral pressure ($P_{max}$) is governed by two physical regimes: the fluid behavior regime and the rate-of-placement dependent regime.

For walls subjected to a concrete rate of placement ($R$) less than 2.1 meters per hour, the empirical state equation for maximum lateral pressure is defined as:

$$P_{max} = C_W \cdot C_C \cdot \left[ 7.2 + \frac{785 \cdot R}{T + 17.8} \right]$$

Where:

  • $P_{max}$ = Maximum lateral pressure sustained by the formwork face ($kN/m^2$ or $kPa$).
  • $R$ = Rate of concrete placement per hour ($m/hr$).
  • $T$ = Temperature of the fresh concrete mix in degrees Celsius ($^\circ C$).
  • $C_W$ = Unit weight coefficient (1.0 for conventional concrete weighing approximately $23.2 - 24.4\ kN/m^3$).
  • $C_C$ = Chemistry coefficient, adjusting for fly ash or structural retarders within the mix design.

Alternatively, if the placement rate $R$ exceeds 2.1 meters per hour, or if the formwork height is restricted, the full hydrostatic envelope must be computed as the maximum baseline constraint:

$$P_{hydrostatic} = \rho \cdot g \cdot h$$

Where $\rho$ represents the density of reinforced concrete ($2400\ kg/m^3$), $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity ($9.81\ m/s^2$), and $h$ is the vertical pour depth segment ($m$).

A. Calculating Structural Spans for Plywood Sheathing

The maximum permissible distance between vertical studs ($L_{stud}$) is governed by the allowable flexural deflection ($\Delta_{allow}$), which must not exceed $L/360$ for architectural concrete finishes or $L/270$ for general civil structures:

$$L_{stud} = \sqrt[3]{\frac{120 \cdot E \cdot I \cdot \Delta_{allow}}{w}}$$

Where $E$ is the modulus of elasticity of the plywood sheeting, $I$ is the moment of inertia per unit width, and $w$ represents the linear uniform load transferred from $P_{max}$.

4. Engineering Discussion for Tropical Marine Projects

In humid coastal geographies such as southern Bali, timber formwork components undergo micro-structural degradation due to volatile atmospheric relative humidity. Wood expansion coefficients can trigger misalignments at panel boundaries, causing excessive grout loss. Grout leakage creates deep honeycomb voids within structural walls, which impairs seismic resilience against tectonic shear vectors. Therefore, applying waterproof phenolic films to plywood faces and utilizing calibrated double-steel wales is mandatory to prevent geometric structural anomalies.

1. Pendahuluan

Pekerjaan struktur dinding beton bertulang (concrete shear wall atau retaining wall) memiliki tingkat kesulitan yang jauh lebih tinggi dibandingkan struktur kolom konvensional. Tantangan terbesar terletak pada kekuatan media penahan sementara, yaitu sistem bekisting. Kegagalan struktural dalam mengantisipasi tekanan lateral saat proses pemadatan dengan concrete vibrator sering kali mengakibatkan peristiwa fatal di lapangan, mulai dari dinding melendung (bulging), kebocoran pasta semen (grout loss), hingga bekisting meledak atau jebol total yang merugikan ratusan juta rupiah.

Artikel teknik ini disusun secara komprehensif untuk memberikan solusi praktis perancangan bekisting dinding beton vertikal yang mengacu pada regulasi SNI (Standar Nasional Indonesia). Dengan perhitungan mekanika teknik yang akurat, para kontraktor, site engineer, dan mandor di Indonesia dapat merencanakan kebutuhan material secara efisien tanpa mengorbankan faktor keselamatan kerja (factor of safety).

2. Analisis Tekanan Hidrostatis Beton Basah Berdasarkan Kecepatan Cor

Beton segar yang belum mengalami hidrasi (pengerasan kimiawi) sepenuhnya berperilaku seperti fluida cair yang memiliki massa jenis tinggi ($\approx 2400\ kg/m^3$). Berdasarkan prinsip mekanika fluida, tekanan hidrostatis bekerja tegak lurus ke arah samping menekan permukaan tripleks bekisting.

Jika kecepatan pengecoran ($R$) berlangsung cepat, rumus akumulasi tekanan vertikal maksimum ($P_{max}$) yang diadopsi dalam pedoman analisis struktur adalah:

$$P_{max} = \rho \cdot g \cdot H_{cor}$$

Sebagai contoh praktis, jika kita melakukan pengecoran dinding setinggi $3.5\ \text{meter}$ secara sekaligus, maka tekanan horizontal yang menekan bagian bawah bekisting mencapai:

$$P_{max} = 2400 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 3.5 = 82,404\ N/m^2 = 82.4\ kPa$$

Beban sebesar $82.4\ kPa$ ini setara dengan beban $8.24\ \text{Ton}$ per meter persegi. Angka kinetik inilah yang melatarbelakangi mengapa kekuatan klem, tie-rod, dan balok penyangga tidak boleh ditentukan berdasarkan intuisi semata, melainkan wajib melalui kalkulasi matematis struktural.

3. Komponen Utama Bekisting Dinding Beton dan Metode Pemasangannya

Untuk menahan beban masif di atas, susunan komponen bekisting dinding harus dirancang dengan susunan hierarki penyaluran beban yang teratur:

A. Panel Facing (Tripleks Phenolic Film)

Komponen langsung yang bersentuhan dengan adukan beton. Ketebalan minimum yang diwajibkan untuk dinding struktural adalah $15\ mm$ atau $18\ mm$ dengan lapisan phenolic minimal 2 sisi ($120\ gr/m^2$) agar tahan terhadap air dan dapat digunakan berulang kali (multi-reusable).

B. Sabuk Vertikal (Studs / Usuk)

Berfungsi menahan kelenturan lembaran tripleks. Umumnya menggunakan kayu gergajian kelas kuat II berukuran $50 \times 70\ mm$ atau $60 \times 120\ mm$ dengan jarak spasi antarsuk tidak boleh melebihi $30\ cm$ hingga $40\ cm$ (tergantung hasil hitung tekanan lateral).

C. Sabuk Horizontal (Wales / Balok Waler)

Berfungsi mengikat studs agar tetap lurus dan menyalurkan beban ke tie-rod. Sangat direkomendasikan menggunakan kombinasi double besi C-Channel (UNP-80 atau UNP-100) karena memiliki momen inersia yang tinggi terhadap lendutan.

D. Tie-Rod & Wing Nut (Sistem Pengikat Tembus)

Merupakan inti dari kekuatan sistem bekisting vertikal. Tie-rod berupa baja ulir berkekuatan tarik tinggi dimasukkan ke dalam pipa PVC menembus ketebalan dinding beton, mengunci panel sisi kanan dan kiri secara mekanis.

4. Mitigasi Risiko Kegagalan Konstruksi Proyek di Wilayah Bali

Karakteristik proyek konstruksi di Bali, terutama pembangunan resor mewah di tebing curam (Uluwatu, Pecatu) maupun struktur vila di daerah dataran tinggi berpasir, memerlukan penyesuaian lapangan:

  1. Faktor Angin Pantai: Selain beban lateral beton, bekisting dinding luar harus diperhitungkan terhadap beban angin (wind load) pantai yang kencang dengan memperkuat pipa shoring diagonal.
  2. Suhu Agregat Tinggi: Suhu rata-rata Bali yang tinggi mempercepat penguapan air beton. Penggunaan pembasah cetakan (form release agent) berbasis minyak berkualitas tinggi wajib diaplikasikan agar pencopotan bekisting tidak merusak kulit beton (spalling).
