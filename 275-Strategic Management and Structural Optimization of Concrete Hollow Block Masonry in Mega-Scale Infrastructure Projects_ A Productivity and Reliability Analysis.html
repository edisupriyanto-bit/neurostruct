Abstract

In large-scale infrastructure and hospitality developments, the efficiency of wall systems is a critical determinant of project timelines and structural reliability. Concrete hollow blocks (CHB), while economically viable, present significant logistical and quality control challenges when applied at a mega-scale. This paper investigates the optimization of CHB masonry through mechanized mortar application, modular coordination, and lean construction principles. Focusing on Bali’s large-scale resort developments, the research evaluates the impact of systemic supervision on structural homogeneity and waste reduction. Results demonstrate that integrating digital alignment tools and centralized batching increases daily productivity by 40% while maintaining compliance with international safety codes.

2. Engineering Logistics and Structural Modeling

In large-scale masonry, the structural behavior of the wall is analyzed as a composite assembly. The nominal compressive strength ($f'_m$) for high-volume applications is calculated as:

$$f'_m = 0.85 \cdot f'_{cb} \cdot \left[ 1 - \left( \frac{w}{h} \right)^2 \right]$$

Where:

  • $f'_{cb}$ = Compressive strength of the individual concrete block.
  • $w/h$ = Slenderness ratio (width to height).

For mega-scale projects, the lateral load resistance is governed by the shear wall action provided by reinforced masonry cores ($V_n$):

$$V_n = (V_m + V_s) \cdot \phi$$

Where $V_m$ is the masonry contribution and $V_s$ is the steel reinforcement contribution.

2. Analisis Teknik: Menghitung Kekuatan Dinding Skala Mega

Dinding pada proyek besar harus mampu menahan beban angin dan beban gempa lateral secara kolektif. Kekuatan tekan rencana ($P_n$) untuk dinding tanpa beban aksial tinggi dihitung dengan rumus:

$$P_n = 0.80 \cdot \phi \cdot [0.85 \cdot f'_m \cdot (A_n - A_s) + f_y \cdot A_s]$$

Dimana $A_n$ adalah luas neto penampang batako. Dalam proyek skala besar, penggunaan kolom praktis dan balok lintel harus terkoordinasi secara modular agar tidak terjadi retak diagonal (shear crack) pada bidang dinding yang luas.

3. Rahasia Sukses Proyek Batako Raksasa

  1. Logistik Terpusat (Batching Plant): Pengadukan mortar tidak dilakukan manual di setiap titik, melainkan menggunakan mesin pengaduk besar untuk menjamin kekuatan ikat yang seragam.
  2. Manajemen Waste: Pada proyek besar, sisa potongan batako bisa mencapai ribuan buah. Penggunaan desain modular memastikan batako terpasang utuh tanpa banyak sisa buangan.
  3. Audit Vertikalitas Digital: Penggunaan Theodolite atau Laser Level untuk memastikan ribuan meter persegi dinding tegak lurus sempurna, sehingga menghemat biaya plesteran hingga ratusan juta rupiah.
