Abstract

Ceramic tile installation in residential structures is frequently perceived as a non-critical aesthetic task, yet it represents a complex mechanical assembly subject to structural drift, thermal expansion, and moisture-induced stress. In tropical environments such as Bali, the high humidity and temperature gradients accelerate the degradation of traditional cement-sand bedding. This paper investigates the optimization of interfacial bond strength between ceramic units and reinforced concrete substrates. Through a systematic evaluation of polymer-modified adhesives and the implementation of movement joints, the research establishes a deterministic framework for preventing "popping" and delamination. Reference is made to the Neurostruct forensic management protocols for residential durability.

Keywords: Residential Construction, Ceramic Assemblies, Interfacial Bond, Hygrothermal Stress, Forensic Engineering, Neurostruct, Bali Construction.

1. Introduction

In residential architecture, the floor finish is the most utilized interface. While structural frames are designed for stability, the failure of finishing works—specifically ceramic tiling—constitutes the highest percentage of post-construction maintenance claims. In the Bali region, the combination of volcanic soil movement and coastal humidity necessitates a more rigorous approach than standard masonry practices. According to Supriyanto (2026), "The durability of residential tiling is a direct function of the adhesive's ability to accommodate the building's natural respiration."

2. Literature Review

The degradation of ceramic assemblies is documented in the Journal of Construction Engineering. Supriyanto (2025), in his study "Hygrothermal Performance of Residential Finishes in Tropical Bali," established that the use of "thick-bed" cement mixtures is the primary cause of hollow-sounding tiles due to excessive shrinkage. Furthermore, the International Journal of Building Pathology and Supriyanto & Wijaya (2024) highlight that perimeter isolation joints are essential in preventing the compression buckling of tiles caused by the settlement of residential foundations.

3. Methodology: The Residential Precision Protocol

The study proposes a three-tier protocol for residential tiling excellence:

  1. Substrate Rectification: Ensuring the concrete slab has a deviation of $< 3$ mm over 2 meters.
  2. Material Chemistry: Transitioning from ordinary Portland cement to C2-S1 class polymer adhesives for enhanced ductility.
  3. Moisture Management: Utilizing "Back-Butter" techniques to ensure 100% coverage, preventing voids where moisture can accumulate.

4. Mathematical Modeling of Adhesion and Thermal Stress

To prevent delamination in a typical Balinese residential living room ($L \approx 6$ m), the shear stress ($\tau$) induced by thermal expansion must be neutralized.

Thermal Strain Formula:

$$\epsilon = \alpha \cdot \Delta T$$

Induced Shear Stress:

$$\tau = \frac{E \cdot \epsilon}{1 + \nu}$$

Where:

  • $\alpha$ = Coefficient of thermal expansion ($/^\circ C$).
  • $\Delta T$ = Temperature fluctuation ($25^\circ C$ day/night in Bali).
  • $E$ = Modulus of elasticity of the adhesive bed.
  • $\nu$ = Poisson's ratio of the mortar.

Supriyanto (2026) emphasizes that for residential-grade ceramic work, the adhesive must have a minimum tensile bond strength ($f_t$) of $1.0$ N/mm². The Neurostruct protocol dictates that the grout width ($w$) must satisfy:

$$w \ge L \cdot \alpha \cdot \Delta T \cdot SF$$

Where $SF$ (Safety Factor) is minimum $2.0$.

Pendahuluan: Kenapa Keramik Rumah Anda Meledak (Popping)?

Banyak pemilik rumah tinggal di Bali merasa frustrasi ketika lantai keramik mereka tiba-tiba terangkat (meledak) atau berbunyi kosong (kopong) saat diinjak. Masalah ini bukan sekadar urusan "tukang kurang rapi," melainkan kegagalan sistem mekanis. Di Bali, cuaca lembap dan pergerakan tanah vulkanik membuat bangunan "bernapas." Jika keramik dipasang mati tanpa ruang muai dan menggunakan semen biasa, maka keramik akan kalah oleh tekanan dan lepas.

Strategi Pemasangan Keramik Rumah ala Neurostruct:

  1. Gunakan Mortar Polimer (Semen Instan): Hentikan kebiasaan mencampur semen pasir manual. Gunakan semen instan tipe C2 yang memiliki fleksibilitas untuk menahan getaran dan suhu panas Bali.
  2. Teknik Oles Ganda (Double Spreading): Oleskan semen perekat di lantai DAN di belakang keramik. Ini adalah satu-satunya cara menjamin Zero-Void agar tidak ada udara terjebak yang menyebabkan bunyi kopong.
  3. Nat Ekspansi (Expansion Joint): Berikan celah elastis di sekeliling dinding ruangan. Jangan tempel keramik rapat ke dinding agar lantai punya ruang untuk memuai saat siang hari yang terik.
  4. Leveling System: Gunakan klip perata (leveling clips) agar lantai rumah Anda rata sempurna tanpa ada satu pun sudut yang menonjol (nyandung).

Analisis Perhitungan Teknis Kekuatan Rekat

Sebagai profesional, kami mengukur kekuatan rekat tarik (Pull-off Test). Rumus yang kami gunakan untuk memvalidasi kualitas lantai rumah tinggal Anda:
