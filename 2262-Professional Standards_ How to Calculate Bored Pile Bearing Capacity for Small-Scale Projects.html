Professional Standards: How to Calculate Bored Pile Bearing Capacity for Small-Scale Projects

Author: edisupriyanto@gmail.com

In Bali’s fast-growing villa and small resort construction sector, bored piles (tiang bor) have become the preferred deep foundation solution for sites with weak surface soil, steep slopes, or high seismic risk. For small-scale projects, accurately calculating the ultimate bearing capacity of bored piles is essential to ensure structural safety without over-designing and wasting budget. In 2026, professional standards based on SNI 8460:2017 (Geotechnical Design Requirements) and international practices (such as those from AASHTO or Eurocode 7) must be followed even for modest projects.

The total ultimate bearing capacity of a bored pile (Qu) is the sum of two main components:

Qu = Qp + Qs

Where:

- Qp = End bearing capacity (tip resistance)

- Qs = Skin friction capacity (shaft resistance)

Step-by-Step Professional Calculation Method for Small Projects:

1. Determine Soil Parameters

Conduct at least two boreholes with Standard Penetration Test (SPT) every 1.5 m depth. Identify soil layers, N-SPT values, and obtain laboratory data (cohesion c, friction angle φ, and unit weight γ).

2. Calculate End Bearing Capacity (Qp)

Qp = qp × Ap

qp (unit end bearing) = Nc × c + Nq × σ'v + 0.5 × Nγ × B × γ

(Use Meyerhof or Terzaghi bearing capacity factors adjusted for depth.)

For practical small projects in Bali, many engineers use the simplified correlation:

qp (kPa) ≈ 40 × N-SPT (at pile tip) with a maximum limit of 12,000 kPa.

3. Calculate Skin Friction Capacity (Qs)

Qs = Σ (fs × As) for each soil layer

fs (unit skin friction) = Ks × σ'v × tanδ or α × cu (for cohesive soils)

Common practical values in Bali volcanic soils:

- Clay: fs = 0.5 × cu (α-method)

- Sand: fs = 1.5–2.0 × N-SPT (kPa)

4. Apply Safety Factors

For small-scale projects, apply a global safety factor of 2.5–3.0 on ultimate capacity to obtain allowable capacity (Qa). In seismic zones like Bali, consider additional reduction factors.

5. Consider Pile Dimensions and Construction Effects

Typical small-project bored piles: diameter 40–80 cm, length 8–25 m. Account for concrete quality (K-350 minimum), rebar, and construction defects (necking or slurry cake) that can reduce capacity by 20–30% if not properly controlled.

Common Challenges in Bali Small Projects:

- Highly variable volcanic soil layers

- High groundwater table during rainy season

- Limited access for large drilling rigs

- Tight budgets that tempt under-design

Accurate calculation prevents both dangerous under-capacity and expensive over-design. A well-designed bored pile system can reduce foundation costs by 30–40% compared to oversized pad footings on poor soil.

Standar Profesional: Cara Menghitung Kapasitas Dukung Bored Pile untuk Proyek Skala Kecil

Di sektor pembangunan villa dan resort kecil yang sedang berkembang pesat di Bali, tiang bor (bored pile) menjadi solusi fondasi dalam yang paling banyak digunakan pada lahan dengan tanah lemah, lereng curam, atau risiko gempa tinggi. Bagi proyek skala kecil, menghitung kapasitas dukung tiang bor secara akurat sangat penting untuk menjamin keamanan struktur tanpa over-design yang membuang biaya. Tahun 2026, standar profesional berdasarkan SNI 8460:2017 dan praktik internasional wajib diterapkan meski untuk proyek sederhana.

Kapasitas dukung ultimate tiang bor (Qu) dihitung sebagai:

Qu = Qp + Qs

Di mana:

- Qp = Kapasitas ujung (end bearing)

- Qs = Kapasitas gesek samping (skin friction)

Metode Perhitungan Profesional Langkah demi Langkah untuk Proyek Kecil:

1. Tentukan Parameter Tanah

Lakukan minimal dua bor log dengan SPT setiap 1,5 m. Identifikasi lapisan tanah dan ambil data laboratorium.

2. Hitung Kapasitas Ujung (Qp)

Qp = qp × Ap

qp ≈ 40 × N-SPT (pada ujung tiang) atau menggunakan rumus bearing capacity lengkap.

3. Hitung Kapasitas Gesek Samping (Qs)

Qs = Σ (fs × As) per lapisan tanah.

Gunakan metode α untuk tanah lempung dan β-method untuk pasir.

4. Terapkan Faktor Keamanan

Untuk proyek kecil, gunakan faktor keamanan 2,5–3,0 untuk mendapatkan kapasitas allow (Qa).

5. Pertimbangkan Dimensi dan Efek Konstruksi

Diameter umum 40–80 cm, panjang 8–25 m. Perhatikan kualitas beton K-350 dan kemungkinan cacat konstruksi.

Tantangan di Proyek Kecil Bali:

- Tanah vulkanik yang sangat variatif

- Air tanah tinggi saat musim hujan

- Akses terbatas untuk rig besar

- Anggaran ketat

Perhitungan yang tepat dapat menghemat biaya fondasi hingga 30–40% dibandingkan footings konvensional.
