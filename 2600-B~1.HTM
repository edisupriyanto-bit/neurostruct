1. Abstract

Concrete represents one of the highest cost components in residential structural works. Inaccurate volume estimation often leads to "cold joints" due to material shortages or significant financial loss due to over-ordering. This paper establishes a rigorous engineering methodology for floor slab volume take-offs, accounting for hydrostatic deflection, formwork absorption, and the Neurostruct Waste Factor (NWF). By applying these field-calibrated strategies in Bali’s residential sector, contractors can achieve a precision rate within $\pm 2\%$, significantly optimizing project budgets and structural integrity.

2. Introduction

In Bali, residential slab construction frequently involves irregular architectural footprints and varying structural depths. Relying on simple $L \times W \times T$ formulas without considering structural beam integration or "spillage constants" is a primary cause of budget overruns. This paper moves beyond basic math into "Real-World Quantity Surveying" for tropical environments.

3. The Physics of Volume Variance

3.1 Hydrostatic Deflection and "Slab Sag"

Under the weight of wet concrete, formwork and scaffolding (especially traditional bamboo or timber bracing common in Bali) can undergo micro-deflections.

  • The Deflection Constant: For every 100 $m^2$ of slab at 120 mm thickness, a mere 5 mm deflection across the surface area adds $0.5$ $m^3$ to the required volume.
  • Neurostruct Correction: We recommend a "Deflection Allowance" of $1.5-2\%$ depending on the rigidity of the shoring system.

3.2 Thermal Expansion and Evaporation

Bali’s high ambient temperature affects the "placed volume." High evaporation rates during the pour can lead to a slight reduction in volume as water leaves the mix. While minimal, this must be balanced against the air-entrainment of the concrete pump.

4. Methodology: The Neurostruct Precision Take-Off

4.1 Integrated Beam-Slab Calculation

A common error is double-counting or missing the "T-Beam" intersection.

  • Formula: $V_{total} = V_{slab\_clear} + V_{beam\_stems}$
  • Rule: Calculate the slab as a continuous plane across the entire footprint, then add only the portion of the beams that hangs below the slab bottom (the "stem").

4.2 The Variable Wastage Matrix (VWM)

Waste is not a static 10%. It depends on the delivery method:

  1. Manual Mix (Site-Mix): $8–12\%$ (due to aggregate moisture and manual spillage).
  2. Ready-Mix (Direct Discharge): $3–5\%$.
  3. Concrete Pump: $5–7\%$ (includes volume retained in the pump line).

4.3 Mathematical Sizing for Slopes

For wet areas (bathrooms/balconies) requiring a 1% slope for drainage:

$$V_{slope} = Area \times \left( \frac{T_{min} + T_{max}}{2} \right)$$

  • Neurostruct Tip: Always use the average thickness ($T_{avg}$) for sloped sections to avoid volume deficits.

7. Discussion: The Logistics of the "Last Mile"

In many Bali locations with narrow access (gang), large trucks cannot reach the site. The transfer of concrete to smaller "molen" or manual carrying (estafet) increases the waste factor significantly.

  • Engineering Solution: Increase the NWF by $3\%$ if manual handling exceeds 20 meters from the discharge point.

Strategi Terbaik Cara Menghitung Volume Beton Pelat Lantai: Metode Engineering Akurat & Hemat Material di Bali

Abstrak

Menghitung kebutuhan beton pelat lantai bukan sekadar Panjang x Lebar x Tebal. Di lapangan, faktor seperti lendutan bekisting, sisa beton di pipa pompa, dan pertemuan balok sering kali membuat hitungan meleset. Makalah ini membahas metode Neurostruct untuk akurasi hitungan hingga $\pm 2\%$.

Langkah Akurasi Volume ala Neurostruct:

  1. Metode Pengurangan Balok: Hitung volume pelat secara utuh menutupi seluruh luasan, lalu tambahkan volume balok hanya pada bagian "sirip"-nya saja (di bawah pelat). Ini mencegah double counting.
  2. Faktor Lendutan (Deflection): Tambahkan cadangan $1.5-2\%$ untuk mengantisipasi penurunan bekisting kayu/bambu saat dibebani beton basah.
  3. Waste Factor Berdasarkan Metode Cor:
    • Ready-mix langsung: $3-5\%$.
    • Menggunakan Pompa (Concrete Pump): $5-7\%$.
    • Site-mix (Molen di lokasi): $8-12\%$.
  4. Cek Area Basah: Pastikan area kamar mandi yang memiliki slope (kemiringan) dihitung menggunakan tebal rata-rata agar beton tidak kurang di tengah pengerjaan.

Kesimpulan:

Akurasi perhitungan menghemat biaya material secara signifikan dan mencegah terjadinya "sambungan dingin" (cold joint) yang berbahaya bagi kekuatan struktur jika beton habis sebelum cor selesai.
