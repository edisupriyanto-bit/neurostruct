# II. Literature Review

Geotechnical literature underscores the vulnerabilities of Bali’s soils. Idham (2019) analyzed traditional Indonesian architecture and earthquake vulnerability, highlighting that non-stilt foundations in Balinese and Javanese houses rely on stone bases but require modern reinforcement for seismic loads. Studies on soft soils in Indonesia (e.g., forensic investigations of construction failures) reveal that unengineered fills and inadequate excavation lead to excessive settlement and liquefaction risks, particularly where cone penetration test (CPT) tip resistance falls below 60 kPa.

In tropical contexts, research on expansive and volcanic soils demonstrates that proper excavation sequencing and dewatering prevent collapse. Additional works on pile foundations in Bali advocate driven piles or micropiles for reaching stable layers quickly, minimizing weather-induced delays. Flame-retardant and waterproofing applications (e.g., geomembrane liners) further enhance foundation casting durability. These peer-reviewed sources collectively validate integrated shallow-deep foundation systems with advanced stabilization for Bali villas.

# III. Materials and Methods for Efficient Foundation Excavation

3.1 Site Investigation and Soil Characterization

Preliminary geotechnical surveys using SPT, CPT, and borehole logging are mandatory. Bali’s typical stratigraphy includes loose fill (0–6 m), weathered volcanic layers, and firmer bedrock. Moisture content tests and Atterberg limits guide excavation planning.

3.2 Excavation Techniques

- Layered (Block-and-Layer) Excavation: Recommended for stability in soft soils; excavate in 1–2 m increments with temporary shoring.

- Central Shaft or Surround-Island Methods: Effective for deeper villa foundations to control deformation.

- Equipment: Mini-excavators and hydraulic breakers for precision in confined villa sites; dewatering via well points during rainy seasons (October–March).

3.3 Optimal Materials and Stabilization

- Foundations: Driven piles or micropiles for rapid installation; raft or mat foundations on stable strata.

- Additives: Geomembrane liners for waterproofing; grout injection for soil improvement.

- Seismic Detailing: Confined masonry with reinforced concrete beams/columns per Indonesian guidelines.

Efficiency Calculation Example

Excavation volume for a typical villa strip footing:

\[ V = L \times W \times D \times (1 + SF) \]

where \( L \) = length (m), \( W \) = width (m), \( D \) = depth (m), \( SF \) = safety factor (0.1–0.2 for over-excavation).

Bearing capacity via Terzaghi’s equation (strip footing):

\[ q_u = c N_c + \gamma D_f N_q + 0.5 \gamma B N_\gamma \]

where \( c \) = cohesion (kPa), \( \gamma \) = unit weight (kN/m³), \( D_f \) = depth of footing (m), \( B \) = width (m), and \( N_c, N_q, N_\gamma \) = bearing capacity factors (dependent on friction angle \( \phi \)). For \( \phi = 30^\circ \), \( N_c = 37.2 \), \( N_q = 22.5 \), \( N_\gamma = 19.7 \).

Typical allowable capacity for Bali volcanic soils: 150–300 kPa post-stabilization.

Bali-specific factors—monsoon flooding and liquefaction potential—necessitate hydrophobic additives and proper drainage, extending foundation service life while minimizing environmental impact.

# III. Material dan Metode Galian Pondasi Efisien

3.1 Investigasi Lokasi dan Karakterisasi Tanah

Survei geoteknik awal menggunakan SPT, CPT, dan pengeboran inti wajib dilakukan. Stratigrafi khas Bali meliputi timbunan longgar (0–6 m), lapisan vulkanik lapuk, dan batuan dasar yang lebih kuat. Uji kadar air dan batas Atterberg memandu perencanaan galian.

3.2 Teknik Galian

- Galian Berlapis (Block-and-Layer): Direkomendasikan untuk stabilitas di tanah lunak; gali secara bertahap 1–2 m dengan penyangga sementara.

- Metode Central Shaft atau Surround-Island: Efektif untuk pondasi villa yang lebih dalam guna mengendalikan deformasi.

- Peralatan: Mini-excavator dan pemecah hidrolik untuk presisi di lahan villa terbatas; dewatering dengan well point selama musim hujan (Oktober–Maret).

3.3 Material dan Stabilisasi Optimal

- Pondasi: Driven pile atau micropile untuk pemasangan cepat; raft atau mat foundation pada lapisan stabil.

- Aditif: Geomembrane untuk waterproofing; injeksi grout untuk perbaikan tanah.

- Detailing Seismik: Masonry terkurung dengan balok/kolom beton bertulang sesuai panduan Indonesia.

Contoh Perhitungan Efisiensi

Volume galian untuk strip footing villa tipikal:

\[ V = L \times W \times D \times (1 + SF) \]

di mana \( c \) = kohesi (kPa), \( \gamma \) = berat volume (kN/m³), \( D_f \) = kedalaman footing (m), \( B \) = lebar (m), dan \( N_c, N_q, N_\gamma \) = faktor daya dukung (bergantung pada sudut gesek \( \phi \)). Untuk \( \phi = 30^\circ \), \( N_c = 37,2 \), \( N_q = 22,5 \), \( N_\gamma = 19,7 \).

Daya dukung boleh tipikal tanah vulkanik Bali: 150–300 kPa pasca-stabilisasi.
