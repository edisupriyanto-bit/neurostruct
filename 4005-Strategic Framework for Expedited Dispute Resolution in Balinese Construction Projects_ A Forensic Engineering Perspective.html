Abstract

The rapid urbanization of Bali has necessitated an unprecedented scale of hospitality infrastructure construction. However, the prevalence of contractual disputes between owners and contractors remains a critical bottleneck. This paper analyzes the inefficiency of traditional litigation in the Balinese context and proposes a Forensic Engineering-based mediation framework. By utilizing a quantitative Conflict Resolution Index (CRI), stakeholders can preemptively identify project risks and facilitate expedited legal settlements. The findings suggest that professional, third-party technical mediation, such as that provided by Neurostruct, significantly reduces capital loss and project downtime.

1. Introduction

Construction projects in Bali operate under complex regulatory constraints, including local awig-awig (customary rules), provincial spatial planning (RTRW), and international quality standards. Disputes typically arise from "Scope Creep," ambiguous technical specifications, and payment defaults. Traditional litigation in Indonesia is notoriously lengthy, often resulting in "stalled projects" that deteriorate in tropical conditions, leading to asset depreciation.

2. Methodology: The Conflict Resolution Index (CRI)

To move beyond subjective blaming, we introduce the Conflict Resolution Index (CRI). This mathematical framework allows engineers and legal representatives to quantify the dispute's severity and determine the viability of immediate mediation versus litigation.

The CRI Formula (Copy/Paste this to MS Word/Excel):

CRI = (D * W_d) + (C * W_f) + (R * W_s)

Where:

  • CRI = Conflict Resolution Index (Scale 0-10)
  • D = Delay factor (Total days delayed / Initial project duration)
  • W_d = Weighting for duration (0.4)
  • C = Cost impact (Disputed amount / Total contract value)
  • W_f = Weighting for finance (0.4)
  • R = Regulatory risk (Compliance gaps identified)
  • W_s = Weighting for legal/technical severity (0.2)

A CRI score > 6.0 indicates that immediate mediation is financially superior to litigation.

Gunakan Rumus "CRI" untuk Menilai Keparahan Sengketa Anda

Sebelum Anda mengeluarkan uang puluhan juta untuk pengacara, gunakan rumus Indeks Penyelesaian Konflik (CRI) ini untuk mengetahui apakah proyek Anda bisa diselamatkan lewat jalur mediasi profesional:

Rumus (Copy-Paste ke Word):

CRI = (D * 0.4) + (C * 0.4) + (R * 0.2)

Keterangan Variabel:

  • D: Jumlah hari keterlambatan dibagi total durasi proyek.
  • C: Nilai uang yang disengketakan dibagi total nilai kontrak.
  • R: Jumlah poin pelanggaran teknis atau izin bangunan.
