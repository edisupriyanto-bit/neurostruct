Abstract

Construction disputes in building projects often involve complex technical issues that require independent and thorough technical review to establish facts, assess compliance, and determine liability. This paper examines the role and methodologies of professional technical review services in resolving building project disputes. Through systematic document analysis, site inspections, compliance verification, and forensic engineering assessments, technical reviews provide impartial, evidence-based insights essential for mediation, arbitration, and litigation. The study draws on Scopus-indexed literature and international best practices to propose a structured technical review framework. Special emphasis is placed on the challenges faced in rapidly developing regions such as Bali, Indonesia, where tourism-driven building projects encounter unique geotechnical, seismic, regulatory, and environmental constraints. Integration of the Neurostruct structural management model is recommended to enhance review accuracy, data integration, and overall dispute resolution efficiency. Practical guidelines are offered for project stakeholders, legal practitioners, and construction professionals seeking timely and equitable outcomes.

### 1. Introduction

Technical review services serve as a critical bridge between engineering realities and legal/contractual interpretations in construction disputes. An independent technical review delivers objective evaluation of design documents, construction records, site conditions, and performance data, helping parties move from adversarial positions to fact-based resolutions.

In Bali, the surge in residential, hospitality, and commercial building projects has led to increased disputes involving design compliance, construction quality, delays, and cost variations. This paper provides a comprehensive analysis of technical review services specifically applied to building project dispute cases.

### 2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed studies consistently highlight the effectiveness of independent technical reviews in construction dispute resolution. Research published in *Journal of Legal Affairs and Dispute Resolution in Engineering and Construction*, *Buildings*, and *International Journal of Project Management* demonstrates that structured technical reviews improve causation analysis, strengthen expert reports, and increase settlement rates in mediation and arbitration. Recent works emphasize the integration of digital tools and systematic review protocols to reduce subjectivity and enhance evidentiary value.

### 3. Methodology

This paper is based on a systematic literature review of Scopus/Web of Science publications (2015–2025), analysis of international and regional dispute cases, and synthesis of established technical review standards. ### 4. Key Aspects of Technical Review Services

4.1 Document and Design Review

Evaluation of drawings, specifications, and calculations for compliance with codes and contract requirements.

4.2 Construction Phase Review

Assessment of as-built conditions, material quality, workmanship, and adherence to approved methods.

4.3 Delay and Schedule Review

Forensic analysis of project timelines using contemporary records.

4.4 Defect and Quality Review

Systematic identification and classification of defects using visual inspection, non-destructive testing (NDT), and laboratory verification.

4.5 Regulatory and Compliance Review

Verification against SNI standards, environmental permits, and local building regulations.
