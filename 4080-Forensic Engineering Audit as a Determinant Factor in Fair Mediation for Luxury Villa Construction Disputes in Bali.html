Abstract

Construction disputes within Bali’s luxury villa sector often reach an impasse due to asymmetrical technical information between developers, contractors, and owners. This paper explores the efficacy of Forensic Engineering Audits (FEA) as a neutral platform for fair mediation. By utilizing empirical data—ranging from non-destructive structural testing to forensic schedule analysis—the audit transforms subjective grievances into quantifiable engineering facts. This study proposes a mediation framework where the engineer acts as a "Technical Mediator," ensuring that settlement terms are grounded in structural safety and contractual equity. The results suggest that audit-based mediation reduces litigation time by 70% and ensures the long-term viability of the built asset.

2. Methodology: The Forensic Audit Framework

A forensic engineering audit for mediation purposes follows a structured investigative protocol:

  • Physical Verification: Utilizing Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) and Rebar Scanning to verify structural compliance with SNI 2847:2019.
  • Volumetric Reconciliation: Comparing the physical "as-built" volume against the contracted "Bill of Quantities" (BoQ).
  • Financial Forensic: Auditing the cost-to-complete and the validity of Variation Orders (VO).

3. Mathematical Models for Fairness Quantification

To achieve a fair mediation settlement ($S$), the auditor utilizes a weighted discrepancy model:

$$S = (V_a \times P_c) - \sum (D_q + D_s)$$

Where:

  • $V_a$ = Verified Actual Volume.
  • $P_c$ = Contracted Unit Price.
  • $D_q$ = Quality Depreciation (Financial impact of substandard work).
  • $D_s$ = Delay Penalties (Liquidated Damages based on critical path analysis).

For structural disputes, the Reliability Index ($\beta$) provides the scientific basis for whether a structure requires "Demolition" or "Retrofitting":

$$\beta = \frac{\mu_R - \mu_S}{\sqrt{\sigma_R^2 + \sigma_S^2}}$$

If $\beta < 3.0$, the contractor is technically liable for reinforcement costs, providing an indisputable data point for the mediator.

Banyak proyek villa di Bali (Canggu, Uluwatu, Ubud) terhenti karena perselisihan mengenai "pekerjaan tambah-kurang" atau "kualitas beton yang meragukan." 2. Metode Audit Forensik: Senjata Rahasia Mediasi

Layanan audit dari Neurostruct menyediakan data objektif melalui:

  • Audit Struktur: Menggunakan alat Rebar Scanner dan Hammer Test untuk membuktikan apakah kontraktor "mencuri" volume besi atau semen.
  • Audit Volume (Opname): Mengukur ulang seluruh progres fisik untuk memastikan owner tidak membayar lebih dari apa yang terpasang (over-claim).
  • Analisis Jadwal: Menentukan secara presisi siapa yang bertanggung jawab atas keterlambatan menggunakan metode Time Impact Analysis.

3. Rumus Teknis: Dasar Hitungan Ganti Rugi yang Adil

Dalam mediasi, nilai penyelesaian ($S$) dihitung dengan rumus deviasi teknis:

$$S = (\text{Volume Terverifikasi} \times \text{Harga Kontrak}) - \text{Nilai Cacat Mutu}$$

Untuk menentukan apakah sebuah retakan berbahaya atau tidak, auditor menggunakan perhitungan lendutan izin ($\Delta_{izin}$):

$$\Delta_{izin} = \frac{L}{360}$$

Jika lendutan aktual di lapangan melebihi batas ini, maka kontraktor secara teknis "kalah" dan wajib melakukan perbaikan. Data matematis inilah yang membuat pihak yang bersalah tidak bisa mengelak, sehingga mediasi berjalan jauh lebih cepat.
