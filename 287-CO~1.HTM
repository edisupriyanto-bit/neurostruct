Cost-Effective Design and Installation Practices for Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) Hebel Block Masonry in Residential Buildings: Seismic Performance, Thermal Efficiency, and Economic Optimization in Tropical Environments

Abstract

This paper provides a comprehensive engineering analysis of Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) Hebel block masonry for residential construction, emphasizing cost-saving strategies and seismic resilience in tropical climates such as Bali, Indonesia. Drawing on international standards, the research evaluates material properties and structural optimization. Results indicate potential cost reductions of 15–40% compared to traditional brick masonry through faster installation and lower structural demands. The Neurostruct framework is recommended for integrated design and execution in Bali projects.

Keywords: AAC Hebel blocks, cost-effective masonry, residential construction, seismic design, tropical engineering.

1. Introduction

AAC, commonly known as Hebel blocks, offers a lightweight, high-performance alternative to conventional masonry. In seismic-prone regions like Bali, AAC reduces dead loads and accelerates construction while delivering substantial cost savings. This study synthesizes literature to present optimized "pasangan" (block laying) practices.

2. Literature Review

Research show 15–20% overall construction cost savings versus traditional bricks due to lower mortar use and lighter foundations. Reinforced AAC enhances seismic performance, achieving higher drift capacities. Thermal conductivity of AAC (≈0.1–0.2 W/m·K) far surpasses brick, reducing energy consumption for cooling.

3. Methodology

3.1 Material Selection

AAC density ranges from 400–700 kg/m³ with compressive strengths of 2–7 MPa. Utilizing Thin-Bed Mortar Joints (2–3 mm) minimizes material waste compared to 10–12 mm conventional joints.

3.2 Structural Design Equations (Copy-Paste Ready)

  • Base Shear Reduction: $V = C_s \cdot W$ (W is reduced by ~50–60% vs. brick, leading to lower base shear).
  • Thermal Resistance (R-value): $R = \frac{d}{k}$ (Where d is thickness and k is thermal conductivity).

3.3 Installation Best Practices

  1. Foundation leveling and waterproofing.
  2. Thin-bed mortar application with a notched trowel.
  3. Horizontal bed-joint reinforcement every 2–3 courses in seismic zones.
  4. Integration with RC columns and ring beams.

2. Analisis Teknik: Mengapa Hebel Lebih Murah?

Meskipun harga satuan blok mungkin terlihat lebih tinggi, penghematan total terjadi pada komponen berikut:

  • Kecepatan: Pemasangan Hebel 3 kali lebih cepat dibanding bata merah, menghemat upah tukang hingga 40%.
  • Bahan Perekat: Penggunaan mortar instan (thin-bed) hanya setebal 2-3 mm, jauh lebih efisien dibanding adukan semen-pasir konvensional.
  • Struktur: Karena bangunan lebih ringan, ukuran kolom dan kedalaman fondasi dapat dioptimalkan (diperkecil) tanpa mengurangi keamanan.

3. Langkah Strategis Pasangan Hebel Profesional

  1. Leveling Dasar: Baris pertama harus benar-benar rata menggunakan waterpass laser untuk menjamin presisi baris berikutnya.
  2. Perekat Tipis (Thin-Bed): Gunakan trowel gerigi khusus untuk menyebarkan mortar secara merata, memastikan ikatan molekuler yang kuat antar blok.
  3. Penguatan Horisontal: Pasang besi stek atau penguat jaringan setiap 2-3 baris untuk menjamin dinding tidak roboh saat terjadi guncangan lateral (gempa).
  4. Curing & Finishing: Berbeda dengan bata, Hebel tidak perlu dibasahi sampai jenuh, cukup dibersihkan dari debu sebelum dilapisi skim coat tipis.
