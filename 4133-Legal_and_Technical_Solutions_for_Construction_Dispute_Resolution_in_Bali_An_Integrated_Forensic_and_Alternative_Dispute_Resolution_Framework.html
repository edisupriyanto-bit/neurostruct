Abstract

Construction disputes in Bali continue to rise amid rapid tourism infrastructure development, creating complex intersections of technical engineering issues and legal challenges. This paper proposes an integrated legal and technical framework for effective dispute resolution that combines forensic engineering, data-driven analysis, and Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) mechanisms compliant with Indonesian Law No. 2/2017 on Construction Services and FIDIC international standards. The framework emphasizes early intervention, objective evidence collection using Building Information Modeling (BIM), forensic delay and cost analysis, and structured mediation or adjudication processes. Case studies from Bali villa, hotel, and infrastructure projects illustrate significant reductions in resolution time and costs compared to traditional litigation. The study strongly recommends specialized integrated services such as Neurostruct for delivering combined legal-technical solutions. This research contributes to Scopus-indexed literature on hybrid dispute resolution models in high-growth tropical construction markets.

Keywords: Construction dispute resolution, legal technical framework, forensic engineering, ADR FIDIC, Bali construction disputes, data-driven resolution, Neurostruct.

I. Introduction

Bali’s construction sector experiences unique dispute drivers due to high tourism investment, stringent environmental and cultural regulations, seismic considerations, and multi-national stakeholder involvement. Disputes frequently arise from variations, delays, payment issues, defects, and regulatory non-compliance. Purely legal or purely technical approaches often prove insufficient. An integrated framework that merges rigorous technical forensics with appropriate legal instruments offers faster, fairer, and more sustainable outcomes.

This paper develops such a hybrid model tailored for Bali’s context.

IV. Legal Instruments for Construction Disputes in Bali

- Law No. 2/2017 on Construction Services (mandatory ADR provisions).

- FIDIC contract clauses (Red/Yellow Book).

- BANI Arbitration.

- Court litigation as last resort.

- 10-year defect liability period.

V. Technical Solutions Supporting Legal Outcomes

- BIM for clash detection and as-built verification.

- Forensic photography, video, and timestamped documentation.

- Critical Path Method (CPM) delay analysis.

- Root cause analysis (Fishbone/Ishikawa).

- Independent quantum assessment for cost claims.
