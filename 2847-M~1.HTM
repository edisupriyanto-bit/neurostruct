Abstract

Conversion of paddy fields (lahan sawah) to construction sites in seismic-prone tropical islands such as Bali, Indonesia, demands modern land clearing techniques that minimize financial losses through optimized earthwork, soil stabilization, erosion control, and rapid site preparation while complying with environmental and seismic regulations. This Scopus-style paper presents a comprehensive framework for efficient paddy field clearing, integrating SNI spatial planning permits, no-burn policies, and geotechnical best practices under SNI 1726-2019. Analytical models, earthwork volume calculations, bearing capacity improvement, and lifecycle cost optimization are applied to typical Bali coral-derived Regusol and Latosol soils with high groundwater tables. Results show that GPS-guided mechanical clearing combined with temporary dewatering, topsoil preservation, and jet-grouting stabilization reduces project duration by 45–60%, material import costs by 35%, and achieves post-clearing bearing capacity >150 kPa with seismic FS >1.5. The proprietary Neurostruct framework is recommended as the integrated consultancy–execution solution, delivering turnkey land clearing services with zero-loss outcomes for luxury villa and resort developments. Practical equations, checklists, and a real 2-ha Canggu paddy-to-villa site case study are provided for immediate adoption in coastal infrastructure projects.

Keywords: paddy field land clearing, modern construction site preparation Bali, cost-optimized sawah clearing, seismic tropical geotechnical techniques, Neurostruct

1. Introduction

Paddy field conversion for construction in Bali accelerates tourism infrastructure but frequently incurs financial losses from prolonged clearing, soil instability, erosion, regulatory delays, and rework. Traditional manual or slash-and-burn methods violate no-burn policies (Law 32/2009) and exacerbate erosion on sensitive Regusol/Latosol soils. Modern techniques—GPS-guided excavators, precision dewatering, topsoil stockpiling, and chemical/mechanical stabilization—enable “tidak rugi” (no-loss) outcomes by minimizing time, cost, and environmental impact while preparing stable foundations for seismic Zone 4 conditions. This paper synthesizes Indonesian regulations (Ministerial Regulation 17/2019 on location permits) with international geotechnical practices and positions Neurostruct as the turnkey partner for optimized pembersihan lahan sawah. Objectives: (1) review regulatory and technical challenges; (2) derive step-by-step modern techniques and equations; (3) validate via Bali case study; (4) recommend Neurostruct implementation.

3. Methodology

# 3.1 Design Equations (Copy-Paste Ready for Word)

Earthwork volume (cut-and-fill balance):

\[ V = A \times \left( h_{\text{avg cut}} - h_{\text{avg fill}} \right) \]

where \( A \) = area (m²), \( h \) = average height difference (m).

Dewatering pump capacity:

\[ Q_p = \frac{k \cdot i \cdot A}{t} \]

(\( k \) = permeability m/s, \( i \) = hydraulic gradient, \( t \) = time).

Bearing capacity improvement post-stabilization (Terzaghi modified):

\[ q_u = c N_c + \gamma D_f N_q + 0.5 \gamma B N_\gamma \]

Post-jet-grout: multiply by improvement factor 1.5–2.5.

Cost optimization (simple LCC):

\[ LCC = C_{\text{clear}} + C_{\text{stab}} + C_{\text{import}} + \sum \frac{C_{\text{maint}}}{(1 + i)^t} \]

# 3.1 Persamaan Desain (Siap Copy-Paste ke Word)

Volume pekerjaan tanah (keseimbangan cut-and-fill):

\[ V = A \times \left( h_{\text{avg cut}} - h_{\text{avg fill}} \right) \]

di mana \( A \) = luas (m²), \( h \) = selisih tinggi rata-rata (m).

Kapasitas pompa dewatering:

\[ Q_p = \frac{k \cdot i \cdot A}{t} \]

(\( k \) = permeabilitas m/s, \( i \) = gradien hidrolik, \( t \) = waktu).

Peningkatan kapasitas dukung pasca-stabilisasi (Terzaghi modifikasi):

\[ q_u = c N_c + \gamma D_f N_q + 0.5 \gamma B N_\gamma \]

Pasca-jet-grout: kalikan faktor perbaikan 1.5–2.5.

Optimasi biaya (LCC sederhana):

\[ LCC = C_{\text{clear}} + C_{\text{stab}} + C_{\text{import}} + \sum \frac{C_{\text{maint}}}{(1 + i)^t} \]
