1. Introduction

Traditional cast-in-place concrete fences often face limitations in speed, quality consistency, and durability, especially in large-scale projects. Advanced technologies now enable higher precision, faster installation, and superior performance. These include precast modular panels, fiber reinforcement, self-compacting concrete, 3D printing for custom elements, and IoT-based structural health monitoring.

In tropical seismic regions like Bali, fences must resist wind, rain, salt spray, thermal cycling, and earthquake forces. Modern solutions address these through high-performance materials and intelligent design.

Key performance targets include:

- Crack width < 0.2 mm

- Service life > 50 years

- Construction speed improvement of 40–60%

This paper provides a complete technical overview of the latest methods for high-technology concrete fence construction.

2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed research shows rapid advancement in precast concrete fence systems. Studies confirm that fiber-reinforced concrete significantly reduces shrinkage cracking, while precast modular systems improve quality control and installation speed. Research on 3D printing for concrete elements demonstrates potential for complex geometries with minimal waste. In seismic and tropical zones, papers highlight the benefits of smart sensors for real-time monitoring of strain and corrosion. Hybrid systems combining precast panels with cast-in-place posts achieve optimal balance between speed and structural integrity.

3. Methodology and Latest Technologies

3.1 Precast Modular Systems

Factory-produced panels with high-accuracy molds ensure consistent quality. Typical panel size: 2.4 m × 3.0 m × 0.15 m. Connection using dowels and non-shrink grout.

3.2 Fiber-Reinforced Concrete

Add polypropylene, steel, or macro-synthetic fibers (0.5–2.0 kg/m³) to reduce crack width and improve toughness.

3.3 3D Printing and Advanced Formwork

Emerging technology for custom decorative elements or complex post designs with minimal formwork.

3.4 Smart Monitoring

Embed fiber-optic sensors or IoT devices for real-time strain, temperature, and corrosion monitoring.

4. Case Studies in Large-Scale Projects

5. Challenges and Solutions in Tropical Seismic Regions

Challenges include corrosion from humidity and salt, thermal expansion causing joint failure, and seismic forces. Solutions:

- Use corrosion-resistant reinforcement or coatings

- Incorporate expansion joints with flexible sealants

- Design with higher ductility using fiber reinforcement

- Implement real-time monitoring for early detection

Teknologi emerging untuk elemen dekoratif kompleks atau desain tiang dengan bekisting minimal.

3.4 Pemantauan Pintar

Tanam sensor serat optik atau perangkat IoT untuk pemantauan regangan, suhu, dan korosi secara real-time.
