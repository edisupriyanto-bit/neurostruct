1. Introduction

Precision in concrete fence construction goes beyond basic functionality to deliver straight, plumb, and aesthetically pleasing walls with minimal defects. Achieving high accuracy requires meticulous control at every stage — from formwork fabrication to final finishing.

Critical tolerance requirements include:

- Verticality: ±5 mm over 3 m height

- Flatness: ±3 mm under 2 m straight edge

- Alignment: ±10 mm over 10 m length

In seismic tropical zones like Bali, fences must also resist lateral forces while accommodating thermal and shrinkage movements. This paper provides a complete technical framework for precision concrete fence construction.

2. Literature Review

In tropical climates, papers highlight the benefits of low-shrinkage mixes and extended moist curing to combat rapid surface drying.

3. Methodology and Precision Construction Techniques

3.1 Formwork Design and Fabrication

Use high-quality plywood or steel formwork with stiffeners to minimize deflection. Ensure form faces are perfectly aligned and coated with release agent.

3.2 Reinforcement Placement

- Minimum reinforcement ratio: 0.25% in each direction

- Clear cover: 25–40 mm

- Precise spacing using spacers and chairs

3.3 Concrete Mix and Placement

Design mix with low w/c ratio (0.40–0.45), superplasticizer, and shrinkage-reducing admixtures. Place concrete in layers with proper vibration to eliminate voids.

3.4 Finishing and Curing

Apply multi-stage troweling for smooth finish. Cure with wet hessian or curing compound for minimum 7–14 days.

3.5 Quality Assurance

Perform dimensional checks at every stage. Use laser levels for alignment and straight-edge for flatness verification.

4. Case Studies in Bali Projects

In Bali’s luxury villa compounds and residential estates, precision concrete fences constructed with rigid formwork and controlled mixes achieved near-perfect straightness and zero visible cracks after several years, even under seismic activity and heavy monsoon conditions.

5. Challenges and Solutions in Tropical Seismic Regions

Challenges include thermal cracking, shrinkage due to high evaporation, and movement during earthquakes. Solutions: shrinkage-compensating concrete, control joints every 4–5 m, flexible connections at corners, and corrosion-resistant reinforcement in coastal areas.

3. Metodologi dan Teknik Konstruksi Presisi

3.1 Desain dan Fabrikasi Bekisting

Gunakan plywood berkualitas tinggi atau bekisting baja dengan stiffener untuk meminimalkan defleksi. Pastikan permukaan bekisting selaras sempurna dan dilapisi release agent.

3.2 Penempatan Tulangan

- Rasio tulangan minimum: 0,25% di setiap arah

- Clear cover: 25–40 mm

- Jarak presisi menggunakan spacer dan chair

3.3 Campuran Beton dan Penempatan

Desain campuran dengan rasio w/c rendah (0,40–0,45), superplasticizer, dan admixture pengurang susut. Tuang beton secara berlapis dengan vibrasi yang tepat untuk menghilangkan rongga.

3.4 Finishing dan Curing

Terapkan troweling multi-tahap untuk hasil halus. Cure dengan hessian basah atau curing compound selama minimal 7–14 hari.
