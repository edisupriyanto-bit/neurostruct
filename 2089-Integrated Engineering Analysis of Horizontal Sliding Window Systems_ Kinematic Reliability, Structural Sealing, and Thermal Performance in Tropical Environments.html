Abstract

Horizontal sliding windows are a ubiquitous architectural element in modern tropical infrastructure, valued for their space-saving kinematics and expansive ventilation potential. However, improper installation often leads to air leakage, water ingress, and mechanical seizure of the sliding tracks. This paper evaluates the technical requirements for high-precision horizontal sliding window installation, focusing on frame leveling, weather-stripping integrity, and structural anchoring. Utilizing the "Geometric Tolerance" ($GT$) index and structural sealant compatibility tests, the research investigates the impact of frame deflection on rollers' longevity. Results demonstrate that a deviation of less than 0.1% in the horizontal track alignment significantly reduces the coefficient of friction ($\mu$), ensuring sustained kinematic reliability in high-salinity coastal regions like Bali, Indonesia.

2. Theoretical Framework: Kinematics and Load Distribution

The operational efficiency of a sliding window is governed by the interaction between the sash weight ($W_s$) and the track resistance. The required opening force ($F_o$) is modeled as:

$$F_o = \mu \cdot W_s + F_{seal}$$

Where:

  • $\mu$ = Coefficient of rolling friction.
  • $W_s$ = Weight of the glass and frame assembly ($N$).
  • $F_{seal}$ = Resistance force from weather-stripping and gaskets.

To prevent structural distortion under wind load ($P_w$), the anchor spacing ($s$) must satisfy:

$$s \leq \sqrt{\frac{8 \cdot E \cdot I \cdot \Delta_{max}}{P_w \cdot w}}$$

Where $E \cdot I$ is the flexural rigidity of the frame and $\Delta_{max}$ is the allowable deflection ($L/175$ per ASTM E330).

3. Methodology: Precision Alignment and Sealing

  1. Sub-frame Calibration: Utilization of 3D laser leveling to ensure the rough opening (aperture) is perfectly square ($90^\circ \pm 0.05^\circ$).
  2. Roller Adjustment: Balancing the $W_s$ distribution across tandem rollers to prevent localized track wear.
  3. Hydrostatic Sealing: Application of high-modulus silicone sealants following the "Three-Point Adhesion" prevention protocol using backer rods.

2. Analisis Teknik: Menghitung Kelurusan Rel

Kunci utama jendela sliding adalah gaya gesek yang minimal. Jika kusen bawah melengkung akibat beban gravitasi atau tekanan baut yang terlalu kencang, roda akan terhambat.

Rumus Lendutan Kusen Bawah

Kusen bawah harus mampu menahan beban kaca tanpa mengalami lendutan ($\delta$) yang melebihi batas toleransi rel:

$$\delta = \frac{5 \cdot w \cdot L^4}{384 \cdot E \cdot I}$$

Dimana $w$ adalah beban merata dari panel kaca. Jika $\delta$ terlalu besar, panel jendela akan "terkunci" di tengah rel. Insinyur profesional menggunakan shims (ganjal) plastik khusus pada titik-titik tumpu beban untuk menjaga kerataan rel di angka toleransi $\pm 1 \, mm$.

3. Langkah-Langkah Pemasangan Standar Profesional

  1. Verifikasi Opening: Pastikan lubang dinding benar-benar siku. Gunakan rumus Pythagoras ($3:4:5$) atau laser cross-line untuk memverifikasi kesikuan sudut.
  2. Pemasangan Sealant Perimeter: Gunakan back-up rod sebelum menuangkan sealant. Hal ini memastikan sealant bekerja secara elastis (dua titik ikat) untuk meredam getaran angin dan mencegah retak.
  3. Pengaturan Roda (Leveling): Setelah panel terpasang, putar sekrup penyetel pada roda untuk memastikan panel tegak lurus dengan kusen samping. Hal ini penting agar kunci (lock) dapat mengunci dengan sempurna tanpa dipaksa.
  4. Sistem Drainase (Weep Holes): Pastikan lubang pembuangan air pada rel bawah tidak tersumbat oleh kotoran semen atau sealant, agar air hujan tidak meluap masuk ke dalam ruangan.
