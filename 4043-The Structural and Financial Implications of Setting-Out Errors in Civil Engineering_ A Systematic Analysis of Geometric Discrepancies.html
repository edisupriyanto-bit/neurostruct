Abstract

The precision of structural setting-out serves as the primary safeguard for the geometric and structural integrity of civil infrastructure. This paper investigates the technical and financial consequences of setting-out errors during the pre-construction phase. Through the analysis of coordinate transformation failures, eccentric loading, and boundary encroachment, this study demonstrates how minor discrepancies in "staking out" can lead to catastrophic structural collapse and irreversible fiscal deficits. By utilizing advanced Total Stations and GNSS technology, the research emphasizes that geodetic verification is a non-negotiable requirement for project success.

1. Introduction

In the lifecycle of civil engineering, setting-out is the definitive moment where digital design meets physical terrain. It involves transferring the $(X, Y, Z)$ coordinates from architectural blueprints to the construction site. In geographically challenging regions like Bali, characterized by complex land boundaries and high seismic activity, even a millimeter-level error in setting-out can compromise the entire structural safety factor. This paper explores the "fatal" errors that often lead to total project loss.

2. Technical Methodology: The Physics of Setting-Out Discrepancies

Setting-out errors typically stem from the improper establishment of Benchmarks (BM) or the failure to account for coordinate rotation. The accuracy of a structural point is verified using the standard deviation:

$$\sigma = \pm \sqrt{\frac{\sum (v^2)}{n-1}}$$

Where $\sigma$ is the standard error and $v$ is the residual of measurements. If the setting-out process ignores this geodetic rigor, the resulting "eccentricity" in column placement creates unintended moments in the foundation.

3. Structural and Legal Consequences of Errors

  • Eccentric Loading: When columns are set out incorrectly, the load does not pass through the center of the pile, causing structural instability.
  • Legal Encroachment: Errors in setting out property lines often result in buildings crossing into neighboring land, leading to court-ordered demolition.
  • Hydrological Failure: Incorrect vertical setting-out (elevation) causes drainage systems to fail, leading to permanent flooding and foundation erosion.

2. Analisis Teknis: Efek Beban Eksentrik Akibat Salah Posisi

Dalam teknik sipil, posisi kolom harus tepat berada di atas titik pusat pondasi agar beban terdistribusi secara merata. Kesalahan setting-out menciptakan eksentrisitas ($e$). Momen tambahan ($M$) yang timbul dapat dihitung dengan rumus:

$$M = P \cdot e$$

Dimana $P$ adalah beban aksial dan $e$ adalah jarak kesalahan setting-out. Jika nilai $M$ melebihi kapasitas desain, struktur akan mengalami keretakan fatal atau keruntuhan total saat terjadi beban dinamis seperti gempa bumi di wilayah Bali.
