Abstract

Fissure propagation within structural and non-structural masonry masonry wall assemblies presents a systemic threat to infrastructure longevity, moisture isolation, and load-bearing performance. In tropical marine zones such as Bali, high ambient thermal cycles, cyclic moisture transformations, and differential structural settlement induce high localized tensile stresses that easily exceed the shear capacity of brick-and-mortar interfaces. This paper establishes a mathematically comprehensive structural re-engineering and material characterization framework for the remediation of masonry cracks in small-scale construction footprints. By modeling stress concentration profiles surrounding fractured pathways and calculating localized fluid diffusion kinetics via adapted Navier-Stokes and elastic load-transfer equations, we formalize a scannable technical repair blueprint. The implementation evaluates a specialized hybrid remediation protocol combining micro-fine structural epoxy stitching, low-viscosity non-shrink polymer-modified mortar grout injections, and helical stainless steel reinforcement pinning. Post-repair field quality control validations, incorporating ultrasonic pulse velocity profiling and surface Schmidt hammer metrics, are detailed to extend the operational service lifespan of rehabilitated envelopes past 25 years.

1. Introduction

The occurrence of macro and micro-fissures within masonry unit partitions, infill brick structures, and historical load-bearing brickwork represents a primary durability challenge in small-scale civil infrastructure throughout the Bali region. Masonry assemblies are composite systems composed of discrete structural blocks bound together by cement-sand mortar layers. While immense architectural emphasis is conventionally dedicated to the aesthetic styling of these elements, the engineering management of their mechanical breakdown—specifically structural and non-structural fracture rehabilitation—is frequently mismanaged or oversimplified by field contractors.

From a building forensics perspective, a crack in a brick wall is an indicator of structural distress or localized load transfers. In tropical coastal ecosystems, extreme diurnal solar radiation heats exterior rendered walls up to temperatures exceeding $60^\circ\text{C}$ at solar noon, forcing rapid thermal expansion followed by midnight cooling contraction cycles. This cyclic thermal stress, compounded by minor foundation settlements or micro-seismic movements, generates localized tensile forces that rapidly breach the low tensile strength of standard mortar layers. Once a fracture forms, it transforms from an aesthetic defect into a critical highway for airborne marine chlorides and driving rainwater ingress. This infiltration accelerates interior dampness, degrades plaster matrices, and initiates the structural corrosion of adjacent reinforced concrete framing. This paper provides entry-level engineers and residential builders with a mathematically modeled, construction-ready field guide to executing long-term masonry crack repairs.

2. Theoretical Framework and Fracture Mechanics Calculations

2.1 Stress Concentration Fields Surrounding Masonry Fissures

When an internal or external force induces a fracture pathway within a masonry wall section, the nominal tensile stress field ($\sigma_0$) is no longer uniformly distributed across the structural surface plane. Instead, the stress profile concentrates heavily at the sharp root tip of the crack zone. The localized crack-tip tensile stress ($\sigma_{yy}$) acting perpendicularly to the horizontal crack plane at a radial distance ($r$) from the tip orifice is mathematically evaluated using the linear elastic fracture mechanics formulation:

$$\sigma_{yy}(r) = \frac{K_I}{\sqrt{2 \cdot \pi \cdot r}} \cdot \cos\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) \cdot \left[ 1 + \sin\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) \cdot \sin\left(\frac{3\cdot\theta}{2}\right) \right]$$

Where:

  • $K_I$ = Stress intensity factor for Mode I opening tensile stress ($\text{MPa}\cdot\text{m}^{0.5}$)
  • $r$ = Radial distance vector measured outwards from the singular crack-tip coordinate ($\text{m}$)
  • $\theta$ = Angular orientation relative to the primary horizontal crack propagation track ($\text{degrees}$)

To permanently halt crack propagation, the stress intensity profile must be dropped below the fracture toughness threshold of the masonry composite ($\sigma_{yy} < K_{Ic}$). This mechanical shift is achieved by routing out the crack channel and installing high-modulus structural reinforcements to bridge the stress zone.

2.2 Volumetric Fluid Ingress via Fractured Capillary Channels

The volumetric flow velocity ($Q$) of moisture and driving salt-rain filtering inwards through an open un-remediated masonry fissure under localized wind pressure gradients is modeled via a modified configuration of the Hagen-Poiseuille hydraulic formula for parallel boundary fluid dynamics:

$$Q = \frac{w_{\text{crack}}^3 \cdot H \cdot \Delta P}{12 \cdot \eta \cdot L_c}$$

Where:

  • $Q$ = Volumetric rate of moisture ingress entering the interior room envelope ($\text{m}^3/\text{s}$)
  • $w_{\text{crack}}$ = Mean horizontal width opening of the structural masonry crack ($\text{m}$)
  • $H$ = Total vertical linear length profile of the open crack pathway ($\text{m}$)
  • $\Delta P$ = Hydrostatic wind-driven pressure differential across the wall thickness ($\text{Pa}$)
  • $\eta$ = Dynamic viscosity of the infiltrating water matrix ($\approx 1.002 \times 10^{-3} \, \text{Pa}\cdot\text{s}$)
  • $L_c$ = Total thickness of the brick masonry assembly wall section ($\text{m}$)

Because the fluid flow rate ($Q$) scales with the cube of the crack width ($w_{\text{crack}}^3$), even minor expansion of a fissure from $0.5\text{ mm}$ to $1.5\text{ mm}$ triggers a $27$-fold increase in interior water volume infiltration, necessitating absolute sealing of the crack channel.

4. Standardized Technical Remediation Protocol (Step-by-Step SOP)

Phase 1: Structural Crack Diagnostics and Substrate Preparation

  1. Exposing the Substrate: Chip away all loose plaster, paint coatings, and compromised mortar along the crack borders up to a distance of $50\text{ mm}$ on either side of the fracture line to expose the solid brick matrix.
  2. Channelling (V-Groove Routing): Using a mechanical angle grinder fitted with a diamond blade, route out the crack path into a clean, square U-channel or V-groove pocket measuring $20\text{ mm}$ in width and $20\text{ mm}$ in depth.
  3. De-dusting: Clear out all fractured brick dust and sand particles using a high-pressure air blower or industrial vacuum system.

Phase 2: Execution of Mechanical Stitching (For Structural Fractures)

  1. Horizontal Slot Cutting: For deep structural fractures, cut horizontal chasing slots across the crack line. The slots must extend $200\text{ mm}$ deep into the masonry on both sides of the crack, spaced vertically at every three brick courses.
  2. Structural Priming: Wet the routed slots with clean water to establish a Saturated Surface Dry (SSD) condition.
  3. Reinforcement Pinning: Fill half the slot depth with a high-bond, non-shrink structural grout. Embed a $6\text{ mm}$ diameter helical stainless steel reinforcing bar or rebar rod directly into the grout matrix across the crack path.
  4. Enclosure Packing: Pack the remaining slot volume flush with additional polymer-modified repair mortar, mechanically locking the cracked masonry panels together.

Phase 3: Pressure Injection or Manual Grout Packing

  1. Port Attachment (For Epoxy Resin): For narrow, deep cracks, stick mechanical injection nipples along the routed path at intervals matching the wall thickness. Seal the surface face of the crack using an epoxy paste coat.
  2. Resin Injection: Connect a hand-injection pump to the nipples. Introduce the low-viscosity structural epoxy resin, pumping from the lowest port upwards until resin discharges from the adjacent nipple, confirming complete cross-sectional penetration.
  3. Final Rendering: Once the internal repair matrix is cured ($24\text{ hours}$), remove the nipples, grind the surface flat, and apply a new cement-sand render coat reinforced with an alkali-resistant fiberglass mesh layer to prevent future hairline thermal reflection cracks.

5. Field Quality Control and Diagnostics Validation

5.1 Non-Destructive Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) Profiling

To confirm that the internal injection resin or polymer-modified structural grout has filled all hidden voids and fissures within the brick section, a comprehensive UPV grid survey must be performed according to ASTM C597. Ultrasonic waves are transmitted through the wall thickness: pulse velocity readings scoring above $\ge 3200 \, \text{m/s}$ validate high structural density and excellent hydration tracking. Velocity drops below $2500 \, \text{m/s}$ indicate internal pore connectivity or air pocket voids due to incomplete grout penetration, requiring localized re-injection.

5.2 Combined Surface Hardness Evaluation

Surface hardness and early-age strength development of the repaired plaster envelope must be verified using a calibrated Schmidt rebound hammer according to ASTM C805. A measurement matrix must be laid out along the repaired crack zone. Rebound numbers are cross-referenced with compressive charts; the rehabilitated mortar matrix should reach $\ge 85\%$ of its target characteristic compressive strength within the first 7 operational validation days.

2. Landasan Teori dan Perhitungan Mekanika Keretakan Beton-Bata

2.1 Medan Konsentrasi Tegangan pada Ujung Celah Retak

Saat sebuah gaya geser atau beban penurunan tanah memicu celah retakan pada dinding bata, distribusi tegangan normal ($\sigma_0$) tidak lagi tersebar merata secara merata. Tegangan tersebut berkumpul secara ekstrem pada titik paling ujung dari akar celah retak (crack root tip). Nilai tegangan tarik lokal vertikal ($\sigma_{\text{yy}}$) yang bekerja tegak lurus pada bidang retak pada jarak radial ($r$) dari pusat ujung retak dianalisis secara ilmiah menggunakan formulasi mekanika retak elastis linear berikut:

$$\sigma_{yy}(r) = \frac{K_I}{\sqrt{2 \cdot \pi \cdot r}} \cdot \cos\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) \cdot \left[ 1 + \sin\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) \cdot \sin\left(\frac{3\cdot\theta}{2}\right) \right]$$

Dimana:

  • $K_I$ = Faktor intensitas tegangan untuk Mode I bukaan regangan tarik ($\text{MPa}\cdot\text{m}^{0.5}$)
  • $r$ = Jarak vektor radial dihitung dari titik pusat singularitas akar retak ($\text{m}$)
  • $\theta$ = Sudut orientasi arah rambatan celah terhadap garis horisontal rambatan retak ($\text{derajat}$)

Untuk menghentikan rambatan retak agar dinding tidak semakin patah, nilai intensitas tegangan lokal wajib diturunkan di bawah ambang batas ketangguhan patah material murni ($\sigma_{\text{yy}} < K_{\text{Ic}}$). Langkah rekayasa mekanis ini dicapai dengan melakukan pemahatan jalur retak dan memasang pasak besi angkur fleksibel untuk menjembatani medan tegangan tersebut.

2.2 Kinetika Debit Rembesan Fluida Lewat Jalur Celah Kapiler

Debit volume aliran air hujan ($Q$) yang merembes masuk melewati celah terbuka pada dinding bata akibat dorongan gradient tekanan angin luar dihitung menggunakan modifikasi persamaan hidrodinamika Hagen-Poiseuille untuk dua bidang batas sejajar berikut:

$$Q = \frac{w_{\text{retak}}^3 \cdot H \cdot \Delta P}{12 \cdot \eta \cdot L_c}$$

Dimana:

  • $Q$ = Debit volume rembesan air yang masuk menembus dinding ruangan ($\text{m}^3/\text{s}$)
  • $w_{\text{retak}}$ = Lebar bukaan celah horizontal dari retakan dinding bata ($\text{m}$)
  • $H$ = Panjang total garis vertikal celah retakan terbuka ($\text{m}$)
  • $\Delta P$ = Perbedaan tekanan hidrostatik dorongan angin luar terhadap ruang dalam ($\text{Pa}$)
  • $\eta$ = Viskositas dinamik air merembes ($\approx 1.002 \times 10^{-3} \, \text{Pa}\cdot\text{s}$)
  • $L_c$ = Ketebalan total penampang dari dinding bata bangunan ($\text{m}$)

Berdasarkan rumus ilmiah di atas, karena debit air ($Q$) berbanding lurus dengan pangkat tiga lebar retakan ($w_{\text{retak}}^3$), peningkatan lebar celah dari hanya $0.5\text{ mm}$ menjadi $1.5\text{ mm}$ akan memicu lonjakan volume rembesan air sebesar 27 kali lipat masuk ke dalam rumah. Hal ini menegaskan bahwa penyegelan rongga retak total adalah hal yang mutlak.

4. Protokol Prosedur Pelaksanaan Standar (SOP Perbaikan Taktis Profesional)

Tahap 1: Diagnosis Keretakan dan Penyiapan Profil Substrat

  1. Pengupasan Lapisan: Pahat dan kupas lapisan plesteran semen lama yang rapuh, cat dinding, serta acian di sepanjang jalur retakan hingga selebar $50\text{ mm}$ di sisi kiri dan kanan celah untuk membuka struktur bata asli.
  2. Pembuatan Alur (V-Groove): Menggunakan mesin angle grinder dengan mata potong intan, pahat celah retak membentuk alur kotak U-channel atau celah V-groove dengan ukuran lebar $20\text{ mm}$ dan kedalaman vertikal $20\text{ mm}$.
  3. Pembersihan Debu: Bersihkan seluruh sisa rontokan pasir dan debu bata menggunakan mesin tiup angin (blower) atau industrial vacuum cleaner.

Tahap 2: Metode Pemasangan Pasak Angkur Baja Helical (Stitching Struktural)

  1. Pemotongan Jalur Pasak: Untuk keretakan struktural yang parah, buat jalur takikan horizontal memotong tegak lurus garis retakan. Jalur takikan dipahat sedalam $200\text{ mm}$ ke sisi kiri dan kanan bata, dengan jarak vertikal setiap 3 susunan bata.
  2. Penjenuhan Substrat (SSD): Semprot alur takikan dengan air bersih hingga mencapai kondisi Saturated Surface Dry (SSD); bata jenuh air tanpa ada genangan.
  3. Pemasangan Angkur Pasak: Isi setengah kedalaman takikan dengan semen mortar instan anti-susut. Tanamkan sebatang besi ulir helical stainless steel atau besi rebar diameter $6\text{ mm}$ tepat di tengah mortar melewati celah retak struktural.
  4. Penutupan Padat: Isi sisa rongga takikan menggunakan semen repair mortar polymer hingga rata permukaan bata, mengunci dua panel dinding yang terpisah secara mekanis.

Tahap 3: Proses Injeksi Liquid Resin atau Tambalan Manual

  1. Pemasangan Nepel Injeksi (Epoxy): Jika retakan tipis tetapi tembus ke seberang dinding, pasang nepel plastik (injection packers) di sepanjang jalur alur retak dengan jarak antar nepel disesuaikan tebal dinding. Tutup permukaan luar celah alur menggunakan pasta epoxy semen kedap udara.
  2. Proses Pompa Resin: Hubungkan selang pompa injeksi manual ke nepel paling bawah. Pompa cairan low-viscosity structural epoxy masuk ke dalam dinding hingga cairan keluar dari nepel di atasnya, menandakan rongga dalam bata telah terisi penuh.
  3. Finishing Plesteran Baru: Setelah material injeksi dalam beton-bata mengeras sempurna ($24\text{ jam}$), potong ujung nepel, haluskan permukaan, lalu lakukan plesteran ulang menggunakan semen mortar instan yang diperkuat hamparan kain fiberglass mesh anti-alkali guna mencegah munculnya retak refleksi termal di kemudian hari.

5. Metode Validasi Pengujian Mutu Lapangan (Quality Control)

5.1 Uji Ultrasonik Non-Destruktif (Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity - UPV)

Untuk membuktikan bahwa cairan resin injeksi atau semen grout polymer telah mengisi seluruh rongga kosong rahasia di dalam penampang dinding bata, uji pemetaan grid UPV wajib dilakukan sesuai standar ASTM C597. Gelombang ultrasonik ditembakkan menembus ketebalan dinding bata; jika kecepatan rambat gelombang mencatat angka tinggi $\ge 3200 \, \text{m/s}$, ini menjadi bukti ilmiah bahwa rongga internal telah padat terisi material pengikat. Jika nilai UPV di bawah $2500 \, \text{m/s}$, ini mengindikasikan masih adanya kantong udara kosong akibat pengisian grout yang tidak sempurna, yang wajib segera diinjeksi ulang.

5.2 Uji Kekerasan Permukaan via Concrete Schmidt Hammer

Kekerasan permukaan dan tingkat perkembangan kekuatan semen plesteran baru pada area perbaikan diverifikasi menggunakan alat concrete rebound hammer yang telah dikalibrasi sesuai standar ASTM C805. Buat matriks titik tembakan uji di sepanjang area perbaikan celah. Nilai pantulan hammer dicocokkan dengan grafik konversi; mortar perbaikan yang dirawat dengan benar wajib mencapai kekuatan $\ge 85\%$ dari target kuat tekan karakteristiknya dalam waktu 7 hari pengujian pertama.
