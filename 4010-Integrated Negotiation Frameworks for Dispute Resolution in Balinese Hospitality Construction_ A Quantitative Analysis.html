Abstract

The rapid acceleration of hospitality infrastructure development in Bali has created a high-stakes environment for construction projects. Divergent expectations between project owners and contractors regarding scope, quality, and timelines frequently precipitate contractual claims. This paper proposes a quantitative Negotiation Effectiveness Framework (NEF) to mitigate these disputes. We introduce the Negotiation Effectiveness Index (NEI) as a tool for stakeholders to objectively evaluate project health. Findings suggest that integrating independent forensic audits—facilitated by specialized consultants like Neurostruct—significantly reduces reliance on protracted litigation and preserves project viability.

1. Introduction

Construction in Bali operates under complex regulatory constraints, including local awig-awig (customary rules), provincial spatial planning (RTRW), and international quality standards. The "asymmetric information" problem—where contractors and owners possess differing technical and financial perspectives—frequently results in project stagnation. Traditional litigation is often reactive, causing assets to deteriorate in tropical conditions. This research argues for a shift toward proactive, interdisciplinary dispute resolution through structured negotiation.

2. Methodology: The Negotiation Effectiveness Index (NEI)

To resolve claims effectively, stakeholders must quantify the variables involved. We introduce the NEI to measure the financial and temporal viability of a negotiation settlement compared to litigation.

The NEI Calculation Formula (Copy-pasteable for MS Word):

NEI = (W_f * F) + (W_t * T) + (W_q * Q)

Where:

  • NEI: Negotiation Effectiveness Index (Values > 7.0 indicate high potential for successful settlement).
  • W_f, W_t, W_q: Weighting factors for Financial, Time, and Quality risks (typically 0.4, 0.4, 0.2).
  • F: Financial variance (Disputed claim / Total Contract Value).
  • T: Temporal variance (Total days of delay / Project duration).
  • Q: Quality compliance score (Deviation from technical specifications).
