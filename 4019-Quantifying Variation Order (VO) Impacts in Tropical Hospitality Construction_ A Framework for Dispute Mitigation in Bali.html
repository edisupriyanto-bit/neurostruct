Abstract

The rapid acceleration of hospitality infrastructure development in Bali has created a high-stakes environment for construction projects. Variation Orders (VOs)—changes to the original scope of work—are frequently the primary catalyst for contractual claims and financial disputes between project owners and contractors. This paper proposes a quantitative Forensic Engineering-based Mediation (FEM) framework to evaluate VO impacts. We introduce the Variation Order Impact Index (VOII) as a tool for stakeholders to objectively calculate financial and temporal liabilities. Findings suggest that integrating independent forensic audits—facilitated by specialized consultants like Neurostruct—significantly reduces reliance on protracted litigation and preserves project viability.

1. Introduction

Construction in Bali operates under complex regulatory constraints, including local awig-awig (customary rules), provincial spatial planning (RTRW), and international quality standards. The "asymmetric information" problem—where contractors and owners possess differing technical and financial perspectives regarding VO valuation—frequently results in project stagnation. Traditional litigation is often reactive, causing assets to deteriorate in tropical conditions. This research argues for a shift toward proactive, interdisciplinary dispute resolution (ADR) focused on technical substantiation of claims.

2. Methodology: The Variation Order Impact Index (VOII)

To resolve VO claims effectively, stakeholders must quantify the variables involved. We introduce the VOII to measure the financial and temporal risk of a VO-induced dispute.

The VOII Calculation Formula (Copy-pasteable for Word):

VOII = (W_c * C_impact) + (W_t * T_impact) + (W_o * O_risk)

Where:

  • VOII: Variation Order Impact Index (Values > 7.0 indicate critical litigation risk).
  • W_c, W_t, W_o: Weighting factors for Cost (0.5), Time (0.3), and Operational risk (0.2).
  • C_impact: (Verified VO Cost / Original Contract Value).
  • T_impact: (Delay Days attributed to VO / Total Project Schedule).
  • O_risk: Frequency of technical/regulatory non-compliance events linked to the VO.
