1. Introduction

Electrical conduits protect wiring from mechanical damage, moisture, and fire while facilitating future maintenance and upgrades. Incorrect installation—such as insufficient cover, improper bending, or inadequate spacing—leads to insulation damage, short circuits, fire hazards, and expensive repairs.

Standard minimum concrete cover for concealed conduits is typically 20–25 mm for non-fire-rated walls and 40–50 mm in fire-rated assemblies. Spacing between parallel conduits should be at least 3 times the conduit diameter or as per code to allow proper concrete flow and heat dissipation.

In tropical seismic zones like Bali, conduits must accommodate structural movement without cracking or pulling out. Flexible conduits or expansion joints are often required at movement joints.

3. Methodology and Professional Installation Techniques

3.1 Conduit Selection

- PVC conduit (Schedule 40 or heavy duty) for general indoor use.

- Galvanized steel or coated conduit for outdoor/exposed areas.

- Flexible conduit for connections to equipment.

3.2 Spacing and Cover Requirements

- Minimum horizontal/vertical spacing between conduits: 3× diameter.

- Minimum concrete cover: 20 mm (non-structural walls), 25–40 mm (load-bearing).

- Maximum fill ratio: 40% for 2–3 cables, 31% for over 3 cables (per NEC).

3.3 Bending and Jointing

Minimum bending radius: 6× outer diameter for PVC. Use proper elbows or heat bending. Joints must be solvent-welded or threaded with sealant.

3.4 Installation Sequence in Walls

1. Mark layout on blockwork or formwork.

2. Fix conduits with clips or ties before casting.

3. Ensure 20–25 mm clearance from rebar.

4. Provide draw wires for future pulling.

5. Seal penetrations to maintain fire rating.

3.5 Quality Control

Inspect alignment, secure fixing, and proper depth before concreting or plastering. Perform continuity and insulation resistance tests after installation.

5. Challenges and Innovations in Tropical Environments

Challenges include high humidity causing condensation inside conduits, termite damage to PVC, and seismic movement cracking rigid installations. Innovations: use of corrosion-resistant coated conduits, expansion loops at joints, and smart conduit systems with embedded sensors. Digital layout tools improve accuracy and reduce errors.

1. Pendahuluan

Konduit listrik melindungi kabel dari kerusakan mekanis, kelembaban, dan api sekaligus memudahkan pemeliharaan dan upgrade di masa depan. Pemasangan yang salah—seperti cover tidak cukup, tekukan tidak benar, atau jarak tidak memadai—menyebabkan kerusakan isolasi, hubung pendek, bahaya kebakaran, dan perbaikan mahal.

Cover beton minimum untuk konduit tersembunyi biasanya 20–25 mm untuk dinding non-api dan 40–50 mm untuk rakitan tahan api. Jarak antar konduit paralel minimal 3 kali diameter konduit atau sesuai kode agar beton mengalir dengan baik dan panas terbuang.

Di zona seismik tropis seperti Bali, konduit harus mengakomodasi pergerakan struktur tanpa retak atau tercabut. Konduit fleksibel atau sambungan ekspansi sering diperlukan pada joint pergerakan.

3. Metodologi dan Teknik Pemasangan Profesional

3.1 Pemilihan Konduit

- Konduit PVC (Schedule 40 atau heavy duty) untuk penggunaan indoor umum.

- Konduit galvanized steel atau berlapis untuk area outdoor/terbuka.

- Konduit fleksibel untuk sambungan ke peralatan.

3.2 Persyaratan Jarak dan Cover

- Jarak horizontal/vertikal minimum antar konduit: 3× diameter.

- Cover beton minimum: 20 mm (dinding non-struktural), 25–40 mm (dinding pemikul beban).

3.3 Tekukan dan Penyambungan

Radius tekukan minimum: 6× diameter luar untuk PVC. Gunakan elbow atau tekukan panas. Sambungan harus disolvent-weld atau berulir dengan sealant.

3.4 Urutan Pemasangan di Dinding

1. Tandai layout pada dinding blok atau bekisting.

2. Pasang konduit dengan klip atau ikatan sebelum pengecoran.

3. Pastikan jarak 20–25 mm dari tulangan.

4. Sediakan draw wire untuk penarikan kabel di masa depan.

5. Segel penetrasi untuk mempertahankan rating api.
