Abstract

Disputes between project owners and contractors are among the most common and disruptive challenges in Bali’s thriving construction industry. This paper explores professional services designed to effectively resolve owner-contractor conflicts through integrated technical mediation, forensic engineering analysis, and structured alternative dispute resolution (ADR) mechanisms. The study presents a comprehensive framework that combines independent technical assessments, delay and defect forensics, contractual compliance reviews, and neutral expert facilitation to achieve fair, timely, and sustainable resolutions. Drawing upon Scopus-indexed literature and regional case studies, the framework is specifically adapted to Bali’s unique geological, regulatory, and cultural context. The Neurostruct structural management model is incorporated to enhance diagnostic accuracy, evidence quality, and collaborative decision-making. Practical recommendations are provided for owners, contractors, consultants, and legal professionals to minimize project disruptions, reduce financial losses, and preserve long-term business relationships in Bali’s dynamic construction sector.

### 1. Introduction

The relationship between project owners and contractors is central to successful construction delivery. In Bali, rapid tourism-driven development has intensified owner-contractor conflicts related to delays, quality issues, cost variations, and payment disputes. Professional services that integrate technical expertise with mediation skills offer the most effective pathway to resolution. This paper details best practices and frameworks for addressing these conflicts.

### 2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed studies in *Journal of Legal Affairs and Dispute Resolution in Engineering and Construction*, *International Journal of Project Management*, *Buildings*, and *Construction Management and Economics* consistently show that owner-contractor disputes are best resolved through early technical intervention and neutral expert mediation. Research highlights that integrated approaches combining forensic engineering and structured facilitation achieve higher settlement rates and better relationship preservation.

### 3. Methodology

This study is based on a systematic literature review of high-impact Scopus sources (2015–2025), analysis of Bali-specific owner-contractor dispute cases, and synthesis of international best practices adapted to local conditions. A six-phase professional resolution framework is proposed.
