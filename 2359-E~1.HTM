1. Introduction

Lateral pressure from fresh concrete is one of the primary loads formwork must resist. Underestimating this pressure can lead to catastrophic formwork collapse, while overestimation results in unnecessarily heavy and expensive formwork. The pressure is not always hydrostatic (full liquid head) because concrete begins to stiffen and develop internal shear strength shortly after placement.

The maximum lateral pressure \( p_{max} \) is a function of:

- Rate of vertical placement \( R \) (m/h)

- Concrete temperature \( T \) (°C)

- Concrete density \( \rho \) (typically 2400–2500 kg/m³)

- Height of form \( h \)

- Rheological properties (yield stress and thixotropy for SCC)

According to ACI 347 (Guide to Formwork for Concrete), for normal weight concrete with placement rates ≤ 2.1 m/h and height ≤ 4.3 m, the pressure is calculated as:

\[

p_{max} = C_w \cdot C_c \cdot [150 + 9000 \cdot R / T]

\]

(with limits: minimum 600 psf or 30 kPa, maximum hydrostatic pressure \( p = \rho g h \)).

For higher rates or taller forms, modified equations apply. In tropical conditions (high temperature, high humidity), concrete sets faster, potentially reducing pressure, but rapid placement or SCC can generate near-hydrostatic pressures.

2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed research has advanced form pressure prediction beyond traditional empirical models. Studies on self-compacting concrete show that thixotropy and structural build-up significantly reduce lateral pressure compared to hydrostatic assumptions. Billberg et al. and others validated that SCC with high yield stress exhibits lower peak pressures. Recent works integrate rheological parameters (yield stress, plastic viscosity) into predictive models, achieving better accuracy than ACI 347 for modern high-performance concretes. In tropical climates, temperature effects on setting time and pressure decay are critical, as higher ambient temperatures accelerate stiffening and pressure reduction.

3. Methodology and Calculation Methods

3.1 ACI 347 Method (Most Widely Used)

For columns and walls with placement rate \( R \leq 2.1 \) m/h and height \( h \leq 4.3 \) m:

\[

p_{max} = C_w C_c [150 + 9000 R / T] \quad (\text{in psf})

\]

where \( C_w \) is weight coefficient, \( C_c \) is chemistry coefficient.

Convert to SI units for practical use:

\[

p_{max} (\text{kPa}) \approx \rho g h \quad \text{(hydrostatic limit)}

\]

3.2 Modified Equations for Higher Rates

For \( R > 2.1 \) m/h or taller forms, pressure increases toward full hydrostatic:

\[

p_{max} = \rho g h

\]

3.3 Rheological-Based Models for SCC

Advanced models incorporate yield stress \( \tau_0 \) and structural build-up rate \( A_{thix} \):

\[

p_{max} \propto \frac{\rho g h}{1 + k \cdot A_{thix} \cdot t}

\]

(reduced pressure with higher thixotropy).

- Always apply a minimum pressure of 30 kPa or full hydrostatic if uncertain.

1. Pendahuluan

Tekanan lateral dari beton segar merupakan salah satu beban utama yang harus ditahan oleh bekisting. Meremehkan tekanan ini dapat menyebabkan keruntuhan bekisting yang katastrofik, sementara overestimasi menghasilkan bekisting yang terlalu berat dan mahal. Tekanan tidak selalu hidrostatis (tekanan cair penuh) karena beton mulai mengeras dan mengembangkan kekuatan geser internal segera setelah pengecoran.

Tekanan lateral maksimum \( p_{max} \) merupakan fungsi dari:

- Laju pengecoran vertikal \( R \) (m/jam)

- Suhu beton \( T \) (°C)

- Densitas beton \( \rho \) (biasanya 2400–2500 kg/m³)

- Tinggi bekisting \( h \)

- Sifat reologi (tegangan leleh dan thixotropy untuk SCC)

Menurut ACI 347, untuk beton berat normal dengan laju pengecoran ≤ 2,1 m/jam dan tinggi ≤ 4,3 m:

\[

p_{max} = C_w \cdot C_c \cdot [150 + 9000 \cdot R / T]

\]

Di kondisi tropis (suhu tinggi, kelembaban tinggi), beton mengeras lebih cepat, berpotensi mengurangi tekanan, tetapi pengecoran cepat atau penggunaan SCC dapat menghasilkan tekanan mendekati hidrostatis. Makalah ini menyajikan panduan profesional lengkap dengan metode perhitungan praktis dan rekomendasi lapangan.

2. Tinjauan Pustaka

Penelitian terindeks Scopus telah memajukan prediksi tekanan bekisting melampaui model empiris tradisional. Studi pada self-compacting concrete menunjukkan bahwa thixotropy dan structural build-up secara signifikan mengurangi tekanan lateral dibandingkan asumsi hidrostatis. Billberg et al. dan peneliti lain memvalidasi bahwa SCC dengan tegangan leleh tinggi menunjukkan tekanan puncak lebih rendah. Karya terkini mengintegrasikan parameter reologi ke dalam model prediksi, mencapai akurasi lebih baik daripada ACI 347 untuk beton berkinerja tinggi modern. Di iklim tropis, efek suhu terhadap waktu setting dan penurunan tekanan sangat krusial.

3.2 Persamaan Modifikasi untuk Laju Tinggi

Untuk \( R > 2,1 \) m/jam atau bekisting lebih tinggi, tekanan meningkat mendekati hidrostatis penuh.

3.3 Model Berbasis Reologi untuk SCC

Model canggih menggabungkan tegangan leleh \( \tau_0 \) dan laju structural build-up \( A_{thix} \).
