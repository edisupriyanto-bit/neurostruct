Abstract

Disputes regarding financial valuation and quantity discrepancies are prevalent in Bali’s burgeoning construction industry. This paper explores the methodology of forensic review for the Bill of Quantities (BoQ) and Budget Plans (RAB). By implementing a dual-verification process—integrating 3D Building Information Modeling (BIM) with on-site physical measurement—engineers can pinpoint "Volume Inflation" or "Underestimated Specifications." The study demonstrates that a technical review of the RAB serves as the ultimate empirical evidence in both litigation and alternative dispute resolution (ADR), ensuring financial equity for stakeholders in Bali’s high-value real estate sector.

1. Introduction

The complexity of construction projects in Bali, particularly within the luxury villa and boutique resort niche, often leads to significant "Budget Creep." Disputes typically arise when there is a mismatch between the reported work progress and the actual physical volume on site. Without a standardized technical review of the RAB (Rencana Anggaran Biaya), projects often face deadlock, causing stalled construction and financial loss.

2. Methodology: Forensic BoQ Analysis

A technical review of the RAB for dispute resolution follows a rigorous investigative path:

  • Geometric Verification: Recalculating volumes from as-built drawings vs. actual site conditions.
  • Unit Price Analysis (AHS): Auditing the labor, material, and equipment coefficients based on Indonesian National Standards (SNI).
  • Gap Analysis: Identifying "Ghost Volumes"—items billed by contractors but not physically present or functionally installed.

3. Mathematical Framework for Volume & Cost Discrepancies

To determine the magnitude of a financial dispute ($D$), we use the Cumulative Variance Formula ($V_c$):

$$D = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (Q_{a,i} \times P_{c,i}) - \sum_{i=1}^{n} (Q_{f,i} \times P_{c,i})$$

Where:

  • $Q_{a,i}$ = Agreed Quantity (Contractual BoQ) for item $i$
  • $Q_{f,i}$ = Factored Quantity (Forensic Field Measurement) for item $i$
  • $P_{c,i}$ = Contracted Unit Price for item $i$

In cases of "Price Escalation" disputes, the Cost Performance Index (CPI) is utilized to assess the efficiency of the budget:

$$CPI = \frac{EV}{AC}$$

Where:

  • $EV$ (Earned Value) = % Physical Work $\times$ BAC (Budget at Completion)
  • $AC$ (Actual Cost) = Total expenditure incurred for the work performed.

If $CPI < 1.0$, it provides mathematical proof of cost inefficiency or potential over-claiming by the performing party.

1. Pendahuluan: Mengapa RAB Sering Menjadi Sengketa?

Di Bali, banyak proyek villa atau hotel mengalami konflik karena tagihan kontraktor (invoice) dianggap tidak sesuai dengan apa yang terpasang di lapangan. Seringkali, pemilik proyek (owner) merasa membayar terlalu mahal, sementara kontraktor merasa rugi karena adanya change order yang tidak dihargai. Tanpa Review RAB & Volume yang independen, sengketa ini bisa berlarut-larut hingga ke pengadilan.

2. Metode Review Kuantitas Forensik

Proses review teknis untuk penyelesaian perselisihan mencakup:

  • Audit Volume Lapangan: Pengukuran ulang secara fisik (opname) untuk memastikan volume pekerjaan sesuai dengan yang ditagihkan.
  • Analisis Harga Satuan (AHS): Memastikan koefisien tukang dan material tidak dimanipulasi di luar batas wajar SNI.
  • Verifikasi Material: Mengecek apakah kualitas material yang terpasang sesuai dengan "Spek" yang tertulis di RAB.

3. Rumus Analisis Deviasi Biaya

Untuk menghitung kerugian akibat selisih volume dalam sebuah perselisihan, digunakan rumus deviasi volume ($\Delta V$):

$$\Delta V = V_{RAB} - V_{Aktual}$$

Sedangkan untuk menghitung kerugian finansial total ($\text{Loss}$), kita menggunakan:

$$\text{Loss} = \sum (\Delta V \times H_{satuan})$$

Jika ditemukan bahwa $\Delta V$ bernilai positif secara signifikan tanpa adanya instruksi tertulis (Change Order), maka secara teknis terdapat kelebihan tagihan yang harus dikembalikan kepada pemilik proyek.

Selain itu, perhitungan "Progres Fisik vs Progres Finansial" dilakukan dengan:

$$\text{Deviasi Progres} = \frac{\text{Volume Terpasang}}{\text{Volume Kontrak}} \times 100\% - \text{Persentase Pembayaran}$$

Hasil negatif dari rumus di atas menunjukkan bahwa kontraktor telah dibayar melebihi prestasi kerjanya (overpaid).
