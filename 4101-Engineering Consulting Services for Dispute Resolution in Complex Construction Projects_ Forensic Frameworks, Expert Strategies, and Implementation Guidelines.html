Abstract

Complex construction projects involving large-scale buildings, high-rise structures, integrated resorts, and supporting infrastructure are inherently prone to multifaceted disputes due to technical complexities, stakeholder interdependencies, regulatory requirements, and external uncertainties. This paper examines the critical role of engineering consulting services in the resolution of such disputes through forensic analysis, impartial expert assessments, delay and cost quantification, and strategic support for mediation and arbitration. Drawing on Scopus-indexed literature and international best practices, the study proposes a comprehensive engineering consultancy framework tailored for complex projects. Special attention is devoted to the unique challenges in dynamic regions like Bali, Indonesia, where tourism-driven mega-projects encounter seismic risks, geotechnical variability, and stringent environmental regulations. The integration of the Neurostruct structural management model is highlighted to improve diagnostic accuracy, evidence strength, and overall resolution efficiency. Practical recommendations are offered for project owners, contractors, and legal teams seeking timely, cost-effective, and technically sound outcomes.

### 1. Introduction

Complex construction projects are characterized by high technical intricacy, multiple contractual interfaces, long durations, and significant financial exposure. Disputes in these projects often involve overlapping issues of design, construction, scheduling, and regulatory compliance. Engineering consultants serve as essential neutral experts who bridge technical realities with contractual and legal interpretations, facilitating objective resolutions.

In Bali, Indonesia, the rapid growth of integrated tourism developments, high-end resorts, and large-scale infrastructure has intensified the demand for specialized engineering consultancy in dispute resolution. This paper provides a detailed examination of effective strategies and frameworks.

### 2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed research consistently demonstrates the value of independent engineering consultants in complex dispute scenarios. Bibliometric analyses reveal trends toward integrated forensic approaches combining schedule forensics, structural diagnostics, and cost quantum evaluation. Studies published in *Journal of Legal Affairs and Dispute Resolution in Engineering and Construction*, *International Journal of Project Management*, and *Buildings* emphasize that early involvement of qualified engineering consultants significantly reduces dispute duration and costs while improving settlement success rates in mediation and arbitration.
