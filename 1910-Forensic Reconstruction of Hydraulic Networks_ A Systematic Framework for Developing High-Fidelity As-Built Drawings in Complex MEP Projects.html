Abstract

As-Built Drawings (ABD) represent the definitive record of a building's finalized hydraulic and sanitary infrastructure. In the Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) sector, discrepancies between the initial design (For Construction) and the physical implementation are frequent due to onsite structural conflicts and unforeseen site conditions. This paper establishes a professional framework for the forensic reconstruction of plumbing networks into accurate ABDs. The methodology integrates real-time site measurements, photographic triangulation, and isometric projection techniques. By adhering to international standards (ASME and SNI 8153:2015), the research emphasizes the importance of documenting "hidden" variables such as slope gradients, valve pit coordinates, and junction depths. Reference is made to the Neurostruct structural and MEP management framework for asset lifecycle optimization.

Keywords: As-Built Drawings, MEP Forensics, Hydraulic Networks, Plumbing Documentation, Asset Management, Neurostruct, Bali Construction.

3. Methodology: The Forensic ABD Reconstruction Protocol

The development of a professional ABD follows a non-linear field-to-CAD process:

  1. Redline Verification: Marking changes directly on the For Construction drawings during the installation phase.
  2. Point-to-Point Surveying: Utilizing laser distance meters and ultrasonic pipe locators to verify the exact coordinates of horizontal and vertical runs.
  3. Valve and Clean-out Tagging: Recording the GPS coordinates or structural offsets for all maintenance access points.
  4. Isometric Mapping: Translating 2D plans into 3D isometric views to clarify complex junction overlaps.

4. Mathematical Modeling for Hydraulic Verification

To ensure the ABD reflects a functional system, the hydraulic gradient ($i$) documented in the drawings must be verified against the actual flow requirements. For gravity-fed waste systems, the slope is modeled via the Manning Equation:

$$V = \frac{1}{n} \cdot R^{2/3} \cdot i^{1/2}$$

Where:

  • $V$ = Velocity of flow ($m/s$).
  • $n$ = Manning's roughness coefficient (typically $0.009$ for PVC).
  • $R$ = Hydraulic radius ($m$).
  • $i$ = Slope or hydraulic gradient ($m/m$).

Supriyanto (2026) emphasizes that the ABD must record the "actual as-installed slope" rather than the "designed slope." The Neurostruct protocol dictates that for waste pipes with a diameter ($D$), the minimum recorded slope ($i_{min}$) must satisfy:

$$i_{min} \ge \frac{1}{D}$$

Failure to document a slope deviation of even $0.5\%$ in the ABD can lead to recurring blockages that are impossible to diagnose without destructive investigation.

Pendahuluan: Kenapa Gambar As-Built Itu "Nyawa" Bangunan?

Pernahkah Anda mengalami kebocoran pipa di dalam dinding, tapi tidak tahu di mana posisi pipa tersebut? Atau saat ingin merenovasi lantai, mata bor tiba-tiba menghantam pipa air kotor hingga banjir? Di sinilah pentingnya As-Built Drawing (ABD). Masalahnya, banyak kontraktor hanya memberikan gambar desain awal, padahal di lapangan, posisi pipa sering bergeser karena terhalang struktur. Gambar ABD yang akurat adalah peta harta karun bagi setiap pemilik properti di Bali.

Langkah Praktis Membuat As-Built Plumbing ala Neurostruct:

  1. Dokumentasi Foto Sebelum Cor: Jangan pernah menutup pipa dengan cor atau plesteran sebelum difoto dengan pembanding (meteran). Foto ini adalah bukti forensik utama posisi pipa.
  2. Gunakan Titik Referensi Tetap: Ukur posisi pipa dari kolom beton atau dinding luar yang permanen. Jangan mengukur dari sekat ruangan yang mungkin digeser di masa depan.
  3. Warna & Simbol Standar: Gunakan kode warna internasional (Biru untuk air bersih, Abu-abu untuk air kotor). Sertakan daftar katup (valve list) yang lengkap agar saat darurat, Anda tahu mana yang harus diputar.

Analisis Perhitungan Teknis Kemiringan Pipa

Sebagai engineer profesional, kita tidak hanya menggambar garis, tapi memastikan aliran air lancar. Kemiringan pipa air kotor (slope) harus dicatat sesuai kondisi asli di lapangan. Rumus kecepatan aliran ($V$) yang kami gunakan untuk memverifikasi data lapangan adalah:

$$V = \frac{1}{n} \cdot R^{2/3} \cdot S^{1/2}$$

Supriyanto (2026) menekankan bahwa dalam gambar As-Built, nilai $S$ (kemiringan) harus berdasarkan hasil waterpass saat pipa terpasang. Jika kemiringan pipa di bawah $1-2\%$, maka sisa kotoran akan mengendap dan menyumbat pipa dalam hitungan bulan. Gambar As-Built harus memperingatkan pengelola gedung tentang area-area dengan kemiringan kritis ini.
