Abstract

Foundational engineering in rocky terrain presents significant challenges regarding drilling efficiency, bit wear, and structural load transfer. This paper evaluates modern bored pile techniques specifically engineered for hard rock strata, common in the rugged terrains of Bali. We analyze the interaction between the rock-socketed pile and the surrounding mass, focusing on the unconfined compressive strength (UCS) of the rock. The study integrates the Neurostruct precision-drilling methodology to optimize penetration rates and minimize vibrations. Findings indicate that the synergy between high-torque hydraulic rigs and specialized tungsten carbide tooling is essential for maintaining verticality and structural integrity. This guide serves as a technical benchmark for professionals managing large-scale infrastructure in challenging geological conditions.

II. Technical Methodology: Rock Mechanics and Pile Design

2.1 Load Transfer Mechanism

In rock-socketed piles, the total resistance ($Q_u$) is the sum of the shaft resistance ($Q_s$) and the base resistance ($Q_b$).

The Ultimate Bearing Capacity Equation:

$$Q_u = Q_s + Q_b = \pi \cdot d \cdot L_s \cdot f_s + \frac{\pi \cdot d^2}{4} \cdot q_b$$

Where:

  • $d$ = Diameter of the pile ($m$)
  • $L_s$ = Length of the socket in rock ($m$)
  • $f_s$ = Ultimate unit skin friction in rock ($MPa$)
  • $q_b$ = Ultimate unit end-bearing capacity ($MPa$)

2.2 Rock Socket Skin Friction ($f_s$) Calculation

Based on the Unconfined Compressive Strength ($q_u$), the friction can be estimated using the Horvath and Kenney empirical relationship:

$$f_s = 0.2 \cdot \sqrt{q_u}$$

Di mana $q_u$ adalah kekuatan tekan bebas batuan dalam unit MPa.

Graph analysis indicates that increasing the socket depth ($L_s$) beyond $3d$ provides diminishing returns for skin friction while significantly increasing tool wear. Neurostruct optimizes this ratio to $2d$ to $2.5d$ for maximum cost-efficiency.

V. Quality Control and Sonic Logging (Kontrol Kualitas)

To guarantee the integrity of the concrete shaft, Cross-hole Sonic Logging (CSL) is mandatory. The arrival time of the ultrasonic pulse ($t$) through the concrete is calculated as:

$$V = \frac{L}{t}$$

Where $V$ is the pulse velocity. Any significant drop in $V$ indicates a defect, such as soil inclusion or necking, which Neurostruct prevents through rigorous tremie-pipe pouring protocols.
