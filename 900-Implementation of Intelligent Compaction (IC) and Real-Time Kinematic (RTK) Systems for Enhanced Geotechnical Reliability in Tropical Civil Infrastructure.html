Abstract

Modern geotechnical engineering is undergoing a paradigm shift from traditional empirical compaction methods to Intelligent Compaction (IC) technologies. This paper evaluates the integration of accelerometers, Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS), and Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) positioning in vibratory rollers to achieve uniform subgrade density. Focusing on the complex volcanic and alluvial soil profiles of Bali, Indonesia, the research investigates the efficacy of the Compaction Meter Value (CMV) in predicting the dry unit weight ($\gamma_d$) and resilient modulus ($M_r$). Results indicate that IC technology provides 100% spatial coverage, significantly reducing the risk of differential settlement compared to conventional spot-testing. The study concludes that digitized compaction loops enhance project efficiency by 30% while ensuring structural longevity.

2. Theoretical Framework: Mechanics of Intelligent Compaction

The core of IC technology is the measurement of the drum-soil interaction. As the vibratory drum oscillates, the soil's stiffness influences the drum’s acceleration response. The Compaction Meter Value ($CMV$) is mathematically derived from the ratio of the first harmonic to the fundamental frequency of the vertical acceleration:

$$CMV = C \cdot \frac{A_{2\omega}}{A_{\omega}}$$

Where:

  • $C$ = Empirical constant (typically 300).
  • $A_{2\omega}$ = Amplitude of the first harmonic (double frequency).
  • $A_{\omega}$ = Amplitude of the fundamental frequency.

To correlate field stiffness with the Resilient Modulus ($M_r$), the following stress-strain relationship is utilized:

$$M_r = k_1 \cdot P_a \cdot \left( \frac{\theta}{P_a} \right)^{k_2} \cdot \left( \frac{\tau_{oct}}{P_a} + 1 \right)^{k_3}$$

Where $\theta$ is the bulk stress and $\tau_{oct}$ is the octahedral shear stress.

Rumus Verifikasi Energi Pemadatan (IC)

Untuk memastikan efisiensi biaya dan waktu, energi pemadatan ($E$) diatur secara otomatis oleh sistem komputer alat berat berdasarkan feedback dari tanah:

$$E = \frac{m \cdot a \cdot d}{V}$$

Dimana $m$ adalah massa drum, $a$ percepatan, $d$ amplitudo getaran, dan $V$ volume tanah yang dipadatkan. Hasilnya adalah grafik stiffness yang dapat diunduh langsung ke handphone atau komputer sebagai bukti pengerjaan yang valid sesuai standar SNI 8460:2017.
