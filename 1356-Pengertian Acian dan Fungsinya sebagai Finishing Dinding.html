INTRODUCTION

In modern construction technology, finishing work plays a very important role in determining the final quality of a building. Even though structural strength depends on foundation, columns, beams, and slabs, the visual and surface quality of a building is determined by finishing works.

One of the most important finishing stages in masonry and concrete wall construction is acian, also widely known as skim coat in international construction practice.

Acian is the final layer applied to plastered walls to create a smooth, uniform, and ready-to-paint surface. It is a thin layer but has a major impact on the aesthetic value, durability, and quality perception of a building.

In tropical regions such as Bali, where humidity, rainfall, and temperature changes are significant, proper wall finishing is essential to prevent surface damage, cracks, and moisture problems.

WHAT IS ACIAN

Acian is a thin layer of cement-based finishing material applied on top of plastered walls or concrete surfaces. It consists of fine cement powder mixed with water and sometimes polymer additives to improve adhesion and flexibility.

The main characteristic of acian is its very thin thickness, typically around 1 mm to 3 mm. Unlike plaster, which is used to level uneven masonry, acian is purely a finishing material designed to refine the surface texture.

Acian creates a smooth surface that is suitable for painting, wallpaper installation, or decorative coating systems.

HISTORY AND USE IN MODERN CONSTRUCTION

In traditional construction systems, wall finishing was often limited to plastering only. However, as construction standards improved, especially in urban development and tourism-driven areas like Bali, the need for high-quality surface finishing increased.

Acian became a standard practice in modern residential houses, hotels, villas, offices, and commercial buildings. It is now considered a mandatory step in achieving high-end construction quality.

FUNCTION OF ACIAN

Acian has multiple important functions in construction:

  1. Surface refinement
    Acian removes small pores, scratches, and uneven textures on plastered surfaces, making the wall smooth and visually uniform.
  2. Aesthetic improvement
    Smooth walls significantly improve the interior and exterior appearance of buildings.
  3. Paint base layer
    Acian improves adhesion between wall surface and paint, reducing paint peeling and ensuring even color distribution.
  4. Protection layer
    Although thin, acian provides additional protection against dust, humidity, and minor surface erosion.
  5. Crack control
    Acian helps reduce micro-cracks caused by shrinkage of plaster or environmental stress.
  6. Value enhancement
    Buildings with high-quality finishing have higher market value, especially in Bali’s property and tourism sector.

MATERIAL COMPOSITION

The quality of acian depends on the materials used. Common components include:

  • Fine cement (high quality OPC cement)
  • Very fine sand (sometimes sieved or replaced with filler powder)
  • Clean water
  • Polymer additives (optional for premium finishing)

The correct mix ratio is very important. Poor mixing can lead to cracking, dusting, or poor adhesion.

APPLICATION PROCESS OF ACIAN

Proper application of acian requires technical skill and timing. The standard process includes:

  1. Surface preparation
    The plastered surface must be clean, dry, and free from dust or oil.
  2. Material mixing
    Cement and additives must be mixed evenly to achieve smooth consistency.
  3. Application stage
    Acian is applied using a steel trowel in a thin and even layer.
  4. Surface leveling
    The surface is smoothed repeatedly to eliminate marks and irregularities.
  5. Curing process
    Proper moisture control is required to prevent rapid drying and cracking.
  6. Final inspection
    Surface quality is checked before painting or finishing work begins.

COMMON PROBLEMS IN ACIAN WORK

Although acian looks simple, many problems can occur if workmanship is not good:

  • Hairline cracks due to fast drying
  • Poor bonding with plaster layer
  • Uneven surface texture
  • Powdering or chalking effect
  • Peeling or delamination
  • Water absorption issues

These problems are often caused by incorrect material ratio, poor curing, or unskilled application.

IMPORTANCE IN MODERN ARCHITECTURE

In modern architecture, especially in luxury villas and commercial projects in Bali, wall finishing quality is extremely important.

Clients do not only look at structural safety, but also:

  • Smoothness of walls
  • Paint quality
  • Visual aesthetics
  • Clean architectural lines

A poor acian finish can reduce property value significantly, even if the structural system is strong.

ACIAn IN BALI CONSTRUCTION INDUSTRY

Bali is one of the fastest-growing construction markets in Indonesia, especially in tourism-related infrastructure such as villas, resorts, hotels, and restaurants.

In this environment, construction quality standards are very high. Acian work must meet premium finishing expectations because international clients demand perfect wall surfaces.

Therefore, professional execution is essential.

QUALITY CONTROL IN ACIAN WORK

To achieve high-quality acian, the following control steps are required:

  • Material inspection before application
  • Monitoring mixing ratio
  • Checking wall moisture condition
  • Supervision during application
  • Final surface inspection under lighting

Good quality control ensures long-lasting and smooth wall surfaces.

PENDAHULUAN

Dalam dunia konstruksi modern, pekerjaan finishing memiliki peran yang sangat penting dalam menentukan kualitas akhir bangunan. Salah satu pekerjaan finishing yang paling penting adalah acian.

Acian merupakan lapisan tipis yang digunakan untuk menghaluskan permukaan dinding setelah plesteran sehingga siap untuk dicat atau diberi finishing lain.

Di Bali, dengan kondisi cuaca lembap dan permintaan bangunan berkualitas tinggi, acian menjadi pekerjaan yang sangat penting.

PENGERTIAN ACIAN

Acian adalah lapisan tipis berbahan dasar semen halus yang diaplikasikan pada permukaan dinding yang sudah diplester.

Fungsi utamanya adalah untuk menghaluskan permukaan agar siap untuk tahap finishing berikutnya seperti pengecatan.

Ketebalannya sangat tipis yaitu sekitar 1–3 mm.

FUNGSI ACIAN

Fungsi acian antara lain:

  1. Menghaluskan permukaan dinding
  2. Meningkatkan estetika bangunan
  3. Dasar pengecatan
  4. Melindungi permukaan plester
  5. Mengurangi retak rambut
  6. Meningkatkan nilai bangunan

MATERIAL ACIAN

Material acian biasanya terdiri dari:

  • Semen halus
  • Air bersih
  • Filler tambahan (opsional)

Kualitas material sangat menentukan hasil akhir.

PROSES PEKERJAAN ACIAN

Tahapan pekerjaan:

  • Persiapan permukaan dinding
  • Pencampuran material
  • Aplikasi lapisan tipis
  • Perataan permukaan
  • Perawatan (curing)

MASALAH YANG SERING TERJADI

  • Retak rambut
  • Permukaan tidak rata
  • Acian mengelupas
  • Debu pada permukaan
  • Tidak melekat sempurna

KESIMPULAN

Acian adalah bagian penting dalam finishing bangunan yang menentukan kualitas estetika dan daya tahan dinding.
