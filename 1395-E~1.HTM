3. Methodology for RAB Calculation of Door Installation

# 3.1 Quantity Take-off

- Measurement of door openings (width × height)

- Number of leaves (single or double)

- Type of door (wooden, glass, automatic)

# 3.2 Material Calculation

For wooden doors with frames:

- Volume of wood = Area × Thickness + frame volume

- Add 8–12% wastage for cutting and fitting in tropical conditions

Copy-pasteable Equation (Material Volume for Wooden Door):

\[ V_{wood} = (W \times H \times t) + V_{frame} \times (1 + W_f) \]

(where \( W_f \) = wastage factor 0.08–0.12)

Copy-pasteable Equation (Total RAB Cost):

\[ C_{total} = (C_m \times Q_m) + (C_l \times Q_l) + (C_e \times Q_e) + C_{overhead} + C_{contingency} \]

# 3.3 Labor Productivity

Typical productivity rates in Bali (2025–2026):

- Wooden door with frame: 4–6 units per worker-day

- Frameless glass door: 2–3 units per worker-day

- Automatic sliding door: 1–1.5 units per worker-day (including testing)
