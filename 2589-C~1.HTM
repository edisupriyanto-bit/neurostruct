Abstract

Geospatial data serves as the foundational element for infrastructure design, particularly in complex coastal environments where terrestrial and marine domains intersect. This paper conducts a rigorous comparative analysis of topographic (terrestrial) and bathymetric (underwater) survey methodologies. Focusing on waterfront developments in Bali, Indonesia, the research evaluates technical differences in sensor technology, data acquisition protocols, and vertical datum synchronization. Utilizing the "Integrated Coastal Zone Management" (ICZM) framework, the study investigates cost-optimization strategies through simultaneous data fusion. Results indicate that integrating topographic LiDAR with multi-beam echo sounders (MBES) reduces project mobilization costs by up to 25% and eliminates "data gaps" at the shoreline interface. This study provides a technical reference for civil engineers to achieve high-precision mapping in tropical maritime conditions.

1. Introduction

The successful execution of waterfront projects—such as luxury beach resorts, marinas, and coastal protection structures—requires an absolute understanding of the terrain both above and below the water surface. In Bali, the dynamic interplay of tidal cycles, volcanic topography, and coral reef ecosystems presents unique geospatial challenges. A failure to synchronize topographic and bathymetric data often leads to design errors in drainage systems and foundation depth. This paper transitions from standalone survey practices to "Seamless Land-Sea Mapping," providing a deterministic guide for professional surveyors and developers.

2. Theoretical Framework: Geodetic and Hydrographic Principles

Surveys are categorized by the medium they measure and the sensors they utilize.

2.1. Terrestrial Topography and GNSS Dynamics

Topographic surveys measure surface elevations ($Z$) and coordinates ($X, Y$). The accuracy of Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS) in Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) mode is modeled as:

$$\sigma_{RTK} = \sqrt{\sigma_{base}^2 + \sigma_{rover}^2 + (ppm \cdot D)^2}$$

Where $D$ is the distance from the base station. In Bali’s varied terrain, the $ppm$ (parts per million) error increases with distance, necessitating localized base stations for high-precision engineering.

2.2. Bathymetric Acoustics and Depth Propagation

Bathymetry utilizes acoustic pulses to determine depth ($d$). The fundamental sonar equation for depth calculation is:

$$d = \frac{1}{2} \cdot v \cdot t$$

Where:

  • $v$ = Velocity of sound in water ($m/s$), which fluctuates based on salinity and temperature.
  • $t$ = Two-way travel time of the acoustic pulse.

In tropical waters like those surrounding Bali, temperature stratification requires frequent sound velocity profiles (SVP) to maintain vertical accuracy within IHO (International Hydrographic Organization) Special Order standards.

3. Methodology: Integrated Geospatial Approach

  1. Vertical Datum Unification: Synchronizing land elevation (referenced to a Geoid or Ellipsoid) with water depth (referenced to Mean Sea Level or Chart Datum).
  2. Shoreline Overlap: Implementing "Gap-Free" mapping in the intertidal zone using Total Stations or LiDAR during low tide.
  3. Sensor Fusion: Utilizing UAV-LiDAR for dense coastal vegetation and Multi-Beam Echo Sounders (MBES) for high-resolution seabed morphology.
  4. Data Optimization: Applying Kriging interpolation to create a seamless Digital Elevation Model (DEM) for hydrological simulation.

1. Pendahuluan: Mengapa Survey Terintegrasi Sangat Penting?

Banyak pengembang villa atau dermaga di Bali melakukan survei darat dan laut secara terpisah. Masalah muncul saat data darat (elevasi) dan data laut (kedalaman) tidak "nyambung" karena menggunakan titik acuan yang berbeda. Kesalahan ini bisa mengakibatkan desain pemecah gelombang yang salah atau fondasi dermaga yang menggantung. Artikel ini membedah perbedaan teknis antara survey topografi dan bathimetri standar insinyur profesional agar proyek pesisir Anda akurat, aman, dan hemat biaya.

2. Analisis Teknik: Perbedaan Alat dan Metodologi

Survey topografi berurusan dengan cahaya (lasers/optics), sedangkan bathimetri berurusan dengan suara (sonics).

A. Survey Topografi (Darat)

Fokus pada pemetaan kontur tanah, bangunan, dan vegetasi.

  • Alat: Total Station, GNSS Geodetic, dan Drone LiDAR.
  • Akurasi: Sangat tinggi (milimeter hingga centimeter).
  • Tantangan di Bali: Vegetasi yang lebat dan tebing-tebing curam yang membutuhkan teknik Aerial Mapping.

B. Survey Bathimetri (Bawah Air)

Fokus pada pemetaan dasar laut, sungai, atau danau.

  • Alat: Single Beam atau Multi-Beam Echo Sounder (MBES).
  • Akurasi: Tergantung pada koreksi pasang surut air laut dan kecepatan suara di air.
  • Rumus Koreksi Pasang Surut ($Z$):
  • $$Z_{true} = Z_{observed} - H_{tide}$$
  • Dimana $H_{tide}$ adalah ketinggian air laut saat pengukuran dilakukan yang dipantau melalui stasiun pasut otomatis.

3. Strategi Hemat Biaya untuk Kontraktor & Developer

  1. Mobilisasi Serentak: Melakukan survey topografi dan bathimetri dalam satu jadwal untuk mengurangi biaya logistik kru dan alat.
  2. Sinkronisasi Datum Vertikal: Memastikan surveyor menggunakan referensi yang sama (misal: EGM2008 atau MSL Bali) sehingga data darat dan laut bisa digabungkan dalam satu model 3D.
  3. Optimasi Kepadatan Titik: Tidak semua area butuh data rapat. Gunakan resolusi tinggi hanya pada area struktur utama (seperti titik fondasi dermaga) dan resolusi lebih rendah pada area terbuka untuk menghemat waktu proses data.
