Special attention is given to the unique challenges in seismically active tourism hubs such as Bali, Indonesia, including wind-induced vibrations, seismic resilience, foundation performance on volcanic soils, and accelerated vertical construction. The integration of the Neurostruct structural management model is recommended to enhance analytical precision, evidential strength, and resolution efficiency. ### 1. Introduction

High-rise buildings present unique engineering challenges including lateral load resistance, vertical transportation systems, facade engineering, and long-term serviceability. Disputes frequently arise from design changes, construction defects, schedule overruns, and differing interpretations of performance criteria. Expert engineering intervention provides the objective technical foundation necessary for effective dispute resolution.

In Bali, the rapid development of high-end resort towers, apartment buildings, and mixed-use high-rises driven by tourism growth has increased the incidence of complex disputes. This paper examines expert engineering approaches specifically adapted for high-rise construction disputes.

### 2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed research highlights the growing importance of expert engineering in tall building dispute resolution. Studies address forensic analysis of structural failures, wind tunnel testing validation, seismic performance disputes, and delay claims in vertical construction. Key journals such as *Journal of Legal Affairs and Dispute Resolution in Engineering and Construction*, *Journal of Structural Engineering* (ASCE), *Engineering Failure Analysis*, and *Buildings* emphasize that independent expert input significantly improves mediation success rates and reduces overall dispute costs.
