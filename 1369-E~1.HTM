3. Methodology for RAB Calculation of Acian Works

# 3.1 Volume Estimation

- Gross wall area (m²)

- Deduct openings (doors, windows)

- Apply plaster thickness (typically 2–3 mm for acian)

Wet volume = Net area × Thickness (m)

Dry volume = Wet volume × 1.27 (standard factor for finishing mortar)

# 3.2 Material Quantification

For a 1 : 0.5 : 5.5 (cement : lime : sand) mix:

- Cement quantity (kg) = (Dry volume × Cement parts / Total parts) × 1440 kg/m³

- Add 5–8% wastage for tropical manual application

Copy-pasteable Equation (Dry Volume):

\[ V_{dry} = V_{wet} \times 1.27 \]

Copy-pasteable Equation (Cement Requirement):

\[ m_{cement} = \left( \frac{V_{dry} \times P_c}{P_{total}} \right) \times 1440 \times (1 + W) \]

(where \( W \) = wastage factor 0.05–0.08)

# 3.3 Labor and Equipment Costs

Labor productivity: 10–12 m²/worker-day for skilled applicators in Bali conditions.

Include costs for scaffolding, mixing equipment, and protective measures.
