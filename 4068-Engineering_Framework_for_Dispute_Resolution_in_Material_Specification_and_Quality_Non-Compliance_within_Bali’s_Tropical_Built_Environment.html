Abstract

Disputes arising from material specification discrepancies and quality non-compliance represent a significant risk to the lifecycle of construction projects in Bali. Given the region's aggressive coastal environment and high seismic activity, adherence to stringent engineering standards is non-negotiable. This paper proposes a systematic "Engineering Dispute Resolution" (EDR) framework. By integrating Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), chemical analysis of material degradation, and comparative compliance mapping against SNI (Indonesian National Standard), engineers can provide objective evidentiary support for dispute adjudication. The study concludes that data-driven forensic audits mitigate the subjectivity often found in construction litigation.

1. Introduction

In the rapidly evolving landscape of Bali’s luxury real estate and hospitality sectors, the gap between "Design Intent" and "Field Reality" is a primary source of legal conflict. Disputes typically center on the substitution of inferior materials (e.g., lower grade steel or non-compliant concrete mixes) and substandard workmanship that leads to premature structural distress.

2. Methodology: The Forensic Quality Audit

To resolve disputes over quality, a three-phase forensic approach is utilized:

  1. Verification of Specification: Cross-referencing Bill of Quantities (BoQ) with procurement invoices and onsite material samples.
  2. In-Situ Performance Testing: Implementing Schmidt Hammer Tests, Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV), and Carbonation depth measurements.
  3. Structural Integrity Simulation: Modeling the impact of used materials on the overall safety factor of the building.

3. Mathematical Models for Quality Assessment

In cases of concrete strength disputes, the characteristic strength ($f'_c$) must be statistically verified from core samples as per SNI 2847. The standard deviation ($s$) and the required average strength ($f'_{cr}$) are calculated using:

$$f'_{cr} = f'_c + 1.34s$$

$$f'_{cr} = f'_c + 2.33s - 3.5$$

If the field results fall below these thresholds, the contractor is technically liable for structural remediation. Furthermore, for steel reinforcement disputes, the yield strength ($f_y$) is evaluated against the design demand ($M_u$):

$$\phi M_n \geq M_u$$

Where $\phi$ is the strength reduction factor. Any value of $\phi M_n < M_u$ constitutes a fundamental breach of safety specifications.

4. Tropical Environment Considerations

Bali’s proximity to the ocean introduces chloride-induced corrosion. Disputes often arise regarding the "Quality of Protective Coatings" or "Concrete Cover Thickness." A technical audit must evaluate the diffusion coefficient of chlorides to determine if the structure will meet its 25-year or 50-year design life.

1. Pendahuluan: Fenomena "Turun Spek" di Bali

Banyak pemilik villa atau hotel di Bali merasa kecewa ketika bangunan yang baru selesai mulai menunjukkan tanda-tanda kerusakan seperti retak rambut, beton keropos, atau besi yang berkarat dalam waktu singkat. Seringkali, ini terjadi karena adanya "substitusi material" sepihak oleh pelaksana lapangan. Sengketa spek dan mutu bukan sekadar masalah estetika, tapi masalah keselamatan dan nilai investasi.

2. Tahapan Penanganan Sengketa Mutu

Seorang tenaga ahli akan melakukan investigasi mendalam melalui:

  • Audit Dokumen: Membandingkan RKS (Rencana Kerja dan Syarat-syarat) dengan material yang terpasang.
  • Pengujian Lapangan (NDT): Menggunakan alat Rebound Hammer atau Covermeter untuk mengecek posisi dan diameter tulangan besi di dalam beton tanpa menghancurkan struktur.
  • Analisis Lab: Mengecek kadar garam pada pasir atau kualitas air kerja yang digunakan, yang sering menjadi penyebab mutu beton drop di daerah pesisir Bali.

3. Rumus Teknis: Dasar Pembuktian Kegagalan Mutu

Dalam sengketa mutu beton, kita menggunakan analisis statistik untuk menentukan apakah beton tersebut "Layak" atau "Gagal". Jika hasil uji tekan silinder menunjukkan nilai yang bervariasi, kita menghitung nilai rata-rata yang diperlukan:

$$f'_{cr} = f'_c + 1.34s$$

Jika nilai lapangan jauh di bawah $f'_{cr}$, maka secara teknis bangunan tersebut dikategorikan sebagai "cacat mutu."

Selain itu, untuk menghitung kerugian finansial akibat penurunan spek material (misalnya dari besi 13mm ke 12mm), digunakan perbandingan rasio kapasitas momen:

$$\text{Rasio Kehilangan Kekuatan} = 1 - \frac{A_{s\_aktual}}{A_{s\_rencana}}$$

Data persentase kehilangan kekuatan inilah yang dibawa ke meja perundingan atau pengadilan sebagai dasar tuntutan ganti rugi.
