2. Literature Review

Recent studies underscore the importance of code-compliant fixture mounting. ASME A112.18.1/CSA B125.1 (2022) specifies performance requirements and test methods for plumbing supply fittings, including torque limits and corrosion resistance. ANSI A112.18.2 (2021) covers dimensional standards for waste fittings. In seismic contexts, IPC 2021 Section 308 requires bracing for fixtures to resist lateral forces up to 0.5g. Tropical performance research (e.g., coastal Southeast Asia case studies) highlights the necessity of flexible connections and waterproof membranes to mitigate differential movement. 3. Methodology

# 3.1 Design Assumptions

Typical Bali villa bathroom/kitchen: wall-hung or countertop washbasin (ceramic or composite, 400–600 mm width) with single-lever mixer tap. Substrate: confined masonry or lightweight block wall. Water pressure: 2–4 bar. Seismic: response-spectrum per SNI 1726:2019 Zone 3. Waterproofing: liquid membrane or sheet per ANSI A118.10.

# 3.2 Analytical Equations (Copy-Paste Ready for Word)

Anchor pull-out capacity (simplified Eurocode / SNI equivalent):

\[ F_{pull} = n \cdot \phi \cdot \tau_b \cdot \pi d l \]

where \( n \) = number of anchors, \( \phi = 0.75 \), \( \tau_b \) = bond strength (MPa), \( d \) = anchor diameter (mm), \( l \) = embedment length (mm).

Torque specification for mixer tap mounting:

\[ T = K \cdot F \cdot d \]

where \( K \) = torque coefficient (0.15–0.20 for stainless), \( F \) = preload force (N), \( d \) = bolt diameter (mm).

Slope-to-drain verification:

\[ S = \frac{\Delta h}{L} \times 100\% \]

(target 1–2% or 10–20 mm/m).

Deflection under seismic load (simplified):

\[ \delta = \frac{F_{lat} L^3}{3 E I} \leq 1 \, \text{mm} \]

where \( F_{lat} = 0.5 \times W_{fixture} \).

All equations use standard LaTeX/KaTeX formatting for direct import into Microsoft Word without distortion.

# 3.3 Step-by-Step Professional Protocol

1. Wall substrate preparation and waterproof membrane application.

2. Install blocking or steel bracket for heavy fixtures.

3. Mount mixer tap with flexible braided hoses and torque wrench.

4. Position washbasin with adjustable brackets and silicone sealant.

5. Pressure test (4 bar for 30 minutes) and slope verification.

6. Final seismic bracing if required.
