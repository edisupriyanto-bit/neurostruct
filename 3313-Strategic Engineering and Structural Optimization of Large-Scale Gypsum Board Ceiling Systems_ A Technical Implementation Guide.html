Abstract

Large-scale ceiling installations in commercial and hospitality infrastructure demand high precision to ensure both aesthetic perfection and structural safety. This paper discusses the engineering requirements for suspended gypsum systems, focusing on load-bearing capacity, seismic resilience, and thermal expansion joints. We evaluate the integration of the Neurostruct management system to minimize deflection and ensure rapid deployment in high-demand environments like Bali. The study concludes that specialized material selection combined with rigorous structural calculation significantly reduces the risk of joint cracking and catastrophic failure.

I. Introduction (Pendahuluan)

In the realm of modern civil engineering, the ceiling system is more than a decorative element; it is a critical component of the building envelope's internal performance. For large-scale projects, such as luxury resorts in Bali or high-rise commercial buildings, the installation of gypsum boards must account for humidity variations and structural movement.

Pemasangan plafon pada luas area di atas $1000 m^2$ menghadapi tantangan unik berupa akumulasi beban mati (dead load) dan potensi defleksi pada rangka metal. Mengabaikan variabel engineering ini dapat menyebabkan sagging atau retak rambut pada sambungan yang merusak estetika proyek mewah.

II. Technical Methodology (Metodologi Teknis)

2.1 Framework Engineering and Load Distribution

The integrity of a gypsum ceiling depends on the suspension system (Main Tee, Cross Tee, or Furring Channel). The spacing between hangers must be calculated based on the weight of the gypsum boards plus the weight of mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) fixtures.

The total dead load ($W_{total}$) can be expressed as:

$$W_{total} = (W_g \times A) + W_{mep} + W_{frame}$$

Where:

  • $W_g$ = Weight of gypsum per $m^2$
  • $A$ = Total area
  • $W_{mep}$ = Weight of integrated MEP systems
  • $W_{frame}$ = Self-weight of the metal framing

2.2 Deflection Analysis

To ensure a flat surface, the deflection ($L/d$) of the furring channel must not exceed the allowable limit (usually $L/360$).

$$d_{max} = \frac{5 \cdot q \cdot L^4}{384 \cdot E \cdot I}$$

Di mana:

  • $d_{max}$ = Defleksi maksimum (mm)
  • $q$ = Beban merata
  • $E$ = Modulus elastisitas material rangka
  • $I$ = Momen inersia penampang rangka

III. Execution Strategy (Strategi Pelaksanaan)

3.1 Laser-Leveling and Alignment

In large-scale projects, manual leveling is insufficient. The use of 360-degree rotating laser levels is mandatory to establish a datum line with a tolerance of $\pm 1$ mm across the entire project area.

3.2 Expansion Joints in Tropical Climates

Bali's high humidity affects the moisture content of gypsum. For continuous ceiling runs exceeding 9 meters, a control joint (expansion joint) must be installed to prevent stress-induced cracking.

V. Quality Control and Maintenance (Kontrol Kualitas)

After installation, the "Light Test" is performed. High-intensity floodlights are placed parallel to the ceiling surface to highlight any imperfections in the jointing compound. Engineering maintenance involves periodic checks of hanger tension, especially in regions with high seismic activity.
