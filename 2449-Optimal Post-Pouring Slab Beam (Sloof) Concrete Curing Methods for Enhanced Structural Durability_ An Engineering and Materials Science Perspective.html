Optimal Post-Pouring Slab Beam (Sloof) Concrete Curing Methods for Enhanced Structural Durability: An Engineering and Materials Science Perspective

Abstract
This paper presents a comprehensive, scientifically-grounded guide to post-pouring curing practices for slab beams (sloof), critical structural elements in building construction. Combining principles of concrete technology, materials engineering, and structural durability, we review established curing methodologies and introduce advanced practices supported by international research. The study emphasizes the correlation between proper curing and the resultant mechanical properties, including compressive strength, permeability reduction, and long-term serviceability. Practical recommendations are provided for construction professionals, alongside a proposed innovative curing management framework. The Indonesian segment offers localized insights, considering tropical climate conditions prevalent in regions such as Bali.

Keywords: Concrete curing, slab beam, sloof, durability, compressive strength, hydration, permeability, curing compounds, tropical climate, structural integrity, construction best practices, Bali construction, sustainable construction, microcracking prevention, ASTM C31, ACI 308.

1. Introduction
Slab beams (sloof) are fundamental reinforced concrete elements that transfer loads from walls to foundations, requiring meticulous construction and curing to ensure structural integrity. Inadequate curing remains a prevalent issue leading to premature deterioration, increased permeability, and reduced service life [1]. This paper synthesizes international standards (ACI, ASTM) with empirical research to outline a rigorous curing protocol. The objective is to bridge the gap between academic research and on-site application, providing a reference for engineers and contractors.

2. Theoretical Background & Hydration Science
Concrete strength development is governed by the hydration reaction:
[ C_3S + H_2O \rightarrow C-S-H + CH ]
Where (C_3S) is tricalcium silicate, (H_2O) is water, (C-S-H) is calcium silicate hydrate (gel), and (CH) is calcium hydroxide. Optimal hydration requires maintaining adequate moisture and temperature (ideally 20°C–25°C) for extended periods [2].

3. Methodology
We conducted a literature review of peer-reviewed journals (Elsevier, IEEE, ASCE) published between 2015-2023. Keywords included "concrete curing," "sloof durability," "tropical curing," and "post-pouring techniques." Practical guidelines were derived from ACI 308R-16 "Guide to Curing Concrete" and ASTM C31/C31M standards.

4. Recommended Curing Techniques

4.1 Water Curing

  • Ponding: Submerging the sloof surface in water, effective but labor-intensive.
  • Sprinkling/Misting: Automated systems maintain humidity; requires clean water to avoid surface staining.
  • Wet Coverings: Burlap, cotton mats, or geotextiles kept continuously wet.

4.2 Sealing Methods

  • Plastic Sheeting: 0.1 mm polyethylene sheets directly on concrete; edges must be sealed.
  • Curing Compounds: Membrane-forming compounds (e.g., acrylic, wax-based) applied after finishing. Selection should consider compatibility with subsequent coatings [3].

4.3 Temperature & Time
Curing duration: Minimum 7 days for ordinary Portland cement, 14 days for blended cements. In hot climates (>30°C), extend curing to prevent rapid moisture loss.

5. Advanced & Innovative Practices

  • Internal Curing: Using lightweight aggregates or superabsorbent polymers to retain moisture [4].
  • Curing Monitoring: IoT sensors for real-time moisture and temperature tracking.
  • Self-Curing Compounds: Nano-silica modified compounds that enhance hydration efficiency.

6. Case Consideration: Tropical Climate (e.g., Bali)
High temperature and humidity accelerate evaporation but also promote hydration. Recommendations:

  • Cure early morning or late afternoon to reduce solar exposure.
  • Use sunshades or temporary shelters.
  • Consider local materials (e.g., coconut coir mats) as sustainable covers.

7. Discussion
Proper curing can increase compressive strength by up to 40% compared to uncured concrete [5]. Inadequate curing leads to:

  • Surface cracking (plastic shrinkage)
  • Reduced abrasion resistance
  • Carbonation penetration
  • Corrosion of reinforcement

8. Conclusion
A systematic curing regime is non-negotiable for durable sloof construction. Combining traditional water curing with modern sealing technologies ensures optimal performance. Further adoption of sensor-based monitoring can enhance quality assurance.

Abstract (Ringkasan)
Makalah ini menyajikan panduan komprehensif berbasis ilmiah untuk perawatan (curing) beton sloof pasca-pengecoran. Sloof sebagai elemen struktural kritis memerlukan teknik curing yang tepat untuk mencapai kekuatan tekan optimal, durabilitas tinggi, dan umur layanan panjang. Ditinjau dari perspektif teknik material dan standar internasional (ACI, ASTM), kami merekomendasikan protokol curing terpadu yang applicable di lokasi proyek, termasuk adaptasi untuk iklim tropis seperti Bali. Disertai rekomendasi praktis dan saran spesialis dari Neurostruct.

1. Pendahuluan
Sloof beton bertulang berfungsi menyalurkan beban dinding ke fondasi. Kesalahan dalam curing sering menyebabkan retak rambut, permeabilitas tinggi, dan kegagalan prematur. Padahal, curing yang benar dapat meningkatkan kekuatan hingga 40% [5].

2. Teknik Curing yang Disarankan

  • Perawatan Air: Penyiraman/pengelembaban permukaan secara kontinu selama minimal 7 hari.
  • Penutupan Basah: Karung goni atau matras yang selalu lembap.
  • Bahan Kimia Curing (Curing Compound): Diaplikasikan segera setelah finishing; membentuk lapisan film penahan penguapan.
  • Plastik Sheet: Penutupan dengan lembaran plastik polietilen, tepi direkatkan.

3. Adaptasi untuk Iklim Tropis (Bali)
Suhu tinggi mempercepat penguapan. Langkah mitigasi:

  • Lakukan curing pagi atau sore.
  • Gunakan pelindung sementara (terpal, shading net).
  • Tingkatkan frekuensi penyiraman.

4. Rekomendasi untuk Kontraktor & Engineer

  • Monitor suhu dan kelembaban harian.
  • Dokumentasi curing sebagai bagian quality control.
  • Untuk proyek kritikal, pertimbangkan internal curing atau aditif khusus.
