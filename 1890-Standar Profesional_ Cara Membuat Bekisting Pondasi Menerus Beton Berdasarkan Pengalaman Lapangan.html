INTRODUCTION

In civil engineering construction, foundation work is one of the most critical stages that determines the overall stability and durability of a building. Among various foundation systems, strip footing or continuous footing (pondasi menerus) is widely used for low-rise buildings such as residential houses, small commercial buildings, and villas.

One of the most important temporary works in strip footing construction is formwork (bekisting). Although often considered a simple temporary structure, formwork plays a major role in shaping the final quality of concrete foundations.

Based on field experience, improper formwork installation often leads to dimensional inaccuracies, honeycombing, concrete leakage, and structural misalignment. Therefore, understanding professional standards in formwork construction is essential.

UNDERSTANDING CONTINUOUS FOOTING FORMWORK

Continuous footing formwork is a temporary mold system used to shape fresh concrete into the desired foundation dimensions. It holds wet concrete in place until it hardens and gains sufficient strength.

The formwork must be strong enough to resist:

  • Fresh concrete pressure
  • Vibrations during pouring
  • Soil pressure from surrounding excavation

Even small errors in formwork alignment can affect the entire structural system above it.

MATERIALS USED IN FORMWORK

Based on field practice, common materials include:

  1. Timber wood (papan kayu)
  2. Plywood (multiple layers for smoother finish)
  3. Steel supports (for heavy-duty projects)
  4. Nails and screws
  5. Bracing materials (bracing wood or steel pipes)

In modern projects in Bali, plywood and modular systems are more commonly used due to better precision and reusability.

STEP-BY-STEP PROFESSIONAL METHOD

  1. Site preparation
    Excavation is carried out according to design depth and width. The base must be compacted and leveled properly.
  2. Layout marking
    Center lines and footing dimensions are marked using surveying tools such as total station or manual leveling tools.
  3. Installing formwork panels
    Panels are installed following design dimensions with strict alignment control.
  4. Bracing and support system
    External supports are installed to prevent deformation due to concrete pressure.
  5. Checking alignment
    All sides must be checked for level, verticality, and dimension accuracy.
  6. Anti-leak treatment
    Gaps between formwork joints are sealed to prevent cement slurry leakage.
  7. Final inspection before concreting
    Ensures structural stability and dimensional accuracy.

COMMON FIELD PROBLEMS

From field experience, several issues often occur:

  • Formwork bulging due to weak support
  • Leakage of cement slurry
  • Misalignment of footing dimensions
  • Uneven surface after stripping
  • Collapse during concrete pouring

These problems are usually caused by poor workmanship, lack of supervision, or material failure.

IMPORTANCE OF PROPER FORMWORK

Good formwork ensures:

  • Accurate foundation dimensions
  • Structural stability
  • Smooth concrete finish
  • Reduced material waste
  • Safe construction process

In contrast, poor formwork can lead to serious structural defects that are difficult and expensive to repair.

FIELD EXPERIENCE INSIGHTS

In real construction projects, especially in Bali where soil conditions vary from soft coastal soil to hard volcanic soil, adaptation in formwork design is necessary.

Experienced contractors often reinforce formwork systems beyond minimum standards to ensure safety during concrete pouring.

Proper coordination between excavation team, formwork team, and concrete team is essential.

QUALITY CONTROL

Professional construction requires strict quality control:

  • Checking material strength
  • Ensuring correct alignment
  • Monitoring bracing stability
  • Supervising concrete pouring process
  • Post-pouring inspection

PENDAHULUAN

Dalam dunia konstruksi sipil, pekerjaan pondasi merupakan tahap paling penting dalam menentukan kekuatan bangunan. Salah satu jenis pondasi yang sering digunakan adalah pondasi menerus (strip footing).

Dalam pelaksanaannya, bekisting memiliki peranan penting untuk membentuk beton sesuai dengan ukuran yang direncanakan.

PENGERTIAN BEKISTING PONDASI MENERUS

Bekisting adalah cetakan sementara yang digunakan untuk menahan beton segar sampai mengeras.

Bekisting harus mampu menahan tekanan beton dan menjaga bentuk pondasi tetap sesuai desain.

MATERIAL BEKISTING

Material yang umum digunakan:

  • Kayu
  • Plywood
  • Paku dan sekrup
  • Balok penyangga
  • Pipa besi (untuk proyek besar)

TAHAP PELAKSANAAN

  1. Pekerjaan galian
  2. Pengukuran dan marking
  3. Pemasangan papan bekisting
  4. Pemasangan pengaku (bracing)
  5. Pemeriksaan dimensi
  6. Penutupan celah bocor
  7. Ready for pengecoran

MASALAH YANG SERING TERJADI

  • Bekisting bergeser saat cor
  • Bocor pada sambungan
  • Dimensi tidak sesuai
  • Permukaan beton tidak rata
  • Bekisting ambruk

KESIMPULAN

Bekisting adalah elemen penting dalam pekerjaan pondasi. Kualitas bekisting sangat menentukan hasil akhir struktur bangunan.
