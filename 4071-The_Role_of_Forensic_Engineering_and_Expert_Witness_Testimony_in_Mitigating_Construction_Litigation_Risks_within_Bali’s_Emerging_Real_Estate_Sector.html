Abstract

Construction disputes in Bali have escalated in complexity, necessitating the involvement of specialized engineering expert witnesses to provide technical clarity in legal proceedings. This paper analyzes the integration of forensic engineering methodologies—such as structural integrity verification, delay analysis, and cost-to-complete valuation—into the litigation framework. The study focuses on how data-driven testimony mitigates the subjectivity of legal arguments. By employing international standards such as those from the Society of Construction Law (SCL) and Indonesian National Standards (SNI), expert witnesses bridge the gap between engineering reality and judicial decision-making.

2. The Theoretical Framework of Expert Testimony

An engineering expert witness operates under the principle of Independency and Technical Integrity. The primary methodologies include:

  • Forensic Investigation: Physical testing of structural elements using Non-Destructive Testing (NDT).
  • Admissibility Analysis: Ensuring technical data meets the "Daubert Standard" or local procedural rules (KUHAP/Civil Law) in Indonesia.
  • Causation Mapping: Determining whether a structural failure was a result of design negligence, contractor error, or Force Majeure.

3. Mathematical Models in Forensic Engineering

To determine liability in structural failure cases, the expert must calculate the Reliability Index ($\beta$). The probability of failure ($P_f$) is assessed through the limit state function:

$$Z = R - S$$

Where:

  • $R$ = Resistance of the structure (Capacity)
  • $S$ = Load effects (Demand)

The Reliability Index is defined as:

$$\beta = \frac{\mu_Z}{\sigma_Z} = \frac{\mu_R - \mu_S}{\sqrt{\sigma_R^2 + \sigma_S^2}}$$

In court, if an expert can prove that $\beta$ is below the safety threshold required by SNI 2847:2019, the contractor or designer can be held legally liable for "Malpractice" or "Negligence."

4. Delay and Cost Overrun Quantification

In arbitration, experts utilize the Time Impact Analysis (TIA) to allocate responsibility for project delays. The formula for Liquidated Damages ($LD$) often debated in court is:

$$LD = (d \times c)$$

Where:

  • $d$ = Number of days of excusable vs. non-excusable delay.
  • $c$ = Daily rate as per contract.

The Expert Witness provides the technical "weight" to determine the value of $d$ through forensic schedule analysis.

3. Rumus Teknis dalam Kesaksian Forensik

Dalam persidangan sengketa mutu beton, saksi ahli sering menggunakan analisis statistik untuk membuktikan ketidaklayakan struktur. Rumus standar deviasi ($s$) untuk kekuatan beton lapangan adalah:

$$s = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n-1}}$$

Jika hasil pengujian menunjukkan variasi kekuatan beton yang sangat tinggi ($s > 5$ MPa) atau rata-rata kekuatan ($\bar{x}$) di bawah kuat tekan rencana ($f'_c$), maka saksi ahli memiliki dasar saintifik untuk menyatakan bahwa kontraktor telah melakukan wanprestasi mutu.

Selain itu, untuk menghitung kerugian finansial akibat material yang tidak sesuai, digunakan rumus:

$$\text{Kerugian} = (Q_{kontrak} \times P_{kontrak}) - (Q_{lapangan} \times P_{lapangan})$$
