Verify wall substrate strength and waterproof membrane integrity.

Step 2: Positioning and Marking

Set sink height 800–850 mm (SNI ergonomic standard); mark mixer tap center 150 mm above sink rim.

Step 3: Anchoring

Install minimum 4 M8 expansion anchors per sink (shear capacity verified per formula above); use flexible braided hoses for mixer taps.

Step 4: Sealing and Waterproofing
