Abstract

Payment disputes are among the most disruptive conflicts in construction projects, often leading to work stoppages, strained relationships, and cascading delays. This paper explores comprehensive solutions for payment-related disputes in Bali construction projects through integrated technical, contractual, and mediation approaches. The study emphasizes forensic analysis of payment claims, progress evaluation, retention assessment, and quantum validation supported by independent engineering evidence. Drawing from Scopus-indexed literature and regional practices, a tailored integrated resolution framework is proposed for Bali’s unique environment. Key challenges such as cash flow issues, design variations, geotechnical complications, regulatory delays, and tourism-sector project pressures are thoroughly addressed. The Neurostruct structural management model is integrated to strengthen technical validation of work progress and payment entitlements. Practical recommendations are provided for project owners, contractors, consultants, and legal teams to achieve faster, fairer, and more sustainable resolutions while maintaining project continuity in Bali’s competitive construction market.
