Abstract

Deep foundation engineering for mega-scale infrastructures requires an optimal balance between bearing capacity maximization and differential settlement minimization. In complex geological formations, such as the stratified alluvial sands and volcanic tuffs characteristic of the Bali region, standalone shallow pad foundations or deep pile foundations can be economically or structurally inefficient. This paper presents a high-efficiency framework for Combined Pad-Pile Foundation (CPPF) systems. By employing a displacement-based design approach and finite element soil-structure interaction modeling, we analyze the load-sharing mechanism between the upper shallow pad base and the underlying pile cluster. The mathematical parameterization accounts for vertical stiffness matrices, skin friction mobilization, and group efficiency factors. The proposed execution methodology reduces concrete volume by up to $26\%$ while restricting total differential settlement within strict international boundaries ($\Delta S \le 15\text{ mm}$), ensuring absolute structural safety under severe seismic and static load combinations.

Keywords: Combined Pad-Pile, Soil-Structure Interaction, Load-Sharing Optimization, Mega-Scale Project, Settlement Control, Neurostruct Bali Engineering.

1. Introduction

The design of foundation systems for high-load, mega-scale commercial structures—such as luxury resort complexes, expansive shopping malls, and multi-story transit infrastructure—is highly sensitive to soil deformation limits. When structures are founded on highly variable stratified soil profiles, structural engineers often face a dilemma: deep pile foundations can be financially prohibitive, while conventional shallow pad footings risk excessive differential settlement.

To address this challenge, the Combined Pad-Pile Foundation (CPPF) system has emerged as a state-of-the-art solution. CPPF systems utilize a composite mechanical mechanism where the shallow pad footing directly resists a portion of the superstructure's static load through shallow soil contact, while an optimized array of deep settlement-reducing piles transfers the remainder into deeper, highly competent strata. This paper provides a mathematically verified framework compliant with Indonesian National Standards (SNI 8460:2017) to optimize load distribution ratios, prevent shear failures, and establish flawless field implementation guidelines for complex large-scale developments.

2. Geotechnical Load-Sharing Mechanics and Mathematical Modeling

2.1 The Global Stiffness Matrix and Load-Sharing Ratio

In a Combined Pad-Pile Foundation, the total ultimate vertical load ($Q_{total}$) applied by the column is shared between the shallow pad footing base ($Q_{pad}$) and the deep pile group ($Q_{pile}$):

$$Q_{total} = Q_{pad} + Q_{pile}$$

The distribution of this load is governed by the relative settlement stiffness of each component. Let $K_{pad}$ represent the vertical stiffness of the isolated shallow footing, and $K_{pile}$ represent the collective vertical stiffness matrix of the pile group. The total system stiffness ($K_{system}$) is modeled as:

$$K_{system} = K_{pad} + K_{pile} - \alpha_{pp}$$

Where $\alpha_{pp}$ is the empirical interaction coefficient quantifying the stress field overlap between the pad base and the pile shafts. The load-sharing ratio ($X_{pile}$) assigned to the pile group is calculated as:

$$X_{pile} = \frac{Q_{pile}}{Q_{total}} = \frac{K_{pile}}{K_{pad} + K_{pile}}$$

In high-load mega-projects, the target design optimization restricts $X_{pile}$ between $0.55$ and $0.75$, ensuring the pad base actively utilizes the upper soil layer's safe bearing capacity without triggering localized shear yield.

2.2 Ultimate Settlement and Differential Deformation Vectors

The ultimate settlement ($S_{total}$) of the composite foundation under working loads must satisfy the serviceability limit states. The settlement is calculated using the consolidated elastic strain method:

$$S_{total} = \frac{Q_{total}}{K_{system}} \le S_{allowable}$$

Where $S_{allowable}$ is capped at $25\text{ mm}$ for multi-story framed commercial configurations according to SNI codes. The differential settlement ($\Delta S$) between adjacent columns spaced at distance ($L_c$) must maintain a strict angular distortion limit ($\beta$) to prevent structural cracking in concrete beams:

$$\beta = \frac{\Delta S}{L_c} \le \frac{1}{500}$$

3.1 Phase 1 & 2: Structural Pile Installation

First, deep piles (either driven spun piles or bored piles) are installed to the design refusal depth. To eliminate vibration damage to nearby structures—especially in high-density tourism zones like Kuta, Seminyak, or Sanur—the use of static hydraulic pile jacks or rotary drilling augers is required.

3.2 Phase 3 & 4: Excavation and Pile-Pad Integration

Following pile installation, the ground is excavated to the footing's base level. The pile heads are chipped back to expose the internal steel reinforcement bars.

CRITICAL REINFORCEMENT BINDING: The exposed longitudinal rebar from the piles must be bent outward at an angle of $45^\circ$ and mechanically tied directly into the bottom steel mat of the upper pad footing. This creates a monolithic, rigid connection capable of transferring heavy moment vectors during seismic events.

3.3 Phase 5: Monolithic Pouring

A thick lean concrete mud-slab ($100\text{ mm}$) is poured as a working floor. The heavy reinforcement cage for the combined pad is assembled, and the concrete is cast monolithically using high-performance self-compacting concrete (SCC) with a minimum compressive strength of $f'_c = 35\text{ MPa}$ ($K-400$).

1. Pendahuluan

Perencanaan sistem pondasi untuk infrastruktur skala mega (mega-scale commercial projects) seperti kompleks resort bintang lima, mall multi-blok, dan gedung bertingkat tinggi di Bali menghadapi tantangan geoteknik yang berat. Lapisan tanah di wilayah Bali sering kali bervariasi secara ekstrem, bergeser dari tanah aluvial lunak, pasir pantai tanpa kohesi, hingga batuan tufa vulkanik keras dalam kedalaman yang tidak seragam. Jika perencana mengandalkan pondasi tapak (pad footing) konvensional, risiko penurunan tidak merata (differential settlement) sangat tinggi. Sebaliknya, jika beralih sepenuhnya ke pondasi tiang pancang dalam (full pile foundation), biaya konstruksi akan membengkak secara drastis.

Sebagai solusi mutakhir, penerapan sistem Pondasi Telapak Terkombinasi Tiang Pancang (Combined Pad-Pile Foundation / CPPF) menjadi opsi terbaik yang menggabungkan keunggulan kedua sistem tersebut. Dalam sistem CPPF, pondasi telapak atas berfungsi memikul beban langsung melalui kontak tanah dangkal, sedangkan tiang pancang di bawahnya dipasang secara strategis sebagai pengontrol penurunan (settlement-reducing piles). Artikel ini menguraikan analisis mekanika interaksi tanah-struktur, perhitungan pembagian beban, serta panduan pelaksanaan lapangan berdasarkan SNI 8460:2017 agar proyek skala besar Anda kokoh, hemat biaya, dan aman dari risiko keretakan akibat gempa.

2. Analisis Mekanika dan Pemodelan Matematika Interaksi Tanah-Struktur

Sistem pondasi kombinasi bekerja dengan memanfaatkan mobilisasi kekuatan tanah secara berlapis. Distribusi beban harus dihitung secara presisi menggunakan matriks kekakuan (stiffness matrix) untuk menghindari kegagalan geser tanah.

2.1 Perhitungan Rasio Pembagian Beban (Load-Sharing Ratio)

Beban vertikal ultimit dari kolom struktur ($Q_{total}$) didistribusikan secara proporsional ke pondasi telapak ($Q_{pad}$) dan kelompok tiang pancang ($Q_{pile}$):

$$Q_{total} = Q_{pad} + Q_{pile}$$

Pembagian beban ini ditentukan oleh nilai kekakuan vertikal masing-masing elemen ($K_{pad}$ dan $K_{pile}$). Sistem kekakuan total ($K_{system}$) dirumuskan sebagai berikut:

$$K_{system} = K_{pad} + K_{pile} - \alpha_{pp}$$

Di mana $\alpha_{pp}$ merupakan koefisien interaksi reduksi beban akibat tumpang tindih tegangan antara dasar telapak dan tiang. Rasio beban yang dipikul oleh kelompok tiang ($X_{pile}$) dihitung dengan persamaan:

$$X_{pile} = \frac{Q_{pile}}{Q_{total}} = \frac{K_{pile}}{K_{pad} + K_{pile}}$$

Pada proyek skala besar, nilai $X_{pile}$ dioptimalkan pada rentang $0.55 - 0.75$. Artinya, tiang pancang memikul sebagian besar beban utama, sementara pondasi telapak memanfaatkan sisa daya dukung tanah permukaan dengan aman.

2.2 Kontrol Batas Penurunan dan Distorsi Sudut

Penurunan total ($S_{total}$) dari pondasi kombinasi dihitung berdasarkan penurunan elastis dan konsolidasi tanah:

$$S_{total} = \frac{Q_{total}}{K_{system}} \le S_{allowable}$$

Berdasarkan regulasi SNI, batas maksimum $S_{allowable}$ untuk gedung komersial adalah $25\text{ mm}$. Selain itu, perbedaan penurunan (differential settlement / $\Delta S$) antara dua kolom yang bersebelahan dengan jarak ($L_c$) tidak boleh melebihi batas distorsi sudut ($\beta$) demi mencegah keretakan pada balok beton:

$$\beta = \frac{\Delta S}{L_c} \le \frac{1}{500}$$

3. Metode Pelaksanaan Lapangan Sistem Pondasi Kombinasi Telapak-Tiang

Penerapan metode CPPF di lapangan membutuhkan ketelitian tinggi, terutama pada bagian koneksi (joint) antara kepala tiang dengan besi pondasi telapak:

Langkah 1: Pemasangan Tiang Pancang atau Bored Pile

Pasang tiang pancang (spun pile) menggunakan metode Hydraulic Static Pile Driver (HSPD) atau bored pile melalui sistem drilling. Penggunaan metode injeksi hidrolik tanpa suara dan getaran sangat direkomendasikan di Bali untuk mencegah komplain dari resort atau villa tetangga di sekitarnya.

Langkah 2: Penggalian Tanah dan Pembobokan Kepala Tiang (Pile Chipping)

Setelah tiang tertanam, lakukan penggalian tanah hingga elevasi dasar pondasi telapak yang direncanakan. Bobok bagian atas beton tiang pancang (chipping) menggunakan concrete breaker hingga menyisakan besi tulangan utama tiang sepanjang minimal $40$ kali diameter besi ($40D$).

Langkah 3: Integrasi Monolitik Pembesian Tiang dan Telapak

Hamparkan pasir urug dan cor lantai kerja (lean concrete) setebal $10\text{ cm}$. Atur besi tulangan utama tiang pancang yang telah diekspos agar ditekuk keluar membentuk sudut $45^\circ$.

KUNCI STRUKTURAL: Besi tiang pancang ini wajib diikat dan diangkur secara kuat ke dalam matriks anyaman besi bawah dari pondasi telapak (pad footing). Hal ini memastikan terjadinya transfer momen lateral dan vertikal secara monolitik saat struktur diguncang gempa bumi tektonik.

Langkah 4: Pengecoran Beton Ready-Mix Mutu Tinggi

Lakukan pengecoran pondasi telapak secara masal menggunakan beton ready-mix dengan mutu minimum $f'_c = 35\text{ MPa}$ ($K-400$). Pastikan pemadatan menggunakan vibrator dilakukan secara merata di sekitar kepala tiang untuk menghindari rongga udara (honeycomb) yang dapat melemahkan kekuatan sambungan pondasi.

4. Kesimpulan dan Rekomendasi Teknik

Dengan memadukan kekakuan dangkal dan dalam, penurunan bangunan dapat dikontrol di bawah batas izin SNI secara sempurna.
