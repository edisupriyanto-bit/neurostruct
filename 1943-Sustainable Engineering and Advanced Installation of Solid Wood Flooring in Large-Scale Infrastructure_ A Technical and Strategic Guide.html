I. Introduction (Pendahuluan)

The global demand for sustainable luxury in large-scale hospitality projects has positioned solid wood flooring as a premium standard. Unlike engineered wood, solid wood is a living material that responds dynamically to its environment. In regions like Bali, where the relative humidity (RH) often exceeds 80%, the engineering challenge lies in controlling the Equilibrium Moisture Content (EMC).

Pemasangan lantai kayu solid bukan sekadar estetika, melainkan tantangan teknik sipil yang melibatkan pemuaian radial dan tangensial. Kegagalan dalam memperhitungkan koefisien muai kayu dapat menyebabkan cupping atau buckling pada luas area di atas $500 m^2$.

II. Technical Methodology (Metodologi Teknis)

2.1 Subfloor Engineering and Leveling

Before installation, the subfloor must meet strict mechanical tolerances. The flatness must be verified using a laser leveler with a tolerance of $\pm 2$ mm over a 3-meter radius.

Formula untuk menghitung beban statis yang diizinkan pada struktur lantai adalah:

$$P_{allow} = \frac{F_b \cdot S}{L \cdot d}$$

Di mana:

  • $P_{allow}$ = Beban yang diizinkan (Allowed Load)
  • $F_b$ = Tegangan lentur desain kayu
  • $S$ = Section modulus
  • $L$ = Span length (Panjang bentang)

2.2 Moisture Content (MC) Analysis

Solid wood must be acclimated to the site for at least 7-14 days. For projects in Bali, the target MC should be between 9% to 12%.

Persamaan untuk menghitung kadar air kayu adalah:

$$MC\% = \frac{W_{wet} - W_{dry}}{W_{dry}} \times 100$$

III. Implementation Strategy (Strategi Implementasi)

3.1 Adhesive Selection and Mechanical Fastening

For large-scale projects, a "Glue-Down + Secret Nail" method is recommended. The adhesive must be a Silane-Terminated Polymer (STP) to allow for microscopic movement without losing bond strength.

3.2 Expansion Gap Calculation

In large areas, expansion gaps must be calculated based on the species-specific coefficient of expansion ($C_e$).

$$G = W \cdot \Delta MC \cdot C_e$$

Di mana $G$ adalah celah ekspansi yang dibutuhkan (Expansion Gap).

V. Maintenance and Longevity (Pemeliharaan)

Sustainability in engineering is defined by the lifespan of the material. For solid wood, this involves a systematic sanding and coating cycle every 10-15 years. Utilizing UV-cured oils can significantly reduce the Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) during the maintenance phase.

VI. Conclusion (Kesimpulan)

Installation of solid wood flooring in large-scale projects is a complex engineering task that requires a deep understanding of wood anatomy and environmental physics.
