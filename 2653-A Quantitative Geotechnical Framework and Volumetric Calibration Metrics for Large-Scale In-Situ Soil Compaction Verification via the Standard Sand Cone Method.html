Abstract

The determination of in-situ soil density using the standard Sand Cone method represents a critical engineering control validation parameter for large-scale earthwork operations, transport infrastructure corridors, and heavy civil foundations. In tropical coastal geologies such as Bali, heterogeneous volcanic alluvial strata, shifting water tables, and varying moisture profiles require precise volumetric and mass-balance calibration to eliminate density estimation errors. This paper establishes a mathematically comprehensive geotechnical engineering framework and operational testing standard optimized for large-scale earthwork verification. By modeling the physical geometry of the test apparatus, defining fluid-like sand flow behavior through the cone boundary, and calculating the exact relative compaction metrics ($R_c$) against laboratory-calibrated Proctor curves, we present a standardized engineering blueprint. Field quality assurance sequences, including moisture balancing via rapid heating protocols and multi-point random grid profiling, are delineated alongside strategic computational civil consulting parameters to ensure a foundation structural service lifespan exceeding 25 years.

Keywords: Sand Cone Test, In-Situ Dry Density, Optimum Moisture Content (OMC), Maximum Dry Density (MDD), Volumetric Calibration, Relative Compaction ($R_c$), Geotechnical Quality Assurance, Bali Civil Infrastructure, Neurostruct Engineering.

1. Introduction

The execution of large-scale transport corridors, sub-base preparation for highways, and massive ground-improvement earthworks for luxury resort complexes across the Bali region represents a primary segment of contemporary tropical infrastructure development. The long-term structural stability of pavements, heavy retaining walls, and civil structures is directly bounded by the load-bearing capacity of the underlying subgrade soil matrix. To secure an unyielding sub-base, mechanical soil compaction operations are executed to decrease the void ratio, maximize the dry density profile, and increase the internal shear strength of the soil stratum.

However, the field validation of completed compaction passes within large-scale civil works presents complex engineering challenges. While automated non-destructive options like nuclear density gauges or light weight deflectometers are gaining traction, the destructive standard Sand Cone method remains the primary reference framework for compliance validation. In tropical environments, heterogeneous volcanic alluvial matrices exhibit high variation in native pore moisture profiles. If in-situ dry density calculations fail to accurately isolate volumetric and moisture variables, false-positive density readings will mask sub-surface compaction deficiencies. These underlying voids later manifest as pavement settlement, retaining wall tilting, and localized shallow foundation failures under dynamic load combinations. This paper establishes a mathematically comprehensive, ready-to-submit engineering calculation framework to guide field technicians and geotechnical engineers through standardized high-volume Sand Cone verification.

2. Geotechnical Framework and Mathematical Formulations

2.1 The Volumetric Sand Cone Apparatus Modeling

The standard Sand Cone test replaces a hand-excavated field test hole with a known volume of calibrated, clean, dry, free-flowing Ottawa sand. To isolate the exact in-situ volume ($V_h$) of the excavated test hole, the mathematical model must subtract the mass of sand that fills the funnel cone assembly from the total mass of sand discharged during the field test operation. The total net mass of sand required to fill the excavated field hole ($M_{\text{sand\_hole}}$) is formulated via the following mass-balance equation:

$$M_{\text{sand\_hole}} = \left( M_{\text{apparatus\_initial}} - M_{\text{apparatus\_final}} \right) - M_{\text{cone\_calibration}}$$

Where:

  • $M_{\text{apparatus\_initial}}$ = Initial mass of the sand cone cylinder filled with calibrated sand before valve activation ($\text{g}$)
  • $M_{\text{apparatus\_final}}$ = Final remaining mass of the apparatus shell after full hole and funnel cavity filling is completed ($\text{g}$)
  • $M_{\text{cone\_calibration}}$ = Standalone calibration mass of sand required exclusively to fill the conical funnel throat below the valve base ($\text{g}$)

Once $M_{\text{sand\_hole}}$ is determined, the actual net in-situ volume of the excavated test hole cavity ($V_h$) is calculated using the bulk density profile of the calibrated calibration sand:

$$V_h = \frac{M_{\text{sand\_hole}}}{\rho_{\text{sand\_bulk}}}$$

Where $\rho_{\text{sand\_bulk}}$ represents the bulk density of the calibrated sand, determined through laboratory calibration cylinders ($\text{g/cm}^3$).

2.2 In-Situ Dry Density and Relative Compaction Matrix Evaluation

The physical wet mass of the material excavated from the field test hole ($M_{\text{soil\_wet}}$) is divided by the excavated volume ($V_h$) to establish the in-situ wet density ($\rho_{\text{wet}}$):

$$\rho_{\text{wet}} = \frac{M_{\text{soil\_wet}}}{V_h}$$

To evaluate the true mechanical alignment of soil particles, the water moisture content ($w$, expressed as a percentage) must be extracted to compute the final in-situ dry density ($\rho_{\text{dry}}$):

$$\rho_{\text{dry}} = \frac{\rho_{\text{wet}}}{1 + \left( \frac{w}{100} \right)}$$

The ultimate validation parameter for civil engineering acceptance criteria is the Relative Compaction ($R_c$, expressed as a percentage), which mathematically scales the in-situ dry density against the Maximum Dry Density ($MDD$) derived via standard or modified laboratory Proctor compaction curves:

$$R_c = \left( \frac{\rho_{\text{dry}}}{MDD} \right) \times 100\%$$

[Excavate Soil & Weigh Mass (Mwet)] ➔ [Discharge Calibrated Sand via Valve] ➔ [Isolate Hole Volume (Vh) via Density Reduction] ➔ [Compute Wet In-Situ Density (ρwet)] ➔ [Extract Water Content (w)] ➔ [Calculate Dry Density (ρdry) vs Proctor MDD]

For large-scale structural foundations, civil design specifications reject any compaction zone scoring below $R_c < 95.0\%$, and require $R_c \ge 98.0\%_c$ for critical subgrade pavement layers.

4. Standardized Technical Application Protocol (Step-by-Step SOP)

Phase 1: Pre-Field Apparatus Calibration

Before deploying the testing apparatus to active heavy earthwork pads, laboratory calibration of the sand is mandatory. The bulk density ($\rho_{\text{sand\_bulk}}$) of the clean sand must be verified using a calibration cylinder of known volume. This must be repeated three times to compute a stable average value. Concurrently, the mass of sand required to fill the conical funnel throat ($M_{\text{cone\_calibration}}$) must be isolated by running the apparatus configuration over a flat, smooth clean glass plate, activating the valve until sand movement ceases, and logging the lost weight.

Phase 2: Field Site Selection and Excavation Control

  1. Surface Planar Cleansing: At the designated compaction pad location, clear away loose, uncompacted surface soil using a square trowel. Establish a perfectly flat, horizontal surface zone matching the footprint of the steel base plate.
  2. Base Plate Pinning: Position the center ring plate over the cleared zone. Drive steel pins firmly through the plate margins into the subgrade to prevent any horizontal slipping during the excavation phase.
  3. Excavation Mechanics: Dig a clean cylindrical hole downwards inside the center plate opening using specialized chisels and spoons. The minimum hole depth profile must reach $100\text{ mm}$ for fine-grained soils or $150\text{ mm}$ for coarse sub-base aggregates. Carefully extract all loose soil and place it inside an airtight container to prevent early moisture evaporation. The internal walls of the excavated hole must remain completely vertical, with smooth boundaries and no undercut pockets that could hide internal air voids.

Phase 3: Volumetric Sand Discharge and Field Moisture Isolation

  1. Apparatus Placement: Invert the fully charged sand cone cylinder assembly and seat the funnel rim exactly into the recessed perimeter of the pinned base metal plate.
  2. Valve Activation: Open the primary flow control valve. Allow the calibrated sand to discharge downwards via pure gravitational flow. The field operator must remain still during this phase; any walking around or machine vibration near the testing zone is strictly prohibited, as mechanical vibration increases sand packing density and skews the target volume reading.
  3. Valve Closure and Mass Tracking: Once the sand stream completely stops moving inside the clear plastic throat jar, close the valve carefully. Lift the cylinder and weigh the remaining apparatus mass to isolate $M_{\text{apparatus\_final}}$.
  4. Moisture Extraction: Extract a minimum $500 \, \text{g}$ representative sub-sample from the recovered wet soil container. Place the soil into a shallow metal pan and dry it thoroughly using a portable field gas burner or laboratory drying oven at $110^\circ\text{C} \pm 5^\circ\text{C}$ to evaporate all free pore water molecules. Weigh the dried soil to establish the moisture content percentage ($w$).

[Laboratory Sand Bulk Density Check] ➔ [Scrape Soil Surface Smooth] ➔ [Pin Metal Base Ring] ➔ [Chisel Vertical Hole 150mm Depth] ➔ [Seat Inverted Cylinder & Open Valve] ➔ [Close Valve & Recover Remaining Sand] ➔ [Dry Wet Soil via Field Burner] ➔ [Compute Relative Compaction Rc]

5. Field Quality Control and Geotechnical Validation Testing

5.1 Dynamic Random Grid Selection Profiling

To provide statistical compliance verification across expansive civil earthwork operations, site testing locations must not be hand-picked by project supervisors. Testing layout coordinates must be generated using an unbiased random grid selection protocol based on total surface area squares. For every $500 \, \text{m}^2$ of active compacted sub-base layer lift, a minimum of one multi-point Sand Cone array must be executed to map relative compaction uniformity.

5.2 Forensic Rectification of Relative Compaction Failures

If the calculated Relative Compaction index scores below compliance parameters ($R_c < 95.0\%$), the designated lift layer fails validation. The geotechnical inspector must flag the coordinate zone for corrective rectification. The applicator must re-adjust the moisture profile of the soil stratum using tractor disk harrows (adding water if the subgrade is dry, or aerating to dry the matrix if it exceeds Optimum Moisture Content limits). Once moisture equilibrium is reached, the layer must receive additional compaction passes using heavy vibratory smooth-wheel or sheep-foot rollers. Re-testing via a new adjacent Sand Cone array is mandatory before subsequent soil lifts can be approved.

2. Landasan Teori dan Perhitungan Geoteknik Mekanika Tanah

2.1 Pemodelan Volumetrik Alat Tabung Sand Cone

Prinsip dasar pengujian Sand Cone adalah menggantikan rongga lubang tanah hasil galian di lapangan dengan material pasir standar (pasir Ottawa) yang telah diketahui berat jenisnya secara pasti. Untuk mengukur volume bersih dari lubang galian tanah ($V_h$), rumus matematika geoteknik wajib memisahkan massa pasir yang mengisi corong kerucut alat dari total massa pasir yang keluar selama pengujian. Massa bersih pasir penentu volume lubang ($M_{\text{pasir\_lubang}}$) dihitung menggunakan persamaan kesetimbangan massa berikut:

$$M_{\text{pasir\_lubang}} = \left( M_{\text{alat\_awal}} - M_{\text{alat\_akhir}} \right) - M_{\text{kerucut\_kalibrasi}}$$

Dimana:

  • $M_{\text{alat\_awal}}$ = Massa total tabung sand cone beserta pasir standar sebelum katup keran dibuka di lapangan ($\text{g}$)
  • $M_{\text{alat\_akhir}}$ = Massa sisa tabung beserta pasir setelah lubang galian dan corong kerucut terisi penuh ($\text{g}$)
  • $M_{\text{kerucut\_kalibrasi}}$ = Nilai konstanta massa pasir yang hanya mengisi corong kerucut bagian bawah di bawah batas katup ($\text{g}$)

Setelah nilai $M_{\text{pasir\_lubang}}$ didapatkan, nilai volume bersih dari lubang galian tanah di lapangan ($V_h$) dihitung berdasarkan rasio berat jenis kering dari pasir standar yang digunakan:

$$V_h = \frac{M_{\text{pasir\_lubang}}}{\rho_{\text{pasir\_bulk}}}$$

Dimana $\rho_{\text{pasir\_bulk}}$ adalah nilai berat jenis padat kering pasir standar yang didapatkan dari hasil pengujian kalibrasi laboratorium sebelumnya ($\text{g/cm}^3$).

2.2 Perhitungan Berat Isi Kering dan Derajat Kepadatan Lapangan

Massa basah tanah asli hasil galian lubang ($M_{\text{tanah\_basah}}$) dibagi dengan volume lubang ($V_h$) untuk mendapatkan nilai berat isi basah tanah lapangan ($\rho_{\text{basah}}$):

$$\rho_{\text{basah}} = \frac{M_{\text{tanah\_basah}}}{V_h}$$

Untuk mengetahui kerapatan partikel padat tanah secara murni, kadar air internal ($w$, ditulis dalam satuan persen) wajib dikeluarkan dari perhitungan guna mendapatkan nilai berat isi kering lapangan (In-Situ Dry Density) ($\rho_{\text{kering}}$):

$$\rho_{\text{kering}} = \frac{\rho_{\text{basah}}}{1 + \left( \frac{w}{100} \right)}$$

Indikator penentu kelulusan utama dalam rekayasa sipil adalah Derajat Kepadatan atau Kepadatan Relatif ($R_c$, ditulis dalam persen), yang membandingkan nilai berat isi kering lapangan dengan batas Kepadatan Kering Maksimum (Maximum Dry Density / MDD) yang didapatkan dari kurva grafik uji Proctor laboratorium:

$$R_c = \left( \frac{\rho_{\text{kering}}}{MDD} \right) \times 100\%$$

Berdasarkan spesifikasi standar nasional, area struktur pondasi utama menolak keras hasil kepadatan di bawah batas kritis $R_c < 95.0\%$, dan mewajibkan angka ketat $R_c \ge 98.0\%$ untuk lapisan tanah dasar perkerasan jalan utama (subgrade).

4. Spesifikasi Prosedur Pelaksanaan di Lapangan (SOP Mandatori)

Langkah 1: Kalibrasi Alat dan Pasir di Laboratorium

Sebelum tim geoteknik berangkat ke lokasi proyek, kalibrasi berat jenis pasir standar wajib diselesaikan di laboratorium. Ukur nilai berat jenis padat kering pasir ($\rho_{\text{pasir\_bulk}}$) menggunakan bejana silinder yang telah diketahui volumenya secara pasti, ulangi sebanyak tiga kali untuk mendapatkan nilai rata-rata yang stabil. Selanjutnya, tentukan berat pasir penyusun kerucut corong ($M_{\text{kerucut\_kalibrasi}}$) dengan cara membalikkan alat di atas kaca datar, buka katup keran hingga pasir berhenti mengalir, lalu timbang massa pasir yang keluar memenuhi corong tersebut.

Langkah 2: Penentuan Titik dan Pemahatan Lubang Galian di Lapangan

  1. Perataan Muka Tanah: Pada titik pemadatan tanah yang diuji, bersihkan sisa makadam kasar atau tanah gembur permukaan menggunakan sendok semen. Buat bidang datar horizontal yang memiliki ukuran luas minimal setara dengan plat besi dudukan sand cone.
  2. Penguncian Plat Dudukan: Letakkan plat besi ring tepat di tengah area rata. Pasang paku angkur besi di setiap sisi sudut plat, lalu pukul masuk ke dalam tanah agar plat tidak bergeser atau bergetar selama proses pemahatan lubang berjalan.
  3. Teknis Pemahatan Lubang: Pahat tanah ke arah bawah secara vertikal mengikuti diameter lingkaran ring besi menggunakan tatah tajam dan sendok galian. Kedalaman lubang minimal wajib mencapai $100\text{ mm}$ untuk tanah berbutir halus, atau minimal $150\text{ mm}$ jika menguji material lapis pondasi agregat. Masukkan seluruh tanah hasil pahatan ke dalam plastik kantong tertutup rapat agar kadar air aslinya tidak menguap terkena angin. Dinding lubang wajib dipastikan tegak lurus tanpa ada bagian bawah yang berongga (undercut).

Langkah 3: Pengaliran Pasir Standar dan Pengukuran Kadar Air Lapangan

  1. Pemasangan Tabung Alat: Balikkan tabung berisi pasir standar yang telah ditimbang berat awalnya ($M_{\text{alat\_awal}}$). Dudukkan corong kerucut tepat di atas ring plat besi yang terpaku di lapangan.
  2. Pembukaan Katup Kran: Buka katup aliran keran secara penuh, biarkan pasir standar mengalir turun mengisi lubang galian murni akibat gaya gravitasi. Selama pasir mengalir, seluruh pekerja di sekitar dilarang keras berjalan kaki atau menyalakan mesin alat berat, karena getaran mekanis sekecil apa pun akan memadatkan susunan pasir standar dan mengacaukan kalkulasi volume lubang (membuat volume terbaca lebih besar dari aslinya).
  3. Penutupan Katup dan Penimbangan Sisa: Setelah pergerakan pasir di dalam botol tabung plastik berhenti total, tutup katup keran dengan hati-hati. Angkat tabung, lalu timbang berat sisa alat beserta pasir yang tersisa untuk mendapatkan data $M_{\text{alat\_akhir}}$.
  4. Pengeringan Sampel Tanah: Ambil sampel tanah basah hasil galian minimal sebanyak $500 \, \text{g}$, masukkan ke dalam cawan besi, lalu bakar di atas kompor gas portabel lapangan hingga seluruh molekul air pori menguap total (atau masukkan ke dalam oven pengering laboratorium suhu $110^\circ\text{C} \pm 5^\circ\text{C}$). Timbang berat tanah kering untuk menghitung persentase kadar air murni ($w$).

5. Metode Validasi Hasil dan Penjaminan Kualitas (Quality Control Lapangan)

5.1 Pemetaan Grid Acak Dinamis (Random Grid Profiling)

Guna menjamin validitas hasil uji pemadatan tanah pada proyek jalan raya bentang panjang atau reklamasi tapak bangunan luas, penentuan lokasi titik uji tidak boleh dipilih secara manual oleh pengawas kontraktor. Titik koordinat pengujian wajib ditentukan menggunakan sistem penentuan acak berbasis grid luasan tanah (Random Grid Selection). Untuk setiap luasan $500 \, \text{m}^2$ hamparan tanah yang telah dipadatkan, minimal wajib diambil satu titik pengujian Sand Cone secara acak untuk memetakan keseragaman nilai kepadatan subgrade.

5.2 Tata Cara Perbaikan Kegagalan Derajat Kepadatan Tanah

Jika hasil perhitungan akhir mencatat nilai Derajat Kepadatan berada di bawah batas minimum yang disyaratkan spesifikasi teknis ($R_c < 95.0\%$), maka hamparan tanah layer tersebut dinyatakan GAGAL dan ditolak oleh konsultan. Kontraktor wajib melakukan perbaikan total: lakukan penggaruan lapisan tanah menggunakan traktor disk harrow untuk memperbaiki kadar airnya (semprot air jika terlalu kering, atau jemur/angin-anginkan jika kadar air melebihi batas Optimum Moisture Content / OMC). Setelah kadar air stabil mendekati nilai OMC laboratorium, lakukan pemadatan ulang menggunakan Vibratory Roller tonase berat secara disiplin. Pengujian Sand Cone Test ulang wajib dilakukan di titik baru yang berdekatan sebelum izin penghamparan layer tanah di atasnya dapat dikeluarkan.

7. Kesimpulan

Mencegah terjadinya kegagalan pondasi bangunan dan menjamin kekuatan jangka panjang tanah dasar (subgrade) pada kondisi tropis pesisir Bali menuntut penerapan uji Sand Cone yang disiplin dan berbasis hitungan ilmiah. Analisis rekayasa membuktikan bahwa perkiraan volume lubang secara manual atau asal-asalan selalu gagal mengantisipasi pengaruh nyata dari porositas tanah alami, variabel geometri corong alat, serta fluktuasi kadar air lapangan. Melalui penerapan aturan baku geoteknik—yang mewajibkan kalibrasi berat jenis pasir standar ($\rho_{\text{pasir\_bulk}}$), isolasi massa corong kerucut ($M_{\text{kerucut\_kalibrasi}}$), serta penentuan koordinat titik uji berbasis grid acak—jalur penjaminan mutu dapat dipertanggungjawabkan secara hukum konstruksi. Kedisiplinan proses pengeringan sampel tanah lapangan yang divalidasi terhadap grafik Proctor Maksimum Dry Density laboratorium menjamin tercapainya target Derajat Kepadatan rencana ($R_c \ge 95\% - 98\%$) secara merata di seluruh area proyek, mencegah bahaya amblasnya tanah, serta mengunci durabilitas infrastruktur properti Anda hingga puluhan tahun ke depan.
