### 1. Introduction

Technical investigations by qualified professional engineers form the foundation of fair and efficient construction dispute resolution. Unlike subjective claims, these investigations deliver objective, science-based findings that clarify liability, quantify losses, and support amicable settlements.

In Bali’s booming construction sector, where villas, resorts, hotels, and infrastructure projects proliferate, disputes frequently arise from design variances, construction defects, unforeseen ground conditions, and schedule delays. This paper details the methodologies, processes, and value of professional technical investigation services.

### 2. Literature Review

Forensic engineering and technical investigation in construction have been extensively studied in high-impact journals. Recent bibliometric analyses on Scopus highlight the growing application of systematic investigation protocols in claim management and dispute resolution.

Key references address delay analysis techniques (Time Impact Analysis, Windows Analysis), defect investigation using non-destructive testing (NDT), and standards for expert reports. Research published in *Journal of Legal Affairs and Dispute Resolution in Engineering and Construction*, *Forensic Engineering*, and *Buildings* consistently shows that robust technical investigations significantly improve settlement rates and reduce resolution costs.

### 4. Core Components of Technical Investigation Services

4.1 Delay and Schedule Investigation

Detailed forensic schedule analysis using Critical Path Method (CPM).

Equation 1 (Clean copy-paste for Word):

Project Delay (days) = Actual Finish Date − Planned Finish Date

Concurrent Delay = Overlapping Period of Contractor and Employer Critical Delays

Entitlement = (Excusable & Compensable Days / Total Delay Days) × Claimed Amount

4.2 Defect and Failure Investigation

Systematic inspection involving visual assessment, non-destructive testing (ultrasonic, rebound hammer, GPR), laboratory testing, and structural modeling to identify root causes.

4.3 Geotechnical and Site Condition Investigation

Particularly relevant in Bali due to volcanic soil and seismic activity. Investigation includes soil testing, foundation performance analysis, and compliance verification.

4.4 Variation and Quantum Investigation

Validation of changed conditions and accurate cost quantification based on contemporaneous records.
