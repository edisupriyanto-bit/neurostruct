2. Literature Review

Material estimation for mortar and thin-layer finishes follows the fundamental principle of converting wet volume to dry volume (typically multiplying by 1.25–1.33 to account for voids and bulking). Common mix ratios for finishing coats range from 1:4 to 1:6 (cement:sand) by volume, with cement-lime-sand mixes (e.g., 1:0.5:5) preferred for better workability and reduced shrinkage in tropical climates.

Studies indicate that for a 3 mm thick acian layer, cement consumption typically ranges from 4–7 kg/m² depending on the mix ratio and wastage factor. International guidelines recommend adding 5–10% wastage for manual application. In hot-humid conditions, richer mixes increase shrinkage risk, while optimized lean mixes with lime or admixtures improve efficiency and durability.

3. Fundamental Calculation Methodology

# 3.1 Wet Volume Calculation

Wet volume = Wall Area (m²) × Thickness (m)

# 3.2 Dry Volume Conversion

Dry volume = Wet volume × 1.25 to 1.33 (commonly 1.27 for finishing mortar)

# 3.3 Material Proportions

For a given mix ratio (e.g., 1 : 5 cement : sand):

- Volume of cement = Dry volume × (1 / Total parts)

- Volume of sand = Dry volume × (5 / Total parts)

Cement quantity (kg) = Volume of cement (m³) × 1440 kg/m³ (density of cement)

Copy-pasteable Equation (Dry Volume):

\[ V_{dry} = V_{wet} \times 1.27 \]

Copy-pasteable Equation (Cement Quantity):

\[ m_{cement} = \left( \frac{V_{dry} \times P_c}{P_{total}} \right) \times 1440 \]

(where \( P_c \) = parts of cement, \( P_{total} \) = total parts in mix ratio)

4. Recommended Mix Designs for Acian in Tropical Climates

- Interior Walls: 1 cement : 0.5 lime : 5–6 sand, thickness 2–3 mm → approx. 4.5–6.0 kg cement/m²
