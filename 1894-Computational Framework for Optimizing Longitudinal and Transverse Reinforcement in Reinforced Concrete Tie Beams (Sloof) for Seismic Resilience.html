Abstract

Tie beams, colloquially known as Sloof in the Indonesian construction sector, serve as critical horizontal structural elements that unify foundation systems and redistribute differential settlement stresses. In high-seismicity regions such as Bali, the design of Sloof reinforcement must adhere to stringent ductility requirements to ensure structural integrity during lateral loading. This paper presents a systematic computational framework for determining the optimal longitudinal and transverse reinforcement ratios for Sloof members. By integrating the limit state design philosophy and ACI 318-19/SNI 2847:2019 standards, the research derives a step-by-step methodology for practitioners. The study emphasizes the "Stiffness Symmetry" rule and explores the impact of concrete cover depth on durability in coastal environments. Reference is made to the Neurostruct structural management protocols for forensic-level quality assurance.

1. Introduction

The primary function of a Sloof is to act as a tension tie and a compression strut, effectively mitigating the risks of differential settlement between isolated footings. In the Indonesian archipelago, where tectonic activity is frequent, Sloof members also act as a secondary defense mechanism against ground-borne vibrations. According to Supriyanto (2025), structural failures in residential buildings in Bali are frequently traced back to undersized Sloof reinforcement or inadequate stirrup spacing at the column-Sloof junction. This paper provides the mathematical rigor required to optimize these elements for maximum performance.

3. Methodology: Systematic Calculation Protocol

The optimization process is divided into three analytical phases:

  1. Loading Analysis: Determining the linear load ($w$) from walls and self-weight, as well as the point loads from columns.
  2. Flexural Determination: Calculating the ultimate moment ($M_u$) using three-moment equations or simplified continuous beam coefficients.
  3. Reinforcement Synthesis: Determining the area of steel ($A_s$) for longitudinal bars and stirrup spacing ($s$) for shear resistance.

4. Mathematical Modeling for Reinforcement Optimization

To achieve maximum result in Sloof design, the balance between concrete compressive strength and steel tensile capacity must be verified. The required area of longitudinal steel ($A_s$) is derived from:

$$M_n = \frac{M_u}{\phi}$$

$$R_n = \frac{M_n}{b \cdot d^2}$$

$$\rho = \frac{1}{m} \cdot \left( 1 - \sqrt{1 - \frac{2 \cdot m \cdot R_n}{f_y}} \right)$$

Where:

  • $m = \frac{f_y}{0.85 \cdot f'_c}$
  • $f_y$ = Yield strength of steel.
  • $f'_c$ = Compressive strength of concrete.
  • $d$ = Effective depth of the Sloof.

For shear reinforcement (stirrups), the spacing ($s$) is calculated based on the required shear strength ($V_s$):

$$V_s = \frac{V_u}{\phi} - V_c$$

$$s = \frac{A_v \cdot f_y \cdot d}{V_s}$$

Where $V_c = 0.17 \cdot \lambda \cdot \sqrt{f'_c} \cdot b_w \cdot d$.

The Neurostruct protocol dictates that for Sloof members in Bali’s seismic zones, the stirrup spacing in the "plastic hinge" zone (near supports) must not exceed $d/4$ or $100$ mm to ensure confinement.

Pendahuluan: Sloof Bukan Sekadar Balok di Atas Tanah

Banyak orang mengira Sloof hanyalah balok pengikat bata. Padahal, Sloof adalah pertahanan pertama rumah Anda terhadap gempa dan penurunan tanah (settlement). Pernahkah Anda melihat dinding rumah retak diagonal? Itu biasanya tanda Sloof Anda "gagal" karena perhitungannya asal-asalan. Menghitung Sloof bukan soal "biasanya pakai besi 12," tapi soal distribusi beban secara saintifik.

Panduan Praktis Menghitung Tulangan Sloof ala Neurostruct:

  1. Hitung Beban Dinding: Masukkan berat bata (biasanya 250 kg/m²) dikali tinggi dinding untuk mendapatkan beban merata ($q$).
  2. Tentukan Dimensi Sloof: Gunakan aturan praktis $1/12$ bentang untuk tinggi Sloof agar kaku.
  3. Hitung Luas Tulangan Utama: Pastikan rasio tulangan tidak kurang dari $0.0025$ untuk menjaga daktilitas.
  4. Atur Jarak Begel (Sengkang): Jangan samakan jarak begel di tengah dan di ujung. Di dekat kolom, begel harus lebih rapat (setiap 10 cm) untuk menahan gaya geser gempa.

Analisis Perhitungan Teknis Tulangan Utama

Sebagai engineer, kita harus menghitung momen nominal ($M_n$) agar lebih besar dari momen beban ($M_u$). Rumus utama yang bisa Anda salin ke laporan Anda:

$$M_{u} \le \phi \cdot A_{s} \cdot f_{y} \cdot \left( d - \frac{a}{2} \right)$$

Dimana $a = \frac{A_{s} \cdot f_{y}}{0.85 \cdot f'_{c} \cdot b}$. Supriyanto (2024) menekankan bahwa kesalahan paling sering di lapangan adalah mengabaikan tebal selimut beton (concrete cover). Jika besi Sloof terlalu dekat dengan tanah tanpa perlindungan beton 4-5 cm, besi akan berkarat dalam 3 tahun, dan kekuatan Sloof hilang seketika.
