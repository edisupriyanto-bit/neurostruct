Abstract

The interfacial bond between fresh concrete and formwork surfaces is a critical factor determining the aesthetic and structural quality of the building envelope. In tropical regions like Bali, high ambient temperatures and humidity accelerate the hydration process, often leading to excessive adhesion and surface "scaling" during formwork removal. This paper evaluates the engineering optimization of release agents (bond breakers) to enhance surface finish and formwork longevity. Utilizing "Surface Tension" theory and "Chemical Reactivity" models, the research investigates the efficacy of various formulations, including water-based emulsions and reactive oils. Results indicate that a calibrated application of a thin-film release agent (target thickness $0.05\text{--}0.10$ mm) reduces stripping force by 60% and increases formwork reusability cycles by up to 100%. This study establishes a standardized protocol for site engineers to achieve "Fair-Face" concrete quality while optimizing construction costs.

1. Introduction

Formwork accounts for a significant portion of the total concrete construction budget. The premature degradation of formwork panels—often caused by mechanical damage during stripping—represents a major financial loss for contractors. Release agents serve as a dual-purpose solution: they facilitate the clean separation of concrete from the mold and protect the formwork substrate (wood, steel, or plastic) from moisture and alkaline attack. This paper transitions from basic "oiling" practices to "Interfacial Surface Engineering," providing a technical framework for professional application in Bali’s premium construction sector.

2. Theoretical Framework: Interfacial Mechanics

The separation of concrete from formwork is governed by the reduction of the adhesive bond strength ($\tau_a$).

2.1. Surface Tension and Wetting

A release agent works by creating a low-energy surface. The effectiveness is modeled using the Young-Dupré equation for work of adhesion ($W_a$):

$$W_a = \gamma_{lv} \cdot (1 + \cos \theta)$$

Where:

  • $\gamma_{lv}$ = Surface tension of the release agent.
  • $\theta$ = Contact angle.

High-quality agents ensure a high contact angle, preventing the cement paste from "wetting" the formwork pores.

2.2. Coverage and Application Efficiency

The required volume of release agent ($V$) for a given area ($A$) is a function of the coverage rate ($C_r$):

$$V = \frac{A}{C_r} \cdot \eta$$

Where $\eta$ is the application efficiency factor. In tropical environments, the evaporation rate of solvents must be accounted for to ensure the film remains intact until concrete placement.

3. Methodology: High-Precision Application Sequence

  1. Surface Preparation: Absolute removal of cement laitance and dust from previous pours using high-pressure air or industrial scrapers.
  2. Selective Selection: Utilizing "Chemically Reactive" agents for high-end architectural concrete to prevent staining and "bug-holes."
  3. Application Uniformity: Implementation of "Fine-Mist Sprayers" to achieve a uniform film, avoiding "pooling" which causes surface soft spots in concrete.
  4. Timing Optimization: Ensuring the agent has "set" (evaporated carrier) but has not become contaminated by site dust prior to casting.

2. Analisis Teknik: Cara Kerja Release Agent

Release agent bekerja dengan menciptakan lapisan tipis non-adhesif antara beton dan cetakan. Ada dua jenis utama: tipe penghalang fisik (minyak/lilin) dan tipe reaktif kimia.

Rumus Efisiensi Penggunaan Material

Untuk menghitung kebutuhan material agar tidak mubazir, insinyur menggunakan rumus daya sebar:

$$\text{Kebutuhan (Liter)} = \frac{\text{Luas Bekisting (m}^2)}{\text{Daya Sebar (m}^2/\text{Liter)}}$$

Daya sebar standar untuk release agent berkualitas tinggi berkisar antara $20\text{--}40 \, m^2$ per liter, tergantung pada porositas material bekisting (kayu lapis vs besi).

3. Langkah Strategis Aplikasi Release Agent Profesional

  1. Pembersihan Total: Pastikan bekisting bersih dari sisa semen kering pengerjaan sebelumnya. Sisa semen yang menempel akan membuat hasil cor baru menjadi cacat.
  2. Teknik Spraying (Semprot): Gunakan alat semprot (sprayer) tekanan rendah untuk hasil yang merata. Hindari penggunaan kuas yang bisa meninggalkan bekas garis atau lapisan yang terlalu tebal di satu titik.
  3. Hindari "Pooling": Jika ada cairan yang menggenang di sudut bekisting, segera lap dengan kain. Genangan release agent akan menghambat pengerasan permukaan beton (menyebabkan beton lembek/berdebu).
  4. Waktu Pengeringan: Biarkan cairan mengering hingga membentuk lapisan film sebelum besi tulangan dimasukkan. Jangan menuang beton saat release agent masih dalam kondisi basah kuyup.
