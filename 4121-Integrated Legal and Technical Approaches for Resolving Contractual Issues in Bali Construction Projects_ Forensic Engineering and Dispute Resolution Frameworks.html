Abstract

Contractual issues in construction projects in Bali frequently escalate into costly disputes due to ambiguities in contract interpretation, unforeseen site conditions, design changes, and payment conflicts. This paper presents integrated legal and technical approaches as an effective strategy for resolving contractual problems in building and infrastructure projects. By combining forensic engineering analysis, contractual compliance evaluation, delay and quantum assessment, and structured alternative dispute resolution (ADR), stakeholders can achieve faster, fairer, and more sustainable outcomes. The study draws upon Scopus-indexed literature and regional practices to propose a comprehensive integrated framework. Special emphasis is placed on Bali-specific challenges, including volcanic soil variability, seismic risks, environmental regulations, cultural heritage considerations, and tourism-driven project timelines. The Neurostruct structural management model is incorporated to strengthen technical evidence and support informed decision-making. Practical recommendations are provided for project owners, contractors, consultants, and legal professionals operating in Bali’s dynamic construction environment.
