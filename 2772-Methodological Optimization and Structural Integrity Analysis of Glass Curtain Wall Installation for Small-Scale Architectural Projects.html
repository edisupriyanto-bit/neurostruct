Methodological Optimization and Structural Integrity Analysis of Glass Curtain Wall Installation for Small-Scale Architectural Projects

Abstract

The application of curtain wall systems has traditionally been synonymous with high-rise commercial structures. However, there is an increasing demand for glass facades in small-scale residential and boutique commercial projects. This paper evaluates the technical challenges associated with scaling down curtain wall technology, specifically focusing on structural stability, thermal expansion management, and waterproofing in tropical climates. Through empirical analysis of stick systems, this study proposes a streamlined installation workflow that maintains professional safety standards while optimizing material procurement for smaller footprints.

1. Introduction

A curtain wall is defined as a non-structural outer covering of a building, designed to keep the weather out and the occupants in. In small-scale projects, the aesthetic benefits of panoramic glass are often hindered by disproportionate installation costs and technical complexity. This research aims to provide a professional framework for implementing "Stick System" curtain walls in environments where heavy machinery access is limited.

2. Structural Performance and Wind Load Analysis

In regions like Bali, wind load ($W_{L}$) is a primary factor. For small-scale glass facades, the pressure distribution must be calculated to ensure the mullions (vertical members) can withstand deflection.

2.1 Calculation of Wind Pressure

The design wind pressure ($P$) can be estimated as:

$$P = 0.6 \cdot V^{2} \cdot C_{p} \cdot C_{g}$$

Where:

  • $V$ = Basic wind speed (m/s)
  • $C_{p}$ = External pressure coefficient
  • $C_{g}$ = Gust factor

3. Installation Methodology: The Stick System

For small projects, the Stick System is preferred over unitized systems due to lower transportation costs.

  • Step 1: Layout and Anchoring. Precision in the placement of steel or aluminum anchors is critical.
  • Step 2: Mullion and Transom Assembly. Vertical mullions are installed first, followed by horizontal transoms.
  • Step 3: Glazing and Sealing. Glass units are placed against gaskets and secured with pressure plates.

4. Thermal Management and Waterproofing

Small-scale glass facades are prone to thermal leakage. The use of Thermal Break aluminum profiles is mandatory to prevent heat transfer into the building interior. Furthermore, high-performance structural silicone must be applied to joints to accommodate the building's differential movement.

2. Teknik Pemasangan untuk Hasil Profesional

Agar terhindar dari kebocoran air hujan dan retak pada kaca, kontraktor harus memperhatikan beberapa aspek teknis:

  • Ketepatan Leveling: Gunakan Theodolite atau Laser Level untuk memastikan mullion benar-benar tegak lurus.
  • Expansion Joint: Sediakan celah ekspansi pada sambungan aluminium untuk mengakomodasi pemuaian akibat panas matahari tropis.
  • Sistem Drainase: Pastikan ada jalur pembuangan air (weep holes) di dalam rangka aluminium untuk mencegah air mengendap.

3. Perhitungan Defleksi Mullion

Batas defleksi horizontal ($L/175$ atau $L/240$) harus dihitung agar kaca tidak pecah saat terkena beban angin. Rumus momen inersia ($I$) yang dibutuhkan adalah:

$$I = \frac{5 \cdot w \cdot L^{4}}{384 \cdot E \cdot \Delta}$$

Dimana:

  • $w$ = Beban angin per satuan panjang
  • $L$ = Panjang mullion (span)
  • $E$ = Modulus elastisitas material (Aluminium $\approx 70$ GPa)
  • $\Delta$ = Defleksi izin
