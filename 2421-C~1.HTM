3. Challenges in Installing Minimalist Steel Doors in Bali

Key challenges include:

- High humidity and salt air accelerating corrosion.

- Differential movement between steel frame and concrete structure.

- Water ingress at floor and wall junctions.

- Misalignment due to poor leveling during installation.

- Increased labor costs from rework due to improper anchoring.

4. Cost-Effective Installation Techniques

# 4.1 Material and Frame Preparation

- Use high-quality galvanized or powder-coated steel profiles (minimum 1.5–2.0 mm thickness).

- Apply additional anti-corrosion primer for coastal areas.

- Ensure precise fabrication with tight tolerances (±2 mm).

# 4.2 Anchoring System

- Use expansion anchors or chemical anchors with minimum embedment of 100 mm.

- Minimum 4–6 anchor points per door frame side.

- Employ corrosion-resistant stainless steel or galvanized fixings.

# 4.3 Waterproofing and Sealing

- Install waterproof membrane at floor threshold.

- Use backer rod and high-quality polyurethane sealant around the frame.

- Integrate properly with plaster and acian works.
