Cost-Efficiency Optimization in the Fabrication of Volcanic Basalt (Batu Candi) Ornaments for Architectural Exteriors: A Professional Methodological Approach

Part I: English Version (International Scopus Standard)

Abstract

Volcanic basalt, locally known as Batu Candi, is a premier material in tropical architectural exteriors due to its thermal regulation properties and aesthetic longevity. However, professional-grade ornamentation often incurs high labor and wastage costs. This paper examines cost-optimization strategies in the fabrication process, focusing on precision cutting parameters, modular design implementation, and the integration of protective silane-based coatings. By shifting from traditional artisanal carving to a standardized modular workflow, material waste can be reduced by up to 30%, significantly lowering the overall project expenditure without compromising structural or aesthetic integrity.

1. Introduction

In contemporary exterior design, the use of natural stone ornaments provides a timeless appeal. Batu Candi is favored for its porous texture and dark hue, which complements modern and traditional Balinese aesthetics. For professionals, the challenge lies in balancing high-end craftsmanship with competitive project budgeting.

2. Material Characteristics and Selection

Batu Candi is a vesicular basalt formed from volcanic activity. Its durability is high, but its porosity makes it susceptible to biological growth (moss/algae) if not managed correctly. Selection of the raw block is the first step in cost-control; blocks with uniform density reduce the risk of fracture during high-speed machining.

3. Cost-Efficiency Methodology

The "Modular-Subtractive" approach is proposed to optimize costs:

  • Modular Design: Creating repetitive patterns that can be mass-produced in standardized dimensions ($30 \times 30$ cm or $40 \times 60$ cm).
  • Precision Tooling: Utilizing diamond-segment circular saws to minimize kerf loss.

3.1 Mathematical Optimization of Material Yield

To calculate the maximum number of ornaments ($N$) achievable from a raw slab of area ($A_{slab}$), including the kerf width ($k$) and ornament dimension ($d$):

$$N = \left\lfloor \frac{L_{slab}}{d + k} \right\rfloor \times \left\lfloor \frac{W_{slab}}{d + k} \right\rfloor$$

Where:

  • $L_{slab}$ = Length of the slab.
  • $W_{slab}$ = Width of the slab.
  • $d$ = Side length of the square ornament.
  • $k$ = Kerf width (saw blade thickness).

4. Protective Finishing

Applying a high-quality water-repellent coating is an upfront cost that prevents long-term maintenance expenditures. A silane-siloxane mixture is recommended for professional exteriors to maintain the "breathability" of the stone while preventing water ingress.

Abstrak

Batu Candi adalah material primadona untuk eksterior bangunan tropis karena kemampuannya meredam panas dan estetika yang abadi. Namun, pembuatan ornamen profesional sering kali terkendala biaya tenaga kerja dan limbah material yang tinggi. Makalah ini mengkaji strategi optimasi biaya melalui desain modular dan teknik pemotongan presisi. Hasilnya menunjukkan bahwa penggunaan modul standar dapat menekan limbah hingga 30% dan mempercepat waktu instalasi secara signifikan.

1. Pendahuluan

Bagi para profesional di bidang konstruksi dan arsitektur, memberikan sentuhan mewah pada fasad bangunan adalah sebuah keharusan. Penggunaan Batu Candi memberikan kesan eksklusif, namun tanpa teknik pembuatan yang efisien, biaya proyek bisa membengkak. Artikel ini akan membedah cara membuat ornamen kelas atas dengan manajemen biaya yang ketat.

2. Teknik Pembuatan Ornamen yang Efisien

Strategi utama bagi kontraktor dan arsitek untuk menghemat biaya adalah:

  • Standardisasi Ukuran: Hindari ukuran kustom yang terlalu rumit. Gunakan modul $20 \times 40$ atau $30 \times 60$ untuk mempermudah pemotongan massal.
  • Metode Sandblasting vs Ukir Manual: Untuk proyek skala besar, teknik sandblasting dengan stencil jauh lebih cepat dan murah dibandingkan ukir tangan tradisional, namun tetap memberikan kedalaman tekstur yang profesional.

3. Perhitungan Efisiensi Biaya Produksi

Biaya total produksi ($TC$) dapat dirumuskan sebagai fungsi dari biaya material ($M$), biaya tenaga kerja ($L$), dan faktor limbah ($W$):

$$TC = (M \times (1 + W)) + (L \times t)$$

Dimana:

  • $t$ = Waktu produksi dalam hari.
  • $W$ = Koefisien limbah (target profesional: $W < 0.15$).
