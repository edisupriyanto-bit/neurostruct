Abstract

The design planning of micro-scale urban drainage systems within high-density residential developments, boutique commercial layouts, and private villa footprints represents a critical component of risk management in sustainable civil engineering. In tropical island environments such as Bali, short-duration high-intensity precipitation events, combined with low soil percolation rates induced by high urban land-cover conversion, trigger rapid peak discharge concentrations. Improper design sizing of open channels, sub-surface conduits, and localized attenuation structures leads to flash flooding, soil saturation-induced foundation settlement, and accelerated pavement degradation. This paper presents a mathematically comprehensive hydro-geomorphological modeling framework and structural design template tailored for small-scale catchments. By integrating rain fluid-flux parameters via the Rational hydrological method, computing uniform open-channel hydraulic flows through Manning’s boundary formulations, and evaluating localized subterranean infiltration configurations, we establish a standardized engineering design blueprint. Field diagnostic validation frameworks, featuring non-destructive soil infiltration testing and cross-sectional laser-profiling metrics, are delineated alongside computational civil alignment parameters to secure an operational infrastructure service lifespan exceeding 25 years.

The execution of localized low-rise civil structures, luxury eco-resorts, and high-density residential villas across the Bali region represents a primary growth driver in contemporary tropical architecture and engineering. However, the operational sustainability and environmental integrity of these small-scale developments are completely bounded by the design engineering performance of their surface water management networks. While immense structural re-engineering focus is conventionally allocated to superstructure framing concrete and deep structural foundations, the micro-scale civil infrastructure that handles rainfall runoff is frequently oversimplified or mismanaged by general builders.

From a building forensics and hydraulic engineering perspective, a failure in micro-scale drainage planning transitions from a localized maintenance nuisance into a structural hazard. In tropical marine zones, heavy monsoon seasons generate high precipitation volumes over brief timelines. When natural porous topsoil is replaced by impervious surfaces—such as concrete pavement grids, building footprints, and asphalt approaches—the natural infiltration capacity drops to zero. This land-cover shift triggers a massive increase in peak surface runoff volume ($Q$). If the receiving open drains or subterranean culverts are sized incorrectly, localized water pooling (ponding) occurs, saturating adjacent retaining walls, eroding soil strata beneath shallow foundations, and inducing early concrete carbonation. This paper delivers a mathematically modeled, scannable field blueprint to guide entry-level engineers and site designers through standardized micro-scale drainage planning in tropical zones.

2. Hydrological Modeling and Hydraulic Calculations

2.1 Hydrological Runoff Estimation via the Rational Method

To properly size a micro-scale drainage system, the design engineer must calculate the peak storm-water runoff discharge rate ($Q$) passing through the designated micro-catchment envelope. For small, single-plot catchments ($\le 50 \, \text{hectares}$), the peak fluid-flux is mathematically evaluated using the classical Rational Hydrological Formula:

$$Q = \frac{C \cdot I \cdot A}{3.6 \times 10^6}$$

Where:

  • $Q$ = Design peak storm-water runoff discharge capacity ($\text{m}^3/\text{s}$)
  • $C$ = Dimensionless runoff coefficient representing land-cover absorption capacity
  • $I$ = Design rainfall intensity determined for a duration equal to the time of concentration ($\text{mm/hour}$)
  • $A$ = Net surface area of the designated micro-catchment catchment zone ($\text{m}^2$)

The composite runoff coefficient ($C_{\text{comp}}$) for a multi-surface villa plot containing mixed layouts is computed using a weighted area matrix:

$$C_{\text{comp}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (C_i \cdot A_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_i}$$

2.2 Open-Channel Hydraulic Sizing via Manning's Equation

Once the target design peak discharge ($Q$) is computed, the structural open-channel or sub-surface conduit must be dimensioned to ensure its gravity-fed conveyance capacity ($Q_c$) satisfies the hydraulic safety constraint ($Q_c > Q$). The steady uniform fluid velocity ($v$) and volumetric discharge capacity ($Q_c$) through a trapezoidal or rectangular open drain are calculated via Manning’s boundary formulations:

$$v = \frac{1}{n_m} \cdot R_h^{2/3} \cdot S^{1/2}$$

$$Q_c = v \cdot A_c$$

Where:

  • $v$ = Mean fluid discharge velocity through the channel section ($\text{m/s}$)
  • $n_m$ = Manning’s roughness coefficient of the internal channel finish material
  • $R_h$ = Hydraulic radius of the channel cross-section, formulated as $A_c / P_w$ ($\text{m}$)
  • $A_c$ = Net cross-sectional wet flow area of the moving fluid matrix ($\text{m}^2$)
  • $P_w$ = Wetted perimeter of the channel cross-section boundary ($\text{m}$)
  • $S$ = Longitudinal slope gradient of the open-drain bed line ($\text{m/m}$)

4. Standardized Technical Planning Protocol (Step-by-Step SOP)

Phase 1: Catchment Forensic Mapping and Soil Conductivity Analysis

The planning sequence must initiate with a comprehensive topographical survey of the project site using optical Total Station or drone-mounted LiDAR equipment. Establish the exact boundary divides of the localized micro-catchment, identifying high elevation points, structural roof discharge tracks, and natural low-point collection nodes. Concurrently, a field engineer must perform an in-situ double-ring infiltrometer test according to ASTM D3385 to determine the localized soil hydraulic conductivity ($K_{\text{soil}}$). This testing validates whether the subsoil layer can support subterranean infiltration wells or if open-channel outfalls are mandatory.

Phase 2: Geometrical Sizing and Bed Slope Alignment

  1. Runoff Calculations: Calculate the peak storm runoff volume ($Q$) using the Rational Method, matching the rain return period to a minimum 5-year frequency for residential projects or a 10-year scale for high-end boutique commercial spaces.
  2. Channel Alignment: Lay out the open concrete drainage channel paths parallel to property boundary walls or driveway lines. Avoid sharp $90^\circ$ directional turns that induce hydraulic turbulence and localized silt accumulation; implement smooth $45^\circ$ transitional bends instead.
  3. Slope Grade Enforcement: Enforce a strict longitudinal slope gradient ($S$) along the channel bed profile. For pre-cast concrete U-ditch gutters, the minimum bed slope must be maintained at $\ge 0.5\%$ ($1:200$), and ideally optimized to $1.0\%$ ($1:100$) to guarantee reliable self-cleansing fluid velocities ($v \ge 0.6 \, \text{m/s}$) that sweep sediment away automatically.

Phase 3: Construction of On-Site Infiltration Well Systems

  1. Excavation: Excavate a vertical shaft to a minimum depth of $2.5\text{ m}$ below ground level, ensuring the shaft base remains at least $1.5\text{ m}$ above the peak seasonal groundwater table line.
  2. Ring Placement: Insert perforated pre-cast concrete rings (diameter $\ge 800\text{ mm} - 1000\text{ mm}$) into the shaft to secure the structural wall envelope.
  3. Filter Core Packing: Pack the outer perimeter gap between the concrete ring sleeve and the raw soil wall with a $150\text{ mm}$ thick layer of washed volcanic river gravel (grain size $20\text{ mm} - 40\text{ mm}$). Fill the bottom core chamber of the well with a $500\text{ mm}$ deep layer of coarse sand to act as a primary sedimentation filter.
  4. Overflow Detailing: Connect the structural roof downpipe arrays directly into the well throat. An elevated high-level overflow PVC bypass line must be detailed near the well top collar, directing extreme storm surges safely out into the secondary municipal concrete open network.

5. Field Quality Control and Hydraulic Validation Testing

5.1 Laser-Profilometric Cross-Sectional Verification

To confirm that the constructed open concrete channels or pre-cast U-ditch lines strictly match the design dimensions and longitudinal gradients specified in the hydraulic model, an extensive cross-sectional laser-profiling alignment sweep must be executed. Any localized bed sag or proud joint configuration that restricts fluid movement or deviates from the design bed slope by more than $\pm 5\%$ must be remediated to prevent permanent sediment ponding.

5.2 Controlled Saturated Hydrostatic Flow Testing

Prior to final project handover and pavement sealing, the completed drainage network must undergo field flow validation. Clean water is discharged into the highest node of the drainage network at a controlled volumetric flow rate matching the design peak discharge ($Q$). Field engineers must trace the fluid velocity path to verify that the channel maintains a minimum freeboard safety margin of $\ge 20\%$ below the gutter lip, confirming that the hydraulic capacity can handle design storm fluid fluxes without cross-boundary spilling.

2. Landasan Teori dan Perhitungan Rekayasa Hidrologi-Hidrolika

2.1 Estimasi Debit Limpahan Air Hujan via Metode Rasional

Untuk menentukan dimensi saluran drainase secara akurat, engineer wajib menghitung debit puncak air limpahan permukaan (peak runoff discharge rate) ($Q$) yang melewati batas kawasan tangkapan air makro. Untuk area tangkapan berskala kecil ($\le 50 \, \text{hektar}$), debit fluida maksimum dianalisis secara ilmiah menggunakan Formulasi Hidrologi Rasional berikut:

$$Q = \frac{C \cdot I \cdot A}{3.6 \times 10^6}$$

Dimana:

  • $Q$ = Debit puncak aliran air hujan rencana penahan banjir ($\text{m}^3/\text{s}$)
  • $C$ = Koefisien limpahan permukaan tanpa dimensi, mewakili kemampuan serap jenis lapisan tanah
  • $I$ = Intensitas curah hujan maksimum rencana berdasarkan waktu konsentrasi wilayah ($\text{mm/jam}$)
  • $A$ = Luas bersih dari total area tangkapan air mikro proyek ($\text{m}^2$)

Nilai koefisien limpahan gabungan ($C_{\text{comp}}$) untuk sebuah lahan vila yang memiliki variasi penutup permukaan dihitung menggunakan rumus rata-rata tertimbang:

$$C_{\text{comp}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (C_i \cdot A_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_i}$$

2.2 Perhitungan Dimensi Saluran Terbuka via Persamaan Manning

Setelah debit puncak rencana ($Q$) didapatkan, dimensi penampang saluran beton terbuka atau pipa selongsong wajib dirancang agar kapasitas tampung alirannya ($Q_c$) memenuhi syarat batas keamanan hidrolika ($Q_c > Q$). Kecepatan aliran seragam ($v$) dan kapasitas debit alir ($Q_c$) melewati penampang saluran kotak dihitung menggunakan Persamaan Batas Manning:

$$v = \frac{1}{n_m} \cdot R_h^{2/3} \cdot S^{1/2}$$

$$Q_c = v \cdot A_c$$

Dimana $v$ adalah kecepatan rata-rata aliran cairan ($\text{m/s}$), $n_m$ adalah koefisien kekasaran dinding dalam saluran (berkisar antara $n_m = 0.013$ untuk beton acian halus hingga $n_m = 0.035$ untuk parit tanah alami), $R_h$ adalah radius hidrolis penampang ($A_c / P_w$), $A_c$ adalah luas penampang basah aliran ($\text{m}^2$), $P_w$ adalah keliling basah saluran ($\text{m}$), dan $S$ adalah kemiringan longitudinal dasar parit saluran ($\text{m/m}$).

4. Protokol Prosedur Pelaksanaan Standar (SOP Perencanaan Drainase Proyek)

Tahap 1: Pemetaan Topografi Lahan dan Uji Daya Resap Tanah

Prosedur perencanaan wajib dimulai dengan melakukan pengukuran topografi lahan secara detail menggunakan alat Total Station atau sensor LiDAR drone. Tentukan dengan pasti batas-batas kawasan tangkapan air, titik elevasi tertinggi, jalur jatuhnya air dari talang atap, serta titik terendah sebagai lokasi pengumpulan air alami. Bersamaan dengan itu, engineer wajib melakukan pengujian Double-Ring Infiltrometer sesuai standar ASTM D3385 langsung di lokasi proyek untuk mengukur nilai konduktivitas hidrolik tanah asli ($K_{\text{soil}}$). Langkah ini penting untuk menentukan apakah tanah sanggup dipasangi sumur resapan bawah tanah atau wajib dibuang keluar lewat saluran kota.

Tahap 2: Penentuan Ukuran Saluran dan Pengaturan Kemiringan (Slope)

  1. Hitung Debit Banjir: Jalankan perhitungan debit banjir permukaan ($Q$) menggunakan Metode Rasional dengan periode ulang hujan (return period) minimal 5 tahun untuk perumahan atau 10 tahun untuk area vila resort komersial premium.
  2. Penyusunan Jalur Parit: Atur jalur parit beton U-ditch sejajar dengan dinding batas lahan atau tepi jalan masuk kendaraan. Hindari membuat tekukan tajam sudut siku $90^\circ$ karena dapat memicu turbulensi aliran air dan penumpukan lumpur sedimen; gunakan tekukan landai sudut $45^\circ$.
  3. Penguncian Kemiringan Parit: Terapkan nilai kemiringan memanjang dasar parit ($S$) secara disiplin. Untuk saluran pre-cast U-ditch beton acian, kemiringan minimal wajib dijaga pada batas $\ge 0.5\%$ ($1:200$), dan sangat direkomendasikan dioptimalkan pada angka $1.0\%$ ($1:100$) guna menjamin kecepatan aliran air mandiri ($v \ge 0.6 \, \text{m/s}$) yang sanggup menggelontorkan endapan pasir secara otomatis.

Tahap 3: Konstruksi Sistem Sumur Resapan Silinder Beton

  1. Galian Sumur: Gali lubang sumur secara vertikal sedalam minimal $2.5\text{ m}$ dari muka tanah luar, dengan syarat dasar sumur harus berjarak minimal $1.5\text{ m}$ di atas batas tertinggi muka air tanah musim hujan.
  2. Pemasangan Buleleng Beton: Masukkan buleleng beton berpori (perforated concrete rings diameter minimal $800\text{ mm} - 1000\text{ mm}$) ke dalam lubang galian sebagai dinding penahan tanah.
  3. Pengisian Lapisan Filter: Isi celah kosong antara bagian luar buleleng beton dengan dinding tanah asli menggunakan batu koral kali cuci (ukuran $20\text{ mm} - 40\text{ mm}$) setebal $150\text{ mm}$. Pada bagian dasar sumur bagian dalam, uruk pasir pasang kasar setebal $500\text{ mm}$ sebagai filter penyaring endapan lumpur.
  4. Detail Pipa Peluap (Overflow): Hubungkan seluruh instalasi pipa pembuangan air hujan talang atap langsung masuk ke dalam sumur resapan. Pasang pipa peluap (overflow) dari bahan PVC berdiameter besar di bagian leher atas sumur yang dihubungkan lurus menuju jaringan parit beton terbuka luar jalan raya, sebagai pengaman darurat jika terjadi badai hujan ekstrem.

Mencegah terjadinya kerusakan air struktural dan menjamin pengendalian air hujan mandiri di dalam lahan proyek pada iklim tropis pesisir Bali menuntut penataan drainase yang disiplin dan berbasis hitungan ilmiah. Analisis hidrologi membuktikan bahwa penentuan ukuran parit pembuangan tanpa rumus yang jelas selalu gagal menahan intensitas curah hujan tropis yang ekstrem, variasi koefisien penutup penutup lahan ($C_{\text{comp}}$), serta syarat batas kelandaian dasar saluran. Melalui penerapan aturan baku rekayasa hidrolika—yang mewajibkan sudut kemiringan memanjang dasar parit ($S \ge 1.0\%$), penggunaan koefisien kekasaran Manning ($n_m$) yang tepat, serta pembuatan sistem sumur resapan buleleng beton berpori terintegrasi—jalur limpahan air dapat dikendalikan sepenuhnya. Disiplin pelaksanaan pada pengecekan elevasi menggunakan laser profiler serta pembuktian kapasitas alir parit lewat uji aliran terkontrol merupakan kunci utama untuk menghentikan risiko pelapukan beton bawah tanah dan penurunan pondasi bangunan, menjaga ketangguhan properti Anda hingga puluhan tahun ke depan.
