Abstract

Structural failures in Bali’s construction sector are multifaceted, stemming from the convergence of aggressive maritime environments, high seismic activity (Zone 4/5), and rapid development cycles. This paper proposes a systematic forensic engineering framework for investigating structural degradation and resolving execution disputes. By integrating Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), chemical carbonation analysis, and Finite Element Modeling (FEM), engineers can objectively differentiate between design negligence and contractor malpractice. The study emphasizes that data-driven investigations serve as the ultimate "Expert Witness" in legal arbitrations, protecting stakeholders' investments in Bali’s luxury hospitality and residential sectors.

1. Introduction

Bali’s unique geographic position necessitates stringent adherence to Indonesian National Standards (SNI). However, the surge in boutique villa and hotel developments often results in "Performance Gaps" where the as-built quality fails to meet the design intent. When cracks, settlements, or collapses occur, the resulting execution disputes require more than legal intervention; they require a forensic engineering investigation to quantify liability and safety risks.

2. Methodology: Systematic Forensic Investigation

The investigative protocol follows a multi-tiered diagnostic approach:

  • Visual Documentation and Crack Mapping: Identifying flexural, shear, and thermal cracking patterns.
  • Non-Destructive Testing (NDT): Utilizing Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) for concrete uniformity and Rebar Scanning for reinforcement density.
  • Destructive Testing (DT): Core drilling and laboratory compression tests to verify the actual characteristic strength ($f'_c$).
  • Chemical Analysis: Testing chloride penetration and carbonation depth in coastal developments (e.g., Uluwatu and Seminyak).

3. Mathematical Framework for Failure Analysis

To evaluate whether a disputed structural element is compliant, the Safety Factor ($SF$) is recalculated based on field-derived data:

$$SF = \frac{R_n \times \phi}{S_u}$$

Where:

  • $R_n$ = Nominal Resistance based on verified field dimensions and material tests.
  • $\phi$ = Strength reduction factor (as per SNI 2847:2019).
  • $S_u$ = Factored load effects (Dead, Live, and Seismic loads).

In disputes involving excessive deflection ($ \Delta $), the expert verifies the field measurement against the allowable limit:

$$\Delta_{allowable} = \frac{L}{360}$$

If $\Delta_{actual} > \Delta_{allowable}$, the contractor is technically liable for structural remediation or retrofitting.

4. Regional Drivers of Execution Disputes in Bali

  1. Chloride-Induced Corrosion: Proximity to the ocean leads to premature rebar oxidation if the concrete cover is insufficient.
  2. Seismic Non-Compliance: Failure to implement ductile detailing (confinement) in columns, which is critical in Bali’s seismic zone.
  3. Soil-Structure Interaction: Inadequate foundation depth in volcanic soils or limestone cliffs, leading to differential settlement.

2. Metode Investigasi Forensik Struktur

Tahapan investigasi profesional mencakup:

  • Audit Mutu Beton: Menggunakan Hammer Test dan Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) untuk mengecek keroposan beton di dalam struktur.
  • Rebar Scanning: Mendeteksi jumlah dan diameter besi tulangan yang terpasang untuk memastikan kontraktor tidak "mencuri" volume besi.
  • Analisis Geoteknik: Mengecek apakah kegagalan disebabkan oleh daya dukung tanah yang tidak memadai di tebing atau lahan lunak.

3. Rumus Teknis: Dasar Pembuktian Kegagalan

Dalam persidangan sengketa, auditor menggunakan perhitungan kuat tekan beton rata-rata ($f'_{cr}$) untuk menentukan kepatuhan mutu:

$$f'_{cr} = f'_c + 1.34s$$

Dimana:

  • $f'_c$ = Kuat tekan rencana (spesifikasi kontrak).
  • $s$ = Standar deviasi hasil uji lapangan.

Jika nilai kuat tekan lapangan secara konsisten berada di bawah standar deviasi yang diizinkan, maka kontraktor secara teknis terbukti melakukan wanprestasi mutu. Selain itu, untuk sengketa lendutan balok, digunakan batas izin:

$$\text{Lendutan Izin} = \frac{\text{Bentang (L)}}{240}$$

Data matematis inilah yang menjadi "Saksi Bisu" yang tak terbantahkan di depan hukum atau arbiter.

4. Solusi Sengketa Pelaksanaan di Bali

Banyak kasus di daerah seperti Canggu dan Uluwatu melibatkan korosi besi beton akibat uap garam. Investigasi akan mengukur kedalaman karbonasi.
