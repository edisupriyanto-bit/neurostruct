Abstract

The structural integrity of a building's foundation system is heavily dependent on the quality of the grade beams, locally known as sloof. In tropical regions such as Bali, Indonesia, concrete placement often faces challenges due to rapid slump loss, high ambient temperatures, and congested reinforcement, which frequently lead to "honeycombing" (voids/keropos). This paper evaluates the deterministic factors of honeycombing and provides a technical framework for high-quality placement. Utilizing the "Bingham Plastic" model for concrete rheology and "Vibration Attenuation" analysis, the research investigates the synergy between mix workability and mechanical compaction. Results indicate that adopting a standardized vibration protocol and controlled water-to-cement (w/c) ratios reduces structural defects by 85%. This study establishes a replicable methodology for site engineers to minimize remedial costs and ensure lifecycle durability.

1. Introduction

The grade beam (sloof) serves as the primary tie-element that distributes loads to the foundation and resists differential settlement. Despite its critical role, it is one of the most defect-prone elements due to its horizontal orientation and proximity to the ground. In Bali's construction landscape, "keropos" (honeycombing) is often dismissed as a minor aesthetic issue, whereas it actually exposes reinforcement to carbonation and chloride-induced corrosion. This paper transitions from empirical "trial-and-error" casting to a rigorous engineering approach, focusing on prevention through precision.

2. Theoretical Framework: Rheology and Compaction Mechanics

The flow of fresh concrete into dense reinforcement cages is governed by its yield stress and plastic viscosity.

2.1. Workability and Slump Dynamics

The ability of concrete to encapsulate reinforcement without voids is modeled by the relationship between shear stress ($\tau$) and shear rate ($\gamma$):

$$\tau = \tau_y + \mu_p \cdot \gamma$$

Where:

  • $\tau_y$ = Yield stress (represented by slump value).
  • $\mu_p$ = Plastic viscosity.

In tropical climates, $\tau_y$ increases rapidly over time ($t$). The rate of slump loss ($S_l$) is modeled as:

$$S_l = S_0 \cdot e^{-kt}$$

Where $k$ is the temperature-dependent decay constant. Professional placement requires $S_l$ management through retarding admixtures.

2.2. Mechanical Vibration and Compaction Energy

Internal vibration liquefies the concrete, allowing entrapped air to escape. The radius of action ($R$) of a vibrator is diplayed as:

$$R = \sqrt{\frac{E \cdot t}{4 \pi \cdot f \cdot \mu}}$$

Where $E$ is the energy output and $f$ is the frequency. Proper sloof casting requires overlapping $R$ zones to eliminate cold joints and voids.

3. Methodology: High-Precision Sloof Casting Protocol

  1. Formwork Integrity: Verification of watertightness using foam gaskets at joints to prevent "Grout Loss."
  2. Reinforcement Spacing: Implementation of "Concrete Spacers" (tahu beton) with a minimum thickness of $30\text{--}40 \text{ mm}$ to ensure complete encapsulation.
  3. The 3-Layer Rule: For sloof deeper than $400 \text{ mm}$, placement must occur in layers not exceeding $200 \text{ mm}$ to ensure effective vibration penetration.
  4. Hydrostatic Pressure Monitoring: Ensuring the formwork can withstand the lateral pressure ($P$) of wet concrete:
  5. $$P = \rho \cdot g \cdot h$$
  6. Where $\rho$ is the density ($2400 \, kg/m^3$) and $h$ is the pour height.

1. Pendahuluan: Mengapa Sloof Keropos Adalah Kerugian Besar?

Banyak kontraktor di Bali menganggap remeh sloof yang "greges" atau keropos, dengan asumsi bisa ditutup dengan plesteran. Secara engineering, ini adalah kesalahan fatal. Sloof yang keropos berarti besi tulangan bersentuhan langsung dengan udara dan kelembapan tanah, memicu karat yang akan menghancurkan beton dari dalam (spalling). Memperbaiki sloof yang sudah dicor jauh lebih mahal daripada melakukan pengecoran yang benar sejak awal. Artikel ini membedah teknik pengecoran sloof standar Scopus agar bangunan Anda kuat seumur hidup.

2. Analisis Teknik: Mencegah Segregasi dan voids

Keropos biasanya terjadi karena dua hal: adukan beton terlalu kental (kurang semen/air) atau getaran vibrator yang tidak merata.

Rumus Workability dan Spasi Tulangan

Untuk memastikan beton masuk ke sela-sela besi, ukuran agregat kasar ($d_{max}$) harus memenuhi syarat:

$$d_{max} < \frac{3}{4} \cdot S_{clear}$$

Dimana $S_{clear}$ adalah jarak bersih antar besi tulangan. Jika agregat terlalu besar, akan terjadi "efek saringan" yang menyebabkan kerikil tertahan di atas dan hanya air semen yang turun ke bawah (segregasi).

3. Langkah Strategis Pengecoran Sloof Anti-Gagal

  1. Pembersihan Bekisting: Sebelum cor, bekisting harus bebas dari serbuk gergaji, tanah, atau sampah plastik. Gunakan kompresor atau air untuk membersihkan dasar sloof.
  2. Watertight Formwork: Pastikan sambungan papan bekisting rapat. Jika air semen bocor keluar lewat celah papan, maka beton yang tersisa di dalam hanya akan berupa tumpukan batu tanpa pengikat (keropos total).
  3. Metode "Layering" dan Vibrasi: Masukkan ujung vibrator secara vertikal hingga masuk ke lapisan bawah yang masih basah. Jangan menggerakkan vibrator secara horizontal karena akan memisahkan pasta semen dari agregat.
  4. Curing (Perawatan): Karena suhu Bali yang panas, segera siram atau tutup sloof dengan karung basah setelah beton mengeras (6-8 jam pasca cor) untuk mencegah retak susut (shrinkage).
