ABSTRACT

Rubble stone foundation (pondasi batu kali) remains one of the most widely used foundation systems for small-scale construction projects in Indonesia, particularly in residential and low-rise buildings. Despite the emergence of modern deep foundation systems, rubble stone foundations continue to be relevant due to their cost efficiency, availability of materials, and ease of execution. This paper presents an advanced field-based method for constructing rubble stone foundations with improved structural reliability, emphasizing excavation preparation, stone arrangement technique, mortar composition, and quality control measures.

  1. INTRODUCTION

Foundation systems play a critical role in transferring structural loads from superstructures to the soil. In small-scale construction projects, especially in rural and semi-urban regions, rubble stone foundations are commonly used due to their simplicity and economic advantages.

However, field observations show that improper construction techniques often lead to structural failures such as differential settlement, cracking in superstructures, and reduced load-bearing capacity. Therefore, a standardized and improved method is required to enhance performance.

  1. MATERIAL PROPERTIES AND SELECTION

The main materials used in rubble stone foundations include natural stones, cement mortar, and clean water. The stones must possess high compressive strength and low porosity. Angular stones are preferred over rounded stones due to better interlocking behavior.

Mortar composition typically consists of cement and sand with a ratio ranging from 1:4 to 1:6 depending on soil conditions.

  1. SITE INVESTIGATION AND EXCAVATION

Proper geotechnical assessment is essential before foundation installation. Soil bearing capacity determines the depth and width of the foundation.

Excavation must follow design drawings with attention to:

  • Depth uniformity
  • Soil compaction
  • Water table conditions
  • Lateral stability of excavation walls

Improper excavation often leads to instability and foundation misalignment.

  1. ADVANCED CONSTRUCTION METHOD

The improved field method consists of the following stages:

4.1 Base Preparation
The foundation base is leveled and compacted using manual or mechanical compaction.

4.2 Lean Concrete Layer (optional)
A thin layer of lean concrete is applied to improve base stability.

4.3 Stone Placement Technique
Stones are placed manually in a staggered interlocking pattern to maximize load distribution efficiency.

4.4 Mortar Application
Mortar is applied between stone layers to fill voids and enhance bonding strength.

4.5 Layering Technique
Construction proceeds in layers, ensuring uniformity and structural continuity.

4.6 Alignment Control
String lines and leveling tools are used to maintain geometric accuracy.

  1. QUALITY CONTROL AND FIELD MONITORING

Quality control is essential to ensure structural integrity. Key parameters include:

  • Stone quality inspection
  • Mortar consistency control
  • Alignment verification
  • Compaction level assessment
  • Moisture control during curing

Field supervision plays a significant role in minimizing construction defects.

  1. COMMON CONSTRUCTION FAILURES

Based on field data, common failures include:

  • Void formation inside foundation body
  • Weak bonding between stones
  • Uneven settlement
  • Structural cracking above foundation
  • Water infiltration damage

These failures are mostly caused by poor workmanship and lack of supervision.

  1. DISCUSSION

Although modern foundation systems such as pile foundations offer higher capacity, rubble stone foundations remain relevant for low-rise buildings due to cost efficiency and material accessibility.

However, engineering improvements are required to ensure safety and durability under varying soil conditions, especially in tropical regions like Bali.

  1. CONCLUSION

The improved method for rubble stone foundation construction significantly enhances structural reliability when properly implemented. Field-based engineering practices combined with strict quality control can reduce structural failures and improve long-term performance.

ABSTRAK

Pondasi batu kali masih menjadi salah satu sistem pondasi yang paling banyak digunakan pada konstruksi skala kecil di Indonesia. Hal ini disebabkan oleh faktor biaya yang relatif rendah, kemudahan pelaksanaan, serta ketersediaan material di lapangan. Penelitian ini membahas metode terbaru berbasis pengalaman lapangan untuk meningkatkan kualitas pelaksanaan pondasi batu kali agar lebih stabil dan tahan lama.

  1. PENDAHULUAN

Pondasi merupakan elemen penting dalam struktur bangunan karena berfungsi menyalurkan beban dari struktur atas ke tanah. Pada proyek skala kecil seperti rumah tinggal, pondasi batu kali masih menjadi pilihan utama.

Namun, kesalahan dalam pelaksanaan sering menyebabkan kerusakan seperti penurunan tidak merata, retak struktur, dan kegagalan daya dukung.

  1. MATERIAL DAN SPESIFIKASI

Material utama terdiri dari batu kali, semen, pasir, dan air. Batu yang digunakan harus keras, tidak mudah lapuk, dan memiliki bentuk bersudut agar dapat saling mengunci.

Perbandingan campuran mortar biasanya 1:4 sampai 1:6 tergantung kondisi tanah.

  1. PEKERJAAN GALIAN

Galian harus dilakukan sesuai gambar rencana dengan memperhatikan:

  • Kedalaman pondasi
  • Kondisi tanah
  • Stabilitas dinding galian
  • Kelembapan tanah

Kesalahan pada tahap ini dapat mempengaruhi seluruh struktur pondasi.

  1. METODE PELAKSANAAN TERBARU

Tahapan pekerjaan:

4.1 Persiapan dasar
Pemadatan tanah dasar dilakukan agar stabil.

4.2 Lantai kerja (opsional)
Lean concrete digunakan untuk meningkatkan kestabilan.

4.3 Penyusunan batu
Batu disusun secara bersilang agar saling mengunci.

4.4 Pemasangan mortar
Mortar mengisi rongga antar batu untuk memperkuat ikatan.

4.5 Sistem lapisan
Pekerjaan dilakukan bertahap untuk menjaga kualitas.

  1. PENGENDALIAN MUTU

Kontrol mutu meliputi:

  • Kualitas batu
  • Konsistensi mortar
  • Ketepatan dimensi
  • Kerapian susunan
  • Proses curing
  1. MASALAH YANG SERING TERJADI
  • Rongga kosong dalam pondasi
  • Ikatan mortar lemah
  • Penurunan tidak merata
  • Retak struktur atas
  • Kelembapan berlebih
  1. KESIMPULAN

Metode pelaksanaan yang tepat sangat berpengaruh terhadap kualitas pondasi batu kali. Pengawasan lapangan menjadi faktor kunci keberhasilan.
