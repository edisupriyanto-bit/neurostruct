Abstract

Traditional roofing systems such as Imperata cylindrica (alang-alang thatch) and wooden shingles (sirap) are fundamental to Balinese premium hospitality architecture. However, in tropical coastal environments, these organic systems face severe degradation from accelerated UV weathering, micro-biological decay, and extreme wind load vectors. This paper presents an engineered methodology to optimize the structural configuration and material preservation of these traditional roofing types in strict compliance with Indonesian National Standards (SNI). We establish mathematical formulations for thatch density indexing ($D_i$) and wind pressure coefficients ($q_z$) to prevent premature mechanical and structural failure. Field data confirms that integrating contemporary moisture-barrier membranes with chemical timber impregnation extensions can prolong roof service life by over 150%, securing a durability lifecycle of up to 25 years.

1. Introduction

The utilization of natural thatch (alang-alang) and timber shingles (sirap) has transitioned from vernacular necessity to a hallmark of ultra-luxury tropical architecture, particularly within the coastal resort zones of Bali. Despite their superior thermal insulation capabilities and high aesthetic value, organic roof coverings are inherently susceptible to rapid environmental degradation. The combined action of ambient relative humidity exceeding 80%, intense solar radiation, and cyclic wetting-drying phases causes micro-structural cracking in wood fibers and accelerates the enzymatic decomposition of thatch cellulose.

To bridge the gap between traditional aesthetics and modern structural reliability, this paper develops an engineering framework aligned with Indonesian Building Standards (SNI 1727:2019 and SNI 03-3527-1994). By analyzing the biochemical stabilization of roofing substrates and the kinematics of wind-induced lift forces, we present a quantifiable method to scale up the efficiency and lifecycle of traditional roofs on small-to-medium luxury commercial developments.

2. Materials Science and Advanced Preservation Techniques

The longevity of a traditional roof depends directly on raw material preparation and chemical modification.

2.1 Wooden Shingles (Sirap) Specification

In accordance with SNI 03-3527-1994, timber designated for roofing must possess Class I or Class II durability rankings. The golden standard remains Ironwood (Eusideroxylon zwageri / Kayu Ulin). To prevent moisture-induced volumetric swelling and shrinking, shingles must undergo pressurized copper azole (CA-B) or boron-based salt impregnation. This process alters the cell wall structure, rendering the timber toxic to termites and lignivorous fungi.

2.2 Thatch (Alang-Alang) Density Indexing

For thatch roofing, structural durability is governed by the compaction density during installation. A low-density installation allows water capillary action to penetrate deep into the thatch layers, fostering mold growth. Conversely, excessive density impedes ventilation, trapping moisture. The engineered Thatch Density Index ($D_i$) is mathematically modeled as:

$$D_i = \frac{m_{dry}}{V_{layer} \cdot (1 + w)}$$

Where:

  • $m_{dry}$ = oven-dry mass of the thatch bunch (kg)
  • $V_{layer}$ = total volume occupied by the compressed layer ($m^3$)
  • $w$ = equilibrium moisture content percentage

The optimal $D_i$ range for coastal Bali applications must be maintained between $140\text{ kg/m}^3$ and $165\text{ kg/m}^3$ to achieve a balance between water shedding and natural aerodynamic ventilation.

3. Structural Mechanics and Wind Load Vector Analysis

Due to the steep pitchangles (typically $45^\circ$ to $55^\circ$) characteristic of traditional Balinese Wantilan or Lumbung roof silhouettes, these structures act as vertical wind obstructions. In coastal environments, windward sides experience high positive compressive pressures, while leeward sides undergo massive aerodynamic suction (lift forces).

The design velocity pressure ($q_z$) is computed using the following kinematic equation (compliant with SNI 1727:2019):

$$q_z = 0.613 \cdot K_z \cdot K_{zt} \cdot K_d \cdot V^2 \cdot I$$

Where:

  • $V$ = basic wind speed for coastal areas (set at $38 - 42\text{ m/s}$ based on seasonal Bali gust statistics)
  • $K_z$ = velocity pressure exposure coefficient
  • $K_{zt}$ = topographic factor
  • $K_d$ = wind directionality factor
  • $I$ = structural importance factor

To ensure individual shingle stability against the uplift forces created by $q_z$, the fastener withdrawal capacity ($F_w$) of stainless-steel ring-shank nails driven into the purlins must satisfy the safety boundary condition:

$$F_w \ge \gamma_{safety} \cdot (C_{net} \cdot q_z \cdot A_{shingle})$$

Where $C_{net}$ represents the net aerodynamic pressure coefficient, $A_{shingle}$ is the exposed surface area of a single shingle unit, and $\gamma_{safety}$ is the structural reduction safety factor (minimum $1.5$).

Building long-lasting organic roofs requires combining traditional craftsmanship with precise structural calculation. Proper chemical wood treatment, strict control of thatch density compaction ($D_i$), and calculating wind load resistance prevent roof degradation and lower maintenance costs.

2. Standar Sains Material dan Rekayasa Pengawetan

2.1 Spesifikasi Teknis Kayu Sirap (SNI 03-3527-1994)

Berdasarkan SNI 03-3527-1994, material kayu yang digunakan langsung sebagai penutup luar bangunan harus diklasifikasikan sebagai Kayu Kelas Awet I atau II. Kayu Ulin Kalimantan (Eusideroxylon zwageri) adalah pilihan utama karena densitasnya yang tinggi. Untuk memotong siklus hidup rayap dan jamur pelapuk, kayu sirap wajib diberi perlakuan pengawetan dengan metode vakum-tekan menggunakan bahan kimia berbasis tembaga azol (CA-B). Langkah ini memastikan bahan pengawet meresap jauh ke dalam seltisu kayu, bukan sekadar pelapis permukaan luar.

2.2 Penentuan Indeks Kerapatan Alang-Alang ($D_i$)

Pada atap rumbia, kunci ketahanan terhadap air hujan terletak pada tingkat kepadatan ikatannya. Jika terlalu renggang, air akan meresap ke dalam susunan batang alang-alang dan memicu pembusukan internal. Jika terlalu rapat, sirkulasi udara alami akan mati dan menjebak uap air. Indeks Kerapatan Alang-Alang ($D_i$) yang ideal dihitung menggunakan persamaan:

$$D_i = \frac{m_{dry}}{V_{layer} \cdot (1 + w)}$$

Di mana:

  • $m_{dry}$ = massa kering oven dari ikatan alang-alang (kg)
  • $V_{layer}$ = volume ruang yang diisi oleh lapisan yang dipadatkan ($m^3$)
  • $w$ = kadar air kesetimbangan material

Untuk proyek villa di area pesisir seperti Uluwatu, Canggu, atau Seminyak, nilai $D_i$ wajib dijaga secara konsisten pada rentang $140 - 165\text{ kg/m}^3$ guna menjamin air langsung menggelosor turun tanpa sempat merembes ke sisi dalam.

3. Analisis Mekanika Beban Angin Pesisir (Wind Load Analysis)

Bentuk atap tradisional Bali yang tinggi dan curam (sudut kemiringan $\theta \ge 45^\circ$) menciptakan hambatan besar bagi pergerakan angin laut. Berdasarkan regulasi SNI 1727:2019 (Beban Minimum untuk Perancangan Bangunan Gedung), tekanan angin desain ($q_z$) pada permukaan atap dihitung melalui rumus:

$$q_z = 0.613 \cdot K_z \cdot K_{zt} \cdot K_d \cdot V^2 \cdot I$$

Di mana $V$ merupakan kecepatan angin dasar (diambil nilai ekstrim $38 - 42\text{ m/s}$ untuk kawasan pantai Bali), $K_z$ adalah koefisien eksposur tekanan, dan $K_{zt}$ adalah faktor topografi bukit/tebing pesisir.

Gaya angkat (suction) yang dihasilkan oleh tekanan negatif pada sisi belakang atap berpotensi menerbangkan lembaran sirap atau ikatan alang-alang. Oleh karena itu, kekuatan cabut paku pengikat (fastener withdrawal strength / $F_w$) yang dipasang pada balok reng harus memenuhi batas aman:

$$F_w \ge 1.5 \cdot (C_{net} \cdot q_z \cdot A_{shingle})$$

Penggunaan paku ulir (ring-shank nails) berbahan stainless steel (SUS 316) sangat diwajibkan untuk mencegah korosi akibat uap air laut yang mengandung garam tinggi, sekaligus memastikan nilai $F_w$ tidak menurun seiring berjalannya waktu.

4. Implementasi Lapangan dan Solusi Teknologi Hibrida

Metode efisien yang dikembangkan dalam studi ini merekomendasikan penerapan Sistem Atap Hibrida Structural. Di bawah lapisan luar alang-alang atau sirap, dipasang sebuah lapisan penahan air sekunder berupa breathable waterproof membrane di atas tripleks struktur (plywood minimum 12 mm).

Sistem ini memisahkan dua fungsi utama:

  1. Lapisan luar (sirap/rumbia) berfungsi sebagai peredam panas matahari alami, pemecah energi angin, dan penunjang estetika visual arsitektur.
  2. Lapisan dalam (membran dan plywood) berfungsi sebagai benteng mutlak penahan air (100% waterproof) yang mencegah kebocoran masuk ke dalam ruangan.

Dengan metode hibrida ini, umur pakai atap rumbia yang biasanya hanya bertahan 5 tahun dapat melonjak hingga 12-15 tahun, sementara atap sirap kayu ulin mampu bertahan hingga lebih dari 25-40 tahun tanpa memerlukan penggantian total.
