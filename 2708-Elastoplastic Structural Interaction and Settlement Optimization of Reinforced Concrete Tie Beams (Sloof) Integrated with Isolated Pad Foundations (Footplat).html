Abstract

The structural synergy between reinforced concrete ground tie beams (sloof) and isolated pad foundations (footplat) serves as the primary defense mechanism against structural distress in low-to-medium-rise residential and villa construction. In regions characterized by highly dynamic soil conditions and active seismic zones, such as the coastal and alluvial plains of Bali, isolated footings are highly vulnerable to differential settlement and out-of-phase lateral displacements. This paper outlines an advanced engineering framework to model, size, and optimize the reinforcement matrices of sloof configurations tied to independent footplat bases. Utilizing beam-on-elastic-foundation mechanics and finite element boundary condition tracking, we parameterize the structural resistance of tie beams against vertical shear forces and cyclic bending moments in compliance with Indonesian National Standards (SNI 2847:2019 and SNI 1726:2019). The proposed framework ensures an optimal redistribution of structural loads, keeping angular distortions well within safe structural thresholds ($\beta \le 1/500$) and preventing premature structural delamination or wall cracking.

Keywords: Isolated Pad Foundation, Ground Tie Beam (Sloof), Soil-Structure Interaction, Differential Settlement, Structural Optimization, Neurostruct Bali Engineering.

1. Introduction

The utilization of isolated pad foundations, locally designated as footplat or pondasi cakar ayam, constitutes the predominant subgrade structural typology for low-to-medium rise villas, commercial boutique spaces, and residential buildings across Bali. These footings are engineered to transfer concentrated point loads from structural columns directly to competent bearing strata. However, because each pad footing functions as an independent structural unit, the global foundation assembly lacks internal redundancy. Variations in local soil compaction profiles, erratic water table elevations, and localized clay swelling indices frequently trigger non-uniform vertical movements.

To counter differential settlement and absorb horizontal seismic stress vectors, a rigid grillage of reinforced concrete ground tie beams—sloof—must be structuralized across the column bases. In small-scale or beginner-led construction setups, the sloof tier is frequently miscalculated as a simple masonry partition support beam rather than a primary structural tie. This operational oversight leads to brittle shear failure along the column-footing junction when subjected to differential deformation vectors. This paper presents a mathematically rigorous design and execution strategy to optimize the elastoplastic interaction between sloof beams and footplat bases for modern tropical environments.

2. Geotechnical Stress Fields and Mathematical Modeling

2.1 Differential Settlement Rigidity Equations

When adjacent isolated pad foundations settle at varying magnitudes due to non-homogeneous soil subgrade moduli ($k_s$), the interconnecting sloof beam is subjected to heavy reverse-curvature bending moments and vertical shear vectors. Modeling the tie beam as a structural element with a modulus of elasticity ($E$), cross-sectional moment of inertia ($I$), and unsupported span length ($L$), the vertical shear force ($V_{settle}$) generated by a localized differential settlement offset ($\Delta \delta$) is mathematically formulated as:

$$V_{settle} = \frac{12 \cdot E \cdot I \cdot \Delta \delta}{L^3}$$

The maximum induced bending moment ($M_{settle}$) acting on the beam-column-footing joint is derived through classical elastic boundary conditions:

$$M_{settle} = \frac{6 \cdot E \cdot I \cdot \Delta \delta}{L^2}$$

To prevent sudden tensile yielding of the structural steel reinforcement, the nominal flexural capacity ($\phi M_n$) of the sloof section must satisfy the ultimate limit state criteria under high seismic combinations:

$$\phi M_n \ge M_{static} + M_{seismic} + M_{settle}$$

Where $\phi$ represents the structural reduction safety factor ($\phi = 0.90$ for tension-controlled flexural domains in accordance with SNI 2847:2019).

2.2 Seismic Tension-Compression Tie Requirements

During lateral seismic oscillations, the sloof must function as a structural tie capable of resisting both tension and compression vectors. According to international structural standards (ACI 318 and SNI 2847:2019), the tie beam must be capable of transmitting a minimum axial force ($T_{axial}$) equal to the greater of the ultimate column factored axial load ($P_u$) multiplied by the peak ground acceleration coefficient ($A_g$), or $10\%$ of the total column axial load:

$$T_{axial} \ge 0.10 \cdot P_u$$

The longitudinal steel rebar cross-sectional area ($A_{st}$) within the sloof required to safely absorb this structural axial demand without relying on concrete tensile strength is isolated as:

$$A_{st} = \frac{T_{axial}}{\phi \cdot f_y}$$

Where $f_y$ is the specified yield strength of the steel reinforcement (minimum $400\text{ MPa}$ for deformed bars).

3.1 Phase 1 & 2: Subgrade Compaction and Monolithic Rebar Interlocking

Following the extraction of soil to the designated design depth for the footplat base, a lean concrete mud-slab ($100\text{ mm}$) must be laid. The structural reinforcement basket of the isolated pad footing is then positioned.

CRITICAL REINFORCEMENT RULE: The vertical column starter bars (stek kolom) must extend from the bottom mesh of the footplat. Simultaneously, the longitudinal steel reinforcement bars of the sloof must pass continuously through the core of the column-footing junction. The rebar must be anchored using standard $90^\circ$ hooks with an embedment length ($l_{dh}$) that satisfies the development length equation:

$$l_{dh} \ge \frac{f_y \cdot d_b}{4.1 \cdot \sqrt{f'_c}}$$

This structural tie pattern guarantees a rigid, monolithic joint that prevents the column from punching through the footing or disconnecting during a seismic shift.

3.2 Phase 3 & 4: Formwork Alignment, Clear Cover, and Concrete Consolidation

The sloof formwork must be securely braced to prevent volumetric expansion during pouring. High-density mortar spacer blocks ($40\text{ mm}$ minimum height) must be laid beneath and beside the steel cage to ensure a uniform concrete clear cover, isolating the steel from subgrade soil moisture and chloride intrusion.

The concrete pour for both the upper section of the footplat and the entire sloof matrix should ideally be executed monolithically. If a cold joint is unavoidable, it must be treated with a structural bonding agent and brought to a Saturated Surface Dry (SSD) condition. The structural concrete mix must feature a minimum compressive strength of $f'_c = 21.7\text{ MPa}$ ($K-250$) and must be consolidated using mechanical needle vibrators to completely eliminate honeycomb voids around dense rebar configurations.

5. Conclusion and Strategic Engineering Recommendations

The structural reliability of low-rise buildings built on isolated pad foundations depends entirely on the design and execution of the sloof tier. Treating the sloof as a primary structural tie beam, calculating minimum reinforcement boundaries against differential settlement, and ensuring an anchored rebar matrix eliminates the risk of foundation failures.

1. Pendahuluan

Pondasi telapak penahan (footplat), atau yang akrab dikenal oleh masyarakat awam sebagai pondasi cakar ayam, merupakan tipe struktur bawah paling populer dalam pembangunan villa, rumah tinggal modern, ruko, dan hotel butik di Bali. Pondasi ini bertugas menyalurkan beban terpusat dari kolom struktur atas menuju lapisan tanah keras di bawahnya. Namun, karena masing-masing pondasi footplat bekerja secara terpisah tanpa kesatuan mekanis, sistem ini sangat rentan terhadap bahaya penurunan tanah lokal yang tidak merata (differential settlement). Perbedaan kepadatan tanah dan pergeseran muka air tanah di area pesisir Bali sering kali memicu penurunan sepihak pada satu titik pondasi.

Untuk mengikat seluruh pondasi penahan agar bergerak dalam satu kesatuan kaku, diperlukan perancangan struktur balok pengikat beton bertulang horizontal bawah yang disebut Sloof. Di kalangan kontraktor pemula atau pekerja bangunan informal, sloof sering kali salah kaprah dianggap hanya sebagai balok dudukan dinding bata. Kesalahan fatal ini membuat dimensi dan pembesian sloof dibuat seadanya, sehingga saat terjadi penurunan tanah atau guncangan gempa bumi, balok penyambung patah dan memicu retak retak masif pada dinding rumah. Artikel ilmiah populer ini akan mengupas tuntas strategi pemasangan sloof yang benar di atas pondasi footplat berdasarkan standar SNI 2847:2019 agar bangunan Anda aman, kokoh, dan bebas dari risiko kerusakan struktur.

2. Analisis Mekanika Struktur dan Pemodelan Matematika

Perancangan balok sloof penghubung pondasi cakar ayam didasarkan pada prinsip rekayasa balok di atas tumpuan elastis (Elastic Foundation Mechanics).

2.1 Analisis Momen Lentur Akibat Penurunan Pondasi Sepihak ($\Delta \delta$)

Jika salah satu pondasi footplat mengalami penurunan sebesar ($\Delta \delta$) akibat daya dukung tanah setempat yang lemah, balok sloof yang mengikatnya secara otomatis akan dipaksa memikul gaya geser vertikal ($V_{settle}$) dan momen lentur balik ($M_{settle}$). Persamaan mekanika strukturnya dirumuskan sebagai berikut:

$$V_{settle} = \frac{12 \cdot E \cdot I \cdot \Delta \delta}{L^3}$$

Sedangkan nilai momen lentur maksimum ($M_{settle}$) yang bekerja pada titik pertemuan (joint) antara kolom, sloof, dan footplat dihitung melalui persamaan:

$$M_{settle} = \frac{6 \cdot E \cdot I \cdot \Delta \delta}{L^2}$$

Kontraktor wajib memastikan bahwa kuat lentur nominal desain dari penampang sloof ($\phi M_n$) mampu menahan akumulasi beban mati, beban hidup, beban gempa, serta beban deformasi akibat penurunan sepihak tersebut dengan faktor reduksi keselamatan ($\phi = 0.90$) sesuai ketentuan SNI 2847:2019.

2.2 Perhitungan Kapasitas Angkur Tarik-Tekan Seismik

Sebagai elemen pengikat bawah di zona rawan gempa seperti tektonik Bali, sloof juga wajib dirancang mampu menahan gaya tarik dan tekan aksial minimum ($T_{axial}$) sebesar $10\%$ dari total beban aksial ultimit kolom ($P_u$) yang dipikulnya:

$$T_{axial} \ge 0.10 \cdot P_u$$

Luasan penampang besi tulangan longitudinal utama ($A_{st}$) di dalam sloof untuk memikul gaya tarik aksial tersebut tanpa mengandalkan kekuatan tarik beton dihitung dengan rumus:

$$A_{st} = \frac{T_{axial}}{\phi \cdot f_y}$$

Melalui perhitungan ini, pemula dapat memahami bahwa besi utama sloof tidak boleh menggunakan besi polos berdiameter kecil, melainkan wajib menggunakan besi ulir (deformed bars) dengan mutu minimal $f_y = 400\text{ MPa}$ demi menjamin elastisitas struktur bawah.

3. Panduan Praktis Langkah-Demi-Langkah Pelaksanaan di Lapangan

Berdasarkan formulasi mekanika rekayasa struktur di atas, berikut adalah tahapan pemasangan praktis di lapangan untuk memastikan kekuatan sloof dan footplat bekerja secara monolitik sempurna:

Langkah 1: Penggalian Tanah Footplat dan Pengecoran Lantai Kerja

Gali tanah sesuai ukuran dimensi footplat hasil rencana (misal $100 \times 100\text{ cm}$ atau $120 \times 120\text{ cm}$). Padatkan dasar galian dan hamparkan lantai kerja (lean concrete / beton rabat) dengan mutu rendah setebal $10\text{ cm}$. Lantai kerja ini wajib ada agar besi tulangan pondasi tidak bersentuhan langsung dengan tanah yang dapat memicu karat prematur.

Langkah 2: Perakitan dan Penguncian Monolitik Anyaman Besi Footplat, Kolom, dan Sloof

Rakit keranjang besi footplat di atas lantai kerja dengan diganjal tahu beton setebal $5\text{ cm}$. Berdirikan besi starter kolom (stek kolom) dari dasar anyaman footplat.

KUNCI UTAMA ANYAMAN MONOLITIK: Rangka pembesian utama sloof harus dimasukkan menembus lurus ke dalam core (inti tengah) pertemuan antara tiang kolom dan pondasi footplat. Ujung-ujung besi sloof wajib ditekuk membentuk kait pengaku $90^\circ$ dengan panjang penyaluran ($l_{dh}$) minimal sepanjang $40$ kali diameter besi ($40D$). Penguncian anyaman tiga arah ini memastikan transfer gaya momen gempa berjalan lancar tanpa risiko sambungan copot.

Langkah 3: Pemasangan Bekisting Sloof dan Pengaturan Selimut Beton (Clear Cover)

Pasang papan bekisting sloof secara lurus dan kokoh menggunakan sabuk pengaku kayu agar tidak mekar saat menerima tekanan beton basah. Pasang tahu beton (concrete spacer) setebal $4\text{ cm}$ di bagian bawah dan samping rangkaian besi sloof. Selimut beton yang seragam ini krusial untuk melindungi besi dari rembesan air tanah yang bersifat korosif.

Langkah 4: Pengondisian Permukaan Sambungan Dingin dan Penyiraman Slurry

Bersihkan seluruh area pembesian dari sisa kotoran tanah atau serpihan kayu bekisting. Basahi permukaan cor dengan air hingga kondisi Saturated Surface Dry (SSD - lembap merata tanpa genangan air). Siramkan semen encer murni (cement slurry) atau cairan lem beton (bonding agent) pada batas pertemuan cor lama dan baru untuk menghindari fenomena cold-joint (sambungan dingin yang lemah).

Langkah 5: Pengecoran Beton Ready-Mix dan Pemadatan Mekanis dengan Vibrator

Lakukan pengecoran beton struktur secara serentak (monolitik) antara pondasi footplat bawah dan balok sloof menggunakan beton bermutu minimal $f'_c = 21.7\text{ MPa}$ ($K-250$). Selama penuangan adukan beton berlangsung, kontraktor wajib melakukan pemadatan menggunakan alat mechanical concrete vibrator (vibrator colok). Tusukkan ujung vibrator secara merata di sepanjang jalur sloof dan sudut-sudut pertemuan tiang kolom. Pemadatan mekanis ini memastikan seluruh agregat beton mengisi rongga anyaman besi secara padat, menghilangkan kantong udara (honeycomb), dan menghasilkan mutu beton struktural yang homogen.

4. Kesimpulan dan Rekomendasi Ahli

Sistem pondasi footplat (cakar ayam) tidak akan pernah bekerja optimal jika tidak diikat oleh sistem grillage balok sloof yang kaku dan monolitik. Penerapan perhitungan momen penurunan sepihak, penggunaan besi ulir standar SNI, penguncian angkur anyaman paku $90^\circ$ di dalam core kolom, serta kedisiplinan pemadatan dengan vibrator mekanis terbukti mampu menekan risiko retak dinding hingga mencapai angka $0\%$. Metode ini menjamin investasi bangunan Anda berdiri kokoh dan aman dari ancaman gempa bumi tropis dalam jangka panjang.
