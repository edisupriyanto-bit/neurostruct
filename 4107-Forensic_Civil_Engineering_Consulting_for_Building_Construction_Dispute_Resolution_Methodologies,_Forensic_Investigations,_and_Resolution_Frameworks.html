### 1. Introduction

Forensic civil engineering consulting has become indispensable in modern construction dispute resolution. By applying scientific principles and systematic investigation techniques, forensic consultants uncover root causes, establish liability, and support equitable settlements. This is particularly relevant for building projects where technical complexity often exceeds the expertise of standard project teams or legal professionals.

In Bali, the boom in hospitality, residential, and commercial buildings has led to frequent disputes involving foundation performance, structural integrity, material failures, and project delays. This paper details the methodologies, frameworks, and value of forensic civil engineering consulting in such contexts.

### 2. Literature Review

Scopus-indexed research demonstrates the growing importance of forensic civil engineering in dispute resolution. Bibliometric analyses and case studies published in *Journal of Legal Affairs and Dispute Resolution in Engineering and Construction*, *Forensic Engineering* (ICE), *Engineering Failure Analysis*, and *Buildings* highlight effective techniques for root cause investigation, delay forensics, and expert testimony. Studies consistently show that independent forensic consultants significantly improve settlement rates and reduce resolution costs by providing clear, defensible technical evidence.
