Comprehensive Guide to Stone Rubble Foundation Work According to SNI Standards

Introduction

Stone rubble foundation, locally known in Indonesia as pondasi batu belah or pondasi batu

kali, is one of the most traditional yet highly effective shallow foundation systems widely

used in residential, low-rise commercial, and rural construction projects across the

archipelago. This type of foundation utilizes locally sourced split stones (batu belah) bonded

with cement-sand mortar to create a strong, stable base that transfers building loads evenly

to the soil. In regions with stable soil conditions and abundant stone resources, pondasi batu

belah offers an economical, durable, and environmentally friendly alternative to more

expensive reinforced concrete foundations.

The reliability of stone rubble foundations has been proven over decades, especially in

earthquake-prone areas of Indonesia where flexibility and mass provide natural damping

against seismic forces. However, to ensure safety, quality, and cost efficiency, all works must

strictly comply with Indonesian National Standards (SNI). The primary reference is SNI

2836:2008 – Tata Cara Perhitungan Harga Satuan Pekerjaan Pondasi untuk Konstruksi

Bangunan Gedung dan Perumahan, which provides standardized unit price calculations,

material indices, labor requirements, and technical specifications for various mortar mixtures.

This standard revises the earlier SNI 03-2836-2002 and serves as the uniform benchmark

for contractors, consultants, and project owners nationwide.

Materials and Mix Proportions

The main materials for stone rubble foundation include:

●​ Batu belah (split stone): Size 15–20 cm, hard and non-porous, free from organic

matter. Typical consumption is 1.20 m³ per 1 m³ of finished foundation.

●​ Portland Cement (PC): SNI 15-2049-2014 compliant.

●​ Pasir pasang (fine sand): Clean, well-graded, maximum 5% silt/clay content.

●​ Optional additives: Lime (kapur) or pozzolanic materials for specific mixtures.

SNI 2836:2008 specifies several standard mortar mix ratios (by volume) with their respective

material indices per 1 m³ of installed foundation:

●​ 1 PC : 3 PP → 202 kg cement, 0.485 m³ sand

●​ 1 PC : 4 PP → 163 kg cement, 0.520 m³ sand

●​ 1 PC : 5 PP → 136 kg cement, 0.544 m³ sand (most common for residential use)

●​ 1 PC : 6 PP and leaner mixes for non-critical structures

●​ Alternative mixes using lime (KP) and sand (SM) for cost optimization

Labor indices (man-hour) are also standardized: 1.5 OH workers, 0.6 OH masons,

0.06–0.075 OH foremen per m³, assuming 5 effective working hours per day. Overhead and

profit (10%) are added to arrive at the final unit price.

Step-by-Step Execution Method

Proper implementation is critical to achieve the required bearing capacity and longevity. The

standard construction sequence is as follows:

1.​ Site Preparation and Excavation: Mark the foundation lines using bouwplank and

shop drawings. Excavate to the designed depth and width (usually 1.5–2 times wall

thickness). Remove soft soil and organic material.

2.​ Base Compaction: Compact the excavated base with a stamper or plate compactor

until 95% Proctor density is achieved. Lay a 10–15 cm sand bedding layer and

compact again.

3.​ Formwork (if required): Install temporary wooden or concrete-block formwork on

both sides of the trench.

4.​ Stone Placement and Mortar Application: Soak stones in water to prevent

absorption of mortar water. Place the largest stones at the bottom for stability. Fill

gaps with smaller stones and pour mortar (consistency like thick cream). Ensure full

contact and no voids. Build layer by layer, maintaining vertical and horizontal

alignment.

5.​ Curing and Backfilling: Keep the foundation moist for at least 7 days. Backfill with

selected soil in layers, compacting each layer thoroughly.

6.​ Quality Control: Visual inspection for alignment, mortar coverage, and stone

interlocking. Sample mortar cubes for compressive strength testing (minimum 5–7

MPa at 28 days depending on design).

Benefits and Challenges

Advantages: Low cost (often 30–50% cheaper than cast-in-place concrete), fast

construction with local labor, excellent thermal mass, and high durability against moisture

when properly executed. It is also sustainable as it maximizes local stone resources.

Challenges: Labor-intensive, requires skilled masons (tukang batu), sensitive to poor mortar

quality or inadequate compaction. In soft soil conditions, additional measures such as wider

footing or soil stabilization may be needed.

Case Studies and Best Practices

Many successful projects in Java, Sumatra, and Sulawesi have used SNI-compliant stone

rubble foundations for houses up to 2–3 stories. Regular supervision and adherence to the

material indices in SNI 2836:2008 ensure projects stay within budget while meeting

structural safety requirements.

Panduan Lengkap Pekerjaan Pondasi Batu Belah Sesuai Standar SNI

Pendahuluan

Pondasi batu belah atau pondasi batu kali merupakan salah satu sistem pondasi dangkal

yang paling tradisional namun tetap efektif dan banyak digunakan di Indonesia untuk

bangunan rumah tinggal, ruko, serta proyek pedesaan. Pondasi ini memanfaatkan batu

belah lokal yang diikat dengan adukan semen-pasir sehingga mampu mendistribusikan

beban bangunan secara merata ke tanah dasar. Di daerah dengan kondisi tanah stabil dan

ketersediaan batu melimpah, pondasi batu belah menjadi pilihan ekonomis, tahan lama, dan

ramah lingkungan dibandingkan pondasi beton bertulang yang lebih mahal.

Keandalan pondasi ini telah terbukti puluhan tahun, terutama di wilayah rawan gempa

karena fleksibilitas dan massa yang memberikan efek redaman alami. Namun, agar aman,

berkualitas, dan efisien biaya, seluruh pekerjaan harus mematuhi Standar Nasional

Indonesia (SNI). Standar utama adalah SNI 2836:2008 – Tata Cara Perhitungan Harga

Satuan Pekerjaan Pondasi untuk Konstruksi Bangunan Gedung dan Perumahan.

Standar ini merevisi SNI 03-2836-2002 dan menjadi acuan seragam untuk penghitungan

harga satuan, indeks bahan, serta tenaga kerja.

Bahan dan Komposisi Adukan

Bahan utama meliputi batu belah ukuran 15–20 cm (1,20 m³ per m³ pondasi), semen

Portland (SNI 15-2049-2014), dan pasir pasang yang bersih. SNI 2836:2008 menetapkan

beberapa komposisi adukan standar, antara lain:

●​ 1 PC : 3 PP

●​ 1 PC : 4 PP

●​ 1 PC : 5 PP (paling umum untuk rumah tinggal)

●​ Campuran dengan kapur untuk optimalisasi biaya

Indeks tenaga kerja juga distandarkan: 1,5 OH pekerja, 0,6 OH tukang batu, serta mandor

per m³ pondasi.

Metode Pelaksanaan Langkah demi Langkah

1.​ Persiapan dan galian sesuai gambar kerja.

2.​ Pemadatan dasar galian hingga kepadatan 95%.

3.​ Lapisan pasir urug 10–15 cm dan dipadatkan.

4.​ Pemasangan batu belah secara berlapis dengan adukan mortar, pastikan tidak ada

rongga.

5.​ Perawatan (curing) minimal 7 hari dan backfilling bertahap.

Pengendalian kualitas mencakup pemeriksaan visual, kesesuaian dimensi, serta uji kuat

tekan adukan.

Keunggulan dan Tantangan

Keunggulan: Biaya rendah, cepat dibangun dengan tenaga lokal, tahan lama, dan ramah

lingkungan. Tantangan: Memerlukan tukang batu terampil serta pengawasan ketat agar tidak

terjadi rongga atau pemadatan kurang.
