Modern Systems for Stone Rubble Foundation Construction: Blending Tradition with

Advanced Techniques

Introduction

Stone rubble foundation, widely known in Indonesia as *pondasi batu belah* or

*pondasi batu kali*, continues to be a popular shallow foundation system for

residential and low-rise buildings due to its cost-effectiveness and use of local

materials. In modern construction, this traditional method is enhanced through

integrated systems that combine the natural strength of split stones with

contemporary engineering practices. Modern systems incorporate reinforced

concrete elements, mechanical compaction tools, high-performance mortar mixes,

and rigorous quality testing to achieve superior load-bearing capacity, seismic

resistance, and long-term durability.

By adopting modern approaches, contractors can overcome limitations of purely

traditional methods—such as potential voids or inconsistent bonding—while

maintaining the economic and environmental benefits of using abundant local stone

resources. These hybrid systems align with Indonesian National Standards (SNI) and

address the challenges of Indonesia’s earthquake-prone regions by improving

structural integrity and reducing settlement risks.

Key Materials in Modern Stone Rubble Foundations

Modern stone rubble foundations utilize premium split stones (batu belah) sized

15–25 cm with rough, irregular surfaces for excellent interlocking and mortar

adhesion. Stones must be hard, non-porous, and free from organic impurities.

Mortar mixes follow optimized ratios enhanced with modern admixtures:

- 1 Portland Cement : 3–4 Sand (with plasticizers for better workability)

- Addition of pozzolanic materials or lime for improved flexibility and reduced

cracking

- High-strength cement compliant with SNI 15-2049-2014

A critical modern upgrade is the integration of a reinforced concrete plinth beam

(sloof) or ring beam on top of the rubble masonry. This creates a hybrid system that

ties the foundation to the superstructure, enhancing box-action behavior and seismic

performance.

Modern Execution Techniques Step by Step

Contemporary construction employs precise planning and technology-assisted

methods:

1. Site Investigation and Excavation: Perform geotechnical soil testing to determine

bearing capacity. Excavate trenches using mechanical equipment for accuracy and

efficiency. Ensure depth reaches stable soil layers, typically below any soft topsoil.

2. Base Preparation with Mechanical Compaction: Compact the trench base to ≥95%

Proctor density using plate compactors or vibratory rollers. Install a drainage layer of

washed gravel or crushed stone at the bottom for better water management—a

modern drainage solution inspired by rubble trench concepts.

3. Alignment and Formwork: Use laser levels, digital theodolites, or advanced

surveying tools for precise horizontal and vertical alignment. Install reusable

formwork systems where needed.

4. Hybrid Stone Placement: Soak stones thoroughly. Place larger stones at the base in

interlocking patterns. Fill voids systematically with high-flow mortar, using vibration

tools or tamping rods to eliminate air pockets. Build in controlled 30–40 cm layers. In

modern systems, embed vertical reinforcement bars or anchors that connect directly

to the upper concrete elements.

5. Integration of Reinforced Concrete Elements: Pour a reinforced concrete plinth

band or sloof directly onto the completed rubble foundation. This modern addition

significantly improves lateral load resistance and prevents differential settlement.

6. Advanced Curing and Quality Assurance: Apply modern curing compounds or wet

curing methods for 7–14 days. Conduct on-site and laboratory tests, including mortar

compressive strength (target 5–10 MPa at 28 days) and non-destructive testing for

voids.

7. Backfilling and Protection: Use selected engineered fill in thin, compacted layers.

Install modern waterproofing membranes if required in high-moisture areas.

Advantages, Challenges, and Sustainability

Modern stone rubble systems offer 30–50% cost savings compared to full concrete

foundations, excellent thermal mass, and reduced carbon footprint by maximizing

local materials. The hybrid approach provides better seismic performance through

integrated reinforcement. Challenges such as labor dependency are mitigated with

skilled training and mechanical aids. In soft soils, combining with wider footings or

ground improvement techniques ensures reliability.

This system promotes sustainability by reducing reliance on energy-intensive

concrete while delivering structures that meet modern safety standards.

Pekerjaan Pondasi Batu Belah dengan Sistem Modern

Pendahuluan

Pondasi batu belah atau pondasi batu kali merupakan sistem pondasi dangkal yang

tetap populer di Indonesia berkat biaya terjangkau dan pemanfaatan bahan lokal.

Dalam era konstruksi modern, teknik tradisional ini ditingkatkan dengan sistem

hybrid yang menggabungkan kekuatan batu belah alami dengan elemen rekayasa

kontemporer. Sistem modern ini mencakup integrasi sloof beton bertulang, adukan

berperforma tinggi, alat pemadatan mekanis, serta pengujian kualitas laboratorium

untuk menghasilkan daya dukung yang lebih baik, ketahanan gempa, dan daya tahan

jangka panjang.

Pendekatan ini mengatasi kelemahan metode tradisional seperti rongga atau ikatan

tidak merata, sambil mempertahankan manfaat ekonomi dan lingkungan dari

penggunaan batu lokal.

Teknik Pelaksanaan Modern Langkah demi Langkah

1. Investigasi tanah geoteknik dan galian menggunakan alat mekanis.

5. Pengecoran sloof beton bertulang di atas pondasi batu untuk integrasi hybrid.

6. Curing modern dan pengujian kuat tekan serta non-destruktif.

7. Backfilling dengan material engineered dan pelindung waterproofing jika

diperlukan.

mekanis. Cocok untuk berbagai kondisi tanah dengan penyesuaian tambahan.
