1. Introduction

Renovating an occupied house requires balancing construction efficiency with the comfort and safety of residents who continue living in the property. Traditional “move-out” renovations are often impractical for homeowners due to cost, logistics, or personal preferences. Phased renovation strategies divide the project into manageable segments, allowing residents to remain in unaffected areas while work proceeds in designated zones.

The core principle is minimizing disruption through careful sequencing:

- Zoning: Divide the house into work zones (e.g., wet areas first, then dry areas).

- Phasing: Complete one zone before moving to the next.

- Protective Measures: Use temporary partitions, dust barriers, and noise control.

Key performance metrics include resident satisfaction scores, dust levels (PM10 < 50 µg/m³), noise levels (< 65 dB during daytime), and project duration variance. In tropical climates like Bali, high humidity accelerates material drying issues and increases mold risk, requiring additional ventilation and moisture control during renovation.

This paper provides a complete engineering framework based on international best practices.

3. Methodology and Best Practices

3.1 Project Planning and Zoning

Conduct detailed site assessment and create a renovation zoning plan. Prioritize high-impact areas (bathrooms, kitchens) during periods when residents can use alternative spaces.

3.2 Phased Scheduling

Use critical path method (CPM) with resident-friendly constraints. Example sequence:

Phase 1: Structural & wet areas → Phase 2: Electrical & plumbing → Phase 3: Finishing & painting.

3.3 Dust, Noise, and Safety Controls

- Install floor-to-ceiling plastic sheeting with zipper doors.

- Use HEPA-filtered negative air machines.

- Schedule noisy work during daytime only.

- Provide temporary kitchen/bathroom facilities if needed.

5. Challenges and Innovations in Tropical Occupied Renovations

Challenges include constant humidity affecting drying times, limited space for material storage, and family routines. Innovations: modular temporary partitions, low-VOC materials, real-time air quality monitoring, and digital apps for resident updates. Climate-adaptive scheduling (avoiding peak rainy season for exterior work) is essential.

1. Pendahuluan

Mer renovasi rumah yang masih dihuni memerlukan keseimbangan antara efisiensi konstruksi dengan kenyamanan dan keselamatan penghuni yang tetap tinggal di properti. Renovasi tradisional “pindah keluar” sering tidak praktis karena biaya, logistik, atau preferensi pribadi. Strategi renovasi bertahap membagi proyek menjadi segmen yang manageable, memungkinkan penghuni tetap berada di area yang tidak terdampak sementara pekerjaan berlangsung di zona yang ditentukan.

Prinsip inti adalah meminimalkan gangguan melalui sequencing yang cermat:

- Zoning: Membagi rumah menjadi zona kerja.

- Phasing: Menyelesaikan satu zona sebelum pindah ke zona berikutnya.

- Protective Measures: Menggunakan partisi sementara, barrier debu, dan pengendalian kebisingan.

Di iklim tropis seperti Bali, kelembaban tinggi mempercepat masalah pengeringan material dan meningkatkan risiko jamur, sehingga memerlukan ventilasi dan pengendalian kelembaban tambahan selama renovasi.

3. Metodologi dan Praktik Terbaik

3.1 Perencanaan Proyek dan Zoning

Lakukan penilaian situs rinci dan buat rencana zoning renovasi. Prioritaskan area berdampak tinggi (kamar mandi, dapur) selama periode penghuni dapat menggunakan ruang alternatif.

3.2 Penjadwalan Bertahap

Gunakan critical path method (CPM) dengan kendala ramah penghuni. Contoh urutan:

Phase 1: Struktural & area basah → Phase 2: Listrik & plumbing → Phase 3: Finishing & pengecatan.

3.3 Pengendalian Debu, Kebisingan, dan Keselamatan

- Pasang plastic sheeting lantai-ke-langit-langit dengan pintu zipper.

- Gunakan mesin negative air dengan filter HEPA.

- Jadwalkan pekerjaan berisik hanya pada siang hari.
