1. Introduction

Lightweight concrete blocks (bata ringan) such as AAC or Hebel are popular infill materials in reinforced concrete (RC) framed structures because they are lightweight, provide good thermal and sound insulation, and allow faster construction. However, these blocks have low tensile strength and tend to crack or separate from the surrounding RC frame due to shrinkage, thermal movement, or seismic forces.

Proper tying (pengikatan) of lightweight blocks to columns and beams is essential to ensure structural integrity, prevent out-of-plane failure, and maintain wall performance. This is particularly important in Bali, where high humidity accelerates shrinkage cracking and moderate-to-high seismic activity requires robust non-structural detailing.

This paper presents practical, beginner-friendly techniques for securing AAC/Hebel blocks to RC columns and beams, covering materials, spacing, installation steps, and quality control. It aligns with international best practices and relevant SNI standards.

2. Literature Review

Research on masonry infill in RC frames highlights that inadequate connections are a primary cause of cracking and detachment during earthquakes. Studies in *Engineering Structures* and *Journal of Building Engineering* recommend the use of wall ties, reinforcement mesh, or mechanical anchors at regular intervals to transfer forces and accommodate movement.

Indonesian standards (SNI 2847:2019) and seismic code SNI 1726:2019 require proper anchorage of non-structural walls to the main frame. Local practices in Bali often combine horizontal bond beams or mesh with vertical ties. Recent papers emphasize hybrid solutions using flexible ties or chemical anchors to allow differential movement while maintaining stability.

3. Theoretical Background and Connection Principles

# 3.1 Differential Movement

AAC blocks have high shrinkage (0.02–0.05%) compared to RC frames. Connections must accommodate this movement while providing lateral restraint.

# 3.2 Anchorage Capacity

The tensile capacity of wall ties or anchors must resist out-of-plane forces. Typical design force per tie is calculated based on tributary area and seismic acceleration.

Simple empirical spacing recommendation (common practice):

- Horizontal spacing: 400–600 mm

- Vertical spacing: 400–600 mm (every 2–3 courses)

# 3.3 Seismic Considerations

In moderate seismic zones like Bali, infill walls should be isolated from the frame with flexible joints at the top and sides, while still providing adequate ties to prevent collapse.

4. Practical Techniques for Tying Lightweight Blocks to Columns and Beams

# 4.1 Recommended Materials

- Galvanized or stainless steel wall ties (flat or twisted)

- Chicken wire mesh or welded wire fabric (diameter 2–3 mm)

- Chemical anchors or expansion bolts for direct fixing to concrete

- L-shaped or U-shaped ties embedded in mortar joints

# 4.2 Step-by-Step Installation for Beginners

1. Prepare the RC Frame: Ensure columns and beams are properly cured. Clean surfaces and mark tie locations.

2. Install Vertical Ties: Fix ties to columns at 400–600 mm vertical intervals using nails, screws, or chemical anchors. Bend ties into the mortar joint.

3. Lay Blocks with Mortar: Use thin-bed mortar (2–3 mm) for AAC blocks. Embed horizontal reinforcement mesh every 2–3 courses.

4. Connect to Beams: At the top of the wall, install flexible connections or leave a 10–20 mm gap filled with compressible material, while providing lateral ties.

5. Corner and Junction Detailing: Use corner ties or L-shaped reinforcement to connect perpendicular walls.

6. Control Joints: Provide vertical control joints every 4–6 m or at changes in wall height to accommodate shrinkage.

# 4.3 Common Mistakes and Solutions

- Insufficient ties → Add more frequent ties (every 400 mm).

- Rigid connection at top → Use flexible sealant or gap.

- Poor mortar quality → Use AAC-specific thin-bed mortar with proper mixing.

# 4.4 Quality Control

- Check tie embedment depth (minimum 50 mm into mortar joint).

- Verify alignment and plumbness.

- Perform pull-out tests on sample ties for critical projects.

5. Applications in Bali Residential and Villa Construction

In Bali villa projects, lightweight block infill is commonly used for internal partitions and external non-load-bearing walls. Proper tying techniques have proven effective in reducing post-construction cracking caused by shrinkage and minor seismic movements. Hybrid solutions combining mesh reinforcement with mechanical ties work particularly well on sloping sites with differential settlement risks.

Practical experience shows that well-tied AAC walls maintain integrity even during moderate earthquakes common in Bali.

1. Pendahuluan

Bata ringan (AAC/Hebel) populer sebagai dinding pengisi pada struktur rangka beton karena ringan, isolasi baik, dan pemasangan cepat. Namun, bata ringan memiliki kekuatan tarik rendah dan rentan retak atau lepas dari rangka RC akibat penyusutan, pergerakan termal, atau gempa.

Pengikatan yang tepat ke kolom dan balok sangat penting untuk menjaga integritas, mencegah kegagalan out-of-plane, dan mempertahankan performa dinding. Hal ini sangat relevan di Bali dengan kelembaban tinggi dan aktivitas seismik.

Makalah ini menyajikan teknik praktis yang ramah pemula untuk mengikat bata ringan ke kolom dan balok RC.

2. Tinjauan Pustaka

Penelitian tentang dinding infill masonry pada rangka RC menunjukkan bahwa sambungan yang tidak memadai adalah penyebab utama retak dan pelepasan saat gempa. Studi merekomendasikan penggunaan wall ties, mesh, atau anchor mekanis pada interval tertentu.

Standar Indonesia (SNI 2847:2019 dan SNI 1726:2019) mengharuskan pengikatan yang tepat untuk dinding non-struktural. Praktik lokal di Bali sering menggabungkan mesh horizontal dengan tie vertikal.

3. Latar Belakang Teori dan Prinsip Sambungan

# 3.1 Pergerakan Diferensial

Bata ringan memiliki penyusutan tinggi dibandingkan rangka RC. Sambungan harus mengakomodasi pergerakan ini sambil memberikan penahan lateral.

# 3.2 Kapasitas Jangkar

Kapasitas tarik tie atau anchor harus menahan gaya out-of-plane. Jarak tipikal: horizontal 400–600 mm, vertikal 400–600 mm.

# 3.3 Pertimbangan Seismik

Di zona seismik sedang seperti Bali, dinding infill sebaiknya diisolasi dengan sambungan fleksibel di atas dan samping, tetapi tetap diberi tie yang memadai.

4. Teknik Praktis Pengikatan Bata Ringan ke Kolom dan Balok

# 4.1 Material yang Direkomendasikan

- Wall ties baja galvanis atau stainless

- Mesh ayam atau welded wire fabric

- Chemical anchor atau expansion bolt

- Tie berbentuk L atau U yang ditanam di mortar joint

# 4.2 Langkah-langkah Pemasangan untuk Pemula

1. Persiapkan rangka RC yang sudah curing.

2. Pasang tie vertikal ke kolom setiap 400–600 mm.

3. Pasang bata dengan mortar tipis dan tanam mesh horizontal setiap 2–3 lapis.

4. Sambung ke balok dengan gap fleksibel di bagian atas.

5. Berikan tie khusus di sudut dan pertemuan dinding.

6. Buat control joint vertikal setiap 4–6 m.

# 4.3 Kesalahan Umum dan Solusi

- Tie kurang → Tambah frekuensi.

- Sambungan kaku di atas → Gunakan sealant fleksibel.

- Mortar buruk → Gunakan mortar khusus bata ringan.

# 4.4 Pengendalian Kualitas

- Periksa kedalaman tanam tie (minimal 50 mm).

- Verifikasi kerataan dan plumbness.

- Lakukan pull-out test pada sampel untuk proyek penting.
