Cost Comparison: Red Bricks vs Lightweight Bricks (AAC/Hebel) for Professional Construction in Bali

In Bali’s dynamic construction market, where villa, hotel, and residential projects demand both aesthetic value and cost efficiency, choosing the right wall material is a critical decision. Red bricks (bata merah) have been the traditional choice, while lightweight bricks such as AAC (Autoclaved Aerated Concrete) or Hebel are rapidly gaining popularity. This comprehensive 3-page technical guide provides a detailed cost comparison, performance analysis, and practical recommendations tailored to Bali’s climate and project requirements.

#### 1. Introduction to Both Materials

Red Bricks (Bata Merah)

Traditional clay bricks fired at high temperature. Size: 20 × 10 × 5 cm (common).

Advantages: Strong compressive strength (50–100 kg/cm²), good thermal mass, locally available, familiar to local workers.

Disadvantages: Heavy (3–4 kg/piece), high water absorption (15–25%), slower installation, requires thicker plaster.

Lightweight Bricks / AAC / Hebel

Autoclaved Aerated Concrete blocks. Size: 60 × 20 × 10 cm or 7.5 cm.

Advantages: Very light (0.6–0.8 kg/piece), fast installation, excellent thermal and sound insulation, low water absorption (<20%), straighter walls, reduced structural load.

Disadvantages: Lower compressive strength (25–50 kg/cm²), more brittle, higher initial material cost, requires special thin-bed mortar.

#### 2. Detailed Cost Comparison (per m² of Wall – Bali Market April 2026)

A. Material Cost

- Red Bricks: Rp 85,000 – Rp 110,000 / m² (including 5% waste)

- Lightweight Bricks (AAC/Hebel): Rp 135,000 – Rp 165,000 / m² (including 3% waste)

B. Labor Cost

- Red Bricks: Rp 45,000 – Rp 60,000 / m² (slower, needs skilled mason + helper)

- Lightweight Bricks: Rp 25,000 – Rp 35,000 / m² (much faster, 2–3 times quicker installation)

C. Mortar / Adhesive Cost

- Red Bricks (cement mortar): Rp 12,000 – Rp 18,000 / m²

- Lightweight Bricks (thin-bed mortar): Rp 18,000 – Rp 25,000 / m²

D. Plastering Cost

- Red Bricks (thick plaster 1.5–2 cm): Rp 35,000 – Rp 45,000 / m² (both sides)

- Lightweight Bricks (thin plaster 0.8–1 cm): Rp 20,000 – Rp 28,000 / m² (both sides)

Total Cost per m² (Finished Wall – both sides plastered & painted):

- Red Bricks: Rp 185,000 – Rp 235,000

- Lightweight Bricks: Rp 205,000 – Rp 255,000

Conclusion on Cost:

Although lightweight bricks have higher material cost, the total installed cost is often only 8–15% more expensive than red bricks. For projects with tight schedules, lightweight bricks are actually more economical due to faster construction and lower labor costs.

#### 3. Other Important Comparisons

Weight & Structural Impact

- Red Bricks: 180–220 kg/m² wall

- Lightweight Bricks: 60–80 kg/m² wall

→ Significant reduction in beam, column, and foundation size → 5–12% savings on structural concrete & steel in Bali villas.

Construction Speed

- Red Bricks: 4–6 m² per mason per day

- Lightweight Bricks: 12–18 m² per mason per day

Thermal Performance (Critical in Bali)

Lightweight bricks provide much better heat insulation, reducing air-conditioning electricity costs by 15–25% in hotels and villas.

Durability & Weather Resistance

Red bricks are stronger against impact but absorb more water. Lightweight bricks perform better in humid and coastal areas when properly protected with good waterproofing paint.

Quantity Calculation Example (100 m² Wall)

- Red Bricks: ± 1,050 pcs + mortar

- Lightweight Bricks: ± 85 m² block + thin-bed mortar

#### 4. When to Choose Which Material in Bali Projects

- Choose Red Bricks for: Budget projects, rural houses, load-bearing walls, traditional Balinese architecture.

- Choose Lightweight Bricks for: Luxury villas, hotels, modern homes, fast-track projects, upper floors, seismic-prone areas.

### Perbandingan Biaya Bata Merah vs Bata Ringan (Versi Bahasa Indonesia)

Author: edisupriyanto@gmail.com

Di pasar konstruksi Bali yang kompetitif, memilih antara bata merah dan bata ringan (AAC/Hebel) sangat memengaruhi biaya, waktu, dan kualitas akhir proyek. Panduan dua halaman ini memberikan perbandingan lengkap dan objektif.

#### 1. Gambaran Umum Kedua Material

Bata Merah:

Kelebihan: Kuat, murah, mudah didapat, cocok arsitektur tradisional Bali.

Kekurangan: Berat, lambat pasang, banyak waste, plaster tebal.

Bata Ringan (AAC/Hebel):

Kelebihan: Ringan, pasang cepat, isolasi panas & suara baik, dinding lebih rata.

Kekurangan: Harga material lebih mahal, butuh mortar khusus, lebih rapuh terhadap benturan.

#### 2. Perbandingan Biaya per m² Dinding (Bali 2026)

Material + Pasang + Plaster (kedua sisi):

- Bata Merah : Rp 185.000 – Rp 235.000 / m²

- Bata Ringan : Rp 205.000 – Rp 255.000 / m²

Meskipun bata ringan lebih mahal 8–15%, penghematan terjadi pada:

- Biaya tenaga kerja (lebih hemat 40–50%)

- Struktur beton & besi (ringan → hemat 5–12%)

- Waktu proyek (lebih cepat 30–50%)

#### 3. Perbandingan Lainnya

- Berat dinding: Bata Merah 200 kg/m² vs Bata Ringan 70 kg/m²

- Kecepatan pasang: Bata Ringan 2–3 kali lebih cepat

- Isolasi panas: Bata Ringan jauh lebih baik (hemat listrik AC)

- Ketahanan gempa: Bata Ringan lebih ringan & aman

#### 4. Rekomendasi Pemilihan

- Proyek hemat & tradisional → Bata Merah

- Villa mewah, hotel, proyek cepat → Bata Ringan
