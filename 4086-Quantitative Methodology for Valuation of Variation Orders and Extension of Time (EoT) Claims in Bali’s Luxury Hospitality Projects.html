Abstract

Variation Orders (VO), widely recognized as Change Orders, represent a significant variable in the financial performance of construction projects in Bali. Due to the bespoke nature of luxury villa designs and unpredictable site conditions in volcanic terrains, accurate valuation of these claims is critical to avoid protracted disputes. This paper proposes a standardized quantitative framework for VO valuation, integrating direct costs, indirect overheads, and prolongation costs. By utilizing the "Discrete Impact Analysis" method, this study provides a mathematical basis for equitable financial adjustments. The results demonstrate that transparent, formula-based claim submissions enhance the probability of approval by project owners and institutional investors.

2. Methodology: Systematic Valuation of Variations

The valuation of a VO must follow the hierarchy defined in the Conditions of Contract (e.g., FIDIC or SIA):

  1. Contract Rates: Utilizing existing Unit Price Analysis (AHS) for similar items.
  2. Pro-rata Rates: Adjusting existing rates based on changes in scale or complexity.
  3. Star Rates (New Rates): Synthesizing new unit prices based on current market quotations for labor, material, and equipment.

3. Mathematical Framework for Claim Calculation

To ensure a comprehensive claim, the Total Variation Value ($V_{total}$) is calculated as:

$$V_{total} = \sum (Q_{var} \times R_{unit}) + \text{OH}_{site} + \text{OH}_{hq} + \text{P}$$

Where:

  • $Q_{var}$ = Verified variation quantity.
  • $R_{unit}$ = Agreed unit rate.
  • $\text{OH}_{site}$ = Site-based overheads (temporary facilities, supervision).
  • $\text{OH}_{hq}$ = Head office overheads.
  • $\text{P}$ = Profit margin (typically 10-15%).

In cases where the VO causes a project delay, the Hudson Formula is applied to calculate prolongation costs:

$$\text{Claim Value} = \frac{\text{HO Overhead \%}}{100} \times \frac{\text{Contract Sum}}{\text{Contract Period}} \times \text{Delay Period}$$

This mathematical approach ensures that the contractor is compensated not just for the "bricks and mortar," but for the extended time resources consumed by the change.

4. Site-Specific Variables in Bali

  • Logistics Escalation: Transporting specialized materials to remote areas like Uluwatu or Ubud often incurs a 5-10% premium not found in standard city rates.
  • Seismic Reinforcement: Changes in structural layout in Bali require immediate re-verification of the Seismic Base Shear ($V$):
  • $$V = C_s \cdot W$$
  • Any increase in effective weight ($W$) due to variations must be accounted for in the structural cost adjustment.

Layanan audit dari Neurostruct menyusun klaim berdasarkan hierarki biaya:

  • Biaya Tidak Langsung: Menghitung dampak perubahan terhadap sewa alat, gaji supervisor, dan listrik kerja.

3. Rumus Teknis: Dasar Hitungan Biaya Tambah

Dalam menghitung nilai tambah akibat perubahan volume galian atau struktur, digunakan rumus deviasi:

$$\Delta \text{Biaya} = (Q_{aktual} - Q_{kontrak}) \times \text{Harga Satuan}$$

Namun, tantangan terbesar adalah jika VO tersebut menyebabkan proyek terlambat. Untuk menghitung biaya overhead kantor pusat yang hilang akibat keterlambatan tersebut, kita menggunakan Rumus Emden:

$$\text{Klaim Overhead} = \left( \frac{h}{100} \right) \times \left( \frac{c}{d} \right) \times n$$

Dimana:

  • $h$ = Persentase overhead kantor pusat (%).
  • $c$ = Nilai kontrak total.
  • $d$ = Durasi kontrak asli (hari).
  • $n$ = Jumlah hari keterlambatan akibat VO.

Rumus ini memastikan kontraktor tetap mendapatkan profit meski proyek berjalan lebih lama dari rencana awal karena adanya perubahan dari owner.
