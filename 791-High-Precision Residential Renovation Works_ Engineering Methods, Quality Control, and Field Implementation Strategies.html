Abstract

High-precision residential renovation is an essential aspect of modern civil engineering, particularly in urban redevelopment and architectural upgrading projects. Unlike new construction, renovation requires careful integration between existing structural systems and new engineering interventions. This paper presents a comprehensive engineering methodology for high-precision residential renovation works, focusing on structural assessment, demolition control, reinforcement techniques, material compatibility, and quality assurance systems. The study is based on field experience in tropical environments such as Bali, Indonesia, where structural aging, humidity, and material degradation significantly influence renovation strategies.

Keywords: renovation engineering, structural repair, precision construction, retrofitting, civil engineering, quality control.

1. Introduction

Residential renovation projects have become increasingly important in modern construction due to urban densification, property value enhancement, and structural aging of existing buildings. Unlike new construction, renovation involves complex engineering challenges such as load redistribution, partial demolition, and integration between old and new materials.

High-precision renovation requires advanced engineering planning to ensure safety, durability, and aesthetic consistency. In regions like Bali, where residential buildings are often exposed to high humidity and coastal environmental conditions, structural deterioration is more accelerated.

2. Engineering Challenges in Renovation Projects

Renovation projects face several technical challenges:

  • Unknown existing structural conditions
  • Material degradation over time
  • Inaccurate as-built drawings
  • Limited working space
  • Load redistribution during modification

These challenges require detailed structural assessment before execution.

3. Structural Assessment Methodology

Structural assessment is the first critical step in renovation engineering:

3.1 Visual Inspection

Identification of cracks, corrosion, and deformation.

3.2 Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Methods include:

  • Rebound hammer test
  • Ultrasonic pulse velocity
  • Rebar scanning

3.3 Load Capacity Evaluation

Structural members are analyzed to determine remaining capacity.

4. Precision Demolition Techniques

Controlled demolition is required to avoid damaging existing structures.

Methods include:

  • Manual dismantling
  • Sectional cutting
  • Low-vibration demolition tools

Precision demolition ensures structural stability during renovation phases.

5. Structural Reinforcement Methods

5.1 Concrete Jacketing

Increasing column or beam dimensions using additional reinforced concrete layers.

5.2 Steel Plate Strengthening

Steel plates are added to existing structures to increase load capacity.

5.3 Fiber Reinforced Polymer (FRP)

Modern method using carbon fiber for high-strength reinforcement.

6. Material Compatibility in Renovation

Material compatibility is critical to avoid future failure:

  • Old concrete vs new concrete bonding
  • Thermal expansion differences
  • Moisture resistance
  • Adhesion improvement using bonding agents

7. Construction Sequencing Strategy

Proper sequencing ensures structural safety:

  1. Structural assessment
  2. Temporary support installation
  3. Controlled demolition
  4. Reinforcement installation
  5. New construction integration
  6. Finishing works

8. Quality Control System

Quality assurance in renovation includes:

  • Continuous site supervision
  • Material testing
  • Alignment control
  • Crack monitoring
  • Load verification

9. Field Experience in Bali Renovation Projects

Based on field observations in Bali residential renovation projects:

  • Many villas require partial structural reinforcement
  • Coastal humidity accelerates steel corrosion
  • Improper past construction practices require correction
  • Space limitations affect equipment usage

Hybrid engineering solutions are often required.

10. Digital Tools in Renovation Engineering

Modern renovation projects utilize:

  • BIM (Building Information Modeling)
  • 3D laser scanning
  • Structural simulation software
  • Cost estimation tools (RAB systems)

These tools improve accuracy and reduce engineering risk.

11. Discussion

High-precision renovation is a multidisciplinary engineering field combining structural engineering, material science, and construction management.

The key success factor is accurate assessment of existing structures combined with precise execution control.

12. Conclusion

High-precision residential renovation requires detailed engineering analysis, controlled demolition techniques, and advanced reinforcement methods. Integration of modern technology and field experience significantly improves structural safety and project efficiency.
