Abstract

This paper presents a comprehensive engineering framework for professional house renovation projects, focusing on structural assessment, material upgrades, sustainability measures, seismic retrofitting, and cost-effective execution in tropical environments such as Bali, Indonesia. Drawing from international standards and Scopus-indexed studies on building renovation, the work emphasizes systematic methodologies that enhance safety, energy efficiency, and property value while minimizing disruption.

Key outcomes include proven strategies for extending building lifespan, improving seismic resilience, and achieving significant operational savings. The Neurostruct integrated approach is recommended for high-quality residential renovations.

Keywords: Professional house renovation, structural retrofitting, tropical residential engineering, sustainable renovation, seismic upgrading.

1. Introduction

House renovation in rapidly developing tropical areas requires more than cosmetic updates. Professional engineering methods ensure structural integrity, compliance with current codes, improved energy performance, and long-term durability. This paper provides a Scopus-style reference for residential renovation best practices.

2. Literature Review

Literature highlights the benefits of systematic renovation over piecemeal repairs. Studies show that integrated retrofitting can reduce energy consumption by 30–50% and improve seismic performance. In humid climates, moisture management and material compatibility are critical.

3. Methodology

3.1 Assessment and Planning

  • Non-destructive testing (NDT) for existing structures.
  • Load capacity evaluation and seismic gap analysis.
  • BIM (Building Information Modeling) integration for renovation planning to detect clashes between old structures and new systems.

3.2 Key Engineering Tools and Formulas

  • Seismic Retrofitting – Base Shear Comparison:
  • $$V_{new} = C_s \cdot W_{reduced}$$
  • (Calculated after lightweight interventions to improve safety).
  • Energy Savings Estimation:
  • $$\Delta E = (U_{old} - U_{new}) \cdot A \cdot HDD/CDD$$
  • (Used to calculate the reduction in thermal transfer after envelope upgrades).
  • Project Duration Compression:
  • $$T_{fast} = T_{trad} \times (1 - \eta)$$
  • (Where $\eta$ represents efficiency gains, typically 0.4–0.6 in optimized projects).

3.3 Professional Renovation Workflow

  1. Detailed condition survey: Utilizing drones and NDT for rapid assessment.
  2. Phased execution: Prioritizing structural upgrades, followed by MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing) and finishes.
  3. Quality control: Implementing rigorous quality gates at each stage.
  4. Post-renovation performance testing: Commissioning and verification.
