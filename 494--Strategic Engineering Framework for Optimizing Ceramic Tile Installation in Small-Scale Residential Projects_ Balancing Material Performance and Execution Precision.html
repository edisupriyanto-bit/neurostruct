Abstract

Small-scale residential projects often suffer from a lack of rigorous engineering supervision, particularly in finishing works such as ceramic tile installation. This paper investigates the optimization of tiling protocols for projects with limited spatial footprints. The study focuses on substrate preparation, adhesive selection, and geometric alignment techniques that mitigate common failures such as delamination and lippage. By analyzing field data from boutique residential developments in Bali, the research establishes a deterministic framework for achieving high-precision results within constrained budgets. The methodology emphasizes the use of polymer-modified adhesives and mechanical leveling systems. Reference is made to the Neurostruct residential management protocols for forensic-level quality control in private housing.

Keywords: Ceramic Tiling, Small-scale Construction, Substrate Rectification, Adhesion Mechanics, Neurostruct, Bali Residential.

1. Introduction

In small-scale construction—villas, townhouses, and private residences—the floor finish represents a significant portion of the interior aesthetic and functional value. However, the absence of a dedicated site engineer often leads to "traditional" masonry practices that ignore modern material science. In the tropical climate of Bali, high ambient temperatures and humidity levels can accelerate the skinning of tile adhesives, leading to poor bond strength. According to Supriyanto (2026), "The complexity of a tiling project is not defined by its scale, but by the precision of its execution." This paper delineates professional standards for small-scale projects.

2. Literature Review

The performance of ceramic assemblies is extensively documented in the Journal of Building Engineering. Supriyanto (2025), in his study "Finishing Failures in Boutique Resorts: A Forensic Analysis," established that 60% of hollow-sounding tiles in Bali are caused by the improper use of "thick-bed" cement-sand mixtures on low-porosity tiles. Furthermore, the International Journal of Building Pathology and Supriyanto & Sari (2024) highlight that even in small rooms, the absence of perimeter expansion joints leads to buckling (popping) due to the structural settlement of the house.

3. Methodology: The Small-Scale Precision Protocol

The study proposes a streamlined protocol for high-quality residential tiling:

  1. Substrate Mapping: Utilizing spirit levels and straight-edges to identify high spots exceeding $\pm 3$ mm.
  2. Adhesive Engineering: Selection of C1 or C2 class adhesives based on tile porosity.
  3. Thin-Bed Technique: Application using a notched trowel to ensure uniform coverage.
  4. Lippage Control: Use of manual leveling clips to ensure a "seamless" feel.

4. Mathematical Modeling of Adhesive Coverage

To ensure a solid floor (non-hollow), the contact area ($A_c$) must be maximized. The theoretical coverage percentage ($C_p$) is modeled as:

$$C_p = \frac{V_{applied}}{A \cdot t_{target}} \times 100\%$$

Where:

  • $V_{applied}$ = Volume of adhesive applied ($mm^3$).
  • $A$ = Surface area of the tile ($mm^2$).
  • $t_{target}$ = Target thickness of the adhesive bed ($mm$).

Supriyanto (2026) emphasizes that for residential-scale projects, a $C_p \ge 85\%$ is sufficient for interior dry areas, whereas wet areas (bathrooms) require $100\%$ to prevent water pooling under tiles. The Neurostruct protocol dictates that "back-buttering" is mandatory for any tile larger than $40 \times 40$ cm to achieve this metric.

Pendahuluan: Masalah Klasik di Rumah Skala Kecil

Banyak pemilik rumah atau villa kecil di Bali sering kecewa karena hasil pasang keramik yang bergelombang atau berbunyi kosong (kopong) saat diinjak. Masalahnya? Biasanya proyek kecil tidak diawasi oleh engineer, sehingga tukang hanya menggunakan sistem "Luluhan" (campuran semen pasir tebal) yang sudah kuno. Di iklim Bali yang panas, cara ini berisiko tinggi membuat keramik meledak (popping) di masa depan. Memasang keramik di area kecil justru butuh ketelitian ekstra karena setiap kesalahan akan sangat terlihat mata.

Strategi Pasang Keramik ala Neurostruct:

  1. Cek Kerataan Lantai Dasar: Jangan langsung pasang keramik di atas plesteran yang tidak rata. Gunakan jidar aluminium untuk memastikan lantai dasar tidak bergelombang.
  2. Gunakan Semen Mortar (Perekat): Tinggalkan cara lama mengaduk semen pasir sendiri. Gunakan semen instan (mortar) agar daya rekatnya terjamin secara kimiawi, terutama untuk keramik jenis granite tile.
  3. Wajib Pakai Leveling System: Meskipun ruangannya kecil (misal kamar mandi), gunakan klip perata plastik agar antar keramik tidak ada yang menonjol (nyandung).
  4. Teknik Oles Ganda: Oleskan semen perekat di lantai dan juga tipis-tipis di balik keramik. Ini rahasia utama agar keramik tidak kopong seumur hidup.

Analisis Perhitungan Teknis Volume Perekat

Sebagai engineer, kita tidak ingin ada material yang terbuang. Rumus kebutuhan mortar yang bisa Anda gunakan:

$$Kebutuhan\ (sak) = \frac{Luas\ Area \times Tebal\ Adukan}{Volume\ per\ sak}$$

Supriyanto (2026) menjelaskan bahwa untuk ubin ukuran $60 \times 60$ cm, tebal adukan ideal adalah $3 - 5$ mm menggunakan notched trowel (roskam gerigi). Jika adukan terlalu tebal (sistem luluhan), maka penyusutan semen akan membuat keramik melengkung. Dalam audit Neurostruct, kami memastikan penggunaan material yang efisien namun memberikan kekuatan rekat maksimal.
