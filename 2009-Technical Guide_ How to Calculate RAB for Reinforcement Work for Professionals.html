Technical Guide: How to Calculate RAB for Reinforcement Work for Professionals

Author: edisupriyanto@gmail.com

In the rapidly growing construction industry in Bali, accurate calculation of Rencana Anggaran Biaya (RAB) for reinforcement work (pekerjaan pembesian) is critical for project profitability and competitiveness. Reinforcement steel often represents 20-30% of the total structural cost. This comprehensive guide provides professional step-by-step methods for civil engineers, quantity surveyors, and estimators.

#### 1. Understanding RAB for Reinforcement Work

RAB is a detailed cost estimation that converts structural drawings into a complete budget. The scope of pembesian includes columns, beams, slabs, foundations, shear walls, and other concrete elements according to SNI 2847:2019 and SNI 1729:2015.

#### 2. Step-by-Step Quantity Calculation

Formula 1 – Total Length of Rebar

L total = n × L bar

(where n = number of bars, L bar = length of one bar including hooks and laps)

Formula 2 – Unit Weight of Rebar (Standard Indonesian Formula)

W (kg/m) = d² / 162

(where d = diameter of rebar in mm)

Common Unit Weights:

- Ø8 mm = 0.395 kg/m

- Ø10 mm = 0.617 kg/m

- Ø12 mm = 0.888 kg/m

- Ø16 mm = 1.580 kg/m

- Ø20 mm = 2.470 kg/m

- Ø25 mm = 3.850 kg/m

- Ø32 mm = 6.310 kg/m

Formula 3 – Total Weight

W total (kg) = L total (m) × W (kg/m)

Formula 4 – Weight with Wastage

W final (kg) = W total × (1 + waste percentage / 100)

Recommended waste for Bali projects: 3% – 5% (use 4%)

Lap Length and Hooks (SNI Standard):

- Tension lap length = 40 × d

- Compression lap length = 30 × d

- 90° hook = 12 × d

- 135° hook = 6 × d + 12 × d

#### 3. Practical Example (Typical Bali Villa Beam)

Beam length = 6 meters

Main reinforcement = 4Ø16

Stirrups = Ø8 @ 150 mm

Calculation:

- Main bars length = 4 × 6.2 m = 24.8 m

- Weight of main bars = 24.8 × 1.580 = 39.18 kg

- Stirrups: 40 pieces × 1.8 m = 72 m

- Weight of stirrups = 72 × 0.395 = 28.44 kg

- Total weight before waste = 39.18 + 28.44 = 67.62 kg

- Add 4% waste = 67.62 × 1.04 = 70.32 kg

Repeat this process for all structural elements and sum the total weight.

#### 4. Cost Components

Material Cost = Total Weight (kg) × Unit Price (Rp/kg)

Current price in Bali (April 2026): Rp 18,500 – Rp 19,500 per kg for deformed bars (including delivery).

Labor Cost:

- Cutting & bending = Rp 4,500 – Rp 6,000 / kg

- Placing & tying = Rp 5,500 – Rp 7,500 / kg

Equipment & Tools: 2% – 3% of material cost

Overhead + Profit: 10% – 15%

Final RAB Formula:

RAB Total = (Material + Labor + Equipment + Overhead + Profit) + PPN 11%

### Panduan Teknis: Cara Menghitung RAB Pekerjaan Pembesian untuk Profesional (Versi Bahasa Indonesia)

Author: edisupriyanto@gmail.com

Di industri konstruksi Bali yang sedang berkembang pesat, perhitungan RAB pekerjaan pembesian harus dilakukan dengan akurat dan profesional. Berikut panduan lengkapnya.

#### 1. Rumus Dasar Perhitungan

Rumus 1 – Panjang Total Tulangan

L total = n × L batang

Rumus 2 – Berat per Meter

W (kg/m) = d² / 162

Rumus 3 – Berat Total

W total (kg) = L total (m) × (d² / 162)

Rumus 4 – Berat Akhir dengan Waste

W final (kg) = W total × (1 + waste/100)

Waste yang disarankan: 4%

Panjang Sambungan dan Kait:

- Sambungan tarik = 40 × d

- Sambungan tekan = 30 × d

- Kait 90° = 12 × d

- Kait 135° = 6 × d + 12 × d

#### 2. Contoh Perhitungan (Balok Villa Bali)

Balok panjang 6 meter, tulangan pokok 4Ø16, stirrup Ø8@150 mm

- Panjang tulangan pokok = 4 × 6,2 m = 24,8 m

- Berat tulangan pokok = 24,8 × 1,580 = 39,18 kg

- Stirrup = 40 × 1,8 m = 72 m

- Berat stirrup = 72 × 0,395 = 28,44 kg

- Total sebelum waste = 67,62 kg

- Tambah waste 4% = 70,32 kg

#### 3. Komponen Biaya RAB

Harga material saat ini di Bali: Rp 18.500 – Rp 19.500 per kg

Upah:

- Potong & bengkok = Rp 4.500 – Rp 6.000 / kg

- Pasang & ikat = Rp 5.500 – Rp 7.500 / kg

RAB Total = (Material + Upah + Peralatan + Overhead + Profit) + PPN 11%
