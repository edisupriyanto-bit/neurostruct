Abstract

Deep foundation engineering in highly congested urban enclaves requires strict management of subterranean stress waves and environmental disturbances. Traditional driven spun piles, while cost-effective in open areas, generate significant elastodynamic shockwaves and lateral soil displacements that risk structural damage to adjacent historical or low-rise buildings. This paper evaluates the technical and geotechnical advantages of bored piling systems over driven displacement piles within high-density urban areas. Utilizing elastic wave equation modeling and shear strain parameterization, we analyze vibration attenuation rates ($v$) and skin friction mobilization ($f_s$) in stratified alluvial sands and volcanic soils characteristic of dense Balinese coastal enclaves. The empirical findings establish that bored piling techniques limit peak particle velocity (PPV) to well within safe international boundaries ($\text{PPV} \le 2.0\text{ mm/s}$), preventing structural distress in neighboring infrastructures while maximizing vertical load-bearing efficiency.

Keywords: Bored Piling, Driven Spun Piles, Elastodynamic Vibration, High-Density Urban Construction, Geotechnical Capacity, Neurostruct Bali Engineering.

1. Introduction

The rapid development of commercial and high-end hospitality real estate within the high-density urban and tourism zones of Bali—such as Kuta, Seminyak, Sanur, and Denpasar—presents critical geotechnical challenges. Constructing multi-story resorts or commercial complexes requires heavy vertical load support systems rooted deep in subgrade strata. Historically, driven displacement piles (such as precast concrete spun or square piles) have been preferred due to fast installation times and reliable quality control.

However, executing high-impact driving operations inside tightly packed urban plots creates substantial risks. The kinetic energy from impact hammers propagates through the soil as body and surface waves, inducing micro-cracks in neighboring concrete foundations, settlement in loose sandy soils, and severe acoustic disturbances. To mitigate these risks, bored piling has emerged as the premier professional standard. This study provides a rigorous geotechnical analysis comparing elastodynamic propagation, skin friction mobilization, and installation parameters between bored piles and driven piles, aligned with Indonesian National Standards (SNI 8460:2017).

2. Geotechnical Wave Mechanics and Mathematical Modeling

2.1 Elastodynamic Shockwave Attenuation Modeling

When an impact hammer strikes a precast driven pile, it transfers compressive stress waves through the pile shaft into the soil. The propagation of this ground vibration as a function of distance ($r$) from the source is governed by Bornitz’s empirical attenuation equation:

$$v_2 = v_1 \cdot \sqrt{\frac{r_1}{r_2}} \cdot e^{-\alpha \cdot (r_2 - r_1)}$$

Where:

  • $v_1$ = Peak particle velocity (PPV) at a known distance $r_1$ ($\text{mm/s}$)
  • $v_2$ = Predicted PPV at the target distance $r_2$ ($\text{mm/s}$)
  • $\alpha$ = Soil coefficient of absorption (ranging from $0.01\text{ m}^{-1}$ for hard tuffs to $0.04\text{ m}^{-1}$ for loose coastal sands)

For adjacent brick masonry or historical Balinese structures, international safety codes restrict the maximum allowable ground vibration to:

$$\text{PPV}_{max} \le 2.0\text{ mm/s}$$

Driven piling frequently generates a PPV exceeding $15.0\text{ mm/s}$ within a $10\text{-meter}$ radius, creating a high probability of structural damage. Bored piling, being a non-displacement rotary excavation technique, maintains a stable baseline ambient vibration profile ($\text{PPV} \le 0.5\text{ mm/s}$).

2.2 Deep Foundation Ultimate Bearing Capacity Formulations

The ultimate vertical bearing capacity ($Q_{ult}$) of a deep foundation unit comprises the total shaft skin friction resistance ($Q_s$) and the ultimate base end-bearing resistance ($Q_b$):

$$Q_{ult} = Q_s + Q_b = \pi \cdot D \cdot \sum (f_{si} \cdot \Delta L_i) + q_b \cdot A_b$$

Where:

  • $D$ = Pile diameter ($\text{mm}$)
  • $f_{si}$ = Unit skin friction capacity of soil layer $i$ ($\text{kN/m}^2$)
  • $\Delta L_i$ = Thickness of soil layer $i$ ($\text{m}$)
  • $q_b$ = Unit end-bearing resistance at the pile tip ($\text{MPa}$)
  • $A_b$ = Cross-sectional area of the pile base ($\text{mm}^2$)

For driven spun piles, unit skin friction ($f_s$) is enhanced due to lateral soil compaction during driving. For bored piles, the drilling process relaxes the surrounding stress field. To compensate for this stress relaxation and achieve identical load metrics without footprint growth, bored piling designs optimize the unit skin friction using the empirical polymer/bentonite slurry stabilization coefficient ($\beta_s$):

$$f_{si} = \beta_s \cdot \sigma'_{vi} \cdot \tan(\phi')$$

Where $\sigma'_{vi}$ represents the effective vertical overburden pressure and $\phi'$ is the effective internal soil friction angle.

3.1 Borehole Stabilization and Excavation Parameters

The borehole is excavated using high-torque rotary drilling rigs. To prevent the collapse of the borehole walls when drilling through loose sand formations below the water table, a high-density temporary steel casing ($2-4\text{ meters}$ length) is driven at the surface. Concurrently, the borehole is continuously filled with a polymer-modified bentonite slurry. The slurry hydrostatic pressure must exceed the active lateral earth and pore-water pressure ($p_w$) at all depths:

$$\gamma_{slurry} \cdot h_{borehole} > K_a \cdot \sigma'_v + u$$

3.2 Reinforcement Alignment and Tremie Concrete Pouring

Once the target design depth is reached and checked via sonic logging tools, cleaning augers remove loose base sediment. The pre-fabricated steel rebar cage—fitted with high-density concrete spacer wheels to guarantee a $75\text{ mm}$ uniform clear cover—is lowered into the slurry matrix.

Concrete pouring must be executed using the submerged Tremie Pipe Method. The bottom of the tremie pipe must remain submerged at least $2.0\text{ meters}$ below the rising concrete level to prevent mixing with the stabilizing slurry. Poured concrete must utilize a high-slump ($180 - 220\text{ mm}$), self-compacting concrete (SCC) mix with a minimum structural grade of $f'_c = 30\text{ MPa}$ ($K-350$).

1. Pendahuluan

Pembangunan proyek komersial, hotel, mall, dan villa mewah di pusat-pusat kota dan area wisata padat di Bali—seperti Kuta, Seminyak, Canggu, dan Sanur—menuntut akurasi tingkat tinggi dalam pemilihan sistem pondasi dalam. Struktur bangunan dengan beban vertikal masif memerlukan penopang yang mampu menembus tanah lunak hingga mencapai lapisan tanah keras (tufa vulkanik). Selama bertahun-tahun, pondasi tiang pancang (driven spun pile) menjadi andalan utama kontraktor karena harganya yang kompetitif dan pengerjaan yang cepat.

Namun, metode pemancangan tiang menggunakan drop hammer atau diesel hammer di area padat penduduk menyimpan bahaya laten yang sangat besar. Energi kejut dari palu pancang merambat melalui tanah sebagai gelombang seismik mikro yang merusak. Getaran ini memicu keretakan dinding beton tetangga, penurunan tanah (settlement) secara mendadak pada tanah pasir, serta polusi suara yang mengganggu kawasan pariwisata. Sebagai alternatif terbaik, Metode Bored Pile (tiang bor tempat) hadir sebagai standar profesional utama. Artikel ilmiah populer ini akan mengupas tuntas analisis rekayasa mekanika gelombang, perhitungan daya dukung tanah, serta panduan pemasangan bored pile berdasarkan SNI 8460:2017 agar proyek galian dan pondasi Anda berjalan aman tanpa komplain lingkungan.

2. Analisis Mekanika Gelombang Tanah dan Pemodelan Matematika

Perbedaan mendasar antara tiang pancang dan tiang bor terletak pada metode transfer energinya ke tanah sekitar selama masa konstruksi.

2.1 Analisis Redaman Getaran Seismik Mikro

Gelombang getaran akibat hantaman pemancangan tiang meluruh seiring pertambahan jarak ($r$) dari titik pusat pancang. Persamaan matematis peluruhan getaran (attenuation wave) dihitung dengan rumus Bornitz:

$$v_2 = v_1 \cdot \sqrt{\frac{r_1}{r_2}} \cdot e^{-\alpha \cdot (r_2 - r_1)}$$

Berdasarkan standar geoteknik internasional untuk pemukiman perkotaan dan bangunan cagar budaya, nilai kecepatan partikel puncak (Peak Particle Velocity / PPV) tidak boleh melewati ambang batas aman:

$$\text{PPV}_{max} \le 2.0\text{ mm/s}$$

Metode tiang pancang konvensional menghasilkan getaran destruktif dengan nilai PPV mencapai $15-20\text{ mm/s}$ pada jarak $10\text{ meter}$, yang dipastikan memicu keretakan dinding bata rumah sekitar. Sebaliknya, metode bored pile menggunakan sistem rotasi bor tanpa ketukan (non-displacement pile), sehingga getaran operasionalnya berada di bawah nilai baku mutu aman ($\text{PPV} \le 0.5\text{ mm/s}$).

2.2 Formula Perhitungan Daya Dukung Fondasi Dalam

Daya dukung ultimit ($Q_{ult}$) pondasi dalam dihitung berdasarkan akumulasi tahanan gesek dinding (skin friction / $Q_s$) dan tahanan ujung tiang (end-bearing / $Q_b$):

$$Q_{ult} = \pi \cdot D \cdot \sum (f_{si} \cdot \Delta L_i) + q_b \cdot A_b$$

Pada tiang pancang, tanah di sekeliling tiang mengalami pemadatan lateral spontan akibat desakan volume tiang. Pada bored pile, proses pengeboran menyebabkan relaksasi tegangan tanah (tanah sedikit melonggar). Untuk menjaga agar nilai gaya gesek ($f_s$) tetap maksimal tanpa perlu memperbesar diameter tiang, cairan stabilisasi lubang bor (polymer/bentonite slurry) wajib disesuaikan kadarnya mengikuti persamaan:

$$f_{si} = \beta_s \cdot \sigma'_{vi} \cdot \tan(\phi')$$

Di mana $\beta_s$ adalah koefisien empiris kualitas cairan polimer pembilas yang menjaga integritas dinding lubang dari risiko longsor internal.

3. Panduan Langkah-Demi-Langkah Pelaksanaan Bored Pile di Lapangan

Untuk mendapatkan hasil pondasi tiang bor yang sempurna tanpa risiko membuat tanah tetangga amblas, ikuti urutan prosedur baku teknik sipil berikut:

Langkah 1: Penentuan Koordinat Presisi dan Pemasangan Casing Sementara

Tentukan titik bor menggunakan Total Station dengan akurasi millimeter. Pasang pipa selubung baja (temporary steel casing) sedalam $2-4\text{ meter}$ di bagian atas lubang bor. Pipa ini berfungsi sebagai pemandu mata bor sekaligus mencegah runtuhnya tanah permukaan (topsoil) akibat pergerakan alat berat.

Langkah 2: Proses Pengeboran dan Injeksi Cairan Bentonit / Polimer

Lakukan pengeboran menggunakan mesin Rotary Drilling Rig. Sembari mata bor (auger atau drilling bucket) mengeruk tanah ke luar, lubang galian wajib diisi cairan bentonit atau polimer secara terus-menerus. Tinggi cairan di dalam lubang bor harus dipertahankan minimal $1.5\text{ meter}$ di atas muka air tanah guna menghasilkan tekanan hidrostatik penyeimbang yang menahan dinding tanah agar tidak longsor.

Langkah 3: Pembersihan Dasar Lubang Bor (Desanding / Cleaning)

Setelah mencapai kedalaman rencana (misal menembus tanah keras sedalam $20\text{ meter}$), bersihkan dasar lubang bor dari sisa-sisa lumpur endapan menggunakan cleaning bucket. Endapan lumpur yang tebal di dasar lubang harus dihindari karena dapat menurunkan kapasitas dukung ujung tiang (end bearing) secara drastis setelah beton mengeras.

Langkah 4: Penurunan Keranjang Besi Tulangan (Rebar Cage)

Masukkan rangkaian besi tulangan yang telah dirakit ke dalam lubang bor secara tegak lurus. Pastikan keranjang besi dilengkapi dengan tahu beton berbentuk roda (concrete wheel spacers) di sekelilingnya dengan ketebalan minimal $75\text{ mm}$. Selimut beton yang tebal ini wajib ada untuk melindungi besi utama dari korosi akibat air tanah yang korosif.

Langkah 5: Pengecoran Beton Metode Pipa Tremie

Lakukan pengecoran beton menggunakan Metode Pipa Tremie (pipa logistik pengecoran yang dimasukkan hingga ke dasar lubang).

PROTOKOL WAJIB KONTRAKTOR: Selama proses pengecoran berlangsung, ujung bawah pipa tremie harus selalu tertanam minimal $2.0\text{ meter}$ di dalam adukan beton segar yang sedang naik ke atas. Beton segar berkekuatan tinggi ($f'_c \ge 30\text{ MPa}$ / $K-350$) dengan nilai slump tinggi ($18-22\text{ cm}$) akan mendorong lumpur polimer naik ke permukaan luar lubang tanpa terjadi pencampuran (segregasi), menghasilkan tiang beton yang padat, homogen, dan tanpa cacat rongga.
