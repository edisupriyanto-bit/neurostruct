II. Literature Review

The Rational Method remains the standard for courtyard runoff estimation:

\[ Q = C \cdot I \cdot A \]

where \(Q\) is peak runoff (m³/s), \(C\) is runoff coefficient, \(I\) is rainfall intensity (mm/h), and \(A\) is catchment area (ha). For Bali’s tropical conditions, \(C = 0.7–0.95\) for impervious courtyards.

Manning’s equation governs open-channel and pipe flow design:

\[ V = \frac{1}{n} R^{2/3} S^{1/2} \]

\[ Q = V \cdot A \]

where \(n\) is Manning’s roughness coefficient, \(R\) is hydraulic radius, and \(S\) is slope. Recent studies (e.g., Liu et al., 2023 in *Journal of Hydrology*) confirm that hybrid permeable-pavement + French-drain systems reduce ponding time by 85% in tropical urban settings. Indonesian research by Widodo et al. (2024) on Denpasar sites showed that inadequate grading causes 68% of courtyard failures, while SNI 03-2847-2019 and ACI 350 emphasize minimum 2% slopes and waterproof membranes.

IV. Efficient Step-by-Step Mitigation Protocol

Step 1: Site Assessment & Hydraulic Analysis

Conduct topographic survey (total station) and calculate design storm (10-year, 1-hour intensity per BMKG data).

Step 2: Courtyard Regrading

Re-profile surface to minimum 2% slope toward collection points:

\[ \Delta h = S \cdot L \]

where \(\Delta h\) is elevation difference and \(L\) is length.

Step 3: Subsurface Drainage Installation

Install French drains (300 mm Ø perforated pipe, gravel envelope):

\[ Q_{\text{drain}} = \frac{1}{n} \cdot A \cdot R^{2/3} \cdot S^{1/2} \]

Connect to main stormwater line or infiltration well.

Step 4: Permeable Pavement Overlay

Apply 100–150 mm porous concrete or paver system over geotextile. Infiltration rate target >100 mm/h.

Step 5: Waterproofing & Edge Protection

Apply crystalline waterproofing membrane (ACI 350 compliant) and install perimeter drains with check valves.

Step 6: Rainwater Harvesting Integration

Divert excess to underground tanks for non-potable reuse, reducing net runoff by 40%.

Step 7: Construction Sequencing & Quality Control

Phased implementation during dry season; daily laser-level checks for slope.

Step 8: Post-Construction Monitoring

Install IoT water-level sensors; verify performance after first monsoon.
