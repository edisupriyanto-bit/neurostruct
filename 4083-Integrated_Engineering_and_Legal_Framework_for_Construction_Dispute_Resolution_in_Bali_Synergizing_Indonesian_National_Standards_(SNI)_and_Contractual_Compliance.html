Abstract

Construction disputes in Bali have become increasingly complex due to the intersection of rapid investment, high seismic risks, and specific local regulatory requirements. This paper analyzes the critical role of the Indonesian National Standards (SNI) and contractual integrity in resolving engineering-related conflicts. By evaluating structural non-compliance as a breach of contract, the study proposes a forensic audit framework to provide objective evidence in mediation and arbitration. The integration of technical compliance with legal-contractual obligations is demonstrated to be the most effective mechanism for dispute mitigation in the Balinese hospitality and residential sectors.

1. Introduction

The construction sector in Bali is a cornerstone of the regional economy, yet it is plagued by frequent disputes between owners and contractors. These conflicts often stem from "Quality Deviations" where the finished structure fails to meet the safety benchmarks set by the Indonesian government. In the context of Bali's Seismic Zone 4 and 5, strict adherence to SNI 1726 (Seismic Design) and SNI 2847 (Structural Concrete) is not merely a technical choice but a contractual obligation.

2. Methodology: Synergistic Dispute Analysis

The research employs a dual-track investigative methodology:

  • Technical Track: Utilizing Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) to verify in-situ material strength against SNI requirements.
  • Contractual Track: Cross-referencing technical findings with the "Standard Conditions of Contract" to identify specific clauses of default or negligence.

3. Mathematical Models for Structural Compliance Verification

In engineering disputes, the "Safety Margin" ($M_s$) acts as the primary forensic metric. The structural capacity ($R_n$) is compared against the actual load demand ($S_u$):

$$M_s = \frac{\phi R_n}{S_u} - 1$$

Where:

  • $\phi$ = Strength reduction factor as per SNI 2847:2019.
  • $R_n$ = Nominal resistance based on field-verified rebar density and concrete grade.
  • $S_u$ = Required strength based on the latest seismic response spectra ($S_{DS}$ and $S_{D1}$).

If $M_s < 0$, the contractor is technically and legally in breach of contract regarding the "Fitness for Purpose" of the structure. Furthermore, for seismic displacement disputes, the inter-story drift ($\Delta$) must comply with:

$$\Delta_{actual} \leq \frac{\Delta_{allowable}}{\rho}$$

Where $\rho$ is the redundancy factor. Failure to meet this inequality provides indisputable data for the arbitrator.

4. The Intersection of SNI and Contract Clauses

Most construction contracts in Bali include a general clause stating that "Work must be performed in accordance with prevailing Indonesian laws and standards." This makes every SNI violation a potential point of litigation.

  1. SNI 1726:2019: Focuses on the ductility of the building. Non-compliant stirrup spacing in columns is a common source of sengketa (dispute).
  2. SNI 2847:2019: Dictates the minimum concrete cover. In Bali’s coastal areas (e.g., Uluwatu), insufficient cover leads to premature corrosion, a "latent defect" often claimed by owners.

1. Pendahuluan: Mengapa Sengketa di Bali Begitu Unik?

Membangun villa atau hotel di Bali (Ubud, Canggu, Uluwatu) menghadapi dua tantangan besar: risiko gempa bumi yang tinggi dan paparan udara laut yang korosif. Sengketa sering muncul ketika bangunan mulai retak atau miring. Masalahnya, kontraktor sering berdalih bahwa itu "hal wajar." Di sinilah peran Konsultasi Sengketa Konstruksi diperlukan untuk membuktikan bahwa retakan tersebut adalah pelanggaran terhadap SNI 2847 atau SNI 1726.

2. Metode Penyelesaian Sengketa Berbasis SNI

Penyelesaian sengketa yang adil harus melalui tiga tahapan:

  • Audit Teknis: Mengecek apakah besi dan beton yang terpasang sesuai dengan gambar rencana dan standar nasional.
  • Analisis Kontrak: Meninjau pasal-pasal mengenai cacat mutu dan tanggung jawab pemeliharaan.
  • Mediasi Teknik: Menggunakan hasil audit sebagai alat bukti utama untuk menuntut perbaikan atau ganti rugi.

3. Rumus Teknis: Dasar Pembuktian di Meja Hijau

Dalam sengketa mutu beton, auditor menggunakan perhitungan kekuatan karakteristik ($f'_c$) untuk menentukan apakah beton tersebut "layak" atau "produk gagal". Rumus penerimaan hasil uji tekan adalah:

$$f'_{rata-rata} \geq f'_c + 1.34s$$

Dimana:

  • $f'_c$ = Kuat tekan rencana dalam kontrak.
  • $s$ = Standar deviasi hasil uji lapangan.

Jika hasil lapangan tidak memenuhi rumus ini, maka secara teknis kontraktor telah melakukan wanprestasi mutu. Selain itu, untuk sengketa lendutan (deflection) pada balok panjang yang sering terjadi di villa mewah, digunakan batas izin:

$$\text{Lendutan Izin} = \frac{\text{Panjang Bentang (L)}}{360}$$

Jika lendutan aktual lebih besar dari batas ini, maka owner berhak menuntut perkuatan struktur atau pembongkaran.

4. Pasal Kontrak dan Kepatuhan SNI

Sebuah kontrak konstruksi yang baik di Bali harus mengikat kontraktor pada standar terbaru. Pelanggaran terhadap jarak sengkang (begel) kolom, misalnya, bukan sekadar kesalahan sepele, melainkan pelanggaran terhadap standar daktilitas gempa yang membahayakan nyawa. Dalam sengketa, data dari Rebar Scan dan Hammer Test adalah "saksi bisu" yang tidak bisa dibantah oleh argumen lisan kontraktor.
