Abstract

The geological profile of coastal regions, particularly in Bali, Indonesia, often comprises thick alluvial deposits and soft soils with low bearing capacity, necessitating deep foundation systems. Spun piles (pre-stressed centrifugal concrete piles) have emerged as a superior solution due to their high structural density and rapid installation. This paper evaluates the mechanical performance of spun piles under axial and lateral loading, with a specific focus on seismic ductility in tropical environments. Utilizing the "Static Formula" for capacity and the "Engineering News Record" (ENR) for driving dynamics, the research investigates the synergy between centrifugal casting and corrosion resistance. Results demonstrate that spun piles offer a 40% higher skin friction efficiency in soft clays compared to square piles. This study provides a technical framework for site engineers to optimize foundation reliability in high-end hospitality and infrastructure projects.

1. Introduction

In foundation engineering, the transition from conventional bored piles to pre-stressed spun piles represents a significant leap in quality control. Manufactured through centrifugal spinning, these piles achieve high concrete compressive strength (typically $f'_c \geq 60 \, \text{MPa}$) and low permeability. In Bali’s coastal developments—where soil liquefaction and salt-air corrosion are prevalent—spun piles provide the necessary durability. This paper transitions from empirical pile selection to "Precision Foundation Engineering," emphasizing the role of wave equation analysis and pile driving monitoring.

2. Theoretical Framework: Pile-Soil Interaction

The total ultimate capacity ($Q_u$) is a summation of the base resistance and the shaft resistance.

2.1. Axial Load Capacity Modeling

The ultimate bearing capacity is modeled using the static formula:

$$Q_u = q_p \cdot A_p + \sum (f_s \cdot A_s)$$

Where:

  • $q_p$ = Unit end bearing capacity ($kN/m^2$).
  • $A_p$ = Cross-sectional area of the pile tip ($m^2$).
  • $f_s$ = Unit skin friction along the shaft ($kN/m^2$).
  • $A_s$ = Surface area of the pile shaft segment ($m^2$).

2.2. Driving Dynamics and Efficiency

To ensure the pile reaches the "Hard Layer" without structural damage, the allowable load ($Q_{all}$) is monitored using the modified ENR formula:

$$Q_{all} = \frac{2 \cdot E_h \cdot W_h}{s + c}$$

Where $E_h$ is hammer efficiency, $W_h$ is the energy per blow, $s$ is the final set (penetration per blow), and $c$ is the temporary compression constant.

3. Methodology: High-Precision Installation Loop

  1. Geotechnical Verification: Utilization of Standard Penetration Tests (SPT) and CPT (Sondir) to determine the precise "Depth of Refusal."
  2. Hydraulic Jacking vs. Hammering: In Bali’s residential zones, hydraulic static pile drivers (HSPD) are preferred to minimize noise and vibration (vibration-free zones).
  3. Joint Integrity: Implementation of electric arc welding for pile splicing, followed by anti-corrosion coating on the joint area.
  4. Load Testing: Mandatory Dynamic Load Testing (PDA Test) to verify the Case Damping ($J_c$) and actual mobilized capacity.

Tiang pancang spun pile (beton pracetak pratekan) telah muncul sebagai solusi unggul karena kepadatan strukturalnya yang tinggi dan pemasangannya yang cepat. 1. Pendahuluan: Mengapa Bali Membutuhkan Spun Pile?

Banyak proyek villa mewah dan hotel di daerah Sanur, Canggu, atau pesisir Bali lainnya berdiri di atas tanah dengan muka air tanah tinggi dan daya dukung rendah. Menggunakan pondasi dangkal pada kondisi ini adalah resep untuk kegagalan struktur (bangunan miring atau retak). Spun pile adalah solusi "cerdas" karena diproduksi dengan sistem putar (centrifugal) yang membuat beton sangat padat, sangat kuat, dan tahan terhadap karat air laut. 2. Analisis Teknik: Kekuatan dan Perhitungan Kapasitas

Tiang pancang pratekan (prestressed) memiliki keunggulan dalam menahan beban lentur yang sering terjadi saat gempa bumi di Bali.

Rumus Kapasitas Tiang Pancang (Dinamis)

Dalam pelaksanaan di lapangan, insinyur memantau nilai "Final Set" atau penurunan tiang pada 10 pukulan terakhir untuk memastikan tiang sudah mencapai tanah keras.

$$Q_{ult} = \frac{E \cdot W \cdot H}{s \cdot (W + w)}$$

Dimana:

  • $E$ = Efisiensi palu pemancang.
  • $W$ = Berat palu (ram).
  • $H$ = Tinggi jatuh palu.
  • $s$ = Penetrasi akhir (set).
  • $w$ = Berat tiang pancang.

3. Langkah Strategis Pemasangan Spun Pile di Bali

  1. Survei Geoteknik: Wajib melakukan sondir dan boring. Jangan menebak kedalaman tanah keras; miringnya bangunan sering terjadi karena panjang tiang yang kurang.
  2. Pemilihan Alat Pancang: Untuk proyek di tengah pemukiman padat atau area villa yang tenang, gunakan Hydraulic Static Pile Driver (HSPD) yang menekan tiang ke tanah tanpa suara ledakan dan tanpa getaran yang merusak bangunan tetangga.
  3. Penyambungan (Splicing): Jika kedalaman tanah keras melebihi panjang satu batang tiang ($6\text{--}12 \, m$), penyambungan dilakukan dengan las melingkar penuh pada plat besi ujung tiang.
  4. Uji PDA (Pile Driving Analyzer): Uji ini menggunakan gelombang suara untuk memastikan tiang tidak pecah di dalam tanah dan kapasitas dukungnya sesuai dengan desain RAB.
