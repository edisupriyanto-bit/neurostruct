Abstract

This paper presents a comprehensive engineering analysis of professional wall plastering methods for residential and commercial buildings in tropical climates. Keywords: Professional wall plastering, tropical construction, durability, crack control, surface engineering.

1. Introduction

Wall plastering is a critical finishing process affecting aesthetics, durability, and moisture resistance. In humid, saline tropical environments like Bali, professional methods are essential to prevent common defects such as cracking, efflorescence, and delamination. This study provides a rigorous framework aligned with international best practices.

2. Literature Review

Research emphasizes proper substrate preparation and admixture use for enhanced adhesion. Polymer-modified plasters show superior performance in aggressive climates. Lifecycle studies highlight the importance of thickness control and curing for longevity.

3. Methodology

3.1 Material Selection and Mix Design

  • Base coat: Cement-sand with admixtures.
  • Finish coat: Finer gypsum or polymer-modified.
  • AAC/Hebel substrates: Special thin-coat or skim formulations.
  • Mix Proportion Example: Cement : Sand : Water = 1 : 4–6 (by volume), with plasticizers 0.1–0.5%.

3.2 Application Techniques

  1. Surface preparation: Cleaning, priming, and hacking if required.
  2. Dash coat: Applied for enhanced bonding.
  3. Multi-layer application: Execution with controlled thickness.
  4. Curing protocols: Moist curing for 7–14 days.

3.3 Key Formulas (Word Compatible)

  • Plaster Thickness and Coverage:
  • $$Volume = A \times t$$
  • (Where A = area, t = thickness; typical 12–20 mm).
  • Adhesion Strength: Target 0.5–1.0 MPa per standards.
  • Moisture Content Control: Optimal substrate moisture <12–15% before application.

2. Analisis Teknik: Plesteran pada Bata Ringan (Hebel/AAC)

Bata ringan memerlukan teknik khusus karena memiliki daya serap air yang tinggi.

  • Tantangan: Jika menggunakan semen biasa tanpa aditif, air dalam adukan plesteran akan terserap habis oleh bata Hebel, menyebabkan plesteran kering seketika dan pecah.
  • Solusi: Penggunaan thin-bed atau skim coat polimer yang dirancang khusus untuk AAC untuk memastikan ikatan molekuler yang kuat.

3. Langkah Strategis Plesteran Profesional

  1. Persiapan Dinding: Bersihkan dinding dari debu dan minyak. Lakukan penyiraman air secukupnya agar dinding dalam kondisi jenuh kering permukaan (saturated surface dry).
  2. Pembuatan Kepalaan (Kamprot): Buat jalur panduan vertikal (kepalaan) yang presisi menggunakan lot atau laser agar dinding tidak bergelombang.
  3. Aplikasi Multi-Lapis: Untuk hasil premium, gunakan lapisan dasar (base coat) untuk perataan, diikuti lapisan akhir (finish coat/acian) untuk kehalusan maksimal.
  4. Proses Curing: Siram dinding yang baru selesai diplester minimal 2 kali sehari selama 7 hari untuk mencegah retak susut akibat penguapan yang terlalu cepat di iklim panas Bali.
