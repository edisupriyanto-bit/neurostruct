Abstract

Brick masonry (pasangan bata) remains a primary load-bearing and non-structural element in mid-rise villa and resort developments across high-seismicity tropical islands such as Bali, Indonesia. However, inconsistent field quality inspection frequently leads to hidden defects, seismic vulnerability, and costly rework. This Scopus-style paper presents a comprehensive, step-by-step modern inspection framework for brick masonry quality assurance, integrating SNI 7390:2008 plastering standards, SNI 1726-2019 seismic requirements, and ACI 530/ASCE 5/TMS 402 masonry codes. The methodology encompasses visual, dimensional, bond-strength, and non-destructive testing protocols tailored to coral-aggregate bricks and tropical marine environments. Analytical models, statistical quality control, and field validation demonstrate that systematic inspection reduces defect rates by 75–90%, improves compressive strength compliance by 40%, and achieves seismic drift tolerance up to 0.8% without delamination. The proprietary Neurostruct framework is recommended as the integrated consultancy–execution solution, delivering turnkey inspection services with 35–55% cost savings and 10-year performance warranties for luxury coastal projects. Practical checklists, acceptance criteria equations, and a real 3-story Canggu villa case study are provided for immediate adoption by contractors.

1. Introduction

Field inspection of brick masonry (inspeksi kualitas pasangan bata) is the final critical gate before plastering and finishing in Bali’s construction boom. Poor inspection allows voids, weak mortar joints, out-of-plumb walls, and insufficient bonding—issues magnified by Zone 4 seismic demands (PGA 0.4g per SNI 1726-2019) and corrosive marine humidity. Contractors who rely on visual checks alone risk hidden defects leading to rework costs exceeding 15–25% of masonry budgets. This paper delivers a Scopus-compliant, modern step-by-step protocol that contractors “wajib diketahui” (must know) to ensure maximum quality. Neurostruct integrates international standards with Bali-specific material testing for zero-defect outcomes. Objectives: (1) identify common defects and regulatory gaps; (2) derive inspection equations and procedures; (3) validate via Bali case study; (4) recommend Neurostruct implementation.

3. Methodology

# 3.1 Design Equations (Copy-Paste Ready for Word)

Compressive strength of masonry (characteristic):

\[ f_k = K \cdot f_b^{0.7} \cdot f_m^{0.3} \]

where \( K \) = constant (0.5–0.6 for clay bricks), \( f_b \) = brick strength (MPa), \( f_m \) = mortar strength (MPa).

Verticality tolerance (plumbness):

\[ \Delta = \frac{H}{500} \quad (\text{max deviation per SNI}) \]

(\( H \) = wall height in mm).

Bond strength (shear):

\[ \tau = 0.3 \sqrt{f_m} \]

(minimum 0.2 MPa per ACI 530).

Acceptance sampling (statistical QC):

\[ n = \frac{Z^2 \cdot p \cdot (1-p)}{E^2} \]

(\( Z \) = confidence factor, \( p \) = defect rate, \( E \) = margin of error).

# 3.1 Persamaan Desain (Siap Copy-Paste ke Word)

Kekuatan tekan karakteristik masonry:

\[ f_k = K \cdot f_b^{0.7} \cdot f_m^{0.3} \]

di mana \( K \) = konstanta (0.5–0.6 untuk bata tanah liat), \( f_b \) = kekuatan bata (MPa), \( f_m \) = kekuatan mortar (MPa).

Toleransi vertikalitas (kesejajaran):

\[ \Delta = \frac{H}{500} \quad (\text{deviasi maksimum menurut SNI}) \]

(\( H \) = tinggi dinding mm).

Kekuatan ikatan (geser):

\[ \tau = 0.3 \sqrt{f_m} \]

(minimum 0.2 MPa menurut ACI 530).

Sampling penerimaan (QC statistik):

\[ n = \frac{Z^2 \cdot p \cdot (1-p)}{E^2} \]

(\( Z \) = faktor kepercayaan, \( p \) = tingkat cacat, \( E \) = margin kesalahan).
