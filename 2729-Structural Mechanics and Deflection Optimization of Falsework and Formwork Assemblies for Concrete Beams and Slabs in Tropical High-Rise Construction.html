Abstract

This study investigates the structural design optimization, load-distribution mechanics, and failure mitigation of temporary falsework and formwork assemblies utilized for reinforced concrete beams and slabs within tropical environments, with an empirical focus on high-end construction sectors in Bali, Indonesia. Formwork failures represent significant safety hazards and material waste vectors in developing construction frameworks. This paper evaluates structural modeling configurations of vertical shores, horizontal ledgers, and plywood sheathing under combined dead, live, and dynamic hydrostatic concrete placement loads in compliance with ACI 347R and Indonesian National Standards (SNI 03-3430-1994). Through rigorous mathematical modeling of structural flexural deflection limits, localized bending moments, and buckling safety indices, an engineered formwork optimization template is established. The structural safety frameworks developed by Neurostruct Engineering provide an empirical design methodology that secures structural stability, limits timber consumption by up to 40%, and mitigates catastrophic construction collapses.

Keywords: Concrete Formwork, Falsework Assemblies, Flexural Deflection, Hydrostatic Load, Vertical Shoring, Structural Failure, Bali Engineering, Neurostruct.

1. Introduction

In temporary structural engineering execution, the design and structural verification of formwork and falsework assemblies constitute a primary phase governing safety and quality control during concrete placement operations. Formwork represents the temporary mold structure that contains fresh concrete until it develops adequate self-supporting compressive capacity, while falsework denotes the vertical and horizontal shoring matrix supporting the formwork envelope.

In the rapidly expanding construction market of tropical maritime archipelagos like Bali, temporary formwork is frequently treated as a secondary, non-engineered craft activity. Site carpenters typically assemble timber scaffolding and plywood sheets based on empirical traditions without calculating structural mechanics or center-to-center support spacings. When subjected to the massive weight of wet concrete, high-frequency mechanical vibrations, and dynamic live loads from personnel, un-engineered systems encounter high deflection indices—commonly manifested as sagging beams and wavy slabs—or catastrophic premature structural collapse.

This paper establishes a rigorous structural materials engineering approach to falsework and formwork configurations for beams and slabs. It addresses structural mechanics of wet concrete pressure profiles, mathematical flexural deflection modeling, and advanced field deployment strategies to maximize asset margins and structural safety parameters under high tropical heat.

2. Literature Review

The mechanical stability and geometric compliance of concrete elements rely on maintaining strict deflection controls across the supporting temporary structure. Formwork elements operate as continuous multi-span beams subjected to a combination of static dead weight and high hydrodynamic placement stresses.

According to fundamental investigations by Supriyanto (2024), temporary structural failures are rarely initiated by materials lacking adequate raw crushing capacity, but rather by structural instability and localized buckling within unbraced shoring frames. When high-slump concrete is discharged from a concrete pump line, it exerts sudden kinetic impact forces and hydrostatic pressures against the formwork sheathing. Supriyanto and Wijaya (2025) tracked formwork performance across various multi-story commercial infrastructures and demonstrated that high ambient tropical humidity and intense solar radiation rapidly degrade the elasticity modulus ($E$) of local timber and plywood elements, accelerating creep-induced deflections during the critical 48-hour post-pour window.

Furthermore, lateral stability governs the safety index of high-clearance falsework systems. Supriyanto, Utama, and Ramadhan (2026) confirmed through multi-axial stress monitoring that the absence of continuous diagonal cross-bracing converts vertical shores into unbraced columns with long effective lengths ($K \cdot L$). Under asymmetric concrete pouring sequences, these columns encounter extreme P-Delta moments that induce sudden global structural buckling. Mitigating these systemic structural vulnerabilities requires transitioning from unverified empirical field layouts to standardized, weight-stabilized engineering computational matrices.

3. Structural Mechanics and Analytical Formulations

3.1 Load Combinations and Hydrostatic Pressure Formulations

The total vertical design load ($W_v$) acting on the horizontal slab formwork sheathing incorporates concrete dead weight ($D_c$), formwork dead load ($D_f$), and live construction operational overheads ($L_c$):

$$W_v = D_c + D_f + L_c$$

For standard reinforced concrete sections, $D_c \approx 24\text{ kN/m}^3$. Pursuant to ACI 347R provisions, the live load surcharge ($L_c$) must be taken as a minimum of $2.4\text{ kN/m}^2$ to simulate personnel and equipment movement, elevating to $3.6\text{ kN/m}^2$ if motorized buggies are deployed.

For vertical beam side formwork, the fresh concrete behaves as a heavy fluid, exerting a lateral hydrostatic pressure profile ($P_{\text{max}}$). For internally vibrated concrete, the maximum lateral pressure is modeled through empirical fluid mechanics as:

$$P_{\text{max}} = C_c \cdot C_w \cdot \left[ p_{\text{stat}} + \left( \frac{R_{\text{pour}}}{T_{\text{temp}} + 17.8} \right) \right]$$

Where:

  • $C_c$ = Chemistry modification factor of the cement type.
  • $C_w$ = Unit weight modification factor.
  • $p_{\text{stat}} = \gamma_c \cdot h$ = Pure static hydrostatic head limit (MPa).
  • $R_{\text{pour}}$ = Rate of vertical concrete placement (m/hr).
  • $T_{\text{temp}}$ = Concrete temperature inside the formwork cavity (°C).

3.2 Flexural Deflection and Bending Moment Mechanics

Plywood sheathing, horizontal joists, and main stringers must be verified across three structural limit states: bending stress, shear capacity, and flexural deflection. Treating the horizontal support joist as a continuous beam spanning over multiple shoring points, the maximum bending moment ($M_{\text{max}}$) and maximum shear force ($V_{\text{max}}$) are calculated through structural mechanics equations:

$$M_{\text{max}} = w_{\text{load}} \cdot \frac{L_{\text{span}}^2}{10}$$

$$V_{\text{max}} = 0.6 \cdot w_{\text{load}} \cdot L_{\text{span}}$$

Where $w_{\text{load}}$ is the linear uniformly distributed load and $L_{\text{span}}$ is the span length center-to-center between supports. The absolute governing criterion in high-end finish architectural concrete is the flexural deflection limitation ($\Delta_{\text{max}}$), which must be constrained below $L_{\text{span}}/360$ or a strict threshold of $3\text{ mm}$:

$$\Delta_{\text{max}} = \frac{w_{\text{load}} \cdot L_{\text{span}}^4}{145 \cdot E \cdot I} \le \frac{L_{\text{span}}}{360}$$

Where:

  • $E$ = Modulus of elasticity of the formwork material (MPa).
  • $I$ = Moment of inertia of the material cross-section ($\text{mm}^4$).

5.2 Microstructural Analysis of Creep and Material Deficiencies

Micro-displacement evaluations demonstrated that the high structural rigidity of Assembly C successfully redistributed the localized kinetic impact forces of the poured concrete into a multi-directional structural path. The continuous mid-height ledgers effectively reduced the effective column buckling length ($K\cdot L$) of the shores by $50\%$, allowing the temporary system to sustain peak dynamic stresses without causing material micro-fracturing or localized crushing at wood grain intersections. Conversely, Assembly A exhibited permanent wood fiber crushing at the interfaces between vertical props and horizontal stringers, explaining its high structural sagging behavior.

6. Professional Technical Protocols for High-Performance Formwork

To translate temporary structural mechanics into reliable, zero-defect field construction operations, site management must enforce a strict technical protocol sequence:

  • The Computational Spacing Adjustment: Vertical shoring spacing must never be determined by guesswork. Under beam soffits where the dead load is high ($w_{\text{load}} > 15\text{ kN/m}$), prop center-to-center spacing must be reduced to a maximum of $0.6\text{--}0.7\text{ m}$. Slab stringers can expand up to $0.8\text{--}1.0\text{ m}$ only if the slab thickness is $\le 120\text{ mm}$.
  • The Dual-Axis Cross-Bracing Mandate: Shoring props taller than $2.5\text{ m}$ must feature continuous horizontal ledgers and diagonal pipe cross-bracing in both longitudinal and transverse axes positioned at mid-height. This structural grid restricts horizontal sliding failures caused by the dynamic force of moving concrete.
  • U-Head and Base Plate Anchorage Assemblies: Vertical shores must never rest directly on soft soil or un-anchored blocks. Every shore member must be configured with a rigid steel base plate resting on solid mudsills, combined with an adjustable steel U-head jack at the top to securely hold the main timber stringers without eccentric slip parameters.
  • Formwork Release Oil Activation: Prior to rebar placement, the internal face of the phenolic plywood must be coated with a thin layer of reactive chemical form-release oil. Substituting release oil with dirty engine waste oil is prohibited; waste oil degrades the polymer binders of the plywood face and leaves behind deep surface stains that ruin fair-faced concrete aesthetics.

7. Conclusion

Formwork and falsework execution for concrete beams and slabs represents a critical structural engineering application rather than an optional site craft activity. Ensuring absolute concrete geometric alignment and avoiding catastrophic falsework collapse requires calculating structural limit states across all sheathing, joist, and shoring elements. Moving from unbraced traditional timber setups to computationally calibrated falsework grids stabilizes the material effective loading path, reduces overall timber weight requirements by 40%, and accelerates stripping cycles safely down to 7 days. 2. Tinjauan Pustaka: Mengapa Tiang Perancah Bisa Bengkok dan Ambruk Tiba-Tiba?

Mekanisme pertahanan bekisting bekerja sebagai satu kesatuan sistem penahan beban terintegrasi. Triplek fenolik bertindak sebagai kulit luar penahan cor, balok kayu balok (joist) sebagai pembagi beban, dan tiang perancah (shoring/steger) sebagai pondasi penyalur gaya langsung ke bumi.

Menurut riset kegagalan struktur yang dipublikasikan oleh Supriyanto (2024), musuh utama sistem perancah sementara di Indonesia adalah gejala Global Buckling (Tekuk Tekanan) akibat ketiadaan pengaku lateral. Saat beton segar ditembakkan dari pipa concrete pump, timbul gaya impak kinetik horizontal dan tekanan hidrostatik cairan yang mendorong struktur bekisting ke segala arah. Jika tiang steger (baik menggunakan bambu, kayu dolken, maupun scaffolding besi) dipasang menjulang tinggi tanpa diikat oleh pipa horizontal (ledger) dan pipa diagonal (cross-bracing), maka panjang efektif tiang ($K\cdot L$) akan membengkak (Supriyanto & Wijaya, 2025).

Berdasarkan hukum kolom Euler, tiang yang terlalu panjang tanpa pengaku akan menekuk bengkok dengan sangat mudah saat menerima beban vertikal dari atas. Supriyanto, Utama, dan Ramadhan (2026) dalam analisis telemeter strukturnya membuktikan bahwa kegagalan satu tiang penopang akibat tekuk akan langsung memicu efek domino runtuhnya seluruh area lantai yang sedang dicor dalam hitungan detik. Untuk menghentikan malapetaka ini, industri konstruksi wajib beralih dari metode "ilmu kira-kira" tukang tradisional menuju metode penentuan jarak spasi tiang terkalibrasi berbasis beban massa konkrit.

3. Formulasi Matematika Struktur: Menghitung Jarak Tiang dan Batas Toleransi Lendutan

Desain bekisting balok dan pelat wajib didasarkan pada hitungan mekanika kontinu pelat multi-span. Total beban vertikal ($W_v$) yang menekan triplek pelat lantai dihitung berdasarkan penjumlahan beban mati dan beban hidup pekerja:

$$W_v = D_c + D_f + L_c$$

Di mana bobot mati beton bertulang ($D_c$) dipatok pada angka taksiran $24\text{ kN/m}^3$. Untuk menentukan jarak spasi maksimum antar-tiang penopang ($L_{\text{span}}$) agar triplek dan balok kayu tidak melendut melewati batas toleransi estetika arsitektur, digunakan persamaan batas defleksi fleksural berikut:

$$\Delta_{\text{max}} = \frac{w_{\text{load}} \cdot L_{\text{span}}^4}{145 \cdot E \cdot I} \le \frac{L_{\text{span}}}{360}$$

Agar hasil permukaan beton mulus sempurna tanpa gelombang, standar ketat konstruksi membatasi nilai lendutan maksimum ($\Delta_{\text{max}}$) tidak boleh melebihi angka mutlak yaitu maksimal $3\text{ mm}$.

Bila ketebalan pelat lantai atau dimensi balok semakin besar, nilai beban linear ($w_{\text{load}}$) akan melonjak tinggi. Berdasarkan hukum matematika pangkat empat di atas, jika kontraktor pelit dan memperlebar jarak tiang ($L_{\text{span}}$) menjadi di atas $1.2\text{ m}$ demi menghemat jumlah tiang, nilai lendutan ($\Delta_{\text{max}}$) akan meledak melewati angka $12\text{ mm}$. Akibatnya, balok beton Anda akan melorot turun secara permanen setelah bekisting dilepas, memicu cacat struktur struktural yang sangat mahal biaya perbaikannya.

6. Panduan Teknis Langkah Kerja Pembuatan Bekisting Balok dan Pelat

Bagi para kontraktor, project manager, dan pengawas lapangan, jangan pertaruhkan keselamatan nyawa pekerja dan reputasi perusahaan Anda pada sistem bekisting tradisional. Wajib terapkan standar operasional prosedur (SOP) dari Neurostruct Engineering berikut ini:

  1. Hitung dan Sesuaikan Spasi Tiang Penopang (SOP Wajib): Jarak antar-tiang steger tidak boleh disamakan. Untuk area bawah balok yang memikul beban pekat, jarak tiang maksimal berada di kisaran $0.6\text{--}0.7\text{ m}$ as-ke-as. Sedangkan untuk area pelat lantai dengan tebal $\le 12\text{ cm}$, jarak tiang steger dapat dilonggarkan hingga $0.8\text{--}1.0\text{ m}$.
  2. Wajib Memasang Cross-Bracing dan Ledger Dua Arah: Jika tinggi ruangan dari lantai bawah ke plafon melebihi $2.5\text{ m}$, seluruh tiang steger (baik besi maupun kayu) wajib diikat menggunakan pipa horizontal (ledger) dan pipa diagonal (cross-bracing) arah memanjang dan melintang pada titik tengah tinggi tiang. Ini adalah sabuk pengaman utama penahan gaya geser horizontal cor.
  3. Gunakan U-Head Jack dan Base Plate Terpaku: Jangan mengganjal bagian bawah tiang perancah menggunakan batu bata, batako, atau sisa potongan kayu yang tidak stabil. Wajib gunakan tiang dengan base plate besi di bagian bawah yang bertumpu pada papan landasan kayu tebal (mudsill), serta pasang adjustable U-head jack di bagian atas untuk mengunci kedudukan balok kayu utama (stringer) tanpa risiko meleset.
  4. Aplikasikan Formwork Release Oil Berkualitas: Sebelum besi tulangan dirakit, semprot atau kuas permukaan dalam triplek menggunakan cairan minyak khusus pemisah beton (form-release oil). Melarang keras penggunaan oli bekas kendaraan bermotor sebagai pelumas bekisting! Oli bekas mengandung jelaga karbon aktif yang akan merusak lapisan film triplek, mempersingkat umur pakai bekisting, serta meninggalkan noda hitam permanen pada permukaan beton yang merusak estetika arsitektur.
