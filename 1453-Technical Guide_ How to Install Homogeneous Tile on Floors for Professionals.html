Technical Guide: How to Install Homogeneous Tile on Floors for Professionals

In Bali’s booming construction and renovation sector, homogeneous tiles (also known as porcelain tiles or full-body tiles) are the preferred choice for luxury villas, boutique hotels, resorts, and modern residences. Their high durability, low water absorption (<0.5%), and uniform color throughout the body make them ideal for Bali’s humid tropical climate and high-traffic areas. This comprehensive 3-page professional guide provides step-by-step instructions, tools, best practices, and quality control methods to ensure a flawless, long-lasting installation that meets international standards (ISO 13006) and local Indonesian practices.

#### 1. Understanding Homogeneous Tile and Project Preparation

Homogeneous tiles are dense, non-porous porcelain tiles fired at very high temperatures. They come in various sizes (30x30 cm, 60x60 cm, 80x80 cm, up to 120x120 cm) and finishes (matt, polished, anti-slip). Before starting, ensure the following:

- Structural floor is fully cured (minimum 28 days for concrete slab).

- Floor flatness tolerance: maximum 3 mm deviation in 3 meters (use straight edge and spirit level).

- Moisture content of substrate < 4% (use moisture meter).

- All plumbing, electrical, and AC installations are completed and tested.

Tools and Materials Required:

- Notched trowel (6–12 mm depending on tile size)

- Rubber mallet, tile cutter / wet saw, angle grinder

- Tile spacers (1–3 mm), laser level, grout float

- Adhesive: high-quality cement-based tile adhesive (Class C2TE or C2TES1 for large-format tiles)

- Grout: epoxy grout for wet areas or polymer-modified cement grout

- Primer, waterproofing membrane (especially in bathrooms and pool decks in Bali)

#### 2. Step-by-Step Installation Process

Step 1: Surface Preparation

Clean the floor thoroughly from dust, grease, and loose particles. Apply primer if the substrate is porous. For high-humidity Bali areas, install a waterproofing membrane and allow it to cure 24–48 hours.

Step 2: Layout Planning

Dry-lay the tiles to determine the best starting point (center of room or from the main entrance). Mark reference lines with chalk or laser level. Plan cuts so that perimeter tiles are at least half-size to maintain aesthetic balance.

Step 3: Mixing and Applying Adhesive

Mix adhesive according to manufacturer instructions (use mechanical mixer). Spread adhesive with notched trowel using the “comb and butter” method for large tiles (>60x60 cm). Apply adhesive to both floor and back of tile (back-buttering) to achieve 95–100% coverage and prevent hollow sounds.

Step 4: Laying the Tiles

Place tiles along reference lines. Press firmly and tap with rubber mallet. Maintain consistent joint width with spacers. Check level every 4–6 tiles using spirit level or laser. Work in small sections (1–2 m²) to avoid skinning of adhesive.

Step 5: Cutting and Fitting

Use wet saw for straight cuts and angle grinder with diamond blade for L-shaped or curved cuts. For large-format tiles common in Bali villas, use rail-guided tile cutters or electric scoring tools.

Step 6: Grouting

Wait 24–48 hours (or as per adhesive instructions). Mix grout to creamy consistency. Apply with rubber float at 45° angle. Clean excess grout immediately with damp sponge. For polished tiles, use haze remover after 24 hours.

Step 7: Final Cleaning and Protection

Clean the entire floor with pH-neutral cleaner. Apply tile sealer on polished surfaces (optional but recommended in Bali’s dusty environment). Protect the floor with cardboard or plywood for 7–14 days during construction.

#### 3. Quality Control and Common Mistakes in Bali Projects

- Avoid hollow tiles: tap-test every tile after installation.

- Expansion joints: provide movement joints every 4–6 meters and at room perimeters (especially important in Bali’s temperature fluctuations).

- Slope for wet areas: minimum 1–2% slope toward drain.

- Common errors: insufficient adhesive coverage, wrong adhesive type for outdoor use, ignoring substrate moisture, or rushing the curing time.

Quantity Calculation Example (for quick estimation):

Area (m²) × 1.05 (5% waste for cutting) = required tile quantity.

Adhesive consumption: 4–6 kg/m² for 60x60 cm tiles.

#### 4. Professional Tips for Bali Construction

- Choose anti-slip tiles (R10–R12 rating) for pool decks and outdoor terraces.

- In coastal areas (Seminyak, Canggu, Nusa Dua), use marine-grade adhesive and grout to resist salt air.

- For underfloor heating or radiant cooling systems (increasingly popular in luxury Bali villas), use flexible adhesive (S1 or S2 class).

- Always follow manufacturer’s technical data sheet and test a small area first.

### Cara Memasang Homogeneous Tile pada Lantai (Versi Bahasa Indonesia)

Author: edisupriyanto@gmail.com

Di industri konstruksi Bali yang terus berkembang, homogeneous tile (ubin porselen full body) menjadi pilihan utama untuk lantai villa mewah, hotel, dan rumah modern. Panduan profesional dua halaman ini memberikan langkah pemasangan yang tepat, akurat, dan sesuai standar.

#### 1. Persiapan Sebelum Pemasangan

Pastikan lantai beton sudah kering minimal 28 hari dan rata (deviasi maksimal 3 mm dalam 3 meter). Kadar air lantai < 4%. Siapkan alat: trowel bergerigi, pemotong ubin basah, waterpass laser, spacer, palu karet, dan adhesive berkualitas tinggi (kelas C2TE atau C2TES1).

Bahan pendukung: primer, membrane waterproofing (khusus area basah), grout, dan sealer.

#### 2. Langkah Pemasangan

Langkah 1: Persiapan Permukaan

Bersihkan lantai dari debu dan kotoran. Oles primer dan pasang membrane waterproofing jika diperlukan.

Langkah 2: Perencanaan Layout

Susun ubin kering (dry lay) untuk menentukan titik awal. Buat garis referensi dengan laser level.

Langkah 3: Pemasangan Ubin

Campur adhesive sesuai petunjuk. Oles dengan trowel bergerigi. Pasang ubin dengan metode back-buttering untuk cakupan 100%. Gunakan spacer dan cek kerataan setiap beberapa ubin.

Langkah 4: Pemotongan

Potong ubin dengan wet saw untuk hasil rapi.

Langkah 5: Pengisian Nat (Grouting)

Tunggu 24–48 jam, lalu isi nat dengan grout. Bersihkan sisa nat saat masih basah.

Langkah 6: Pembersihan Akhir

Cuci lantai dengan pembersih netral dan pasang pelindung selama 7–14 hari.

#### 3. Tips Khusus Proyek Bali

- Gunakan ubin anti-slip untuk teras dan kolam renang.

- Di daerah pantai, pilih adhesive tahan garam.

- Beri joint ekspansi setiap 4–6 meter.

- Hitung kebutuhan: luas lantai × 1,05 (cadangan potongan 5%).
