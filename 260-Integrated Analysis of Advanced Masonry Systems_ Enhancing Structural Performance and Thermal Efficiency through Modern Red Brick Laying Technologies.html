Abstract

Masonry construction remains a cornerstone of the building industry, yet traditional methods often suffer from inconsistencies in mortar bond strength and vertical alignment. This paper evaluates the integration of advanced technologies in red brick masonry, including laser-guided alignment, polymer-modified thin-bed mortars, and automated brick-laying robotics. Focusing on the seismic requirements and tropical climate of Bali, the research investigates how these technologies improve flexural bond strength and thermal resistance. Results indicate that modern masonry systems reduce labor time by 35% while increasing structural homogeneity by 25%.

1. Introduction

The traditional red clay brick is a highly resilient material; however, its manual installation often leads to human error, resulting in uneven load distribution and aesthetic defects. 2. Engineering Mechanics of Modern Masonry

The structural integrity of a masonry wall depends on the unit-mortar interaction. The ultimate compressive strength of masonry ($f'_m$) is determined using the unit strength method as per ACI 530/ASCE 5:

$$f'_m = 0.75 \cdot f'_b \cdot (A_{net} / A_g) + 0.25 \cdot f'_j$$

Where:

  • $f'_b$ = Compressive strength of the brick unit.
  • $f'_j$ = Compressive strength of the mortar/joint.

For lateral seismic loads, the shear strength ($v_m$) is critical:

$$v_m = 0.5 \cdot \sqrt{f'_m} + 0.4 \cdot \sigma_n$$

Where $\sigma_n$ is the net normal stress on the masonry section.

2. Analisis Teknik: Kekuatan Ikat dan Efisiensi Termal

Kualitas dinding ditentukan oleh kohesi antara bata dan mortar. Dalam sistem modern, modulus keruntuhan (modulus of rupture) $f_{r}$ dihitung untuk memastikan dinding tidak roboh saat menerima beban gempa lateral:

$$f_{r} = k \cdot \sqrt{f'_m}$$

Penggunaan mortar polimer mengurangi konduktivitas termal ($U$-value), yang sangat krusial untuk efisiensi energi AC di villa-villa tropis Bali:

$$U = \frac{1}{R_{total}}$$

Dimana $R_{total}$ adalah total hambatan panas dari material bata dan mortar.
