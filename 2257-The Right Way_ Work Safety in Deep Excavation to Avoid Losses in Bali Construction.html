The Right Way: Work Safety in Deep Excavation to Avoid Losses in Bali Construction

Author: edisupriyanto@gmail.com

Deep excavation (pekerjaan galian dalam) is one of the most hazardous stages in construction, especially for villa basements, swimming pool foundations, retaining walls, and multi-level resort developments in Bali. In 2026, with stricter regulations and higher project values, implementing the right safety practices is no longer just about compliance — it is a critical strategy to prevent financial losses, project delays, injuries, and legal problems.

Deep excavation in Bali presents unique risks due to the island’s steep topography, unstable volcanic soils, high groundwater tables during the rainy season, and frequent seismic activity. Collapses, soil sliding, flooding, and falling objects can cause devastating consequences: worker fatalities, equipment damage, rework costs, and even total project suspension by authorities.

The Right Safety Practices for Deep Excavation:

1. Comprehensive Planning and Risk Assessment

Before any digging begins, conduct thorough geotechnical investigations and soil testing. Develop a detailed excavation safety plan that includes shoring design, dewatering systems, and emergency procedures. All plans must align with SNI standards and obtain proper permits.

2. Proper Shoring and Trench Support Systems

Never rely on natural soil slopes for deep excavations (>1.5 meters). Use professional shoring methods such as soldier piles with timber lagging, sheet piles, diaphragm walls, or hydraulic trench boxes. In Bali’s soft soils, combining these with soil nailing or ground anchors provides extra stability.

3. Safe Excavation Sequencing

Excavate in controlled stages rather than opening large areas at once. Maintain proper benching or sloping (1:1 or gentler ratio) where shoring is not used. Always keep heavy equipment and spoil piles at least 2 meters away from the edge.

4. Dewatering and Groundwater Control

Bali’s high rainfall and permeable soils often cause groundwater ingress. Install sump pumps, well points, or deep wells early. Poor dewatering leads to base heave, sidewall collapse, and weakened bearing capacity.

5. Daily Safety Inspections and Monitoring

Appoint a competent safety officer to inspect excavation walls, shoring integrity, and signs of movement every shift. Use inclinometers, piezometers, and crack monitors for real-time data on critical sites.

6. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and Emergency Readiness

All workers must wear harnesses with lifelines when entering excavations deeper than 1.2 meters. Provide rescue equipment, first-aid stations, and trained rescue teams on site. Ensure clear access/egress ladders every 7–8 meters.

7. Training and Competency

Only certified operators and supervisors should handle deep excavation. Regular toolbox meetings and safety drills significantly reduce human error.

Common losses from unsafe deep excavation in Bali include:

- Costly rework after collapse (can reach 30–50% of excavation budget)

- Stop-work orders from Disnaker or building authorities

- Insurance claims rejection due to negligence

- Reputation damage affecting future projects

Cara Tepat: Keselamatan Kerja pada Pekerjaan Galian Dalam agar Tidak Rugi

Pekerjaan galian dalam merupakan salah satu tahap paling berisiko tinggi dalam konstruksi, terutama untuk basement villa, pondasi kolam renang, dinding penahan tanah, dan pembangunan resort bertingkat di Bali. Di tahun 2026, dengan regulasi yang lebih ketat dan nilai proyek yang semakin besar, penerapan keselamatan kerja yang tepat bukan hanya soal kepatuhan, melainkan strategi penting untuk mencegah kerugian finansial, keterlambatan, kecelakaan, dan masalah hukum.

Galian dalam di Bali memiliki risiko khusus karena topografi curam, tanah vulkanik yang tidak stabil, permukaan air tanah tinggi saat musim hujan, serta aktivitas gempa yang sering. Longsor, runtuhnya dinding galian, banjir, dan benda jatuh dapat menyebabkan korban jiwa, kerusakan alat berat, biaya perbaikan mahal, hingga penghentian proyek oleh pihak berwenang.

Cara Tepat Keselamatan Kerja Galian Dalam:

1. Perencanaan dan Penilaian Risiko Menyeluruh

Lakukan investigasi geoteknik dan pengujian tanah sebelum menggali. Buat rencana keselamatan galian yang lengkap termasuk desain penyangga dan sistem dewatering.

2. Sistem Penyangga (Shoring) yang Profesional

Jangan pernah mengandalkan lereng tanah alami untuk galian dalam (>1,5 meter). Gunakan soldier pile, sheet pile, trench box, atau ground anchor yang dirancang ahli.

3. Urutan Galian yang Aman

Gali secara bertahap, jaga jarak peralatan berat minimal 2 meter dari tepi galian, dan terapkan benching atau sloping yang benar.

4. Pengendalian Air Tanah

Pasang pompa sump, well point, atau deep well sejak awal untuk mencegah banjir dan pelemahan tanah.

5. Inspeksi Harian dan Monitoring

Lakukan pemeriksaan setiap shift oleh petugas keselamatan. Gunakan alat monitoring seperti inclinometer untuk mendeteksi pergerakan tanah.

6. Peralatan Pelindung Diri dan Kesiapan Darurat

Wajibkan penggunaan full body harness, ladder akses yang aman, dan tim rescue yang terlatih.

7. Pelatihan dan Kompetensi

Hanya operator dan supervisor bersertifikat yang boleh bekerja. Lakukan toolbox meeting rutin.

Kerugian akibat galian dalam yang tidak aman di Bali sangat besar: rework mahal, stop-work order, penolakan klaim asuransi, hingga rusaknya reputasi perusahaan.
