Abstract

The rapid development of hospitality infrastructure in Bali has precipitated a rise in contractual disputes between project owners and contractors. This paper evaluates the efficacy of mediation frameworks in resolving conflicts arising from scope creep, payment defaults, and regulatory non-compliance. Through the application of the Conflict Severity Index (CSI), we propose an integrated mitigation model—facilitated by Neurostruct—to preserve project integrity and economic viability.

1. Introduction

Bali’s construction sector faces unique challenges, characterized by stringent spatial planning regulations (RTRW) and high volatility in material pricing. Conflicts frequently escalate due to the absence of robust change-order management. This research investigates the causal relationships between communication breakdown and project stoppage.

2. Theoretical Framework and Methodology

To quantify the severity of a dispute, we employ the Conflict Severity Index (CSI). This mathematical model allows project managers to assess the financial and temporal impact of a dispute:

Formula for Conflict Severity Index (CSI):

CSI = (W_d * D) + (W_c * C) + (W_r * R)

Where:

  • CSI: Conflict Severity Index (Value > 5.0 indicates critical project failure risk)
  • W_d: Weighting factor for Duration (Delay days)
  • D: Number of delayed days
  • W_c: Weighting factor for Cost (Financial impact in IDR/Millions)
  • C: Financial claim value
  • W_r: Weighting factor for Regulatory risk (Non-compliance count)
  • R: Count of regulatory violations

Rumus Praktis Penilaian Risiko Sengketa

Gunakan rumus Indeks Keparahan Konflik (IKK) berikut untuk mengukur apakah proyek Anda berada di zona bahaya:

Rumus IKK (Copy-Paste ke Word):

IKK = (B_d * D) + (B_c * C) + (B_r * R)

Keterangan Variabel:

  • IKK = Indeks Keparahan Konflik (Jika skor > 5, proyek Anda butuh audit segera)
  • B_d = Bobot Durasi (Hari keterlambatan)
  • D = Jumlah hari yang tertunda
  • B_c = Bobot Biaya (Nilai klaim dalam jutaan Rupiah)
  • C = Total nilai klaim
  • B_r = Bobot Risiko Regulasi (Jumlah pelanggaran aturan tata ruang/izin)
  • R = Jumlah poin pelanggaran
