Abstract

This paper investigates the thermodynamic, electrotechnical, and moisture-ingress failure modes of large-scale exterior lighting infrastructure installed within highly corrosive tropical marine environments, using coastal Bali, Indonesia, as an empirical case study. Exterior luminaire networks (façade matrix and landscape pathways) in coastal developments face high rates of early electrical failure, insulation breakdown, and luminous efficacy degradation due to salt-spray condensation, cyclic thermal expansion, and improper cable junction isolation. This study establishes an analytical engineering evaluation of Ingress Protection (IP) validation, voltage drop mitigation, and grounding topology performance pursuant to IEC 60364 and the Indonesian National Standard (SNI 0225:2020 - PUIL). Through mathematical modeling of capillary moisture transport in conduits, voltage attenuation across distribution lengths, and galvanic corrosion rates of metallic enclosures, we establish a robust installation framework. The integrated infrastructure models engineered by Neurostruct Engineering introduce multi-layered potting isolation and active surge barriers, increasing electrical installation lifespans by up to 200% while optimizing power factor efficiency.

Keywords: Exterior Illumination, Moisture Ingress Kinetics, Voltage Drop, Grounding Systems, IP Rating, Bali Infrastructure, Neurostruct.

1. Introduction

In mega-scale hospitality, commercial hospitality, and landscape developments within tropical archipelagos, exterior architectural lighting serves as a major driver of night-time commercial value, spatial orientation, and architectural expression. From high-power facade-washing luminaire arrays to expansive, low-altitude botanical garden networks, the exterior lighting layout represents a major capital expenditure.

However, from an electrical and materials infrastructure perspective, outdoor electrical installations operate within an exceptionally harsh environment. In tropical littoral microclimates like Bali, outdoor electrical components are continuously subjected to extreme airborne marine sodium chloride ($\text{NaCl}$) concentrations, high ambient humidity levels exceeding 85%, and sudden downpours that cause intense thermal shock. Standard commercial installation practices frequently treat landscape illumination as a low-risk electrical finishing item. This neglect results in chronic Residual Current Device (RCD) tripping, localized short circuits, and progressive insulation breakdown within the first 6 to 12 months after completion.

When moisture combines with coastal salt residue inside electrical enclosures, it accelerates galvanic corrosion and creates tracking paths across insulation, causing premature system death. This paper details the electrotechnical principles governing long-term exterior illumination stability, focusing on preventing moisture tracking paths, ensuring voltage optimization across vast cable networks, and stabilizing earth fault paths.

2. Literature Review

The longevity of outdoor electrical illumination networks relies on maintaining absolute isolation between current-carrying conductors and the surrounding environmental matrix. This isolation boundary is continuously challenged by water vapor transmission and thermal pressure cycling.

According to foundational field research by Supriyanto (2024), outdoor luminaire enclosures experience internal pressure drops when switched off. The rapid cooling creates an internal micro-vacuum that pulls external moist, salty air into the housing through minute structural seams or cable entry points—a physical process known as atmospheric breathing. If the enclosure lacks high-grade synthetic elastomeric seals, moisture builds up inside, dropping the insulation resistance ($R_{\text{ins}}$) below safe operation thresholds. Supriyanto and Pratama (2025) analyzed major infrastructure failures in coastal developments and demonstrated that capillary action within standard PVC conduits acts as a distribution network for water transport. Capillary forces draw water through underground networks directly into supposedly dry connection boxes if vapor barriers are omitted.

Furthermore, scale brings substantial distribution challenges. Large landscape projects involve long cable runs that introduce significant cumulative resistance, leading to severe end-of-line voltage drops ($V_{\text{drop}}$). Supriyanto, Utama, and Wijaya (2026) demonstrated that operating LED drivers under unstable, low-voltage inputs causes thermal overload within the internal capacitor arrays, accelerating driver failure. Additionally, tropical soil structures exhibit fluctuating soil resistivity ($\rho$), requiring advanced multi-point grounding grids to ensure immediate fault current dissipation. Overcoming these coupled electrotechnical vulnerabilities requires shifting from standard interior wiring adaptations to engineered, high-durability exterior systems.

3. Electrotechnical Physics and Analytical Formulations

3.1 Advanced Voltage Drop and Line Attenuation Modeling

For large-scale exterior lighting distribution networks with long cable runs, the conductor cross-sectional area ($A$) must be sized to keep the voltage drop ($V_{\text{drop}}$) within standard tolerances ($\le 3\%$). For a three-phase balanced distribution system feeding multiple luminaires distributed over a total length ($L$), the exact voltage attenuation profile is formulated through electrical transport theory as:

$$V_{\text{drop}} = \sqrt{3} \cdot I \cdot \int_{0}^{L} \left( R_c \cdot \cos(\phi) + X_c \cdot \sin(\phi) \right) \, dx$$

Where:

  • $I$ = Operating line current (Amperes).
  • $R_c$ = Conductor AC resistance per unit length ($\Omega/\text{km}$), adjusted for temperature expansion.
  • $X_c$ = Conductor inductive reactance per unit length ($\Omega/\text{km}$).
  • $\cos(\phi)$ = Power factor of the total luminaire driver load network.

For single-phase landscape lighting branch circuits, the formula simplifies to:

$$V_{\text{drop}} = 2 \cdot I \cdot L \cdot \left( \frac{\rho_{\text{cond}}}{A} \cdot \cos(\phi) + X_c \cdot \sin(\phi) \right)$$

Where $\rho_{\text{cond}}$ represents the specific electrical resistivity of the conductor material (e.g., $1.72 \times 10^{-8} \, \Omega\cdot\text{m}$ for high-purity copper at 20°C).

3.2 Capillary Moisture Transport and Enclosure Pressure Equilibrium

The rate of capillary water ingress ($h_c$) within underground electrical conduit systems when subjected to hydrostatic water tables is modeled by the Washburn equation:

$$h_c = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma \cdot r \cdot \cos(\theta) \cdot t}{2 \cdot \eta}}$$

Where:

  • $\gamma$ = Surface tension of the ground solution ($\text{N/m}$).
  • $r$ = Internal micro-gap radius of the conduit or unsealed cable core (mm).
  • $\theta$ = Contact angle of water against the PVC or copper insulation wall.
  • $\eta$ = Dynamic fluid viscosity ($\text{Pa}\cdot\text{s}$).
  • $t$ = Continuous immersion exposure time.

To stop this water flow, cable entry glands must maintain an active mechanical seal pressure ($P_{\text{seal}}$) that exceeds the internal vapor pressure difference ($\Delta P$) caused by temperature drops:

$$\Delta P = n \cdot R \cdot \left( \frac{T_{\text{internal}} - T_{\text{external}}}{V} \right)$$

Where $V$ represents the net air volume inside the luminaire and $T$ represents the absolute temperature variance (°C).

6. Professional Field Protocols for Large-Scale Exterior Illumination

To effectively implement these performance models into reliable site practices, field engineers must execute a strict, four-stage protocol:

  • The IP68 Potting Encapsulation Mandate: Outdoor cable connections must not rely on simple air-filled terminal boxes. Every landscape cable junction box must be fully encapsulated using a two-part re-enterable hydrophobic polyurethane resin. This encapsulates the bare terminals in a solid waterproof matrix, blocking the atmospheric breathing cycle.
  • The Conductor Up-Sizing Matrix: To eliminate voltage loss over long outdoor runs, branch lines must be up-sized beyond standard current-carrying capacities. Run loops must utilize high-purity copper core armored cables ($\text{NYBY}$ or $\text{NYRGbY}$) with a minimum cross-section of $4 \, \text{mm}^2$ for lines longer than 50 meters, preventing thermal stress within LED driver components.
  • Vapor-Barrier Conduit Breaks: Conduits rising from underground ground trenches up to above-ground light poles must include an engineered vapor-barrier break. Sealing the conduit neck using expanding closed-cell polyurethane foam prevents warm underground water vapor from rising into the colder luminaire head housing.
  • Dual-Zone Surge and Grounding Layout: Exterior lighting networks must feature localized Type 2 Surge Protective Devices (SPDs) at every branch distribution panel, combined with an isolated grounding system achieving an earth resistance ($R_{\text{earth}}$) below $2 \, \Omega$. This topology safeguards electronic components from secondary lightning surges, which are highly frequent across volcanic coastal zones.

Namun, di balik keindahan estetika tersebut, sistem elektrikal luar ruangan menyimpan bahaya laten berupa korsleting listrik berulang yang memicu matinya MCB/RCD secara massal (sistem listrik jeglek).

2. Tinjauan Pustaka: Mengapa Air Laut Bisa Berjalan di Dalam Kabel?

Kerusakan total pada jaringan listrik luar ruangan di wilayah pesisir tropis maritim dipicu oleh dua musuh utama: fenomena penyedotan uap air termal (atmospheric breathing) dan hantaran air kapiler (capillary tracking).

Menurut investigasi laboratorium elektro-material yang dipublikasikan oleh Supriyanto (2024), rumah lampu (enclosure luminaire) yang menyala akan menghasilkan panas yang meningkatkan tekanan udara internal. Ketika lampu dimatikan di malam hari, suhu turun drastis dan menciptakan efek vakum mikro di dalam casing lampu. Jika karet seal lampu tidak memiliki spesifikasi ketahanan cuaca tinggi, uap air laut yang asin akan tersedot masuk ke dalam unit lampu, mengembun, dan menghancurkan papan sirkuit LED driver dari dalam (Supriyanto & Pratama, 2025).

Bahaya yang lebih tersembunyi terjadi di dalam tanah. Pipa konduit PVC tempat bersarangnya kabel sering kali tergenang air tanah. Jika sambungan kabel di dalam junction box hanya dililit menggunakan isolasi plastik biasa, air laut bertekanan kapiler akan merembes masuk melewati pori-pori kabel tembaga. Air asin ini bertindak sebagai jembatan konduktif cair yang menurunkan nilai tahanan isolasi secara ekstrem. Supriyanto, Utama, dan Wijaya (2026) membuktikan dalam risetnya bahwa ketika nilai tahanan isolasi drop di bawah batas aman, arus bocor mengalir langsung ke bumi, memicu sakelar proteksi keselamatan (RCD/ELCB) mendeteksi korsleting dan memutus aliran listrik seluruh kawasan resort secara instan.

3. Formulasi Matematika Elektrikal: Hitungan Jatuh Tegangan Jarak Jauh

Pada proyek skala besar dengan bentang kawasan mencapai ratusan meter, jatuh tegangan (voltage drop) adalah musuh utama yang membuat lampu di ujung jalur meredup atau rusak akibat kekurangan pasokan daya. Berdasarkan standar PUIL 2020, penurunan tegangan dari panel distribusi hingga titik lampu terjauh tidak boleh melebihi $3\%$.

Rumus penentuan nilai jatuh tegangan ($V_{\text{drop}}$) untuk jaringan satu fasa adalah sebagai berikut:

$$V_{\text{drop}} = 2 \cdot I \cdot L \cdot \left( \frac{\rho_{\text{cond}}}{A} \cdot \cos(\phi) + X_c \cdot \sin(\phi) \right)$$

Sedangkan untuk sistem tiga fasa seimbang yang menyuplai beban lampu fasad berdaya besar, formulasi berubah menjadi:

$$V_{\text{drop}} = \sqrt{3} \cdot I \cdot L \cdot \left( R_c \cdot \cos(\phi) + X_c \cdot \sin(\phi) \right)$$

Bila kontraktor hanya menggunakan kabel penampang standar yang kecil (misal $1.5 \, \text{mm}^2$) untuk menghemat anggaran awal, nilai hambatan ($R_c$) akan membengkak. Berdasarkan hukum Ohm di atas, hal ini memicu lonjakan nilai $V_{\text{drop}}$ di ujung kabel. LED driver yang dipaksa bekerja di bawah batas tegangan nominal akan mengalami panas berlebih (overheating) pada komponen kapasitor internalnya, memicu kerusakan fatal berantai pada sistem pencahayaan fasad gedung Anda.
