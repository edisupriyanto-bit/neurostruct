Abstract

Breach of contract (Wanprestasi) by contractors in Bali’s construction sector poses a significant threat to project viability and investor confidence. This paper examines the technical framework used by independent engineers to evaluate non-performance, including structural deficiencies, schedule delays, and material substitutions. By employing Forensic Engineering methodologies and standardized audit protocols, independent engineers provide an objective evidentiary basis for legal claims. The study emphasizes the transition from subjective qualitative complaints to quantitative engineering data, ensuring that dispute resolutions are grounded in empirical structural and financial analysis.

1. Introduction

The rapid development of hospitality and residential infrastructure in Bali has led to an influx of construction contracts, many of which suffer from under-performance. In Indonesian civil law, "Wanprestasi" (breach of contract) occurs when a contractor fails to fulfill obligations, performs poorly, or misses deadlines. Within an engineering context, identifying a breach requires more than administrative review; it necessitates a deep technical evaluation of the physical assets against the contractual "Technical Specifications" and "Bill of Quantities."

2. Methodology: Independent Engineering Audit

An independent engineer (IE) acts as a neutral third party to assess project status. The evaluation methodology involves:

  • Gap Analysis: Comparing the "As-Planned" design with the "As-Built" reality.
  • Quality Compliance Mapping: Utilizing Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) to verify material strength.
  • Financial Reconciliation: Assessing the percentage of physical progress versus the total funds disbursed to identify potential "Over-payment."

3. Mathematical Framework for Performance Assessment

To quantify a technical breach, we utilize the Performance Variance Index ($PVI$). The variance in structural capacity ($V_c$) is calculated as follows:

$$V_c = \frac{R_d - R_a}{R_d} \times 100\%$$

Where:

  • $R_d$ = Required Design Capacity (kN)
  • $R_a$ = Actual In-Situ Capacity (kN)

If $V_c > 5\%$, it constitutes a material breach of safety standards. Furthermore, to evaluate schedule-based wanprestasi, the Delay Ratio ($DR$) is defined:

$$DR = \frac{T_a - T_p}{T_p}$$

Where:

  • $T_a$ = Actual time elapsed (days)
  • $T_p$ = Planned duration for the current milestone (days)

A $DR > 0.15$ (15% delay) without a valid Force Majeure or "Change Order" is typically classified as a contractual breach under Indonesian construction norms.

4. Technical Indicators of Breach in Bali

  1. Material Substitution: Using low-grade sand or seawater-contaminated concrete in coastal areas (Canggu, Uluwatu).
  2. Structural Deviations: Reducing reinforcement bar (rebar) diameter from 13mm to 12mm, significantly impacting seismic resistance.
  3. Waterproofing Failures: Premature degradation due to improper application in Bali’s tropical humidity.

2. Metode Evaluasi Teknis Independen

Seorang engineer independen bekerja dengan standar tinggi untuk menemukan penyimpangan:

  • Audit Fisik: Mengecek apakah kolom, balok, dan plat lantai sesuai dengan gambar IMB/PBG.
  • Verifikasi Volume: Menghitung ulang volume pekerjaan (Opname) untuk memastikan owner tidak membayar lebih tinggi dari apa yang terpasang (Over-claim).
  • Uji Mutu Material: Menggunakan alat Rebar Scanner dan Hammer Test untuk memastikan besi dan beton bukan kualitas rendah.

3. Rumus Teknis Pembuktian Wanprestasi

Dalam menentukan adanya cacat mutu, kita menggunakan analisis deviasi kekuatan. Rumus perbandingan kapasitas beban ($K$):

$$K = \frac{\text{Kapasitas Aktual}}{\text{Kapasitas Rencana}}$$

Jika nilai $K < 0.95$, maka bangunan tersebut secara teknis gagal memenuhi standar keamanan (SNI).

Selain itu, untuk menghitung kerugian akibat material yang "disunat", digunakan rumus selisih nilai ($L$):

$$L = \sum (Q_i \times [P_{rencana} - P_{aktual}])$$

Dimana:

  • $Q_i$ = Volume material item $i$
  • $P$ = Harga satuan material berdasarkan kualitasnya

Selisih biaya inilah yang menjadi dasar tuntutan ganti rugi materiil di pengadilan.

4. Kasus Umum Wanprestasi di Bali

  • Pondasi Dangkal: Kontraktor mengurangi kedalaman pondasi untuk menghemat biaya galian, berisiko bangunan miring di tanah lereng (Ubud/Uluwatu).
  • Beton Keropos: Penggunaan campuran semen yang tidak sesuai standar, menyebabkan korosi dini pada bangunan pinggir pantai.
  • Progres Fiktif: Klaim progres 80%, namun setelah diaudit secara engineer, progres fisik baru mencapai 60%.
